Brief Bio
Dr. Babasaheb R. Sankapal is a renowned Indian physicist and materials scientist, currently serving as Professor in the Department of Physics at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. He is a former Head of the Department of Physics, Chairman of the Board of Studies, and Associate Dean (Examinations) at VNIT.He earned his Ph.D. in 2001 from Shivaji University, Kolhapur, under the guidance of Prof. C. D. Lokhande. Following his doctorate, he gained international experience as a Scientist at the Helmholtz Centre in Berlin, Germany; a JSPS Postdoctoral Fellow at Gifu University, Japan; and a Research Associate at the University of Wisconsin, Milwaukee, USA.Dr. Sankapal is widely recognized for his contributions to nanomaterials and energy technologies. His research focuses on the synthesis and application of nano- and quantum materials in solar cells (including dye-sensitized and quantum dot-based), supercapacitors (electrochemical energy storage), and gas sensors. He employs simple, cost-effective chemical methods such as chemical bath deposition and SILAR to develop high-performance, flexible devices.Key Achievements and RecognitionsListed among the World’s Top 2% Scientists (Stanford University database) for consecutive years, including 2020–2025 (in both career-long and single-year categories).Ranked 104th in India and 6919th globally in Materials Science according to Research.com (2025 rankings).Achieved a strong AD Scientific Index ranking (India rank under 400 in recent listings).Top in VNIT among faculties as per Scopus h-Index and Citations & Google Scholar h-Index Awards and HonorsYoung Scientist Outstanding Work Presentation Award by the Materials Research Society of Japan (MRS-J) in 2005.Selected by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, to participate in the Lindau Meeting of Nobel Laureates in Germany (2001).Recipient of the Vidyan Ratn Award from Vidarbha Gaurav Pratishthan, Nagpur.·Vice-President (Nagpur Region) & Fellow of Maharashtra Academy of Sciences (FMASc)-(ALF1073) Recent HighlightsParticipated as a Senior S&T Leader in the Emerging Science, Technology, and Innovation Conclave (ESTIC-2025) in New Delhi, inaugurated by Hon’ble Prime Minister Narendra Modi.Appointed as a Member of the National Expert Advisory Committee (NEAC) under the Department of Science and Technology (DST) for the New & Emerging Energy Storage Technologies (NEST) program (announced in 2025).
