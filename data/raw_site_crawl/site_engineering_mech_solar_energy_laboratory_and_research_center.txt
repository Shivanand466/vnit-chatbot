The Solar Energy Laboratory and Research Center at VNIT Nagpur is committed to education and the development of advanced energy conversion technologies and sustainable energy solutions. This includes the development of energy storage technologies, promoting technology indigenization, harnessing solar energy, and improving energy conversion methods.
