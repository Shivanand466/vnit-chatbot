Dr. Shrabony Adhikary
Associate Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. Shrabony Adhikary is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. She is a distinguished academic and researcher specializing in Geotechnical Earthquake Engineering, with expertise in foundation design, soil amplification, soil-structure interaction, machine foundations, and performance-based seismic design. Since joining VNIT in 2016, she has been actively engaged in teaching, research, and mentoring, contributing significantly to the advancement of earthquake-resistant infrastructure in India.
Dr. Adhikary completed her B.Tech in Civil Engineering from the National Institute of Technology, Durgapur, in 2008, where she was awarded the Institute Gold Medal for securing the highest CGPA. She pursued her M.Tech in Soil Dynamics from the Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee, in 2010, and was honored with The Indian Service of Engineers Award for her outstanding academic performance. She continued her doctoral studies at IIT Roorkee, earning her Ph.D. on the topic “Effect of Soil Conditions on the Seismic Response of Structures.” Her doctoral research provided valuable insights into the influence of soil properties on seismic response, which has direct implications for safer structural design.
At VNIT Nagpur, Dr. Adhikary has successfully executed several prestigious research projects funded by national and state agencies. She received the SERB Early Career Research Award (DST) with a funding of ₹32.32 lakhs for her project “Site Classification Scheme and Amplification Factors for Indian Seismic Design Code.” She was subsequently awarded the SERB POWER Grant for Women Researchers with a funding of ₹26.72 lakhs for developing “Guidelines for Seismic Analysis of Concrete Gravity Dams – IS 1893 (Part V).” Currently, she is leading a major project titled “Assessment of Local Site Effects Specific to the State of Punjab: A Pre-requisite for Seismic Risk Reduction,” funded by the State Disaster Management Fund, Government of Punjab, with a total budget of ₹98.24 lakhs.
