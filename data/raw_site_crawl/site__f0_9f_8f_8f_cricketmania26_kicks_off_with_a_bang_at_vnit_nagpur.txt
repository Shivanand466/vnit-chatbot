🏏 Cricketmania’26 Kicks Off with a Bang at VNIT Nagpur.
vnit_web
November 9, 2026
News
The 26th edition of Cricketmania ’26 began on an exciting note with a friendly cricket match between current students and former members of the VNIT Cricket Team at the VNIT Cricket Ground.
The occasion was graced by Mr. Aditya Joshi, Founder of Cricketmania; Dr. Umesh Deshpande, Dean (Student Welfare); Dr. Pramod Padole, Former Director, VNIT; Dr. Atul Ballal, Faculty In-charge of Cricketmania; and Mr. Pratik Singh, President of Cricketmania, along with several alumni and cricket enthusiasts.
Beyond the game, the match celebrated the spirit of sportsmanship, strengthened alumni–student bonds, and provided an opportunity for alumni to share their valuable experiences and guidance with current students.
With this memorable beginning, Cricketmania ’26 is all set to bring more excitement from 1 to 9 December 2026.
