Dr. Arvind Kumar
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Currently, he serves as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, India. Prior to this, he was an Assistant Professor at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore, from 2020 to 2022. Dr. Kumar has been recognized for his significant contributions to research and academia. In 2025, he received the prestigious Young Faculty Research Fellowship (YFRF) Award from the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. In 2024, he was honored with the Scientific High-Level Visiting Fellowship (SSHN) by the Government of France, which facilitated his short-term research visit at the University of Rennes - IETR. His achievements include multiple prestigious fellowships and grants, such as the MeitY Fellowship, DST Young Scientist Awards (2018 & 2022), and the EuCAP-2019 Student Grant in Poland, Europe. He has also received various travel grants from the Government of India, including DST ITS (2018 & 2022), MeitY Travel Support, and CSIR Travel Grants (2019 & 2023). Beyond his research and academic endeavors, Dr. Kumar is a Senior Member of IEEE, actively contributing to advancements in field of SIW based antennas
