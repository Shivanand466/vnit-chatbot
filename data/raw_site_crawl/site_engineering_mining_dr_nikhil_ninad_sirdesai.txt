Dr. Nikhil Ninad Sirdesai​
Brief Bio
Dr. Nikhil Sirdesai is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mining Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur. His expertise includes rock mechanics and excavation engineering, with significant contributions to understanding the physico-mechanical response of rocks under various thermal conditions. Dr. Sirdesai holds a PhD from the IITB-Monash Research Academy (2013-17) and an Integrated Dual Degree (B.Tech-M.Tech) from IIT BHU (2007-12), Varanasi. His field experience includes his tenure as Management Technical Trainee (MTT) in Monnet Ispat and Energy Limited (2012-13) and in consultancy services extended during his ongoing tenure as Assistant Professor. He has contributed over 50 research papers in reputed international and national journals, conference and books. He has received several awards, including the Outstanding Young Scientist award at the 5th ISRM Young Scholars’ Symposium on Rock Mechanics (2019). He has received 1 research grant worth INR 30 lakhs from Science and Engineering Research Board (SERB) under the start-up research grant (SRG) program in 2019 to analyse the behaviour of rocks under high temperatures. He is a life time member of various national and international societies such as ISRM, MEAI, ISEG, ISCA, ISRMTT , IEI and SME. He also serves on the review and editorial board of several prestigious international journals related to rock mechanics, tunneling and engineering geology.
