Dr. Rahul Tarodiya
Brief Bio https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=IPgwiJYAAAAJ&hl=en
Dr. Rahul Tarodiya is currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur. He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, IIT Roorkee, where his research focused on the performance and wear characteristics of centrifugal slurry pumps handling solid-liquid mixtures. He completed his M.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering from IIT (BHU) Varanasi and his B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering from S.G.S.I.T.S Indore.
Dr. Tarodiya has also worked as a Kreitman Postdoctoral Fellow at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Israel, from 2020-2022, where he carried out research on surface erosion, CFD-DEM simulations, and particulate flow behavior in engineering systems.
He has published several research articles in reputed international journals, including Powder Technology, Wear, Advanced Powder Technology, Journal of Tribology, and Atmospheric Pollution Research.
