Dr. Yarlagadda Madhavi
Assistant Professor
Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Yarlagadda Madhavi has joined the department as an Assistant Professor in June 2022. She did her B.Tech. from RGUKT, Nuzvid and Ph.D. from NIT Warangal and ARCI Hyderabad. Her doctoral thesis was on “High cycle plain-fatigue and corrosion-fatigue behavior of micro arc oxidation coated aluminum alloys”. Her research interests are in the area of Surface Engineering, Fatigue, Corrosion, Wear and Tribology, Additive Manufacturing. She taught courses like Metallurgical Thermodynamics and Kinetics, Mineral Processing, Superalloys, Particulate Technology.
