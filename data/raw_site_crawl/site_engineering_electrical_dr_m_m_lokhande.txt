Dr. M.M. Lokhande
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Makarand M. Lokhande, Associate Professor, Electrical Engineering Departmnet, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur received B.E. from Nagpur University from G.H. Raisoni College of Engineering, Nagpur and M.E. (Power System) from University of Pune, India, in 2001 and 2003 respectively in Electrical Engineering and PhD from Energy Science and Engineering Department of IIT Bombay, India in 2010. Since July 2009 to August 2013 he was with Pandit Deendayal Petrolueum Univesity Gandhinagar in Gujarat, India. Since 2013 to June 2015 he was with Electrical Engineering Department, Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology Surat, and Since June 2015 he is with Department of Electrical Engineering, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur. His research interest are electric vehicle, solar photovoltaic, microgrids, power electronics and machines.  He has several publications in international journals and conferences. He has also completed one DST project on Electric Vehicle and one DST project is under process. Under his supervision nine PhD have completed and six are ongoing. He is a Senior IEEE Member and Life member of ISTE. He teaches course based on Power Electronics, Electrical Machines, Electric Vehicle, Renewable Energy Systems.
