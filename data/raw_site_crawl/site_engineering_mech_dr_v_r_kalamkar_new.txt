Dr. V. R. Kalamkar
Brief Bio
Professor Vilas R. Kalamkar has been affiliated with the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, India, since September 2012. He completed an M.E. program at VJTI, Mumbai, in 1999 and earned his doctoral degree from IIT Bombay in 2007. Prior to that, he completed his B.E. in mechanical engineering in 1991 at the Government College of Engineering, Amaravati (MH).Currently, Dr. Vilas R. Kalamkar holds the position of Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur. His research is primarily centred on several key areas, including renewable energy, computational fluid dynamics (CFD) for thermofluid systems, thermal energy storage, and biomass combustion.Dr. Kalamkar's academic and research contributions are extensive, with over 40 research publications in esteemed international journals and more than 60 conference publications to his name. His expertise extends beyond academia, as he has successfully managed various defence research projects and consulting assignments. Additionally, he has played a pivotal role in the development of diverse testing facilities tailored for research in solar and biomass combustion applications.His innovative work has led to the acquisition of a patent in recognition of his niche contributions to the field. Dr. Kalamkar's commitment to advancing knowledge is further evidenced by his mentorship of numerous Ph.D. and M.Tech. students, contributing to the development of the next generation of researchers and engineers.

About Product:
Current Scenario:
Approximately 2.4 billion people globally still cook with solid biomass and kerosene on open flames and inefficient cookstoves. In India, about 660 million people have not switched to modern, clean cooling methods and rely on biomass fuels for cooking that employ traditional cook stoves. Toxic pollutants such as particulate matter (soot), carbon monoxide (CO), and other harmful gases are produced during the inefficient burning of solid biomass fuel, resulting in a high level of indoor air pollution. This pollution is linked to conditions such as heart disease, strokes, lung cancer, and chronic illnesses, contributing to 3.2 million annual deaths worldwide, with 0.8 million occurring in India. Inefficient and incomplete biomass cooking is also a significant source of deforestation and consequent climate forcing.
Eco-Hybrid biomass cookstove (Patented Technology)
Eco-Hybrid biomass cookstove is an advanced biomass cookstove scientifically designed, featuring a novel hybrid draft concept that integrates both natural and forced air supply with an affordable fan power to reduce toxic emissions such as CO and PM2.5 resulting from biomass combustion. The invention applies tangentially injected forced secondary air and optimized primary air to Improve stove performance. This method effectively lowers toxic emissions and enhances efficiency by improving combustion performance. The cookstove is capable of burning solid biomass fuels in their original form, unlike existing cookstoves in the market, most of which require pre-processed fuels. The invention helps to provide a safe cooking experience to the user in terms of Tier 4 level exhaust emissions while ensuring overall efficiency up to 30%. The design was developed by using modem design of experimentation techniques coupled with statistical techniques and robust parameter optimization. The optimized stove design can perform uniformly over a range of surface area to volume ratio of fuel sticks, inlet area ratio at the fuel inlet, and a range of pot diameters.
