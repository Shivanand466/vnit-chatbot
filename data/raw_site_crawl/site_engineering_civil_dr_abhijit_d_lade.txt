Dr. Abhijit D. Lade
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. Abhijit D Lade is an Assistant Professor at the Department of Civil Engineering VNIT, Nagpur. Prior to his PhD, he worked for six years in industry and teaching. He completed his Ph.D. in Fluvial Hydraulics at the Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Guwahati in 2021. His research interests are experimental hydraulics, open channel flows,  turbulence, river engineering, Microplastic transport, hydraulic structures, scour and erosion studies, etc.
