Tasks Completed (June 24-March 25)
Organized a PI coordinator orientation program in VNIT
Total 90 coordinators from 60+ institutions physically attended the workshop
Person from NCI (IIT Delhi) was present for the workshop
PIs were guided by Dr. Shridhar Kumbhar about how to write project proposals
Connected a total 6 NGOs with our UBA activity. PIs will be benefited by the ground level experience of the NGOs
Motivated PIs to submit the project proposals under UBA scheme. Total 36 project proposals are currently submitted
Personal visits and talks motivated new institutes to register as PI under UBA. So far, 33 new institutes have registered as PI under UBA.
Now, total 111 Institutions are registered as PI under RCI-VNIT
Physically visited 58 PI institutions 35 from Nagpur, 4 from Wardha, 5 from Bhandara, 2 from Gondia, 3 from Gadchiroli and 9 from Chandrapur
Physically visited the VNIT adopted villages — ‘Bodhalai Mendha’ & ‘Petha’ (Etapalli) in Gadchiroli. The villages are in remote areas and having unique developmental challenges due to tribal population
Connected ‘Gram Sabhas’ in these adopted villages
Physically visited the VNIT adopted villages ‘Chirwah’ in Nagpur and ‘Nandora’ in Bhandara district
Conducted Online Orientation program for PI Coordinators. Total 50 PI coordinators were present online. Full RCI team was present physically. Shri. Vaibhav ji Surange from Vanvasi Kalyan Ashram was the chief speaker
Success Stories as RCI
Login problems of 5 PIs are solved
Fund problems of 3 PIs are solved
Identified the weak and strong PIs by physical visits and categorized them into four groups namely, A, B, C and D. Here, A and B are motivated and capable PIs and C and D are the PIs who lack one or both of these qualities 32 PIs were dormant due to no visit and no hand holding from RCI-VNIT. Due to physical visits, these institutes have now stated working actively.
