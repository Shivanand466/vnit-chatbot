Dr. J. G. Suryawanshi
Brief Bio
Dr.  J. G. Suryawanshi is presently Professor in Mechanical Engineering Department at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur India. He did his Bachelors in Mechanical Engineering from RTMNU, Nagpur in 1993. He obtained his M. Tech. in Thermal Engineering from IIT Delhi in 1995.  He earned his PhD in 2008 from VNIT, Nagpur. Prof. Suryawanshi joined VNIT (Then VRCE) in 1998. His research areas include IC Engines, Alternative Fuels for IC Engines and Thermal Engineering. He has guided 6 PhD students till date and 2 PhD students are pursuing PhD research under his guidance. Additionally, he has mentored over 40 master's students and 30 undergraduate students. He has successfully completed 2 research & development projects. Dr Suryawanshi is recipient of Carrier Award for Young Teachers conferred by AICTE, in  2005. He delivered many invited lectures in India and abroad including University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, USA. He has more than 65 papers in peer-reviewed journals and international and national conferences of repute. He has also authored three book chapters. Prof. Suryawanshi visited various countries like the United States of America, United Kingdom, Italy and Hongkong for presenting his research work. He served as Associate Dean (Exam) of the institute during Jan 2021 to Jan 2024.
