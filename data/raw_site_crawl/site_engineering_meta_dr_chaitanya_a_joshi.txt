Assistant Professor
Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Chaitanya Joshi joined the department in May 2022. He taught subjects like Metal Working Processes, Welding and Joining of Materials, Solidification Processing and Advanced Foundry Technology, Engineering Metallurgy. His research interest is in mathematical modelling for studying microstructure evolution.
