International Collaborative Research:
The department successfully completed two collaborative project of three year each with Earth Resources Centre (U.K.) in the area of Impact of Mining on Tropical Forests(1990-1994) and with Camborne School of Mines, U K in the area of Mining and Environment (2003-2006).
Joint Research Project with Camborne School of Mines, University of Exeter, U K on “Mine Environment and Human Health” April 2003 to March 2006
Joint Research with University of Exeter, U.K. on Impact of Mining on Environment close to tropical forests in central India April 1991 to March 1996
