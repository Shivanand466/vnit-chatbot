Brief Bio
Sarika Bahadure, PhD, Faculty, Department of Architecture & Planning, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), NagpurDr.Sarika joined the profession in 1999 and have since been actively engaged in teaching, research, and academic administration. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Architecture, a Master’s degree in Urban Planning, and a PhD from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, India- a Centrally Funded Technical Institute.Her research areas are Sustainability, Urban Planning, Urban Open Spaces, Neighbourhood, Energy, Design and Pedagogy (Teaching Learning methods). She has over 70 research publications in reputed international and national journals, conferences, and book chapters, including 36 indexed in Scopus.Dr.Sarika has delivered lectures on varied topics at various forums like Short-Term Training Programs, workshops, conferences, and multiple Institutions at national and international platforms on the topics like Mixed Land use, Assessing Urban Sustainability, Disaster Assessment- Hazard, Vulnerability, Capacity, and Risk,  Resource Consumption, and Population Growth, Innovative Presentation Techniques, Writing Research Paper, Lateral Thinking a tool for Design, Thinking Ability & Research, Design Concept Development, Enhancing Thinking Ability, Space Psychology, etc.She has Coordinated Various Programs/ Short Term Courses/Workshops like Digital Approaches for Assessing Built Environment Sustainability, Assessing Urban Sustainability (One-week Self-Sponsored STTP), “Quest for Best” Enhancing Design Thinking (One-week workshop), Sustainable Cities (One-week Self-Sponsored STTP), Measuring Urban Sustainability (2-days Workshop), Green Building Awareness, Bamboo Architecture, Architectural Modelling, Exploring Forms through Models, etc.She is a Program Committee and Editorial Review Board Member of the various publication groups and a few National Conferences. She has reviewed more than 50 research manuscripts for SCI, Scopus, and other reputed journals and conferences. She has been appointed as an External Examiner/ Jury for courses like Planning Studio, Architectural Design, Advanced Building Techniques, Project Final Year, Seminar, Sustainable Architecture, etc. at Local and National Institute.Dr.Sarika has memberships in seven Professional Societies like the Council of Architecture, Regd. No. CA/99/25519; the Institute of Town Planner, New Delhi, India, Indian Institute of Architects (IIA), Bamboo Society of India, Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage (INTACH), etc. She is an Accredited Professional for IGBC and ASSOCHAM’s Green and Eco-friendly Movement. She has received several prestigious awards, including the Commendation Prize for the Prof. V.N. Prasad Best Thesis Award (ITPI, 2009–10), Best Paper Presentation Awards for her research on Power of Doubling: Population Growth and Resource Consumption at ICSHUD 2017 (London, UK) and Assessing Urban Energy Efficiency Strategies Adopted by Cities at ACE 2019 (Singapore), and secured First position in the Temple Towns National Competition (2025), where her team received certificates, a memento, and a cash prize of ₹1,00,000.
