Dr. D. A. Jolhe
Brief Bio
Dr. Dhananjay A. Jolhe is currently working as Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur. He holds Ph.D. in Mechanical Engg., M.Tech. in Industrial Engg., and B.E. in Production Engg. He has accomplished academic with 30 years of experience in Engineering Education. He deals with academic and research domains such as ergonomics, methods improvement, work system design, smart manufacturing and industry 4.0/5.0, quality engineering, manufacturing and system simulation, and business strategy analysis.
Dr. Jolhe authored four books on Engineering Drawing published by McGraw Hill Education which are recommended as textbooks and reference books by renowned institutions such as IITs, NITs, and platforms like NPTEL and SWAYAM. He has supervised many M. Tech. and B. Tech. projects, nurturing the next generation of engineers. Dr. Jolhe holds professional certifications in Industry 4.0, Lean Six Sigma, and Marketing Analytics, reflecting his commitment to lifelong learning and professional excellence. Notably, Dr. Jolhe served as a Jury for the Digital Transformation (DX) Award constituted by the Confederation of Indian Industries (CII) multiple times. Beyond academia, he has conducted training programs and delivered number of expert lectures, sharing insights with aspiring minds.
