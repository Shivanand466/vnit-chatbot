About Hostel
V.N.I.T. has excellent residential facilities to cater the need of more than a thousand students. There are seven Boys Hostels and Four Girls Hostels. Each hostel is having a telephone, T.V., Sports Items, water coolers, water heaters and well equipped mess facilities. It is mandatory for hostel inmates to join the mess in his / her hostel, managed by committee of students’ representatives. All hostels are having independent mess where vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals are available. All residents have to bear the wages of the mess cooks, servants, helpers and cleaners. Ministerial staff is provided by the Institute.
The Associate Dean (Hostel Affairs) looks after the management of the hostels along with a team of Wardens and Assistant Registrar (Hostel).
