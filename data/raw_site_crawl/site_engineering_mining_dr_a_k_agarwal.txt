Dr. A.K. Agarwal​
Brief Bio
Dr. Ajay Kumar Agarwal completed his B.E. in Mining Engineering from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (formerly V.R.C.E.), Nagpur, India, in 1986. He obtained his M.Tech. in Mine Planning & Design from Indian School of Mines, Dhanbad, and earned his Ph.D. from RTM Nagpur University. In addition, he holds AMIE in Civil Engineering and a Master’s degree in Civil Engineering.Dr. Agarwal has been granted two patents, with two more recently published/filed. He has an extensive research portfolio, with over 125 technical papers published in reputed national and international journals and conference proceedings.He received the Best Paper Award for his work on Design of Water Efficient Building, published in the Journal of the Indian Water Works Association (IWWA). He has also authored two book chapters on geosynthetics, focusing on the stability of spoil dump slopes and the fundamentals of slope stability.He has successfully completed three R&D projects and over 25 consultancy projects for various industries. He has served as an expert member on technical panels of both the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission and the Maharashtra Public Service Commission. In addition, he has held several administrative positions in different capacities.With more than 38 years of experience in academics, Dr. Agarwal is currently serving as an Associate Professor in the Department of Mining Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur.
