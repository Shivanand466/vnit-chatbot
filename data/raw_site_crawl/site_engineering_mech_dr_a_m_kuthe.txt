Research Interest
Dr. Abhaykumar Madhusudan Kuthe is presently Professor (Higher Administrative Grade) in Mechanical Engineering Department at Visvesvaraya National Institute of technology (VNIT) Nagpur India.Dr. Abhay began his career in 1986 after Bachelor of Engineering (Mechanical) by joining Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) Nashik India. He obtained his M.Tech from IIT Roorkee and joined Birla Consultancy Services (BICS) as Senior Software Engineer. He left BICS and joined VNIT in 1993.He earned his PhD in 2001. His core of research is application of 3D printing for customized orthopedic implant and bone tissue engineering. The capabilities of RP equipment were extensively exploited to make custom build metallic implant that were implanted in human bodies by surgeons in several complicated medical cases viz. tempero-mandibular joint, metatarsophalangeal joint, and some cases of cancer. He is presently adjunct professor for research in leading medical hospital. He has guided 12 PhD students till date and 2 PhD students are pursuing PhD research under his guidance. Three of his PhD students started company (start-up) based on their PhD research work in biomedical engineering.He has earned 8 patents for niche work undertaken by him and authored 2 books. He has successfully completed 4 research & development projects having financial outlay of US$ 6 million and has authored 74 research papers.  He delivered 100+ invited lectures in India and abroad including MIT USA, KTH Sweden, Nagoya University Japan, University of Auckland New Zealand. Creation of well-equipped CAD-CAM Centre at VNIT speaks volumes of his passion for raising the bar of academic standard. The all-encompassing contribution of his in application of 3D printing in design and development of patient specific implant and logical extension in bone tissue engineering expands the conventional boundaries of research. His goal is the deployment of research for societal benefit by amalgamating the medical science and engineering science, using his rich experience of 30 years in higher education institute.

The title of the project is ” SMART Foundry 2020 (SMART ≡ Sustainable Metalcasting using Advanced Research and Technology). SMART manufacturing or Industry 4.0 leverages information & communications technologies to democratize production and make it more sustainable. India, with its large base of SMEs, human resources and IT infrastructure can adopt this paradigm to meet challenges of employability, employment and entrepreneurship. Relevant technologies like 3D CAD, process simulation, 3D printing, rapid tooling, and process data analytics can be readily applied to metal casting – the mother industry that feeds all other sectors. Engineering and polytechnic institutes can replicate SMART system to support their theory and lab courses, and train students in smart manufacturing technologies to become job-creators instead of jobseekers. The institutes can also take up projects in collaboration with SMART Foundry team and local industry, thereby contributing to ‘Make in India’, ‘Digital India’, and ‘Startup India’ missions. Link: www.smartfoundry2020.in
