Ph.D Program
The Ph.D program is a postgraduate research oriented program. The scholar works in an area of his/her interest under the supervision of a faculty member(s) of the department. The program involves preparatory coursework with a certain number of credits and independent research work by the scholar, leading to a dissertation at the end of the program.
M.Tech. Program
The M.Tech. program is a two-year course oriented postgraduate program. The student has to take a set of core courses and a set of electives. The course work is spread across the first two semesters with an option of taking electives in the third semester. This is followed by a project in the third and fourth semester in which the student can take up a project of her or his interest, supervised by a faculty member of the department.
