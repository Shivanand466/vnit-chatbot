Institute Gathering
IG 2025 (Winner)
IG 2024 (2nd Position)
IG 2023 (2nd Position)
The Department of Chemical Engineering has a strong track record in the Institute Gathering (IG), securing 1st position in 2025
, 2nd positions in 2023 and 2024
, and achieving three consecutive victories from 2017 to 2019
. The department was also the runner-up in 2020
.
The Institute Gathering provides a platform to showcase students’ talents in cultural and sports events. This consistent success reflects the students’ skills and the effective leadership of the Department Council.
