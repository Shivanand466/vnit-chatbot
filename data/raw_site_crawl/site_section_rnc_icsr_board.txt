Industrial Consultancy and Sponsored Research (ICSR) Board
The ICSR Board plays a pivotal role in addressing issues related to the execution of sponsored research projects, consultancy activities, patents, technology transfers (ToTs), and start-up initiatives at VNIT Nagpur. It also reviews and approves industry-sponsored research proposals submitted by the faculty members.
Constitution of ICSR Board
The Industrial Consultancy and Sponsored Research (ICSR) Board at VNIT Nagpur was constituted as per Resolution 96.09 of the 96th meeting of the Board of Governors held on 13th September 2024. The Board is chaired by the Director, VNIT Nagpur, and its constitution includes the Dean (R&C) (Ex-Officio), the Registrar (Ex-Officio), the Heads of selected departments, and the Associate Dean (C&II) (Ex-Officio) as the Member Secretary. Special invitees to the Board include the Associate Dean (Research), the Associate Dean (IPR and Innovation), and two Professors nominated by the Chairman, ICSR Board.
