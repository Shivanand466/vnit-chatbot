Dr. Rahul Biswas
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Rahul Biswas is an Assistant Professor in the Applied Mechanics Department at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur. He holds a distinguished academic background, having completed both his M.Tech and Ph.D. in Structural Engineering from the esteemed NIT Patna. His primary research interests center around Concrete Technology and the utilization of Sustainable Materials. Additionally, he is actively involved in exploring the application of Machine Learning in the field of Structural Engineering. His research has contributed significantly to the scholarly community, with publications in international journals, national journals, and presentations at conferences. In recognition of his expertise, He serves as an Academic Editor for two reputable journals, "Advances in Civil Engineering" and "Plos One." His editorial role reflects a commitment to maintaining high standards in academic publications and fostering advancements in Structural Engineering. Furthermore, he actively engages in the peer-review process, contributing as a Reviewer for various international journals such as ASCE, Elsevier, Springer, among others. He is also a lifetime member of the Indian Concrete Institute (ICI).
