Pre-Final year B.Tech Students have provision to pursue 8 week long internship during their summer vacation.
Companies offer Summer Internships to B.Tech 2nd year, 3rd year and M.Tech 1st year students. Summer internships offer an opportunity to the students to work in a professional space, on the technologies they are learning on campus. This exposure helps them when they decide to join the industry as working professionals. The internships are a great way for the companies to attract the good students and monitor them during their tenure. Based on the performance of the intern, the companies extend a Pre-Placement offer (PPO) to the interns and inform the T & P department about their decision. If an intern accepts the PPO he/she is not eligible to appear for Campus Placements (depending on the category of the offer as per Placement policy).
To visit the campus for intern’s selections, please download the Internship Announcement Form, fill it up and mail it back to us at tepvnit_ngp@vnit.ac.in
Note:
B. Tech students, upon completion of their 3rd year, can pursue summer internship for 8-10 weeks. (Tentatively 12th May 2025 to 19th July 2025)
M.Tech students have provision to pursue a year-long internship (01 July 2024 to 30th April 2025) in their final year.
