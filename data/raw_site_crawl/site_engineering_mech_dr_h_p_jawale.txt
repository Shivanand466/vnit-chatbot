Dr. H.P. Jawale
Brief Bio
Dr. Hemant P. Jawale is an Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering specializing in Parallel Manipulators, Planar Mechanism and Manipulators for robotic applications. The areas of interest for teaching and research include Design Engineering, Product Design and Industrial Product Development, Kinematics of Machines, and Mechanical Measurements. He earned his Graduate degree in Mechanical Engineering from Amravati University. After serving for a year in Mahindra & Mahindra Tractors Limited, Nagpur as a Design Engineer, he pursued his post-graduation in Machines and Mechanisms Design from VRCE (Now VNIT Nagpur) with a special interest in Engineering Design. He has chosen Teaching as a profession and has a total teaching experience of more than 25 years. He has been serving at the Mechanical Engineering Department VNIT since 2008. He has adapted chapters in the key textbook "Theory and Design for Mechanical Measurements”, 7th Edition, by Richard S. Figliola to cater to the needs of Indian readers.  Under his guidance, 2  students completed their Ph.D. degree. He co-guided 2 Ph.D. students and 2 Ph.D. students pursuing their doctoral research under his supervision. Additionally, he has mentored over 25 master's students and supported the projects of more than 30 groups of undergraduate students. He is looking after the Mechanical Measurements Laboratory as lab Incharge.
