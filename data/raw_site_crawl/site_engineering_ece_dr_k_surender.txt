Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Myself K.Surender was born in Tamil Nadu, India . I received his B.E. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Bharathidasan University in 2004, M.Tech. degree from National Institute of Technology, Trichy and Ph.D degree from indian institute of Technology Bombay in 2006 and 2021 respectively. My research interest includes dynamic modeling of renewable energy resources, control and instrumentation.
