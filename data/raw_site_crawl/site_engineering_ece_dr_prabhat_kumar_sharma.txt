Dr. Prabhat Sharma
Brief Bio
Dr. Prabhat Sharma is with the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, India. He is a Senior Member of IEEE and served as Chair of the IEEE Nagpur Subsection (2022–2023). He was a Visiting Researcher at the University of Southern California (USC) from September 2023 to March 2024 under the SERB SIRE Fellowship.

He serves on the editorial boards of several prestigious journals, including IEEE Transactions on Communications, IEEE Transactions on Molecular, Biological and Multi-Scale Communications, IEEE Communications Letters, and IEEE Wireless Communications Letters. He is also the Nodal Officer of the VNIT 5G Laboratory supported by the Department of Telecommunications, Government of India. Recently, he presented India’s 6G progress to the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India at India Mobile Congress 2025.

Dr. Sharma has led more than ten externally funded research projects and has authored over 130 research publications in leading international journals and conferences. He is also the Founder of Sarvaksh, a 6G deep-tech startup.

His current research interests include 6G and beyond wireless systems, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS), quantum, molecular and biological communications, machine learning and AI for communications, game theory, and biomedical communication systems.
