Dr. Mansi A. Radke​
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Curently working as an Assistant Professor in Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur (VNIT) in the department of Computer Science and Engineering since April, 2012.
Bachelors (B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering) from Pune University, Cummins College of Engineering for women, India – 2004.
Masters in computer science from University of Maryland, Baltimore County, USA - 2008.
Ph.D. in the area of Geographical Information Retrieval from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, Maharashtra, India - 2018.
Worked in Industry for 5 years in reputed firms like Persistent Systems (Nagpur, India), SAP (Washington DC, USA), Take Supply Chain, (Austin, Texas, USA), and Cummins India Limited (Pune, India).
Academic experience of 14 years at various places including University of Maryland, Baltimore County, Ramdeobaba College of Engineering, Nagpur, India, Huston-Tillotson University at Texas, Austin, USA.
Areas of interest include Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning.
