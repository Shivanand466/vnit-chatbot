Dr. P. D. Sawarkar
Brief Bio
Dr. Pravin Sawarkar graduated in Mechanical Engineering from Nagpur University in 1997, received Masters degree from Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, Mumbai in 2008 and Ph D from Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai in 2018. He is in teaching profession since 1997. He has guided more than Ten M Tech dissertations and three research scholars are pursuing Ph D under his supervision. He is actively engaged in research in thermal science. His current fields of research are Heat transfer enhancement using inserts in pipe flow, Solar energy, Thermal management of photovoltaic panels, Battery thermal management system, Combustion engineering and research on Vortex tube. He acted as Reviewer in some International journals and conferences.
