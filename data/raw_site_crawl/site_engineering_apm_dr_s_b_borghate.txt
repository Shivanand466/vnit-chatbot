Dr. Sanjay B. Borghate
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Sanjay B Borghate  graduated in Civil Engineering (B.E.) from Visvesvaraya Regional College of Engineering (VRCE), Nagpur (now V.N.I.T., Nagpur) in 1992. He completed his Masters in Structural Engineering (M. Tech) from same institute in 1996. He worked as a Lecturer in SRKNEC, Nagpur from 1996-1998. He joined the Institute in 1998 as a Lecturer.  He has awarded  Ph.D.  in Structural Engineering by VNIT, Nagpur in Sepermber 2009. His PhD thesis title is “Behaviour of Flat Slab Buildings under Static and Dynamic Loadings”
