Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. V. P. Singh completed his MSc and PhD degree both from Department of Mathematics, IIT Kharagpur, India under the guidance of Prof. Debjani Chakraborty. His research interests include Operation Research, Optimization Techniques, Multi-Objective Optimization, Vehicle Routing Problem in mixed environment, Fuzzy Stochastic Optimization. He joined VNIT Nagpur in March 2018. Currently, He is working as an Assistant Professor Gr-I in the Department of Mathematics, VNIT Nagpur, India. He is having more than 9 years of Teaching and Research Experience.  He has published many research papers in the various international journal and conferences. He is also the reviewers of National and International Journals. He has visited the countries like USA and Italy for presenting his research works in top quality conferences.
