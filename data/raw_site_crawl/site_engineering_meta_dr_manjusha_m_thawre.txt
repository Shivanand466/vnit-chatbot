Bio bio
Dr. Manjusha M.  Thawre is currently an Associate Professor at Department of Metallurgial and Materials Engineering, VNIT Nagpur. Her areas of interest are Fatigue behaviour of Metals & Composites (High Cycle Fatigue ),  High Temperature Materials Joining of Metals, Testing of Metals, Mechanical Metallurgy, NDT,  Deformation Behaviour of Materials
