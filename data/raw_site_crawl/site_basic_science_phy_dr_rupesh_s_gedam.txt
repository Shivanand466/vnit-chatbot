Brief Bio
Dr. R S Gedam is working as a Professor in Physics, VNIT Nagpur. He is Fellow of Maharashtra Academy of Sciences. He completed his Ph.D. from VNIT Nagpur. He is having total 29 years teaching and research experience. His area of research is glasses, dielectrics, photoluminescence, nomaterials and energy materials. He has guided 22 M.Sc, 9 B.Tech and 8 Ph.D. students. He has published more than 75 research papers in SCI journals. Also he has published 2 book chapters. He has three Indian Patent on glasses for water treatment and antimicrobial activity. His current interest includes nonmaterials for water treatment, air purifications, self cleaning and antimicrobial activities.
Dr. Gedam has taught/teaching Physics, several materials science and engineering courses to undergraduate and post graduate students. He has organized various Short Term Training Programme (STTP), conferences, seminars and workshops. Dr Gedam is also associated with various Universities and Institutions.
