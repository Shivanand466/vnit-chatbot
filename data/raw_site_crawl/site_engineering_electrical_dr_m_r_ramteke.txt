Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Prof. Manoj Kumar Ramteke Born on 12 June 1964 and obtained AMIE (Electrical Engg), M.Tech. (Electronics) and PhD (Electrical Engg) from RTM Nagpur University in 1988, 1993 and 2008 respectively.He joined the institute in August 1989. He is senior member of IEEE and Fellow of Institute of Engineers (India). He Presented papers in various IEEE international conferences in France, Spain, Japan, Australia, Poland , USA and many more. His research area is Power Electronics Converters, FACTS Devices.
