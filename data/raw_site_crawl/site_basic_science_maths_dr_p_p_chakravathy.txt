Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Pramod Chakravarthy, born in Podilavaripalem, a small village near Cherukupalli, Guntur (Dt.), Andhra Pradesh.  He did his schooling and Intermediate from Government Junior College, Kavour.  He did his graduation from SVRM College Nagaram.  He did his Post graduation in M.Sc. Applied Mathematics from National Institute of Technology, Warangal during 1994-96. He qualified National level examinations like Joint CSIR-UGC- NET-JRF in 1996 and Qualified GATE-96 with 85.06 percentile.  He obtained his Ph.D. degree from National Institute of Technology, Warangal in 2004 under the able guidance of Prof. Y.N. Reddy.   He started his career as lecturer in Mathematics in Kavikulguru Institute of Technology & Science,  Ramtek , Nagpur, Maharastra.  Later he shifted to Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science, Warangal, and served as senior lecturer in Mathematics till May 2006.He joined as a lecturer in Mathematics in Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur on 31-05-2006, a premier Institution and an Institute of national Importance.  In 2010 he got selected as Associate Professor in Mathematics and selected as Professor of Mathematics in March 2018.His Broad area of Specialization is Numerical Analysis and till now he published 55 research papers in international journals and 2 papers in international conference proceedings. He successfully completed  3 research projected sponsored by Department of Science and Technology, National Board for Higher Mathematics, Department of Atomic Energy etc.  Completedn 5 Ph.Ds and 3 are ongoing.He organized Four Short term training programmes and two national conferences at VNIT Nagpur. Visited University of Nevada, Las Vegas, USA in March 2012.  London University in 2014 and Beging in 2016. Beijing Computational Science Research Centre, Beijing, China during August 201619th international conference “Computational and Mathematical Methods in Science and Engineering” (CMMSE – 2019) which was held at Rota, Cadiz, Spain during July 2019.   Given more than 36 invited talks in various institutions like, IIT Kanpur, NIT Warangal, NIT Surat, NIT Jamshedpur, NIT Raipur, NIT Uttarakhand, Nagpur University and other premier educational institutions.
