Dr. Ramsha Karampuri
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Ramsha Karampuri received the B.Tech. degree from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad, India, and the M.Tech. degree from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, India in the year 2006 and 2009 respectively. He has been awarded Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering from National Institute of Technology Warangal, India in the year 2019. His work experience includes three years at Priyadarshini College of Engineering, Nagpur from 2009 to 2012, three years at S R Engineering College, Warangal from 2017 to 2020 and six months at University of Sevilla, Spain from 2013 to 2014. His research interests include Multiphase Electrical drives, Power electronics Converters for Renewable Energy Systems and Internet of Things.
