Dr. Abhishek Singh
Brief Bio
Dr. Abhishek Singh is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur.
He received his Ph.D. in Power Systems from the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi, in 2025.
Before joining VNIT Nagpur, he worked with GE Vernova and Landis+Gyr as a Research Scientist, where he contributed to energy management systems, distribution energy resource management, load forecasting, machine-learning-based energy analytics, and optimization of power system applications.
His research interests include microgrid energy management, active distribution networks, uncertainty modelling, demand response, optimization techniques, renewable energy integration, and resilient power system operation.
