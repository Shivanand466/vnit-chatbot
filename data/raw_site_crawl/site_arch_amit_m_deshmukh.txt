Amit M.Deshmukh
Associate Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. Amit M Deshmukh is serving as Associate Professor at the Department of Architecture and Planning, VNIT, Nagpur. An Architect and Urban Planner by qualification he has varied experience as Professional and Academician in Architecture, Urban Planning and Interior design.
* Teaching experience 16+ Years (Architecture (B.Arch),  Urban Planning (M.Tech), PhD Supervisor)
* Professional experience 18+ years (City Development plans JNNURM, Architecture & Interiors)
* Expert Member :Directorate of Town Planning Department, Government of Maharashtra
* Expert jury Member: Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority (NMRDA): Expert Jury member
* School of Planning and Architecture, Vijayawada (SPA-V) Expert Jury member
* Fellow Member of Indian Institute of Architects F/15196
* Fellow Member Institute of Town Planners India (FITP)/2025/0005
* Registered with Council of Architecture, New Delhi  CA/96/20599
* Associate Member Institute of Interior Designers
* Post Graduate Teaching in Urban Planning Studio (DP Proposals, Landuse plans, TP Schemes) & Theory
* Undergraduate Teaching in Architecture (Studio & Theory)
