Brief Bio
Dr. Bibhudatta Dash is  Associate Professor of English in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. An interdisciplinary scholar with a strong and diverse publication record, Dr. Dash has published extensively in internationally recognized journals of Taylor & Francis, Springer Nature, Elsevier, Sage, Brill among others. His research and publications engage with Literature & Cultural Studies, Media & Film Studies, Indology & Religion, Gender & Minority Studies, and Digital Humanities. In recognition of his academic expertise, Dr. Dash has also been invited to serve as a reviewer for several reputed international journals published by leading academic publishers, including Nature, Springer Nature, Oxford, Brill, Emerald, Taylor & Francis, Frontiers, Alliance, Bentham Science, among others. His scholarly work reflects a sustained inquiry into contemporary cultural, social, and intellectual questions from interdisciplinary perspectives.
