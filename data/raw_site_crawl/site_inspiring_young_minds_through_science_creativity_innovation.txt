Inspiring Young Minds Through Science, Creativity & Innovation!
vnit_web
October 6, 2026
News
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur successfully hosted Junior Scientist’26, a school-level science initiative under AXIS’27, on 4th October 2026, witnessing enthusiastic participation of 1,500+ students from Classes 5–12.
Inspired by the vision of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, Day 1 featured four exciting competitions—Ex-Quizit, Junior Scientist Olympiad (JSO), Math-a-Maze and Model United Nations (MUN)—challenging students in science, knowledge, analytical thinking, creativity, communication and leadership.
✨ What’s Next – 11th October 2026:
🔹 Catapultikon
🔹 Model-O-Thon
🔹 Arduino Expo
The journey continues to inspire young learners to Question. Experiment. Create. Innovate.
🤖 Robotics & Embedded Systems Workshop | 17–18 October 2026
In collaboration with MakersNext, AXIS’27 will conduct a two-day hands-on workshop for Classes 6–12 on Robotics, Electronics and Coding, featuring interactive activities, practical experiments and design challenges.
