Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. (Mrs.) A. V. Deshpande, Professor, Former HoD, Department of Physics, VNIT, Nagpur is having 31 years of teaching and research experience. She has guided 7 Ph. D. students. She has also guided 19 M. Sc. and 6 B. Tech. students for their projects. Her research area is glasses, glass ceramics, composites, solid electrolytes for lithium batteries, nano glass ceramics. She has presented her research work at international conferences at Germany and Australia.  She has successfully completed a project sponsored by ARMREB, DRDO on “Development of Lithium Disilicate based Nano Glass -ceramics For Armor Applications” as Principal Investigator. Under this project, mechanically strong glass ceramics were developed for armor applications. The developed armor material was tested for ballistic impacts at DMRL, Hyderabad. She has published around 43 research papers in international, national reputed journals and conference proceedings. She has published a book chapter.
