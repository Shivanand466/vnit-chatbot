Procedure To Apply For Transcripts
A. Following documents shall be furnished along with the application
Xerox copies (to be attested by the Registrar) of Mark Sheets / Grade Cards from First to Final year / semester.
Original copies of Mark-Sheets / Grades Cards from First to Final year / Semester – for verification. (Originals will be returned immediately after verification).
Receipt (in original) of the fees paid in Institute Account.
* The challan for payment of fees shall be obtained from Academic Section.
B. Fees chargeable (w.e.f. 16.08.2007)
First four sets : Rs. 800/-
Every subsequent set : Rs. 100/- per set
