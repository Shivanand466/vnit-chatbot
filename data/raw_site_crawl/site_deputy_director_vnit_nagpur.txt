Dr. Babasaheb R. Sankapal
Deputy Director VNIT, Nagpur
Brief Bio :
Dr. Babasaheb R. Sankapal took over the charge as
Deputy Director of VNIT, Nagpur on 14 st
September 2026.  He i s
Professor in the
Department of Physics at
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT)
, Nagpur, Maharashtra, India and a renowned Indian physicist and materials scientist. He is a former
Head of the Department of Physics
,
Chairman of the Board of Studies
, and
Associate Dean (Examinations) at VNIT.
He earned his
Ph.D.
in 2001 from
Shivaji University
, Kolhapur. Following his doctorate, he gained international experience as a
Scientist at the
Helmholtz Centre in Berlin,
Germany
; a
JSPS Postdoctoral Fellow at
Gifu University
,
Japan
; and
Research Associate at the
University of Wisconsin
, Milwaukee,
USA
.
Dr. Sankapal is widely recognized for his contributions to nanomaterials and energy technologies
. His research focuses on the synthesis and application of nano- and quantum materials in solar cells
(including dye-sensitized and quantum dot-based), supercapacitors
(electrochemical energy storage), and gas sensors
. He employs simple, cost-effective chemical methods such as chemical bath deposition and SILAR to develop high-performance, flexible devices.
Key Achievements and Recognitions
Listed among the
World’s Top 2% Scientists
(Stanford University database) for consecutive years, including 2020–2025 (in both career-long and single-year categories).
Ranked 104 th in India and 6919th globally in
Materials Science according to Research.com (2025 rankings).
Achieved a strong AD Scientific Index ranking (India rank under 400 in recent listings).
Awards and Honors
Young Scientist Outstanding Work Presentation Award by the Materials Research Society of Japan (MRS-J) in 2005.
Selected by the
Department of Science and Technology (DST)
, Government of India, to participate in the
Lindau Meeting of Nobel Laureates in Germany (2001).
Recipient of the
Vidyan Ratn Award from Vidarbha Gaurav Pratishthan, Nagpur.
Vice-President (Nagpur Region) & Fellow of Maharashtra Academy of Sciences
(FMASc)
Recent Highlights
Participated as a
Senior S&T Leader in the
Emerging Science, Technology, and Innovation Conclave (ESTIC-2025) in New Delhi, inaugurated by
Hon’ble Prime Minister Narendra Modi
.
Appointed as a
Member of the National Expert Advisory Committee (NEAC) under the
Department of Science and Technology (DST) for the
New & Emerging Energy Storage Technologies (NEST) program (announced in 2025).
Felicitated by DST as VNIT representative for sanction of 1 Crore- in Annual Quantum Conclave under National Quantum Mission (NQM) for the establishment of UG teaching laboratories in quantum technologies at IIT Mumbai
Research, Teaching, and Impact
With 27+ years of research experience and 19+ years of teaching, Dr. Sankapal has:
Secured 10 major research projects worth approximately
₹2.82 crore
.
As Head of Department, successfully obtained a
DST-FIST grant of around
₹2.19 crore for the department.
Led initiatives including the launch of a
B.Tech in Engineering Physics in 2026 program at VNIT.
Supervised 18 Ph.D. scholars, working 8 and supervised 25 M.Sc. project students
. Many of his Ph.D. graduates have pursued postdoctoral positions in prestigious institutions across Germany, Japan, the UK, USA, Denmark, Spain, South Korea, and Israel. Notably, two of his students have been included in the World’s Top 2% Scientists list (2024 & 2025).
Served as Coordinator for MoUs between VNIT and
Virginia Commonwealth University
, USA, and
CSIR-IICT
, Hyderabad.
Publications and Intellectual Contributions
Authored over 233 research papers in high-impact journals.
Scopus metrics
: h-index ≈62, citations 9500+ (average ~40 per paper).
Google Scholar metrics
: h-index ≈70, citations 11000+ (average ~45 per paper), with an average journal impact factor of ~5.
Edited one book (Springer), contributed three book chapters (Springer & Elsevier).
Holds three granted Indian patents
, filed 03 Indian Patents (01 with IIT Madras)
Dr. Sankapal has delivered many invited talks and participated in international meetings in countries including Germany, Japan, USA, Switzerland, Poland, and France. He is also a sought-after speaker on topics such as NIRF rankings, scientific writing, project drafting, nanomaterials, supercapacitors, solar cells, and career opportunities after science degrees.
Work continues to advance sustainable energy solutions and nanotechnology applications, making significant contributions to India’s research landscape in emerging energy technologies.
