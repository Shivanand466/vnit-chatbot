Dr. Rajendra Prasad Soni
Brief Bio
Dr. R. P. Soni, is currently working as an Assistant Professor in the department of Mechanical Engineering, VNIT Nagpur. He has completed his M.Tech. and Ph.D. from Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati. His PhD work focuses on hydrdoynamic instability of convection flow with density inversion of water. His research area includes, Battery Thermal Management System, Solar-thermal Energy, CFD, Hydrodynamic Instability,Cooling of Electronic devices, Microfluidics and Bio-medical applications. He is also interested in exploring areas such as Data Driven CFD, application of AI-ML in computation Fluid Dynamics.
