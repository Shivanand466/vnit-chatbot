🧹 Swachhata Hi Sewa 2026 | Swachhata Tug of War Competition 💪 vnit_web
September 25, 2026
News
As part of the Swachhata Hi Sewa Campaign 2026, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur organized a Swachhata Tug of War Competition on 23 September 2026 at the Institute Sports Ground.
Organized by the Physical Education & Sports Section under the coordination of Dr. Awadhesh Pratap Singh, SAS Officer, the event brought together 4 departmental teams, with 6 male and 2 female students per team, promoting inclusive participation, teamwork and sportsmanship.
The competition highlighted the values of collective responsibility, cleanliness, discipline, physical fitness and team spirit, reinforcing the message that a clean and healthy environment is everyone’s responsibility.
