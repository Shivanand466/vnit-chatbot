Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Oroosa Subohi completed her post-graduation in Physics from Osmania University, Hyderabad, India in the year 2000, obtained her M.Phil degree in Physics from Barkatullah University, Bhopal in 2009 and was awarded Ph.D(Physics) from Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, India in the year 2015. Her area of research includes advanced functional materialsfor applications in ferroelectric capacitors, solidstate referigeration and spintronics – their synthesis,characterisation and applications. She has authored a book on Aurivillius Phases materials and has more than 30 articles on ferroelectric and multiferroic ceramics in journals of international repute to her credit and has contributed one book chapter. She has guided 12 students for their master’s dissertation and is currently supervising 5 Ph.D scholars. She is a life member of Materials Research Society of India.
