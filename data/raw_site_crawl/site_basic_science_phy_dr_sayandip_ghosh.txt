Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Sayandip Ghosh is a theoretical condensed matter physicist. He graduated (B.Sc. with honours in Physics) in 2009 from the University of North Bengal. Thereafter, he went to Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur to pursue the M. Sc.-Ph.D. (dual degree) in Physics. He completed his M.Sc. in 2011 and continued as a Ph.D. student at the same institute. He received his Ph.D. in 2017 under the supervision of Prof. Avinash Singh. Dr. Ghosh subsequently joined TU Dresden, Germany in 2017 as a postdoctoral research associate in Prof. Carsten Timm's group. Afterwards, in 2019, he joined Prof. Marco Polini's group at the Italian Institute of Technology, Genoa and University of Pisa, Italy as a postdoctoral fellow where he continued till 2021. Afterwards, he spent a brief tenure as a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Sherbrooke, Canada with Prof. Ion Garate in 2022, before joining VNIT Nagpur as an Assistant Professor in Physics in the same year.
