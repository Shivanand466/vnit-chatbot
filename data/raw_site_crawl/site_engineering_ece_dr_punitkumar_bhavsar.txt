Dr. Punitkumar Bhavsar
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Punitkumar Bhavsar is working as an Assistant Professor in Electronics and Communication Engineering Department at VNIT Nagpur. He pursed bachelors in Electronics Engineering from BVM College of Engineering, masters’ study in System Modeling and Control from IIT Roorkee and PhD from IIT Gandhinagar. His research area involves cyber physical systems and signal processing.
