Brief Bio
My desire to learn more about civil engineering and the opportunities it provides motivated me to continue my studies and contribute to the industry through relevant research. Under the direction of Dr. K. S. Reddy, I received my Ph.D. in Pavement Engineering from IIT Kharagpur.There have been countless unresolved issues with roads and the construction process, which grabbed my attention in investigating road construction improvisation using various technologies and alternatives.Pavement Material Characterization, Pavement Design, and Cementitious Material Applications in Pavement are among my research interests as a highway engineer. If you’re serious about pavement development research, and want to contribute through some innovative ideas, then you can approach me through mail.
