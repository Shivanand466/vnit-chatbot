Dr. N. K. Lautre
Brief Bio
Dr. NITIN KUMAR LAUTRE is working as Associate Professor  in the Mechanical Engineering department of VNIT Nagpur. He joined  the institute as  Assistant Professor since 07th December 2008.  Contact details :- 0712-280-1872,  nitin@mec.vnit.ac.in
Before joining VNIT Nagpur he was selected by UPSC as Lecturer (Mechanical Engg.) for Punjab Engg. College, Chandigarh, where he worked as a Lecturer for 5 years and as Senior Lecturer for 3 years.
He also served Hindustan Aeronautics Limited for about 01 years and seven months as Assistant Engineer (Quality Control and Inspection).
He did his B.E. (UG) from Nagpur University in 1997 and then completed his M.Tech (PG) from VRCE, Nagpur University in 1999.  He did his Ph.D. from IIT ROORKEE in 2016.
His Area of specialization is in Production and Industrial Engineering, New process development, welding with Additive manufacturing, and  Supply Chain Mangement.
He taught subjects like Metrology & Quality Assurance,  Computer Aided Drafting & Modeling, Manufacturing Processes, Fluid Machines, Engineering Drawing, Supply Chain Management,  Statistical Quality Assurance, Advanced Turbo Machinary, Production planning & control, Engineering economics, Introduction to dessign engineering, Solid Mechanics, Mechanical behaviour of materials,  Fluid Mechanics, Geometrical Drawing & Engineering Graphics, etc.
He has published  FOURTY papers in international Journals and THIRTEEN papers in national and international conferences. He has FOUR granted patents in his credits.
He has successully completed one SERB- CRG project worth of 34 lakhs. He is currently working o other project with funding from IHFC, IIT Delhi for increasing TRL level of the granted pated beyond TRL 4.
He has supervised TWO Ph.D., 29 PG, and many UG candidates.  At present he is guiding  ONE Full time Ph.D, ONE Full time POstdoc Reseracher (from 2026) and FIVE Part time Ph.D.
He is a fellow member of the Institute of Engineers, ASME, ISME, IEEE, and ISTE
