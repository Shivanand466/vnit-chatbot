Miles Apart, Memories Together – Let’s Reunite at VNIT!” ❤️
✨ **Reconnect. Relive. Rejoice. Reunite!** ✨
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, is delighted to invite its global alumni community to the **VNIT Global Alumni Meet 2027**, being organized in association with the local team of VNIT Alumni Association.
📅 **29–30 January 2027 (Friday & Saturday)**
📍 VNIT Nagpur, India
Two memorable days filled with nostalgia, networking, knowledge sharing and new opportunities!
🌟 **What’s in Store?**
🔹 Explore the latest research and innovations at VNIT.
🔹 Visit Centres of Excellence.
🔹 Reconnect with batchmates and fellow alumni.
🔹 Interact with students, faculty and student clubs.
🔹 Discover VNIT’s innovation and incubation ecosystem.
🔹 Explore opportunities for mentorship and collaboration.
🔹 Campus tour and hostel nostalgia.
🔹 Engage with Deans and the Training & Placement Section.
🔹 Enjoy delightful meals and gala dinners!
This is more than a reunion – it is an opportunity to strengthen lifelong bonds, celebrate shared memories and contribute towards shaping the future of VNIT.
**An invitation from Prof. Prem Lal Patel, Director, VNIT Nagpur, to come together and build a stronger, future-ready VNIT!**
💰 **Registration Fee:** ₹2,500/-
Additional spouse/child: ₹1,500/- per person.
🗓️ **Last Date for Registration: 31 December 2026**
📲 Scan the QR code on the invitation and register today!
🤝 **Connect. Contribute. Create. Collaborate.**
Let’s come home to VNIT! ❤️
