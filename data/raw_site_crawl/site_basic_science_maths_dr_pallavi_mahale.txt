Dr. Pallavi Mahale
Brief
Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Pallavi Mahale is a professor in the Department of Mathematics, VNIT Nagpur. She has obtained her Ph.D from IIT Madras in year 2009 under the guidance of Prof M.Thamban Nair. Her area of interest is the regularization of non-linear  ill-posed operator equations.
