Dr. (Mrs.) Vaidehi Dakwale
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. (Mrs.) Vaidehi A Dakwale is Assistant Professor at Civil Engineering Department, VNIT, Nagpur. She has done BE, ME (Construction Technology and Management) and PhD (Civil Engineering). She is actively contributing in peer review journals, National International conferences. She has delivered several expert talks in various institutions and reginal traning center for WRD and PWD engineers. She has a teaching experience of around 15 years and Industrial Experience of around 3 years.
