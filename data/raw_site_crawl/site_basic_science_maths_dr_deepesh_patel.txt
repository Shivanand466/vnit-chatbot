Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Deepesh Kumar Patel received the Ph.D. degree in Mathematics at S.V. National Institute of Technology, Surat, India.  He is presently working as Assistant Professor in Department of Mathematics, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, India. His research interests are in the areas of fixed point theory and their applications in nonlinear analysis. He has published and reviewed various research articles in reputed journals.
