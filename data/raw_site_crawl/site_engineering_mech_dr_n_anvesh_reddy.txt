Dr. N Anvesh Reddy
Brief Bio
Dr. Anvesh Reddy Nandyala is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. Dr. Nandyala earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Delhi in 2020, following his M.Tech from IIT Guwahati in 2012 and B.E. from Kakatiya University, Warangal in 2010. His research focuses on structural health monitoring, ultrasonic-guided waves, smart structures, and dynamics. Dr. Nandyala worked as an Adhoc Assistant Professor at NIT Andhra Pradesh from June 2021 to June 2022 and joined VNIT Nagpur in June 2022. He received an Early-Doctoral Fellowship from IIT Delhi and fellowships for his Ph.D. and M.Tech from the Ministry of Human Resource Development, India. Dr. Nandyala's notable publications include research on damage severity assessment in composite structures and the behavior of Lamb waves in composites.
