Externally Sponsored Projects
The Department has always been a hub of world-class cutting-edge research activity that would benefit the public, private and government agencies in general. Our faculty members aim to develop innovative solutions to challenging engineering problems of all functional and interdisciplinary areas of Chemical engineering including energy, environment, health-care, and advanced materials. Apart from our institute and industrial support for research, our faculty members receive research fundings from major Indian agencies such as DST-SERB, DBT, CSIR, DRDO, DSIR, DAE, etc. and other agencies in abroad. Several research publications, patents, and books have been published and new courses have been augmented from the outcomes of our research activities.
