Dr. Ajay A. Likhite​
Professor
Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Ajay Likhite joined the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering in 2009 as Associate Professor. He has worked in industry for about four years and in academics at SRKNEC for 21 years. His research interest lies in the field of solidification processing, process control, foundry technology and failure analysis. Currently he holds the position of Professor in the Department of MME. At the institute level he was Associate Dean (PR) from October 2028 to January 2021. Currently he is Associate Dean (Industry Institute Interaction & Alumni)He has delivered invited talks in Slovania, UAE, China, South Korea etc.
