TITLE: Sport Competitions on 29th & 30th August 2025 to Celebrate National Sports Day.
FETCHED: 2026-11-19
CRAWL: full-2026-11-19

To commemorate India’s deep rooted tradition of sports, National Sports Day is celebrated on 29 th
August, the birth anniversary of the Hockey Legend Major Dhyan Chand.
On this occasion, the Physical Education & Sports Section will organize the following competitions on 29 th
& 30 th
Aug. 2025.
Sr. No.
Competition
Date & Time
Prizes 1.
Cross Country Run (Men & Women) of 05 kms.
29 th
Aug. 2025 at 05:00 pm in the sports complex.
First 06 winners of Men
& First 06 winners of Women category will be awarded with Medals & Certificates.
2.
Power Lifting Competition for boys (in Weight Categories: 59 kg., 66 kg., 74 kg., 83 kg., 93 kg., 105 kg., 120+ kg.,) 30 th
Aug. 2025 from 10:00 am onward in the sports complex.
First 02 winner of each weight category will be awarded with Medals, Certificates and wrist support / Knee Caps
Special Prize:
Best Lifter & Best Body Builder will be awarded with a Track Suit.
3.
Body Building Competition for boys (in Weight Categories: 60 kg., 65 kg., 70 kg., 75 kg., 80 kg., 85 kg., 90 kg., 90+ kg.,) 30 th
Aug. 2025 from 04:00 pm onward in the sports complex
