TITLE: Congratulations to Mr. Ankit Mishra for winning first prize in poster and video making competition organized by Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA)
FETCHED: 2026-11-19
CRAWL: full-2026-11-19
---
Ankit Mishra, a third year student from the department, got first prize in video and poster making competition organised by UBA under regional level. Prize money for winner was 10k INR. The video and poster competition was organised by Unnat Bharat Abhiyan(UBA) in which VNIT was regional coordinating institute. Total 67 institutes participated from 6 districts in Vidarbha region which were Nagpur, Chandrapur,Wardha, Gadchiroli,Bhandara,Gondiya. Main purpose of UBA is rural development and VNIT played an important role in guiding and monitoring the activities of participating institutes in this region. In order to create awareness among rural citizens, competition of video and poster making was organised under UBA on COVID awareness. Check out his prize winning video
