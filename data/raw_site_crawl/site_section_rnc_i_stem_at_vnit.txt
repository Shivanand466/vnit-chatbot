I-STEM AT VNIT
Indian Science, Technology and Engineering Facilities Map (I-STEM)
I-STEM @ VNIT Nagpur
I-STEM is a national initiative of the Government of India designed to map, share, and optimize the use of publicly funded R&D facilities across the country. The portal enables researchers from academia and industry to locate, book, and utilize advanced scientific equipment based on availability and proximity.
At VNIT Nagpur, all facility bookings internal or external: are processed exclusively through the I-STEM portal, ensuring transparency, accessibility, and efficient utilization of resources.

Booking Procedure through I-STEM Facility:
Please visit https://www.istem.gov.in/ and
Register as Researcher/User Registration category (if not already done) to create your user profile by providing the required details.
After receiving the user id and password via email,
Login to the website and search for the equipment’s full/abbreviation name and select the city as Nagpur or Advanced search option. It can also be directly accessed for booking facilities using the URL given in the
Department-wise listed below table
.
Select the user category
, type of sample analysis, and proceed with equipment reservation.
After a slot is allocated, the user will receive a
FBR number which can be provided along with the Requisition Form, receipt of
Payment
(VNIT Online Payment Portal) and samples for data collection.
After booking the facility kindly send the duly filled Requisition Form along with the samples and payment receipt to –
Department/facility name:
__________
(office), Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), South Ambazari Road, Nagpur, Maharashtra – 440010, India
.
It is also requested to contact the concerned department Coordinator/faculty-incharge for guidance and support regarding utilizing the mentioned facilities or any payment related queries.
