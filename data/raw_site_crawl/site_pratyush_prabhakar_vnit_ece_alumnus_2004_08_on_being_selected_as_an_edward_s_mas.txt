VNIT is Proud of Pratyush
Heartiest Congratulations to Pratyush Prabhakar, VNIT ECE alumnus (2004–08), on being selected as an Edward S. Mason Fellow at Harvard University and joining the Mid-Career Master in Public Administration Program at Harvard Kennedy School.
Adding another feather to his cap, Pratyush has been elected Vice President (Academic) at the Harvard Kennedy School of Government.
The Edward S. Mason Program empowers accomplished leaders from developing economies to create meaningful impact in society and the world.
VNIT Nagpur proudly congratulates Pratyush on this outstanding achievement.
We are proud to have such distinguished alumni representing VNIT on the global stage.
Congratulations, Pratyush! You have mad the entire VNIT family proud.
