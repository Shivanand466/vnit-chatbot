Payment of Faculty Recruitment Application Fees to SBI Collect.
Application fees of Rs. 1000 for General & OBC candidates / Rs.2000 for Indian nationals applying from abroad  can be paid through SBI Collect as per following link
:
https://www.onlinesbi.com/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm
Instructions :
After opening the link, select
‘Maharashtra’ and under
‘Educational Institute’
, then select
‘Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur’ then click
‘Faculty Recruitment Application Fees’
. Pay the fees through online internet banking or debit / credit card.
Take the printout of the receipt with transaction number and enclose with the front page of the application form.
