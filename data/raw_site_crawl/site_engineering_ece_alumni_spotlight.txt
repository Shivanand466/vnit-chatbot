We are delighted to share that our esteemed alumnus, Dr. Amartansh Dubey, has joined as a faculty member in the Department of Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi.
Dr. Dubey completed his B. Tech. from VNIT Nagpur in 2016, M.Phil. in (2021) and Ph.D. (2022) in Electronic and Computer Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.
The VNIT Nagpur community takes immense pride in Dr. Dubey’s accomplishments and wishes him continued success in his academic and research journey!
