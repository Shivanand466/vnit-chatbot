About Us
Why recruit at VNIT?
Institute of National Importance
:
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (previously V.R.C.E) is among the 30 most premier Institutes of Engineering in India. Established in 1960, VNIT has led technological development and innovation in Central India for over 60 years. According to the NIT Act of 2007, the status of “Institute of National Importance” has been conferred upon VNIT. VNIT Nagpur is ranked 41st in the National Institutional Ranking Framework 2023.
Varied Disciplines:
With B.Tech degree in 8 disciplines, M.Tech degree in 21 disciplines along with BArch and Masters in Pure Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Maths), VNIT Nagpur plays host to technical excellence in various fields. Also, with excellent hostel facilities which cater to almost 4000 students, some of the best technical brains graduate from VNIT every year.
Academic Excellence:
The top ranked students from JEE Mains as well as GATE opt for VNIT for pursuing graduate and post-graduate every year respectively. With scholarships from OPJEMS, Tata Crucible, RECPD, SPDC and several others, our students portray the best to offer. The library boasts over two hundred thousand books, IEEE e-research papers, e-journals, ACM digital library & scientific publications. VNIT is a part of the National Knowledge Network & C-DEEP by which VNIT collaborates with other institutes of National importance.
