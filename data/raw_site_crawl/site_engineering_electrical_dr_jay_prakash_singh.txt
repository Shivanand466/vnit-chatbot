Dr. Jay Prakash Singh
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Jay Prakash Singh currently working as Assisiatnt Professor in EE Department in VNIT, Nagpur Since June 2020. Jay Singh completed his B.Tech from SCITM, BPUT (Odisha) in 2010. Jay completed his M.Tech and PhD in 2014 and 2018, respectively, from NIT Silchar, India. He received Gold Medal as Best Doctoral Award for his PhD Degree. He worked at SIT, Pithoragarh (UK) for eight months and REC, Rewa (MP) for 1.9 years under NPIU, TQUIP-III. He also worked at COSBIKE, Maneshar, in an EV manufacturing company, from Jan. 2011 to July 2012. Jay does research in Nonlinear Control System, Controller for EV charging station, Chaos theory, Nonlinear Dynamics, Robotics control and Fuel cell Technology.
