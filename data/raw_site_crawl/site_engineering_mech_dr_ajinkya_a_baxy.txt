Dr. Ajinkya A Baxy
Brief Bio
• Dr. Ajinkya Baxy completed his Masters and PhD from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras in 2021.
• He worked as a senior porject scientist in the MMMES Lab at IIT Delhi before joining the Department of Mechanical Engineering at VNIT Nagpur in 2022.
• In VNIT, Dr. Baxy is engaged in teaching Solid Mechanics, Mechanical Vibration, Machine Design, Engineering Drawing at UG and PG level.
• His research interests include topics such as vibration, multibody dynamics, control and cellular solids. He is also exploring the application of AL and ML in mechanical systems.
