Dr. Karthik Balasundaram
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
As a born minimalist, my love for environment has continuously driven me to look for practical solutions to various environmental pollution problems. With a perfect blend of strong academic background, interdisciplinary skill set and scientific temper,  I continuously strive to achieve high standards both in my teaching and research. If your intellect is challenged by  environmental pollution problem and you are ready to give nothing but the best then I am just an email away.
Research Interest
Environmental chemistry
Physio-chemical methods for removal of heavy metals and emerging contaminants from water and wastewater
Resource recovery from waste
Environment risk assessment.
