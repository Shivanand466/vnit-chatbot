About the Program
VNIT Nagpur is proud to announce its First 5G Summer School
, designed exclusively for
Undergraduate Students
. Through hands-on sessions
, expert lectures
, and real-world experiments
, Participants will explore the key pillars of 5G and future wireless systems. Utilizing VNIT’s state-of-the-art 5G Lab infrastructure
,
GNU Radio and
USRP platforms
, students will get an unparalleled opportunity to learn
, experiment
, and innovate
.
Key Topics Covered
Introduction to 5G Architecture and Standards 5G Core and RAN Concepts
Software-Defined Radio (SDR) Fundamentals
Network Slicing and Edge Computing
Hands-on with GNU Radio and USRPs
Emerging Trends: 6G and Beyond
Highlights
Live Demos on VNIT’s 5G Testbed
Project-based Learning
Certificate from VNIT Nagpur
Best Project Awards
