Dr. Prince Kumar Rai
Brief Bio
Dr. Prince Kumar Rai is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at VNIT Nagpur, India. Previously, he served as an Assistant Professor at VIT, Vellore, and held an Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship at IIT Bombay. He also worked as an IGSTC Postdoctoral Industrial Fellow at Airbus Central Research & Technology, Munich, Germany. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Jodhpur. His research focuses on advanced manufacturing, surface engineering, microfabrication, and corrosion engineering, with applications in energy and environmental technologies. He has authored more than 12 peer-reviewed journal articles and several conference papers, and has two patents published till date.
