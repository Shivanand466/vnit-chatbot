TITLE: Pahal-e-Sindoor
FETCHED: 2026-11-19
CRAWL: full-2026-11-19

Under the inspiration of the Ministry of Education and the guidance of Honorable Chairman Shri M. Madan Gopal and Director Prof. (Dr.) P. L. Patel, VNIT organized a series of patriotic activities under the Har Ghar Tiranga campaign. Events included ;
3rd to 10th August 2025 – Digital Poster Making Competition on the theme Role of Viksit Bharat.
10th August 2025 – Quiz Competition on Independence Day and the Role of Youth in Nation Building.
12th August 2025 (Morning) – Anti-Ragging Day observance, featuring a talk by DCP Shri Rushikesh Reddy on the ill effects of ragging and the importance of the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan.
12th August 2025 (Evening) – Har Ghar Tiranga Rally organised by the VNIT Sports Section.
13th August 2025 – Rangoli Competition on the theme Azadi.
14th August 2025 (Evening) – Pahal-e-Shindoor cultural celebration, a grand event featuring four VNIT cultural clubs — Octaves, HallaBol, Grooves, and Mag.Com — presenting a two-hour Sangeet Natika.
15th August 2025 (Morning) – Flag Hoisting Ceremony by Honourable Chairman and Director, followed by a marchpast by security personnel and a recital of Vande Mataram by students.
15th August 2025 (Morning) – Nukkad Natak by HallaBol Drama Club on the sacrifices of freedom fighters and the responsibilities of citizens today.
15th August 2025 (Morning–Afternoon) – Blood Donation Camp organised by VNIT Prayaas Club, in collaboration with six government and private blood banks.
Approximately 3800 persons including Students, Faculty and Staff attended these events, reflecting VNIT’s unity, pride, and service to the nation.
