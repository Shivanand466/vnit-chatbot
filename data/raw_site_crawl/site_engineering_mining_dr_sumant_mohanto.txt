Dr. Sumant Mohanto​
Brief Bio
Dr. Sumant Mohanto is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mining Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur. He specialized in the area of Rock Mechanics and Ground Control and his current work focuses on pillar stability in underground metalliferrous mines. He has experience in teaching as Adhoc faculty at National Institute of Technology Suratkal, Karnataka. He is proficient in finite element (FE) software packages such as ANSYS, ABAQUS and PHASE2 and also statistical softwares such as IBM SPSS. He obtained his PhD degree from Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India under the supervision of Prof. Debasis Deb and Dr. Abhiram Kumar Verma. He also has research experience as Senior Research Fellow and Research Associate in one research project (Hindustan Copper Limited, Malanjkhand) and 5 consultancy projects (Uranium Corporation of India Limited, Mohuldih and Turamdih), respectively during the PhD tenure acting as one of the key persons in conducting research and writing final project report. He also worked as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mining Engineering at Malla Reddy Engineering College (A), Hyderabad for one semester. He obtained his M. Tech in Rock Engineering and Underground Structures from Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi and B. Tech in Mining Engineering from Orissa School of Mining Engineering, Keonjhar.
