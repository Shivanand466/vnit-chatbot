High penetration of wind energy introduces variability and uncertainty, affecting power system stability. Traditional methods struggle with real-time prediction and control. This research explores AI and machine learning techniques to accurately predict, analyze, and enhance transient, voltage, and frequency stability in wind-integrated power systems.

M S Ballal
Digital Twin for the condition monitoring and health assessment of electrical components like EV Battery Pack, Solar PV Plants, industrial motors, etc.
Scholars will architect Digital Twins for EV batteries, solar plants, and motors, fusing physics-based models with real-time data. This research targets Cyber-Physical Synergy, evolving traditional monitoring into autonomous, predictive “digital nervous systems.” By transforming raw electrical signatures into actionable intelligence, you will redefine reliability and drive the technological resilience of the global green energy transition.
