Dr. Praveen Pawar
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Praveen Pawar is a dedicated and accomplished researcher in the field of wireless communication. With a strong commitment to academia, Dr. Pawar has served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of ECE at the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Bhopal, from January 2020 to May 2022. In June 2022, he joined the Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. Currently, he holds the position of Assistant Professor in the Department of ECE at VNIT Nagpur. His research focuses on Wireless Resource Management, 5G/6G technologies, Internet of Things (IoT), Stochastic Geometry, Data Analytics and AI/ML.
