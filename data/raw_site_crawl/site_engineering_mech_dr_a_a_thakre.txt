Dr. A. A. Thakre
Brief Bio
Dr. Avinash A. Thakre is working as an Assistant Professor in Mechanical Engineering Department in VNIT, Nagpur fom 2006. He has completed B. E. (Mechanical Engineering ) from Nagpur University in 2002. He completed M.Tech. in Machine Design in 2005 from IT-BHU, Varanasi. He complted Ph. D. from VNIT, Nagpur in 2017. Research interest includes Soft Solids, BioTribology, Vibration damping using polymers.
