🏆 A Moment of Pride for Team Velocity! 🏎‍🟀 vnit_web
October 12, 2026
News
We are immensely proud to share the remarkable achievements of Team Velocity, the student technical club of our institute, at the prestigious competition organised by Morphin Motorsports, held in Coimbatore from 4–7 October 2026.
🌟 Outstanding Achievements: 🥈 2nd Position in Design Debate among 40+ participating teams
🏅 Best Emerging Team Award
These accolades are a testament to the team’s dedication, technical excellence, and spirit of innovation, bringing great laurels to our institute.
We have witnessed firsthand the students’ unwavering commitment, tireless efforts, and enthusiasm in designing and developing their new Go-Kart, ‘Karna’. Their teamwork, perseverance, and passion for engineering truly deserve heartfelt appreciation. 👏
We extend our sincere gratitude to the HoD (Mechanical Engineering), Associate Dean (Student Activities and Sports), Dean (Student Welfare), and our Hon’ble Director Sir for their constant encouragement, guidance, and unwavering support.
🎉 Heartiest congratulations to Team Velocity! May this achievement mark the beginning of many more milestones, innovations, and victories in the future.
Keep innovating. Keep racing. Keep making us proud! 🏁
