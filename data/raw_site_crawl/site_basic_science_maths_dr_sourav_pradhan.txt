Brief Bio
Dr. Sourav Pradhan
He has received the Doctoral Degree (Ph.D.) from IIT Kharagpur and Master Degree (M.Sc.) from IIT Madras
His research interests are Modeling and Analysis of Queueing Systems, Parameter Estimation, Stochastic Processes.
He has published 14 research articles in reputed journals. He has visited USA for presenting a research work in a conference. He is currently guding 3 research scholars.
