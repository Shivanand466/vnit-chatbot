About Ph. D.
Faculty members are supervising part time Ph.D. since 1970 and subsequently full time Ph.D started since 2009. Until now, 57 nos. of Ph.D. have been completed in department majorly in the areas of steel structures, concrete structures, wind engineering, earthquake engineering etc. Recently faculty members have also started supervising Ph.D. in the areas of blast and impact, advanced materials, structural fire engineering, corrosion engineering, and precast structures.

M.Tech. Schemes
Department of Applied Mechanics offers two M. Tech programs, namely,
M. Tech. in Structural Engineering, M. Tech. in Earthquake Structural Engineering and M. Tech. in Forensic Structural Engineering
. These are four semester program, wherein student has to complete certain number of credits as indicated in Table below. Each subject (or course) has certain number of credits. There are two types of subjects: Core and Electives. Core courses are compulsory and some courses from electives are to be taken to complete the required credits.

Curriculum:
Department of Applied Mechanics offers two M. Tech program, namely,
M. Tech. in Structural Engineering, M. Tech. in Earthquake Structural Engineering and M. Tech. in Forensic Structural Engineering
. Student has to complete certain number of credits wherein, each subject (or course) has certain number of credits. The number of credits attached to a subject depends on number of classes in a week. For example a subject with 3-1-0 (L-T-P) means it has 3 Lectures, 1 Tutorial and 0 Practical in a week. This subject will have four credits (3×1 + 1×1 + 0x1 = 4). If a student is declared pass in a subject, then he/she gets the credits associated with that subject. Depending on marks scored in a subject, student is given a Grade. Each grade has got certain grade points as follows:
