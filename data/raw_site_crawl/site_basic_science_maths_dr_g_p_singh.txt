Brief Bio
Dr. Gyan Prakash Singh is Professor(HAG) of Mathematics, and Dean ( Faculty Welfare ) VNIT, Nagpur. Recently Prof. Singh is elected as a Fellow of Maharashtra Academy of Science. He is also Fellow of Royal Astronomical Society, London, U.K . He has worked as Head of the Department (three terms) and Dean(students wefare) 2013-2016, Dean(Planning and Development) 2018-2021. Prof. G. P. Singh joined VNIT (formerly VRCE ) as Lecture in 1995 and selected as Professor in 2010, upgraded to Professor (HAG scale) in 2019 . Dr. G. P. Singh did his Ph D  in 1992 from Institute of Technology (Presently IIT) BHU, Varanasi in 1992. Dr. Singh has also worked at Department of Mathematics,IIT Madras as a Post Doctoral Fellow(National Board for Higher Mathematics) till 1995. He is a visiting associate of Inter University Centre for Astronomy and Astophysics(IUCAA) Pune. Prof. Singh has published more than 90 research papers with good citations in google scholar ( https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=_qIwRmkAAAAJ&hl=en ), web of science ( https://www.webofscience.com/wos/author/record/M-4142-2015 ), scopus (https://www.scopus.com/authid/detail.uri?authorId=35414434300 ) . He has guided 7 Ph.D. , 18 PG students. He has visited TIFR in 1993 for month as visiting scientist, University of Zululand South Africa for 3 months in 1996 and for two months in 1998 as visiting post doctoral Fellow. Prof. Singh has organised 9 conferences/seminars /workshops and participated in more than 50 such events. He has delivered 24 invited talks in conferences/seminars /workshops and several motivational talks to school students. He is helping more than 5 autonomus engineering colleges is designing/upgrading syllabus as a subject expert member of board of studies. He has worked as Council member of Indian Mathematical Society 2017-2020.  He is life member of Indian Mathematical Society, Indian Association for General Relativity and Gravitation, Indian Science Congress Association, Indian Society for Technical Education. In addition to academic and research contribution, Prof. G. P. Singh has significantly contributed to the Institue administration as Chief Warden, Warden, Welfare Officer, Chairman and member of various committees,.
