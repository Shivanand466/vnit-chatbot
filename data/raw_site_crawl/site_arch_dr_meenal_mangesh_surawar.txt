Dr. Meenal Mangesh Surawar
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. Meenal Surawar, currently serves as an Assistant Professor at the Department of Architecture & Planning, VNIT, Nagpur. She has done her B. Arch. (1997) from VRCE (then & VNIT now), Nagpur & continued her architectural & interior design professional practice for a decade. She did her M. Tech. in Urban Planning (2011) & PhD (2015) from VNIT, Nagpur & started her career in academia. She is a recipient of a doctoral degree with a PhD thesis on Urban Heat Island studies & has 24 years of experience in professional practice & in academics. Her core competencies are Urban Heat Island, Geographic Information Systems & Remote Sensing, Housing, Architectural Design, Landscape Design, Climate Responsive Architecture, Theory of Architecture & Net Zero Energy Efficient Building Design. She has been teaching at VNIT, Nagpur since June 2016 to undergraduate, post-graduate & PhD students. She is actively involved in research & publishing her work as well as serving various administrative services at the institute level. Currently, she is researching Urban Heat Island impacts on various aspects such as Urban Sprawl, Urban Growth Boundaries, Compact Cities, Urban Flooding, Blue Green Infrastructure, Walkability & Building Envelope. She published several refereed peer reviewed journals & international conference research papers, organised & attended various workshops during her tenure. She is associated with reputed publishers like Springer Nature, Elsevier, Taylor and Francis, etc. as an author and reviewer. She also contributes through research projects in the domain of Urban Planning & often shares her work through various Faculty Development programmes as a guest speaker. She also serves as a faculty mentor every year to an interdisciplinary team for the Solar Decathlon India Challenge from 2020 onwards.She is an avid reader & as writing is her desired hobby, she recently authored a book, titled ‘ACUMEN of Architectural Built’ published on Jan 2, 2023, by Metro Books, N. Delhi. She has a unique voice that shines in her book, wherein she tries to highlight an emergent need to understand & analyse an architectural form. She wanted to contribute, through her journaling, to the much wider freshmen, who are our future assets to develop a sustainable built environment. She believes that architecture is well perceived through their experiential spaces by the students &, thereby, conducts study tours, which were although a part of the academic curriculum, every year for better exploration & enjoys travelling with students.She follows her routine by exercising, loves to play badminton & sometimes cycling too, while listening to music is her favourite pastime.
