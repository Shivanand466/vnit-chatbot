VNIT Nagpur Organises PoSH Act Awareness Programme vnit_web
November 13, 2026
News
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, through its Internal Complaint Committee (ICC), organised a full-day
PoSH Act Awareness Programme on 5th November 2026 at the Multi Activity Centre, VNIT Nagpur, for PG and PhD students, faculty and staff.
The programme was inaugurated in the presence of Hon’ble Director
Dr. Prem Lal Patel
, who welcomed the distinguished speaker
Ms. Preeti Sakhre
, associated with the Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India.
Dr. Madhuri Choudhari, Chairperson, ICC
, introduced the speaker and highlighted the significance of awareness about the PoSH Act, 2013.
Ms. Preeti Sakhre sensitised participants about the origin and provisions of the PoSH Act, workplace safety, prevention and redressal mechanisms, the
SHe-Box online complaint portal
, Nirbhaya Committee and Pink Cell initiatives. Through case studies and audio-visual examples, she explained workplace harassment, relevant IPC provisions, legal consequences and the role of national and state-level committees. She also interacted with the VNIT ICC members to discuss various aspects of effective implementation.
The programme was conducted in two sessions and witnessed enthusiastic participation from 590 participants
, comprising:
175 PhD Scholars 101 PG Students 182 Faculty Members 127 Staff Members
An engaging question-and-answer session further enriched the participants’ understanding of women’s safety, rights and institutional redressal mechanisms.
The programme concluded with the felicitation of the speaker and a formal vote of thanks by
Ms. Poonam Uike, ICC Member
.
VNIT Nagpur remains committed to fostering a safe, respectful, inclusive and harassment-free campus environment.
