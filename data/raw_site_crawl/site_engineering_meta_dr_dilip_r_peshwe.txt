Bio bio
Dr. Dilip R. Peshwe is currently professor and Head of the Department of Metallurgial and Materials Engineering, VNIT, Nagpur. He has an experience of 39 years. His areas of interest are physical metallurgy, welding, casting process, wear of engineering materials, tribology, polymers & composites.
