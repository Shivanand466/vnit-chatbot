Prof. Uttkarsha Dhurve
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
Prof. Utkarsha Pramod Dhurve is an Assistant Professor in Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, (VNIT) Nagpur. She holds a bachelor’s degree in Architecture from VNIT, Nagpur and master’s degree in Landscape Architecture from School of Planning and Architecture, Delhi. She has a professional experience in the industry for more than 3 years working in Smart cities and Manufacturing projects. As a professor in Architecture and Planning department her expertise lies in subjects like Landscape Design, Basic Design, Architectural History, Theory of Architecture and Building Construction. Outside work she enjoys hand-works, gardening and travelling.
