About Hostel
The APJ Abdul Kalam Hostel serves as a contemporary accommodation facility for postgraduate and doctoral students.
The hostel is named in honour of the esteemed scientist and 11th President of India, Bharat Ratna Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam (15 October 1931 – 27 July 2015), whose life and accomplishments symbolize inspiration and excellence in the fields of science, technology, education, and public service. Dr. Kalam, affectionately known as the “Missile Man of India,” played a crucial role in advancing India’s missile program, overseeing successful initiatives for ISRO and DRDO.
He led significant defence projects, particularly the Agni and Prithvi missiles, and coordinated India’s Pokhran-II nuclear tests, establishing India as a nuclear power.
The APJ Abdul Kalam Hostel offers:
Spacious, well-furnished 380 rooms—typically arranged for single occupancy—equipped with essential furniture including beds, tables, chairs, and cupboards for each resident.
Ethernet connectivity and water coolers on every floor.
Recreational facilities: TV rooms, sports rooms, and table tennis.
24/7 security featuring CCTV surveillance and dedicated hostel staff.
Access to newspapers and magazines for residents.
An IoT-enabled 10kg front load washing machine, situated on the ground floor, which students can operate through a mobile application.
The Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Hostel not only offers accommodation but also aims to cultivate a nurturing environment for future leaders, scientists, and socially responsible citizens, in alignment with Kalam’s enduring principles.
