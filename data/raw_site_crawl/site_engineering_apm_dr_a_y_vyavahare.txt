Dr. Arvind Y. Vyavahare
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Arvind Vyavahare graduated with a degree in Civil Engineering from the Government College of Engineering, Aurangabad (Ch. Sambhajinagar) in 1999. He obtained his M.Tech. in Structural Engineering from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur in 2003. In 2016, he earned his Ph.D. in Structural Engineering from the same institution, with his research focusing on the "Investigation into Structural Steel Semi-Rigid Connections."
