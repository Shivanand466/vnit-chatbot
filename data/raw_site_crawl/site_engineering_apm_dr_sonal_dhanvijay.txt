Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Sonal Dhanvijay is currently serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Applied Mechanics at VNIT Nagpur. Her academic journey commenced at VNIT Nagpur, where she earned her Bachelors of Engineering degree in 2010. She pursued higher education at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) in Bangalore, culminating in the successful completion of her doctoral studies. Dr. Dhanvijay transitioned to the industry, working as a software designer for mechanical testing equipment at ITW India Pvt Limited, BiSS Division, Bangalore, from 2019 to 2020. In 2020, she returned to academia and assumed therole of Assistant Professor in the Department of Applied Mechanics at VNIT Nagpur. Her research interests encompass Experimental Structural Dynamics, Random Vibration Analysis, and Hybrid Simulation. She has conducted an SERB-sponsored High end workshop (KARYASHALA) on Introduction to Random Vibrations and Structural Reliability and is currently engaged in a project titled Real-Time Hybrid Simulation using Shake Table system," sponsored by SERB. Dr. Dhanvijay was awarded the IASSAR Scholarship, enabling her attendance at the 12th International Conference on Structural Safetyand Reliability (ICOSSAR 2017) held at TU Wein, Vienna, Austria. She is a life member of the Association for Consulting Civil Engineers and the Indian Concrete Institute,
