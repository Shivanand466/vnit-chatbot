Bio
Brief Bio
Welcome to the official homepage of Dr. Udaysinh T. Bhosale. He is a theoretical physicist. He graduated (B.Sc. in Physics) from Sir Parshurambhau College, Pune, Maharashtra. Thereafter, he went to the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, to pursue an M.Sc. in Physics. He completed his M.Sc. in 2008. He then again joined in PhD program at IITM under the guidance of Prof. Arul Lakshminarayan and completed it in 2014. He then joined IISER Pune as an institute postdoctoral fellow in 2015 and continued as National Postdoctoral Fellow till 2018 with Prof. M. S. Santhanam. He then joined as an institute postdoctoral fellow in the Physical Research Laboratory with Prof. Dilip Angom and continued till May 2020. Subsequently, he joined as an assistant professor in physics at VNIT Nagpur.
