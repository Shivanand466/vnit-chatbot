Brief
Bio
Brief Bio
An educator with expertise in Literature, English Language Teaching, Gerontology, Mental Health, Blue Humanities and Digital Humanities. Her focus is on enhancing communication skills, exploring the link between literature and gerontology, and addressing Mental Health issues. In the realm of digital humanities, she leverages technology for literary studies and aims to motivate the next generation of scholars by highlighting the transformative power of literature in lives.
