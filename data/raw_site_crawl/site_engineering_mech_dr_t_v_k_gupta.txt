Dr. T. V. K. Gupta
Brief Bio
Dr. T V K Gupta works in manufacturing specialization particularly related to aerospace alloys like Inconel 718. His research areas include Metal Additive Manufacturing, Freeform Surface Machining, Laser Cladding, Residual Stresses Evaluation, etc. He worked on Abrasive Water Jet Machining for micro channel fabrication during PhD at IIT Kanpur. He was a visiting scholar at University of San Diego, USA; Hong Kong State University and University of Birmingham, UK. His teaching interests include Manufacturing Processes, Advanced Machining Processes, CAD/CAM. His professional experiences include, Grade-I Research Engineer at IIT Kanpur (2000-2010) where he established a Hi-Tech Fabrication Facility famously known as 4i-Lab (Ideation, Innovation, Incubation and Implementation) and as Visiting Faculty at PDPM IIITDM Jabalpur (2010-2013). Currently he is Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, VNIT Nagpur.
