Brief
Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Neelima Bhengra, Assistant Professor at Mathematics Department VNIT Nagpur joined the institute on June 2022. She was awarded with PhD degree in the year 2020 from  Indian Institute of Technology (IIT ISM) Dhanbad. Her research interests are Mathematical modeling in Differential Equations, Elastodynamics, wave mechanics, and solid mechanics. She brought her passion for research and teaching to the forefront, inspiring students with her depth of knowledge and innovative approach to scientific inquiry. Her work has been published in prestigious scientific journals and presented at international conferences, earning her recognition in the academic community.
