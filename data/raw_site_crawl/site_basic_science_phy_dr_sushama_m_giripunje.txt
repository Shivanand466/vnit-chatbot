Brief Bio
Dr. Sushama Milind Giripunje is working as Professor in Physics, at VNIT Nagpur. She completed her  PGDCA(postgraduate advance diploma in Computer application) from ADCC Centre,Nagpur, affiliated to Bombay Technical Board, M.Sc.(Physics) and Ph.D.(Physics) from Institute of Science Nagpur. She is having 28 years teaching and research experience. Her area of research includes Polymers, Nanomaterials, advanced functional materials, Energy harvesting materials & their applications. She has guided 23 students for their master’s dissertation and 6 Ph.D. students. She has published more than 40 research papers in SCI journals and contributed one book chapter. She has been granted 2 Patents based on her research work. She is currently supervising 7 Ph.D scholars. She is a life member of Materials Research Society of India, Astronomical Society of India, The Indian Association Of  Physics Teachers(IAPT), Asian Polymer Association(APA) etc.
