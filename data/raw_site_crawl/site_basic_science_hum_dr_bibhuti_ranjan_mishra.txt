Brief Bio
Dr. Bibhuti Ranjan Mishra is an Associate Professor of Economics in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences at the Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, India. He holds a Ph.D. in Economics from the School of Economics, University of Hyderabad, and is a recipient of the prestigious ICSSR Doctoral Fellowship.
His primary research interests lie in Macro-econometrics, Public Finance and Policy, and Energy Economics. An active scholar, Dr. Mishra has published 19 international peer-reviewed papers since 2017 in leading journals such as Energy, Resources Policy, Economic Analysis and Policy, and Environmental Science and Pollution Research. He has also successfully completed two research projects funded by the Indian Council of Social Science Research (ICSSR).
At VNIT Nagpur, his teaching portfolio includes courses such as Principles of Economics, Money & Financial Markets, Building Economics, and Economics for Engineers. Beyond the classroom, Dr. Mishra serves the broader academic community as an editorial board member for Springer Nature’s SN Business & Economics and Humanities and Social Sciences Communications, and as a reviewer for GIAN proposals and roughly 40 high-impact international journals.
