TITLE: VNIT Nagpur Alumni reunite in Boston!

CRAWL: full-2026-11-19

On Nov 5, 2025, Dr. Diwakar Z. Shende (VNIT Alumni Coordinator) hosted a powerful meet-up during the 2025 AIChE Annual Meeting.
Alumni from Texas A&M, Imperial College, IIT Kanpur, NC State & IIT Bombay pledged support for internships, skill modules & global collaborations.
Proudly guided by legends Er. Surendra Dudani Ji (’73 EEE) & Sanjana Malani (’07 EEE).
🤝 Stronger together — for VNIT’s future!
