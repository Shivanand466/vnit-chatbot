Brief Bio
Dr. Sourav Bhattacharya was born on March 21, 1992, in Agartala, Tripura. He completed his Higher Secondary Examination in 2010 and subsequently pursued a B.Sc (Hons) in Mathematics at Presidency College, Kolkata, graduating in 2013. That same year, he enrolled in the Master’s Program in Pure Mathematics at the University of Calcutta's Department of Pure Mathematics, completing his degree in 2015. In August 2016, Dr. Bhattacharya began his Ph.D. in Mathematics at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, USA, with the support of a full scholarship and a graduate teaching assistantship. His doctoral research focused on Topology, Dynamical Systems, and Ergodic Theory. He graduated with a Ph.D. in May 2021 and received the Chernov Brin Best Ph.D. Student Award for his outstanding performance in graduate studies in Dynamical Systems. In June 2022, Dr. Bhattacharya joined the Department of Mathematics at VNIT Nagpur as an Assistant Professor. His research interests include Topology, Dynamical Systems, and Ergodic Theory. He is dedicated to advancing the field of mathematics and mentoring future mathematicians.
