A proud moment for the VNIT community!
Delighted to share that  Dr. Dinesh Keskar (VRCE 1975-Mech), our Distinguished Alumnus  has been honoured with the prestigious Kautz Master Alumni Award by the University of Cincinnati.
Dr. Dinesh has had an outstanding four-decade career in the aerospace industry, including senior leadership roles at Boeing, and has made a significant contribution to the growth of Boeing’s presence in India and the Asia-Pacific region.
It is truly heartening to see a fellow VNIT alumnus achieve such distinction on the global stage.
Congratulations, Dinesh sir, on this well-deserved honour! It is a matter of great pride for VNIT and  VNIT alumni community.
Sharing the University of Cincinnati’s feature on his remarkable journey.
