Dr. R. V. Uddanwadikar
Brief Bio
Dr. Rashmi V. Uddanwadiker is a Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur, where she has been serving since 2006. She holds the distinction of being the first woman faculty member in the Mechanical Engineering Department at the institute.With a distinguished career spanning 29 years in teaching and research, Dr. Uddanwadiker has made significant interdisciplinary contributions in the field of Biomedical Engineering, working closely with orthopaedic surgeons, plastic surgeons, and dental specialists to translate engineering innovations into practical medical solutions.Since January 2024, she has been serving as the Associate Dean (Training and Placement) at VNIT Nagpur, becoming the first woman in the institute’s history to lead the placement division—a milestone reflecting her leadership and institutional impact.Dr. Uddanwadiker has successfully supervised 12 doctoral and 38 Master’s students, with 8 PhD scholars awarded and several ongoing. Her scholarly contributions include 13 granted patents, 44+ publications in reputed international journals, and 8 best paper awards at prestigious conferences. She has also authored 3 books and holds 2 copyrights.Her excellence has been recognized with notable honors, including the Inspiring Woman Researcher Award (2014) and the Woman Innovator Award (2019).An accomplished innovator and entrepreneur, she is the Founder Director of NU Ossa Mediquip Pvt. Ltd., a startup focused on manufacturing academic dissection models for ENT surgeons. The company successfully graduated from the VNIT incubation ecosystem and was subsequently acquired by a larger medical equipment firm.Dr. Uddanwadiker has developed a wide range of impactful products and technologies across healthcare and industrial domains, including:Surgical helmet for orthopaedic surgeonsCustomized nasal implants for Asian populationsGastrostomy feeding button for infants with congenital anomaliesSnoring control device for Class II malocclusionAdvanced surgical tools for rhinoplastyAaRaM device for painless injectionsCervix measuring deviceMultipurpose frying and ergonomic roasting machines for small-scale industriesPedal-operated agarbatti manufacturing workstationHer research also extends to pioneering technologies such as:Techniques for measuring skeletal muscle strength and fatigueImage-processing-based bone–implant alignment without X-ray dependencySoftware for digital dental restoration (design, analysis, and manufacturing)Simulation-based coaching systems for sports biomechanics (e.g., cricket straight drive)Biomechanics of lingual orthodontics
