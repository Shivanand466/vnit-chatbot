BTech Course Highlights
BTech core courses to cover entire GATE (CSE) syllabus
BTech core courses to be completed by 3rd year
A wide range of electives are included to cover most of the areas of current research in computer science and engineering.
To add industry perspective, practicing people are invited to conduct full courses.
Department offers 3 Open Courses and 4 other Department Courses
In a Minor course, a student has to complete 1 extra subject per semester from 3rd to 6th Semester
BTech Higher Sem Courses Overview
Maths – Prob & Stats(3), Discrete Maths (3), Linear Algebra (4)
Theory – Data Structures (3/4), Programming Languages(4), Algorithms(5), Theory of Computation(5)
Systems – Digital Ckts(3), Computer Organization (4), OS (5), Networks(5), Compilers(6), Databases (6), Security (6), S/W Engg(6)
Electives – OOP (4), Web Prog (4/5), Fuzzy (5), Image Proc(6), AI (7), ML(8), Information Retrieval (7), Pattern Reco (7), Cryptography(7), Deep Learning (8), Game Theory (8), Business Intelligence (8), etc.

MTech Course Highlights
MTech core courses to cover computer science essentials – Mathematics, Programming, Theory, and Systems
MTech electives to be in research areas of faculty and/or teaching support provided by industry – CS Core, Image Processing, Security, Bio, NLP, Systems Design, Data Analytics, AI/ML, Fuzzy, etc.
MTech students can complete their theory courses in 2 semesters, leaving 2 semesters for project work
