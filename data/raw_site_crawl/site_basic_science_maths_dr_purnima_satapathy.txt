Dr. Purnima Satapathy
Brief
Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Purnima Satapathy currently working as an assistant professor at VNIT Nagpur from August 2020. Her area of specialization is Lie symmetry analysis and Partial Differential Equations. She received her Doctoral degree from Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur in the year 2019. Dr. Purnima has received postdoctoral fellowship from Israel for post PhD research. She has received prestigious fellowships and awards from graduation onwards. She has keen interest for teaching and has taught various mathematics courses. Dr. Purnima actively participates in conferences and workshops, contributing to the advancement of mathematics.
