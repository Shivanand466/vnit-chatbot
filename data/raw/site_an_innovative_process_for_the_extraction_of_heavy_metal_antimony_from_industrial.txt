VNIT Nagpur Develops Smart Technology for Antimony Recovery from Industrial Waste
An innovative process for the extraction of heavy metal antimony from industrial waste
, developed under the leadership of
Prof. (Dr.) Shriram S. Sonawane
, Professor, Department of Chemical Engineering, VNIT Nagpur, has been granted an Indian Patent and featured prominently in leading newspapers, including
Hitavada, Tarun Bharat and Lokmat
.
The achievement reflects VNIT Nagpur’s commitment to translating cutting-edge research into innovative, sustainable and industry-relevant technologies.
