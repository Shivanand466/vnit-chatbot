Procedure for online registration of Ph.D. students (Admitted in January 2026 and earlier) for Winter-2026
All Students are required to select all backlog courses (Compulsorily) (if any), without selecting Backlog courses (if offered by the department) registration process will not move ahead.
Approach Faculty Advisor (FA) for Approval of Registration. FA will do Approval online.
Student will have to abide by the suggestions of FA on changes in the registered courses. Without Approval of registration by FA, student's registration will remain incomplete and student name will not appear in the roll list of the subjects he is registering for.
After approval of registration by FA, student shall login to his/her LOGIN area and ensure that registration status has become "REGISTRATION APPROVED". Else, registration will be treated as incomplete & name of student will not appear in roll list.
