TITLE: VNIT, Nagpur Secures Rupees 232 Lakhs Grant to Pioneer TEXMiN-Mining CPS Centre of Excellence
FETCHED: 2026-11-19
CRAWL: full-2026-11-19
---
The Honorable Director, VNIT, Nagpur, extends heartfelt felicitations to Dr. Anupam Kher, Department of Mining Engineering, and his esteemed team for securing the sanction fund letter to establish the TEXMiN-Mining Cyber Physical System (CPS) Centre of Excellence at VNIT Nagpur.
A total budget of ₹232.00 Lakhs has been approved to develop Cyber-Physical System (CPS) solutions as per the TEXMiN mandate for Technology Development (TD) and Innovation & Entrepreneurship (IE). The CoE is approved for a duration of four years and will focus on:
1. Developing CPS-based solutions for the mining sector 2. Supporting technology development projects 3. Establishing pre-incubation facilities for students 4. Enabling collaborative research with industry
The CoE will serve as a strategic platform to enhance digital technologies in mining, boost industry-academia linkages, and drive student entrepreneurship.
We acknowledge the tireless efforts of the coordinating faculty members:
– Dr. Anupam Anant Kher, Assistant Professor, Mining Engineering
– Dr. R. R. Yerpude, Professor, Mining Engineering
– Dr. Y. B. Katpatal, Professor, Civil Engineering
– Dr. R. D. Lokhande, Associate Professor, Mining Engineering
We extend our sincerest wishes to the team for success in this pioneering endeavor.
