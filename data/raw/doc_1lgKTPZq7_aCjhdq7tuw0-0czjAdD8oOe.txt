8. List of Practicals
Preliminary Practices
Body Scan Meditation - Sequential awareness of bodily sensations to build present-moment focus and relaxation.
Rhythmic Breath Awareness - Regulated breathing practices to stabilize the mind and body before meditation.
Core Meditation Practices
Breath Meditation - Anchoring attention on natural breathing to cultivate calm and focus.
Mindfulness of Five Senses - Observing sensory input (sight, sound, smell, taste, touch) without judgment.
Mindfulness of Daily Actions - Bringing awareness to routine activities like walking, eating, or writing.
Loving-Kindness Meditation - Generating feelings of goodwill towards self and others.
Compassion Meditation - Developing empathy for the suffering and challenges of others.
Gratitude Meditation - Fostering appreciation and contentment through reflective awareness.
Visualization Meditation - Using mental imagery to evoke specific mental states or aspirations.
Walking and Observation Meditation - Practicing awareness during slow walking, with attention to environment.
Mindfulness of Mental States
Mindfulness of Eight Worldly Concerns - Observing reactions to gain/loss, praise/blame, fame/disrepute, pleasure/pain.
Mindfulness of Attachment - Recognizing clinging tendencies and cultivating equanimity.
Mindfulness of Aversion (Anger) - Identifying aversive thoughts and applying calming antidotes.
Mindfulness of Ignorance - Reflecting on habitual patterns and unawareness of true causes of discontent.
Meditation on Self-Purification - Introspective practice to acknowledge and release negative tendencies.
Meditation on Emptiness - Analytical meditation on the absence of inherent existence in phenomena.
Wisdom Meditation - Cultivating clarity and insight into the nature of the mind and reality.
Mindfulness in Communication and Interaction
Mindful Speaking - Bringing awareness and intention to verbal expression.
Mindful Listening - Deep listening practices with full attention and empathy.
Mindful Eating Meditation - Engaging fully with the sensory and experiential aspects of eating.
🔹
Lecture (1 hour/week)
 Introduction of weekly topic
 20 minutes - Teaching Component
 Presentation via slides with stories, real-life analogies, and references from Indian Knowledge Systems
 The meditation is aligned with the teaching topic
 20 minutes - Guided Meditation
 10 minutes - Q&A Session
 Ensures understanding and engagement
 Open floor for clarification or brief reflections
Outcome: Conceptual clarity + guided experiential anchor.
🔹
Tutorial (1 hour/week)
 Deeper practice of the technique introduced in the lecture
 30 minutes - Extended Meditation Session
 May include silent sitting, walking, or contemplative meditation
 1:1 or small-group discussions facilitated by the instructor
 30 minutes - Personal Discussion / Doubt Clearing
 Topics:
 Observations during practice
 Difficulties in meditation
 Clarifications on method or philosophy
 Encourages self-awareness and refinement of practice
Outcome: Deepening of practice and personalized guidance.
🔹
Lab (2 hours/week)
 Practice sessions directly aligned with List of 10 Practicals
 Two 30-minute Practice Sessions (Total 1 hour)
 Example:
 Week 2: Mindfulness of Five Senses
 Week 1: Breath Meditation & Body Scan
 … up to Week 10
 Group Sharing / Peer Reflection
 Remaining 1 Hour - Reflective & Interactive Components
 Book Review Presentations
 Mini Research Article Discussions
 Personal Journal Reflections
 Mindful Communication Role-Plays
Outcome: Integration of theoretical and personal experience through active learning.
