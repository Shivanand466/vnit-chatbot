Dr. Sandeep  Panchal​
Brief Bio
Dr. Sandeep Panchal is presently working as Assistant Professor (Grade-I) in Department of Mining Engineering, VNIT Nagpur. He completed B. E. (Mining Engineering) from CTAE (MPUAT), Udaipur in 2006. Later he obtained M. Tech (Mining Engineering) from IIT Kharagpur in 2008. He received Ph. D. from IIT Kharagpur in 2018. His teaching and research areas of interest include Rock Mechanics, Ground Control, Pastefill Technology and Rock Blasting. He has academic and research experience of 18 years. Presently he is Supervisor/Co-supervisor of 6 Ph.D. students and hitherto, he has guided/Co-guided 1 Ph.D. and 11 M. Tech (Excavation Engineering) students. He has authored/Co-authored journal papers/ book chapters and presented research papers in international conferences held in India, Spain, Austria, Turkey, Singapore, Thailand, and Sri Lanka. He has received TAMOTIA AWARD as IIME best paper award in International Conference. He is a life time member of IEI, ISRM, ISRMTT, MGMI and MEAI. He has conducted National Workshop and Short-Term Training Program and has delivered expert lectures in industry, Short-Term Training Program and Certification program. He has worked in many consultancy projects as Co-investigator/member. He has handled various additional responsibilities at institute and departmental level mainly, Hostel warden, Faculty In-charge of Student’s club, Member of Vigilance Cum Academic Disciplinary Action Committee, Angi-ragging squad and Disciplinary Action Committee for act of indiscipline, NBA Coordinator (UG, Mining), TEQIP Coordinator (Mining), Alumni Coordinator (Mining),Conclave Coordinator (Mining), Faculty Coordinator of Student Mentor Program, Faculty Advisor (UG & PG), Departmental Exam Coordinator, Faculty In-charge of Industrial Training and Mine Tours (UG & PG).
