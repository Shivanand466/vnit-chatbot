Greetings of the day!
We hope you received our previous communication regarding the Kotak Kanya Scholarship 2025-26. The scholarship is currently open for girl students who have completed Class 12 and are currently enrolled in government schools/colleges.
Program details are shared below for your reference.
Eligibility:
Open for meritorious girl students across India.
Applicants must have scored 75% or more marks or equivalent CGPA in Class 12 board examinations.
Applicant's annual family income must be less than INR 6,00,000.
Meritorious girl students who have secured admission to the first year of graduation programs in the academic year 2025-26 in institutes of repute like NIRF/NAAC accredited for professional graduation degrees like Engineering, MBBS, Integrated LLB (5 Years), Integrated BS-MS/BS-Research, in ISER, IISC(Bangalore), or other professional courses (Design, Architecture, etc.).
Award: Scholarship of INR 1.5 lakh* per year
Application Deadline: August 31, 2025
Application URL: http://www.b4s.in/a/em_KKGS4_20250624_1
