About the STTP
Digital technologies offer powerful tools for assessing and improving sustainability in the built environment. These technologies enable more efficient resource management, optimised energy consumption, and reduced environmental impact through data-driven insights and automation. Digital Approaches support energy efficiency, reduce environmental impact, improve decision-making, and increase resilience.
Key Digital Approaches:
Internet of Things (IoT) and Sensors
Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML)
Digital Twins
Building Information Modelling (BIM)
