Sub: Registration for OC/HM courses for the Session Winter 2026
All the students who are interested to register for OC / HM courses are instructed to note the following for the Session Winter 2026,
Important note:
All students under Minor Degree Program are instructed to opt "OC / HM" Course of Slot "A" only, as all Minor courses are going to be offered compulsorily in Slot "B".
List of various courses offered for Winter 2026 is enclosed with this notice.
For every OC or HM, course maximum number of students to be allowed is mentioned against individual course.
Students have to apply through Google Form for OC/HM during the registration schedulegiven below. Process of applying for Oc / HM through Google Form is Mandatory.
Registration of OC/HM courses is not linked with the payment of fee.
A student can register for maximum ONE OC or ONE HM course, in a semester.
Registration done for OC or HM type course shall not be changed or cancelled in any circumstances.
If it is found that, a student has registered in 2 or more OC/HM courses, his / her name will be deleted from all the OC/HM courses (he / she has applied for W26). Requests for consideration in such cases will not be entertained.
AWR Students / Extra Year students are also instructed to follow the above procedure. Prevailing practice of allocating OC / HM courses at the time to registration to such students is discontinued. Request for allocation / allotment of OC / HM courses at the time of AWR Registration will not be entertained.
VISVESVARAYA NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, NAGPUR
Exam/Regn W-26/1393 May 08,2026
A student can earn maximum 12 credits for OC & maximum 6 credits for HM (Excluding 5 credits of 1st Year HM / HU Courses) during his/her program of study, however total credit can be earned for OC + HM Courses should not be exceeded 12 Credits (during the complete duration of course).
No offline requests for these OC or HM courses will be taken into consideration.
