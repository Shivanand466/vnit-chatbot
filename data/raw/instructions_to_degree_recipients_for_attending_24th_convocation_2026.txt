2
Collection of Scarves
Scarves will be available in your respective departments only 14th September 2026 (Monday) from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
No scarves will be distributed on the day of the convocation.
3
Parking Instructions
Parking near the auditorium will be restricted. Kindly cooperate with volunteers and security personnel to ensure smooth parking arrangements.
4
Seating & Timing
Degree recipients must be seated before 8:15 AM.
Late entries will NOT be permitted under any circumstances, including unforeseen travel delays.
Once seated, recipients must remain seated until the ceremony concludes.
5
Auditorium Entry
Enter through the main entrance near the old gym.
6
Mobile Phones & Cameras
Mobile phones and cameras are not allowed inside the auditorium.
Please arrange for safe storage beforehand.
7
Program Schedule on 15th Sept 2026 (Duration ~ 03 hours):
Academic procession enters
(Entry gates of the auditorium will be closed.)
Report by Director, VNIT
Convocation address by Chairman, BoG
Convocation address by Chief Guest
Award of degrees (Ph.D., PG, then UG)
Award of medals and prizes
Oath by all recipients, led by the Visvesvaraya Gold Medal recipient
Vote of thanks
National Anthem
Academic procession exits
8
Post-Program Arrangements
Snack boxes will be distributed to degree recipients after the ceremony upon presenting the food coupon.
Venue details will be announced during the event.
9
Seating for Parents
Parents are not permitted inside the auditorium during the ceremony.
