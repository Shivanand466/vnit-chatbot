Mr. Akash Yelne, an alumnus of VNIT, 2015 graduate interacted with the students of Metallurgical and materials Engineering as well as Other Departments on 7th October 2021, sharing his journey from joining VNIT to currently working at Tesla, San Francisco, addressing various queries of students regarding academics, higher studies as well as finding the right job.
The event was organised by the Department of MME.
