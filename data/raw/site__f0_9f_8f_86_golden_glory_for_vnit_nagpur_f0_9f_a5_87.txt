🏆 GOLDEN GLORY FOR VNIT NAGPUR! 🥇 vnit_web
November 2, 2026
News
Heartiest congratulations to the VNIT Nagpur Women’s Handball Team for securing 1st Position and winning the Gold Medal along with the Champion Trophy at the All India Inter-NIT Handball (Men & Women) Tournament, held at NIT Rourkela from 30 October to 1 November 2026.
Adding to the glory, Ms. Ujjwala Singh of VNIT Nagpur was adjudged the Best Women Player of the Tournament and honoured with an individual trophy. 🏆
The achievement reflects the dedication, teamwork and sporting spirit of our players. Prof. Dr. Prem Lal Patel, Director, VNIT Nagpur, encouraged and motivated the team, inspiring them to strive for excellence.
A remarkable achievement bringing pride and honour to VNIT Nagpur!
👏 Congratulations to the entire team, coach and everyone associated with this success!
