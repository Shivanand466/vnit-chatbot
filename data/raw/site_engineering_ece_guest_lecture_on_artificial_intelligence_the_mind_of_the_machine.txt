The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, is pleased to announce a distinguished guest lecture organized in collaboration with
Queen Mary University of London
.
The lecture will be delivered by
Dr. Paulo Oliva
, School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Queen Mary University of London.
Lecture Details:
Topic:
Artificial Intelligence – The Mind of the Machine
Date:
21 April 2026 (Tuesday)
Time:
10:00 AM – 11:00 AM
Venue:
Lecture Hall 1 (LH-1), ECE Department
About the Lecture:
This session aims to provide valuable insights into the rapidly evolving field of Artificial Intelligence, exploring the conceptual foundations and emerging advancements that are shaping the future of intelligent systems.
The lecture also seeks to foster academic exchange and strengthen research collaboration between VNIT Nagpur and Queen Mary University of London.
Who Should Attend:
All undergraduate, postgraduate, and research students with an interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and related domains are encouraged to attend.
