About Club
S.H.E.L.L is the official cybersecurity club of NIT Nagpur, dedicated to fostering a strong security culture. Since its founding in 2017 by Aaditya Gupta, the club has grown into a powerful team of security lovers.
Our alumni have gone on to work in top security roles, excelling in penetration testing, cryptography, web security, and more.
⚡
What We Do
· Compete &amp; host Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions
· Conduct hands-on workshops on security topics
· Engage in bug bounty &amp; penetration testing
· Contribute to open-source cybersecurity projects
· Provide mentorship &amp; guidance for students
