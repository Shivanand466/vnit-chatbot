Terms & Conditions
This ad-hoc appointment is purely temporary (not exceeding 3 months at a time ) and is made only to ensure uninterrupted execution of the Sponsored Research Project during the pendency of the regular appointment process through advertisement. However, the cumulative duration of such ad-hoc appointments against a sanctioned position under this provision in a given project shall not exceed 6 months.
The remuneration/fellowship for this ad-hoc appointment shall be paid strictly under the Manpower head of the sanctioned project budget subject to availability of funds and shall be released only on submission of the prescribed Pre-Receipt Voucher , duly verified by the Principal Investigator, and subject to availability of funds.
The appointment shall automatically lapse on completion of the approved period or on the joining of the candidate selected through the regular advertisement process, whichever is earlier, and shall not confer any claim for continuation under this approval.
No separate appointment letter or experience/service certificate shall be issued. A copy of the approval ( Form SRP-03(D) ) and the joining report ( Form SRP-03(D)-1 ) shall be sufficient for processing the payment on submission of the Pre-Receipt Voucher.
Project staff shall be entitled to proportionate leave of 2 days for a 3-month period and shall not be entitled to any other kind of leave. Further, personnel appointed under this ad-hoc provision shall not be entitled to HRA, irrespective of the funding agency provisions.
