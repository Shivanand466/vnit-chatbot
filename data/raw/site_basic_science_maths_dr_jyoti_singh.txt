Bio
Brief Bio
Jyoti Singh received her PhD in Mathematics from Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology, Allahabad in 2015. She has been a post-doctoral fellow at Harish Chandra Research Institute, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai, and IIT Bombay. Her area of research is Commutative Algebra. She is particularly interested in the algebraic theory of D-modules, local cohomology, and methods of prime characteristics.
