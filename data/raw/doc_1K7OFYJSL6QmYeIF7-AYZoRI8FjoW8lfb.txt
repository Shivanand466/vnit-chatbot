Subject: Payment of Outstanding Hostel Fees
It is hereby informed that all hostel residents who have not yet paid their hostel fees for the "Winter 2026" session are required to pay their hostel fees on or before 15th Aug 2026.
Students failing to pay the outstanding fees by the stipulated date shall be liable to a fine of 5,000/- per month. Students who have neither paid the hostel fees nor obtained prior permission from the Associate Dean (Hostel Affairs) are liable for the aforementioned fine.
