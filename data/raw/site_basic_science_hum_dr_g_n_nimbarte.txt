Dr. G.N. Nimbarte​
Brief
Bio
Dr. G. N. Nimbarte serves as an Associate Professor in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences at VNIT. His research interest revolve around rural sociology, where he has orchestrated numerous impactful social outreach programs. His scholarly pursuits are evident through his research in esteemed national and international journals. Complementing his academic achievements, Dr. Nimbarte is also a prolific author, with seven insightful books on various sociological topics to his credit.In recognition of his significant contributions, Dr. Nimbarte was honored with the prestigious Rugn Mitra award, underscoring his commitment to addressing pressing health challenges.
