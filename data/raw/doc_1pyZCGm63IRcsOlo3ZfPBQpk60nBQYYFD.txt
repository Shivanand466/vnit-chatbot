HOSTEL SECTION
NOTICE
Students who are required to stay in hostels during Winter Vacation 2025, (for academic purpose only, for ex- appearing for re-examination etc.) are instructed to submit an application (in the prescribed format available (Annexure I) on the institute website, at Hostel Notice Board) clearly indicating the reason and period of stay duly approved & endorsed by their Faculty Advisor, to their respective Hostel Block during working hours.
Mess facility for these students would be available as per the Notice HS/202526/Mess/3296 dated 25/11/2025(Notice available on Institute's WebSite).
