MoU & Collaborations at VNIT Nagpur
VNIT Nagpur formalizes academic, research, and industry partnerships through Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) and Research Collaboration Agreements. These agreements enable joint research, faculty and student exchange, technical cooperation, and collaborative innovation. The Office of the Dean (Research & Consultancy) coordinates the MoU process in line with institute-level guidelines and statutory requirements. All proposals must follow the defined approval workflow, and standard formats are to be used to ensure consistency and compliance.
