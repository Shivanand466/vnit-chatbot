PRATIBIMB Research Newsletter – Reflecting Excellence and Progress
Pratibimb is VNIT Nagpur’s official research newsletter, highlighting the institute’s academic achievements, research progress, and innovation-driven activities. The newsletter aims to: (i) Strengthen VNIT’s engagement with industry, academia, and funding agencies by showcasing research capabilities, (ii) Enhance VNIT’s visibility and reputation in the national and international research community and (iii) Attract research collaborations, project funding, and academic partnerships.
