Brief Bio
Dr. Jaipal is an Associate. Professor of English in the Depratment of Humanities and Social Scineces, VNIT Nagpur.  Teaching English for Corporate Communication course to B. Tech and B. Arch First Year students. I also teach Organizational Communication course as Humanities Elective Paper for B. Tech Third Year students. Taught Techincal Communication Skills course as a Dept. Elvetive Course to Third Year Computer Science Students, taught Communication Skills course to M. Sc First Semester students .  Area of Research Interest are Phonetics, Linguistics, ELT Methods, LSRW Testing and Evaluation, Indian Literature, and Queer Studies. Publishied a few Research Papers in National and International Peer Reviewed Journals and Conference Preecedings, Oranised 4 National Conferences, 3 STTP and Completed One ICSSR sponsered Project  of Rupees 5 Lakhs. Presented Research Papers more than 22 at National and International Conferences & Seminars. Delivered expert lectures at National Level FDPs and Conferences. Holding Administrative responsibilities; Warden for S. Ramanujan Boys Hostel, Departmental Coordinator for PhD Program, worked as Departmental Examinations Coordinator, Board of Studies, Dept. Faculty coordinator for T & P section, and Course coordinator for HULA101 Communication Skills. worked as a member in Non Teaching Staff Recuritment Committee 2022, also member in Institute Hostels' Mess Tender Committee 2024. Chairman,  Hostel Guest Rooms Development Committee 2025-26,  Member of Anti-Ragging Squad since June 2024 till date, Member of Unnat Bharat Abhiyan Participating Institute VNIT Nagpur, Member of Planning, Development and Exterior Maintanance Committee.
