Greetings of the day!
We are pleased to inform you about the Reliance Foundation Postgraduate Scholarships 2025-26 , which is currently open for students pursuing first-year full-time regular postgraduate courses in select future-ready courses from Engineering, Technology, Energy and Life-Sciences . Sharing more programme details below for your reference:
Eligibility:
Be a resident Indian citizen
First year PG Students who secured a score of 550 - 1,000 in the GATE Examination.
Students who have scored 7.5 or above in their Undergraduate CGPA (or % normalised to CGPA)
Eligible degree programmes - Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics & Computing, Electrical/or Electronics Engg., Chemical Engg., Mechanical Engg., Renewable & New Energy, Material, Science & Engg. and Life Sciences.
Award: Up to INR 6,00,000 over the duration of the degree
Application Deadline:
October 4, 2025
Application URL: http://www.b4s.in/a/em_RFS13_20250821_1
Helpline: For any additional questions, please text 'hi' on WhatsApp to 7977 100 100 , call our helpline on (011) 4117 1414 or email us at RF.UGScholarships@reliancefoundation.org
We would request you to share this scholarship information among students in your institution so that they can avail this opportunity. In case, you have any queries or need any assistance regarding this scholarship programme, please feel free to contact us.
Note: The Reliance Foundation does not charge any application fees to apply for this scholarship program
