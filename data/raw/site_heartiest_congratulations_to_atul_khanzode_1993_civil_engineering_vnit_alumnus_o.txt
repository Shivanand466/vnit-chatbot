🎉 A Proud Moment for VNIT Nagpur! 🎉
Heartiest congratulations to Atul Khanzode (1993, Civil Engineering), VNIT alumnus, on his election to the prestigious National Academy of Construction (NAC), USA!
Atul has been selected among only 45 new members inducted in 2026, joining a distinguished community of 496 leaders committed to advancing the construction industry through leadership, innovation and service.
Founded in 1999, the National Academy of Construction recognizes individuals for exceptional leadership and significant, long-term contributions to the construction industry.
This prestigious international recognition is a matter of immense pride for the entire VNIT fraternity and stands as a testament to Atul’s professional excellence, leadership and enduring contribution to the construction industry.
🌟 Heartiest congratulations, Atul!
Wishing you continued success and many more milestones ahead.
VNIT Nagpur celebrates your achievement and is proud of your remarkable journey!
