Dr. Swati Bhave
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. Swati Bhave is an Assistant Professor at VNIT Nagpur, since June 2020. She did her Masters from IIT Guwahati in Water resources Engineering and Ph. D from IIT Roorkee in Hydraulics Engineering. Before joing VNIT Nagpur, She was teaching in RGUKT Basar, Telengana. Her research field areas are on Open channel flow, hydraulic structures, CFD. She is active-reviewer in various journals and conferenes. Also have published many papers in reputated journals in various journal, conferences and book chapters.
