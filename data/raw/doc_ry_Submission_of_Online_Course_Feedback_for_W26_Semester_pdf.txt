, VISVESVARAYA NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, NAGPUR
November 13,2026
NOTICE:
Subject:
Mandatory Submission of Online Course Feedback for W26 Semester – UG & PG Higher Semester Students – reg.
As per the direction of the competent authority, all UG & PG Higher Semester students are hereby informed that submission of online course feedback through AIMS for all courses registered during the Winter Semester 2026 (W26) is mandatory.
Students are advised to take note of the following:
1. Submission of course feedback for all registered courses is compulsory.
2. Students who fail to submit the feedback will not be permitted to register for the Summer
Semester 2027 (S27) through AIMS.
3. The last date for submission of course feedback is Tuesday, 17 November 2026. No
extension will be granted.
All students are therefore advised to complete the course feedback process on or before the
stipulated deadline to avoid any inconvenience during S27 semester registration.
This is issued with the approval of the competent authority.
Exam./
Dy. Registrar (Acad. & Exams.)
