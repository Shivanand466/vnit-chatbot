Application Procedure: Executive M. Tech in CSE (Generative AI)
1. Eligibility Criteria:
Bachelor's degree in Engineering/Technology (in CSE/IT or related disciplines)
OR
E. / B. Tech. in any discipline with valid GATE score in CS / DA.
Minimum one-year of experience after graduation.
2. Online Application
Applications are accepted only in online mode through Google Form.
Visit the official VNIT admission website:
o https://vnit.ac.in or https://vnit.ac.in/engineering/cse/academics/

Click on the Google form link for ' Executive M. Tech Admissions - Summer 2026 '.
Complete the online application using a valid email ID and mobile number.
3. Fill Application Form
Fill in personal, academic, and professional details.
Upload the required documents in the prescribed format:
o Academic transcripts and degree certificates
o Proof of work experience (letter from employer or relevant documents)
o Recent passport-sized photograph
o Identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
o NoC from the current employer (if applicable)

Pay the non-refundable application fee of Rs. 1200/- online at https://pay.vnit.ac.in/home by choosing the payment category 'Admission / Application Fee Executive M Tech CSE Gen AI Application Fee'
Keep the receipt in pdf and transaction reference for future correspondence.
Applications without Application Fee will not be considered.
5. Submission of Application
Review the application form and ensure all details are accurate.
Submit the application.
Visit the VNIT website to know the updates and results.
Selection Process
1. Scrutiny and Selection
All applications will undergo initial screening for eligibility based on the minimum academic and professional requirements.
Shortlisting of candidates will be carried out in the following order of preference:
o GATE score (if available)
o CGPA/percentage obtained in the Bachelor's degree
o Number of years of relevant work experience
o Marks obtained in the PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) group at the higher secondary level
Shortlisted candidates may be invited for an online interview or counselling session, if deemed necessary.
The final selection will be based on the shortlisting process and performance in the interview/counselling (if applicable).
Waiting list will be operated if required.
