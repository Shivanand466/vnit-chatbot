!! CIRCULAR !!
The Govt. of India invites attention on the caste discrimination due to which victim students take extreme  step like suicide. To curb the caste discrimination, students /parents can lodge a complaint to Institute Authority.
Parents can also send an email at complaint@vnit.ac.in
NOTE
The particular of complainant and details of the complaint will be kept strictlyCONFIDENTIAL.
Complaint can also be lodged in person in a register maintained in REGISTRAR’S OFFICE and available on alll working days.
