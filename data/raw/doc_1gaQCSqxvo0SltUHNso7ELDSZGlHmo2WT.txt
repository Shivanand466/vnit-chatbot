The proposal for approval of fees for international students admitted under the Quota SII and ICCR was submitted to the 83rd Finance committee meeting vide item No. F83.09.01 dated 11/03/2025, under any other item with permission of the Chair and the point numbered F 83.09.01 titled, 'To approve the fees structure for International Students under "Study in India" and "ICCR quota". Resolution
for Action to be taken
F 83.09 Any other Item(s) with the permission of Chair.
F 83.09.01 To approve the fees structure for International Students under "Study in India" and "ICCR"quota.
The Institute has constituted a committee for preparing the fees structure for International Students under "Study in India" and "ICCR" quota.
The Comparison of fees for International Students at VNIT Nagpur with other NITs and few Tier-I self-funded institutes was done by Dean (IAA), VNIT Nagpur, and the committee.
Accordingly, the proposed fees structure was enclosed at Annexure- X.
The FC was requested to kindly consider and recommended the same to BoG, for its approval.
Resolution: The FC suggested that the proposal should be reviewed in light of fees structure available in 'Status report on SII admissions across NIT's for SII admissions while the fees of ICCR students be finalized as per existing practices in other NITs in the country. The final recommended fee structure be sent to the MoE for their concurrence.
Dean (IAA) studied the SII and ICCR quota fees in the other NITs for UG and PG students and the proposed structure of fees for the SII And ICCR Quota is presented herewith.
