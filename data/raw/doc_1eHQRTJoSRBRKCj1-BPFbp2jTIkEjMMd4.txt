HOSTEL ADMISSION PROCEDURE FOR UG (B.Tech/B.Arch) 1 ST YEAR STUDENTS
The rooms will be allotted on FIRST COME FIRST SERVE basis on triple sharing basis for Boys and double sharing basis for Girls.
Step-wise procedure to be followed before physical reporting:
Make Online Payment Through Online-Payment on VNIT website before reporting to VNIT. (Please refer Annexure-I for details on page no. 4-5)
Go on the website https://www.antiragging.in/affidavit_university_form.php
OR
Scan QR
Fill the undertaking Registration form. The detailed procedure for filling the form is given in Annexure-II (on page no: 6-8).
After submission of Anti- ragging form take screen shot of a generated reference number & keep it safe. This reference number is essential for hostel booking.
Step-wise procedure to be followed during physical reporting:
After completing Academic reporting procedure and collecting the Provisional Reporting Letter from Academic admission counter, visit to Hostel admission Counter.
Submit the hard copy of Provisional Reporting Letter , Hostel Fee Payment Receipt and Reference no. of online Anti-ragging form at the Hostel Counter.
Student will fill a google form (link will be provided on reporting to hostel counter)
Hostel admission form (in duplicate) will be issued by Hostel Counter. Please fill all details in the form. (2 Passport size photographs will be required)
Hostel Block and Room will be allotted to student and hostel allotment letter will be issued at Hostel counter.
Submit the Hostel Admission form to the Supervisor of the allotted Hostel Block.
Supervisor will allow student to take the possession of shared room (2 or 3 seater).
IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS
 It is mandatory for all the students who intend to avail the hostel facility to strictly follow all the directives and guidelines of the institute as well as Hostel.
 Submit the following documents in hard copy at Hostel counter at the time of Hostel Admission
 Provisional Reporting letter from Academic Section
 Hostel Fee payment Receipt
 2 Passport size photographs
 Online Anti-ragging form reference number.
 Submit the following documents in hard copy at the time of reporting to Hostel
 Hostel Admission Form along with hostel fee payment receipt and hostel allotment letter.
VNIT has separate hostel buildings for B. Tech. / B. Arch. Boys & Girls. For 1 st year (boys) 3 students would be accommodated in one room (triple sharing basis) & For 1 st year (Girls) 2 students would be accommodated in one room (double sharing basis). Hostel rooms are equipped with study table, chair, cot, cupboard with sufficient number of ceiling fans and tube lights.

GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS:
Payment of hostel fee is to be made by any of the procedures given in Annexure - I (preferable 1 st method)
Hostel Room Allotment will be done on First Come First Serve basis.
Keeping personal furniture, appliances etc. are not allowed in the hostels.
Use of all types of motorized vehicles and electrical appliances viz. iron / hotplates/ induction cookers, refrigerators etc. is strictly prohibited (except Air Coolers in summer) for hostel inmates.
Laptop is permitted
Parents, relatives, friends are not allowed to stay in the hostel with students.
Student should note that it is mandatory to open a savings bank account in the name of student at any nationalized bank.
Items which are required for personal use viz. Mattress, Bed sheets; Bucket and mug etc. are to be brought in / required to be purchased by students.
VNIT campus is approx. 6 Kms from the Nagpur Railway Station.
Hostel rules to be strictly followed by all the students.
