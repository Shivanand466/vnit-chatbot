About Us
MISSION
The Mission of the civil engineering department is to develop students into capable engineering graduates and post graduates by imparting appropriate high-quality education in civil engineering so that they could be readily adapted by the service sector to meet the challenges faced by the Nation. The program strives for excellence in engineering education and profession. It also aims to promote allaround development of the personality of students by suitably involving them in cocurricular and extra-curricular activities.
VISION
To contribute effectively to the national endeavour of producing a quality human resource of world-class standard in civil engineering by developing a sustainable technical education system to meet the changing technological needs of the country incorporating relevant social concerns and building an environment to create and propagate innovative technologies for the economic development of Nation.
The institute was established in June 1960 and later named in honour of the eminent engineer, planner and statesman, Sir M. Visvesvaraya. The Department of Civil Engineering is one of the finest and oldest engineering departments of the Institute and stands with an immortal reputation. The department was formed along with the Institute itself and therefore is as old. The Department has highly educated and wellexperienced faculty members who endeavour to produce the finest engineers, contributing incredibly to the nation. The alumni of the department are widespread in India and abroad, occupying high positions in their respective fields. It has an intake of 120 students per year under UG Course and 100 per year under PG Courses Head Of Department, Civil Engineering Department The Department of Civil Engineering is one of the oldest departments of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology. The Civil Engineering department offers a Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering and Master's Degrees in five specializations, namely Environmental Engineering, Water Resources Engineering, Transportation Engineering, Construction Technology and Geotechnical Engineering. The B. Tech. course in civil engineering and M. Tech. Courses in Environmental Engineering as well as Water Resources Engineering were accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) and have been granted 'AA' grade. The department has highly qualified faculty members with a specialization in various civil engineering disciplines. The faculty members have a very good research record, having published a number of research papers in SCI/SCOPUS Journals each year and the department has around 50 full-time research scholars. Faculty members are also providing consultancy services to various government and other organisations in all aspects of civil engineering. The department is providing testing and consultancy services in the areas of Concrete Technology, Water Distribution Systems, Structural Engineering, Water Quality, Air Pollution, Geotechnical Engineering, Transportation Engineering, Water Resources Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Construction Management and Remote Sensing & GIS. Civil Engineering Department also organises various short term training programs and international/national conferences regularly. Faculty members of the department have also been granted several patents for their innovations. The department has several completed and ongoing Research and Development projects from SERB DST, CMPDI Coal India Ltd., PERCMinistry of mines, British Council UK, National Atmospheric Research Laboratory (NARL), Maharashtra Pollution control board (MPCB), etc. The department has sixteen laboratories with necessary experiments and advanced equipment which are available to students. The department has an excellent work environment where there is always an attempt to aspire to excel and improve through input from the external world. Academic audit of programs is carried out at regular intervals. The department has a highly active student community of around 730, who are ready to venture and ready to contribute in the technological world. Even in the most challenging recent situation, the faculty members in the department have achieved all the milestones in time and in the most appropriate manner with full reciprocation from the students of the department.
Dr. Abhay Tawalare Professor Incharge, Civil Engineering Department

Technical Visit
A visit to Lower wardha project and bridge construction IIIT Nagpur, was organized by Civil Engineering Department, VNIT Nagpur. The primary objective of the visit was to sensitize students about the practical aspects of canals, hydraulic structures, Basalt rock layers and associated Reinforced structures. Professors, Dr. Yashwant Bhaskar Katpatal, Dr. Abhay Tawalare, Dr. A B Mirajkar and Dr. Srinivasan V. had accompanied the students during the visit.
Institute Gathering
Our annual Institute Gathering is a memorable 10 day event where the college en-sembles to participate in a multitude of activities involving sports, cultural, literary and design. This event is instrumental in laying emphasis on traits like organizational skills, engaging active participation, management, hard work and spirit of teamwork. The incentive to cultivate these traits is the grand trophy for victory and enthusiasm each. The main aim of Institute gathering is to reveal the hidden talent in students. It is a platform to showcase the expertise of students in wide range of events including Cultural and sports. Students adroitness complemented by leadership of Depart-ment council has led to this tremendous success
