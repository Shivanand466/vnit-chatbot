Dr. Joydeep Sengupta
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Joydeep Sengupta is an Assistant Professor with the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, India. His current research interests include microwave solid state device High frequency communication, biomedical engineering and underwater and optical communication. He is the incharge of Center for EMI & EMC.. He is serving on the editorial boards of IEEE Transactions on Communications, IEEE Microwave Magazine and many more SCI journals.
