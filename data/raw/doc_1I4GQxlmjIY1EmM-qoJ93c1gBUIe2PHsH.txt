Who Can Register?
 Open to all B.Tech students from any branch.
 Available for students in 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year.
 Note: Students who have already registered for OC/HM courses this semester are not eligible to register for this course.
About the Course
This course explores the scientific and experiential foundations of mindfulness and meditation, and integrating ancient wisdom. Students will learn to:
 Understand and regulate emotions
 Improve focus, clarity, and stress resilience
 Practice guided breath-based meditations and reflective discussions
The course includes weekly experiential sessions to help you directly apply mindfulness tools to daily academic and personal challenges.
Execution Guidelines
 100% attendance is mandatory for all lecture, tutorial, and lab sessions.
 Silence must be maintained during meditation. Speaking is permitted only during designated discussion times.
 No mobile phones or electronic devices are allowed during any session. All devices must remain on silent mode.
 Active participation in all practices and discussions is expected.
