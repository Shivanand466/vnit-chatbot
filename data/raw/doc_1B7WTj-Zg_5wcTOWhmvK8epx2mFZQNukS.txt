VNIT Nagpur Participates in Study in India (SII) Nodal Officers Workshop South Chapter
Date: 14 July 2026
Dr. T. V. K. Gupta, Associate Dean (International Affairs), VNIT Nagpur, participated in the Study in India (SII) Nodal Officers Workshop - South Chapter , held at IISc Bangalore and organized by the Ministry of Education, Government of India .
The workshop was attended by representatives from around 186 Institutes/Universities and was inaugurated by Dr. Sharma, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Education and Dr. Bala Datta, Dean (International Affairs), IISc .
The key discussions focused on increasing international student intake under the SII Programme, strengthening international partnerships, offering scholarships and enhancing student support and internationalization practices.
Important areas highlighted included establishing an International Students Grievance Cell , publishing the fee structure on the International Affairs web portal, introducing a semesterwise student feedback system , providing pre-arrival support to incoming students, offering mental health and mindfulness initiatives , and strengthening the International Affairs Office .
The workshop also included a session by the FRRO, Bengaluru , on challenges related to the enrolment and compliance of international students, followed by a presentation by QS Ranking representatives on global rankings.
Dr. T. V. K. Gupta, Associate Dean (International Affairs), VNIT Nagpur, during the Study in India (SII) Nodal Officers Workshop - South Chapter held at IISc Bangalore on 14 July 2026 .
