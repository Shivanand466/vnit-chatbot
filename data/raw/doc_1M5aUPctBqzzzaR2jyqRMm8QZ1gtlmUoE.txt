National Coordinator, SPARC, Prof Rabibrata Mukherjee Visits VNIT, Nagpur.
Date:
11 August 2026
Venue:
Senate Hall & Department of Chemical Engineering, VNIT Nagpur
It was a pleasure to welcome Prof. Rabibrata Mukherjee, Prof of Chemical Engg & Dean SFA, IIT Kharagpur and , to VNIT Nagpur .
During his visit, Prof. Mukherjee interacted with our faculty members and Ph.D. scholars and provided valuable insights into the SPARC project scheme , including the submission guidelines, proposal preparation strategies, and key aspects to consider while developing competitive research proposals. His expertise and guidance offered our academic and research community a better understanding of the opportunities available through the SPARC initiative and how to effectively develop strong international collaborative research proposals. We sincerely thank Prof. Rabibrata Mukherjee for his valuable time, insightful interaction, and encouragement towards strengthening international academic and research collaborations at VNIT Nagpur. The visit was organized by the Dean International Affairs Office and coordinated by Prof Diwakar Shende. Dean IAA, Prof Kishor Bhurchandi graced the event.
