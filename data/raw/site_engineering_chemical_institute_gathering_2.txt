Institute Gathering
IG 2024 (2nd Position)
IG 2023 (2nd Position)
Department of Chemical Engineering emerged as the winner of IG 2025. Earlier, the department secured 2nd positions in IG 2023 and 2024, achieved three consecutive victories in IGs from 2017–2019, and was the runner-up in IG 2020.
The main aim of Institute gathering is to reveal the hidden talent in students. It is a platform to showcase the expertise of students in wide range of events including Cultural and sports. Students adroitness complemented by leadership of Department council has led to this tremendous success.
