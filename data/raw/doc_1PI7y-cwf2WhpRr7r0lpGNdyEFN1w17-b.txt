Notice: Cloak Room Facility for 5th Year B.Arch
This is to inform 5th Year B. Arch students (boys and girls) residing in the hostels that cloak room facilities are available for their use in Vikram Sarabhai for Boys and Dr. Anandi Gopal Joshi for Girls.
Students are to ensure that no valuables are kept and advised to properly label their belonging with their ID, Name and Enrollment No.
Cloak room facility can be availed by the student under their own risk.
