October 1, 2026

As part of the Swachhata Hi Sewa (SHS) Campaign 2026, VNIT Nagpur organised two engaging activities under “Swachh Pathshala – Yuva for Swachhata” on 21 September 2026, promoting awareness, creativity and active participation in the cleanliness movement.
🧠 Quiz Competition | Cleanliness Campaign
Organised by the Hindi Committee of VNIT, the quiz witnessed enthusiastic participation from students and employees. Participants were rewarded for correct answers, encouraging greater awareness about cleanliness and responsible civic practices.
Coordinators: Dr. Bharti Polke, Mr. Sikandar Khatri & Ms. Poonam Uike.
🎨 Poster-Making Competition | Swachhata Mein Sahbhag, Swachh Bharat, Viksit Bharat*
Held at the Department of Architecture and Planning, the competition provided students a creative platform to express their ideas on cleanliness and sustainability through themes including “My Clean City,” “Waste to Resource,” and “Our Clean Environment.” A consolation prize was awarded in recognition of creative effort and participation.
Coordinator: Dr. Sarika Bahadure, Associate Professor, Department of Architecture and Planning.
🌱 Through knowledge, creativity and youth participation, VNIT Nagpur continues to strengthen the message of Swachhata, sustainability and responsible citizenship.
