Achievements, Rankings and USPs: Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, India, established in 1960, is a top-tier engineering institute with lush green campus of 214 acres in heart of Nagpur city, ranked 44 th in NIRF 2025, 1201-1500 in THE World University 2025 and 401-500 in the Asia Univ. 2025 rankings. Known for excellence in core engineering, research, and strong industry ties, it ensures high placements with companies like Google, Oracle and Tata Motors. VNIT stands out for its focus on sustainability, innovation, SDGs and vibrant campus life with 32 student clubs, supported by modern infrastructure, technical fests, and active student clubs, making it a preferred choice for the engineering education. Centrally located VNIT offers strong connectivity with industry and International collaborations with Top ranked global universities for exchange of student/faculty up to 6 months. Very strong Global Alumni Network.
