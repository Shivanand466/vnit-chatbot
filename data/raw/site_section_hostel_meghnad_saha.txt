About Hostel
Dr. Meghnad Saha Boys’ Hostel is dedicated to the eminent Indian astrophysicist Dr. Meghnad Saha, honouring his legacy of scientific excellence and innovation. The hostel has been thoughtfully designed to provide a comfortable and enriching residential experience for its occupants.
With a capacity of 99 single-seater rooms, the hostel ensures privacy, personal study space, and a quiet environment conducive to academic focus. Each room is well-ventilated and furnished with the essential amenities required for independent living.
The hostel features clean and well-maintained common areas, recreational spaces, and study zones that encourage community interaction while supporting a balanced academic lifestyle. A supportive and collaborative atmosphere helps students from diverse backgrounds grow personally and academically during their stay at VNIT.
Dr. Meghnad Saha Boys’ Hostel stands as a tribute to a distinguished scientist and serves as a welcoming home for students as they pursue their academic and professional aspirations.
