Dr. A. B. Andhare
Brief Bio
Dr.Atul Andhare has completed his B. E. Mechancial Engineering from the Government College of Enginering Karad, Maharashtra in 1987. Later he obtained his M. Tech. in Design of Machines and Mechanisms from VRCE Nagpur in 1994. In 2007, he was awarded Ph. D. by the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay Mumbai.  Dr. Atul has worked in various organisations such as VNIT Nagpur, IIT Bombay, SVNIT Surat and Government Polytechnics of Maharashtra, during last 35 years. He had also worked in industry for four years immediately after graduation. He joined VNIT Nagpur in July 2008 and since then working in VNIT. He has handled various resposibilities at the Institute level. Some major responsibilities were - Associate Dean (Training and Placement), Hostel Warden, Chairman M. Tech. Admission Committee, etc. So far he has guided ten Ph.D. theses and more than 35 M. Tech. dissertations. His areas of interest are  - Machine design, Machine condition monitoring, machining of super alloys, etc.
