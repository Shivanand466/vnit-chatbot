Subject: Request for Approval – Revised Academic Calendar for Summer Term 2025
With reference to the Minutes of the Meeting regarding Revision of Summer Term Rules for UG Programs, dated October 21, 2024, this is to inform you that the Summer Term 2025 will
be offered for courses across First, Second, Third, and Final Year programs. As per the new structure, both Odd and Even semester courses will be conducted simultaneously during the Summer Term. In view of this, the previously allocated 4 days for Mid-Summer and End-Summer Term examinations are found to be insufficient. To address this, the proposed revised Academic Calendar allocates 8 days for examinations covering slots A to H, scheduled in two shifts — one each for Odd Semester and Even Semester courses.
The revised calendar is attached herewith for your kind review and approval. Thank you for your attention and consideration.
