Dr. S. S. Chiddarwar
Brief Bio
Dr. Shital S. Chiddarwar is a professor of Mechanical Engineering with a focus on Robot Motion Planning, Machine Vision, Artificial Intelligence, and Adaptive Control. She earned her B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from VRCE (now VNIT) Nagpur and pursued M.Tech. in Production Engineering from Nagpur University. Her passion for advancing the field led her to complete a Ph.D. in Robot Motion Planning from IIT Madras. With a wealth of experience spanning 12 years in academia, Dr. Chiddarwar has made significant contributions to her field. She boasts an impressive publication record, with 21 research papers in SCI journals, 62 presentations at international conferences, and the authorship of 3 books. Notably, she has adapted key texts like "Mechatronics" by Bolton and "Robotics" by J J Craig to cater to the needs of Indian readers. As a dedicated mentor, Dr. Chiddarwar has successfully guided 6 Ph.D. students, with 4 currently pursuing their doctoral research under her supervision. Additionally, she has mentored over 25 master's students and supported the projects of 45 undergraduate students. Her commitment extends beyond the classroom, as she leads IvLabs, a unique robotics and AI lab at VNIT.
