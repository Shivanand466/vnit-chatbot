NATIONAL OVERSEAS SCHOLARSHIPS FOR ST STUDENTS
1. INTRODUCTION
It is a Central Sector Scheme of Ministry of Tribal Affairs under which scholarships are given to the meritorious Schedule Tribe (ST) students for pursuing higher education in reputed Institutes/Universities abroad. The Scheme will be implemented through the Indian Embassies/Missions abroad, Ministry of External Affairs. The provisions of this scheme guideline will be applicable from 2021-22 and onwards.
2. SALIENT FEATURE OFTHE SCHEME
2.1. SCOPE
Financial assistance is provided for pursuing post graduate courses (Masters, Ph. D and Post-Doctoral Research) in reputed universities across the world. Bachelor level courses in any discipline are not covered under the scheme.

(ii). One child in a family and one timeassistance

(iii). Income Criteria
Scholarships will be paid to the students whosefamilyincome from all sources does not exceed Rs.6,00,000/- (Rs. Six lakhs only) per annum .
The family income shall be computed in the following manner: -
In case where both father and mother are working, the combined income of both of them from all sources shall be taken into account in computing total family income.
In case any other member of the family, other than father and mother, is an earning member, his or her income shall not be included in computing total family income.
In case only one parent is alive, the income of that parent shall be taken into account for considering total family income. If other sibling or family member is an earning member, their income shall not be included in computing total family income.
In case of an orphan, supported by a guardian, income criteria shall not apply.
Note 1: Definition of Income - Income means gross income including income from all sources without and exemption and deductions available in the Income Tax Act.
Note 2: Income certificate is required to be taken once only i.e. at the time of admission to course which will be valid for the entire duration of the course.
Note 3: The income certificate given at the time of admission should be taken in the same year for which the admission is taken. In case of salaried employee, the income of previous financial year will be considered for the purpose of eligibility. For example, if a candidate applies afresh for academic year 2021-22, the family income for financial year 2020-21 would be required.

4.0. SELECTION PROCEDURE
4.1. ON-LINE APPLICATION THROUGH PORTAL
The applications for scholarships under the schemewill be invited online through National Overseas Scholarship Portal (NOSP) developed by the Ministry (overseas.tribal.gov.in). The opening and closing of the portal would be announced through an advertisement in Employment News, other prominent News Dailies, through Institutes/Universities, State Governments and through the Ministry's Portal. Applicants are advised to go through the Instruction Manual before applying online and for any query, may refer to the helpdesk module given on the portal.
4.2. REGISTRATION PROCESS:
Before filling application form online, candidates are required to register themselves on the NOSP and on Digi Locker. Aadhaar is mandatory for registration on portal. The applicant would be required to upload documents through Digi locker.
4.3. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FILLING DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR FILLING APPLICATION FORM
Applicant will be required to upload the following documents on the portal for registration:
 Latest Coloured Passport Size Photograph
 ST certificate issued by the competent authority
 PVTG certificate issued by the competent authority (if applicable)
 10 th Certificate/Marksheet in support of Date of Birth
 Graduation / Post-Graduation mark sheet [Aggregate marks in %] /equivalent % marks in case of CGPA / Ph. D completion certificate, wherever applicable (Conversion sheet of aggregate marks into percentage to be issued by Institute/University)
 Offer of admission
 Income Certificate
The candidates need to ensure that they fulfil all the eligibility criteria at the time of applying for scholarship. If it is found that the candidate does not have the eligibility or the candidate fails to upload the required document which are mandatory to prove their eligibility, the application will be liable to be rejected.
4.4. PREPARATION OF MERIT LIST
All applications received within the stipulated period, will be scrutinized for the eligibility criteria. Separate merit list will be drawn for each field of study as mentioned in para 3.3. above. The eligible applications will be considered for award of scholarship as per the procedure given below:
First priority will be given to the candidates who have already got admission and are pursuing their studies in the top 1,000 ranked foreign Institutes/Universities (as per the latest QS world ranking). The merit list will be drawn based on the ranking of the Institute. In case of a tie between the QS rankings of two or more candidates, merit will be decided on the basis of marks/grades secured by the candidate in the qualifying examination for higher studies for which the scholarship has been applied for.
Note: -
If selected, the student will be entitled for scholarship prospectively from the date of issuance of award letter. No reimbursement will be made for the fee already paid by the student to the University and the expenses incurred on Insurance etc. However, the Maintenance and Contingency and Equipment allowance will be given in proportion to number of months for the remaining financial year from the date of issue of Award letter. For subsequent years, the student will be eligible for scholarship covering all fee and other expenses (mentioned in para 3.5) for the remaining duration of the course (as mentioned in para 3.4).
In case sufficient number of students are not selected as mentioned in para (a) above, merit list will be drawn from the candidates who have already secured preliminary letter/offer of admission from top 1,000 ranked foreign
Institutes/Universities (as per the QS world ranking) and are yet to join. Merit list will be drawn in similar manner as mentioned in para 'a' above; i.e based on the ranking of the Institute and in case of tie in ranking of university/institution, the marks/grades secured by the candidate in the qualifying examination.
In case sufficient students are not selected as per para 'a' and 'b' above, the vacant slots will be filled from among the remaining eligible candidates through personal interview by a committee of experts constituted by the Ministry. The candidates who have cleared GRE/GMAT/TOFEL etc. will be given preference.
Note: The finally selected candidates will be required to secure admission and join in one of the top 1,000 ranked foreign Institutes/Universities abroad (as per the QS world ranking) within 2 years from the date of communication of Award Letter. On expiry of this specified period of time, the award will automatically get cancelled.
Note:
In case, the candidates who are selected as per para 'b' and 'c' do not secure admission and join in one of the top 1,000 ranked foreign Institutes/Universities abroad within 2 years from the date of communication of selection, such unfilled seats will be carried over to the next selection year immediately after the expiry of such time period. For example, if the date of provisional award is 01.09.2019, the candidate is required to get admission latest by 30.9.2021. In case the students fail to take admission within the prescribed time limit, such slots will be carried over to the selection year 2021-22.
Verification of the selected candidates as per para 'a' above will be done through the Indian Embassy/Mission in the respective country. Verification of the shortlisted candidates as per para 'b' and 'c' will be done through the respective State/UT.
The short-listed candidates who are required to appear in Interview before the Expert Committee will be entitled to reimbursement of second-class train fare/bus fare from the place of residence to the place of interview, by shortest route.
Provisional merit list of eligible candidates will be posted on the Ministry's website.
4.5. CONSTITUTION OF EXPERT COMMITTEE:
The Ministry will constitute a Selection Committee with domain experts. The Chairperson and members of the committee will be entitled to be paid economy air fare and the rate of hotel accommodation and D.A. would be as admissible to them as per eligibility in their concerned department/University/Institute. Further, honorarium of Rs.2500 a day will be paid to the members. If the members of the Committee are from New Delhi then a sitting fee inclusive of travelling allowance not exceeding Rs. 5000/- a day will be payable, for each day of their participation.
5. ROLE OF INDIAN EMBASSY/MISSION ABROAD
Before releasing scholarships to the candidates, the Indian Embassies/Missions abroad shall see the overall performance/ behaviour of the candidate.
In order to have a better connect with the students and status of their studies the student will submit progress report on the NOS Portal once in every 6 months. The Indian Mission abroad shall obtain performance reports from the university/institution where the awardee is pursuing his/her studies for which the award was given under the scheme and update the information thereon and other relevant information such as joining reports, discontinuation of studies etc., on online portal of the Ministry (https://overseas.tribal.gov.in/) as per the prescribed format.
The Mission shall inform this Ministry any serious adverse developments in case of the awardee which requires decision towards further continuation of the award or otherwise.
Before leaving the country, the student will be required to take permission ofIndian Mission and inform the Ministry on the portal.
The Indian Mission will also be responsible for activities as mentioned in para 4.4(e)
6. OTHER CONDITIONS
Selected candidates shall furnish all such documents and enter into such agreements before their departure as shall be decided by the Government of India from time to time. In case the candidate is already residing abroad, he/she shall furnish all relevant documents and enter into such agreements prior to the release of the scholarship by the Indian Mission.
The candidate shall not change the course of study or research and the Institute for which scholarship has been sanctioned.
It will be the responsibility of candidate to obtain the appropriate visa for a country wherein he/she intends to study.
In case the awardee has received overpayment through Indian Mission abroad or any other Government agency, he/she shall refund the same to Indian Mission abroad.
After completion of the course, the awardee shall upload the course completion details along with brief of study in the Alumni Module of NOS portal of the Ministry before his return tickets are booked.
7. CANCELLATION OF AWARD
The award of the scholarship will be cancelled and no financial support will be provided. Also, the Ministry of Tribal Affairs may recover the already paid award amount in case of any of the following:
The awardee has taken admission by submitting false documents or not conforming to the guidelines governing the Scheme;
The awardee returning to India without successfully completing the course for which he/she has been selected.
Note: If the failure to complete the course is due to ill-health, he would require to submit certificate of medical officer nominated by the Indian Mission abroad.
The student is involved in any illegal activity or antinational activity or misconduct or associated with use of narcotic substances or involved in violation of law of the host country or in the event of any police case against the candidate.
8. LITIGATION
Any litigation on matters arising out of this scheme in India will be subject to sole jurisdiction of the courts situated in Union Territory of Delhi. The litigations arising abroad will be attended to by the Indian Missions abroad.
9. CHANGE IN THE PROVISIONS OF THE SCHEME:
The provisions of this Scheme can be changed at any time with the approval of Minister of Tribal Affairs, Government of India.
