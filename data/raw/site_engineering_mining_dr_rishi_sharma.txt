Dr. Rishi Sharma​
Brief Bio
Dr. Rishi Sharma is working as an Assistant Professor Department of Mining Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. His research interests include sustainable iron ore agglomeration, low-carbon drying technologies, dust generation potential of rocks and acid mine drainage. His broader interests also encompass underground coal mining, mass production technologies, underground metalliferous mining and the sustainable utilization of mining and metallurgical wastes.
