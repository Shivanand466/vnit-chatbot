Development for College
1. Telephone Directory App
Overview : Our team developed the VNIT Telephone Directory app to modernize and simplify the way faculty and staff access contact information across the institute. Previously, searching through a 400-page physical directory was a time-consuming and frustrating process for deans, professors, and staff members. Finding someone's contact details meant flipping through numerous pages, and the printed information would often become outdated quickly.
Features : The digital directory transforms this experience by providing instant access to all contact information through an intuitive, searchable interface. Users can now quickly find contacts by name, department, or position, with additional features like direct call/email integration and categorized sections for different departments and administrative units. One particularly useful feature is the ability to find laboratory contact numbers, which was especially difficult in the paper directory.
Outcomes : The app has significantly improved efficiency by reducing search time from minutes to seconds. The ability to update contact information in
real-time ensures that faculty and staff always have access to the most current information, unlike the static printed directory. The categorized structure and global search feature have made it especially valuable for new faculty members and staff who may not be familiar with the institutional structure.
2. CRC Manager App
14:18
CRC Manager_
Overview : Our team developed the CRC Manager app to streamline the classroom booking process for college deans and professors at VNIT. Previously, professors had to go through a cumbersome manual process to reserve CRC classrooms, which involved paperwork, multiple approvals, and physical key management. This system was time-consuming and prone to scheduling conflicts.
Features : The CRC Manager app transforms this experience with its intuitive digital solution. Professors can now easily view classroom availability, make booking requests, and manage their reservations through a secure role-based access system. The innovative OTP-based key handover system ensures secure access control, while the guard interface simplifies key management and tracking
Outcomes : Since implementing the app, we've seen significant improvements in booking efficiency, reduced administrative burden, and better resource utilization. The calendar view and real-time status updates have particularly helped eliminate double bookings and streamlined the entire process from reservation to key return.
