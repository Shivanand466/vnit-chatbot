Advertisement for the Position of Post-Doctoral Fellow (PDF) Year 2025-2026
VNIT, Nagpur invites applications in the form of research proposals from highly motivated and research oriented scholars for the Post-Doctoral Fellow (PDF) position for the Academic Year 2025-2026. Candidate can visit website www.vnit.ac.in to identify the Domain Research Area of the faculty members (mentor) working in the respective Departments. If the Post-Doctoral Fellow is availing any other fellowship; he/she is not eligible to apply. Candidates who meet the eligibility criteria for admission may apply through the available form on the institute's website ( www.vnit.ac.in).

Minimum Eligibility:
Candidate should apply within 5 years after completion of Ph.D.
 Ph.D. with relevant research/industrial experience.
 Candidate should have minimum 2 Publications in SCI/SCIE/AHCI/SSCI Journals.
 Candidate should have good academic records throughout and good writing skills.
 Candidate submitted the Ph.D. thesis and waiting for thesis defense are also encouraged to apply.
 Candidate should produce copy of Ph.D. at the time of personal interview.
 No Objection Certificate , if employed.
 Maximum Age 40 years.
 Candidate completed their Ph.D. from VNIT Nagpur are not eligible to apply.
Financial support:
The position is for maximum period of 2 years.
 Non lapsable Contingency grant Rs. 25000/- per year.
 Financial support for attending and presenting paper in one National Conference only.
 The candidates will also be provided with suitable Hostel/Campus accommodation as per VNIT Nagpur norms if requested and if available. Otherwise, he/she will be paid HRA as per the norms.
The fellowship for Post-Doctoral Fellow is governed as follows: -
The fellowship for Post-Doctoral Fellow is governed as follows | Financial support: Year: First Year — Amount (Rs./Month): 60,000/- 65,000/-; Condition: -------------------- After one SCI/SCIE/AHCI/SSCI Journal acceptance /publication based on work at VNIT after joining PDF.
The fellowship for Post-Doctoral Fellow is governed as follows | Financial support: Year: Second Year — Amount (Rs./Month): 70,000/-; Condition: After minimum two SCI/SCIE/AHCI/SSCI journals acceptance /publication based on work in VNIT and satisfactory performance report given by post-doctoral committee.
Candidates may also note the following points:
No TA/DA will be given to the candidates called for the interview.
This is full time, non-transferable, temporary position.
Candidates will be short listed for the interview based on merit of submitted research proposal, experience and availability of faculty for guiding Post-Doctoral work.
Decision of selection committee of the VNIT, Nagpur will be final.
For any further information /clarification, the applicants may contact directly by email to associatedean_pgphd@vnit.ac.in
HOW TO APPLY: The interested candidates may apply in the prescribed application form (format attached) with complete information, research proposal and attachments (publications/certificates/documents) in one single pdf file. The completely filled applications may be sent by e-mail on associatedean_pgphd@vnit.ac.in latest by October 15, 2025 . The date and time of interview will be informed to the candidate by email only.
The application should contain the following documents:
A covering letter
Duly filled application form
Copy of Ph.D., M. Tech. and B. Tech. Degree Certificates and all Semester Mark sheets
First page of research publications in SCI/SCIE/AHCI/SSCI/Scopus Indexed Journals.
Detailed Research Proposal covering Title, Research Summary, Keywords, State of the art, Objectives, Methodology and Research Plan, Expected Outcomes, Key References and other technical details (if any).
Experience certificate(s), if any
