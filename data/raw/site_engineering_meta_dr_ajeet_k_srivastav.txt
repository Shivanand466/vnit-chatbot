Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Ajeet Kumar Srivastav is currently working as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur. His research mainly focuses on understanding the growth and stability of metal/metal oxide nanostructures to develop materials for sustainable technologies. He has been recently awarded with the Commonwealth Professional Fellowship by the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (UK). Also, he got featured as Crystal Growth & Design Emerging Investigator in a special virtual issue by American Chemical Society. He is a recipient of Early Career Research Award and the International Travel Support by Science and Engineering Research Board, Government of India. Prior to joining VNIT, he earned his Ph.D. degree from Indian Institute of Technology Madras in 2014. His doctoral research contributes an analytical approach to predict the nanograin instability and its growth behaviour in thermodynamically stable nanocrystalline binary alloys. Earlier, he has done his M.Tech. from Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur where he got the opportunity to work at Leibniz Institute for Solid State and Materials Research Dresden (Germany) under the DAAD-IIT Master Sandwich Program. He received his B.Tech. degree from National Institute of Technology Rourkela in 2006.
