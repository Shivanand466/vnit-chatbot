DIRECTOR SIR'S VISIT TO THE LIBRARY
The newly appointed Honourable Director, Prof. Dr. Prem Lal Patel, visited the library and took time to explore each section thoroughly. He also toured the general reading area, attentively observing the space to ensure it provides a comfortable and supportive environment for students' learning.
During his visit, Sir actively engaged with the library staff, shared valuable suggestions and expressed a strong interest in enhancing both the library's services and infrastructure.
Leadership Transition in Associate Dean (ET&LS)
On January 4, 2024, Dr. K. L. Wasewar, Professor and Head of the Department of Chemical Engineering, officially handed over the responsibilities of Associate Dean, Education Technology & Library Services (ET&LS), to Dr. Anupama Kumar, Professor, Department of Chemistry. Dr. Wasewar had assumed this role on January 4, 2021, for an initial term of two years, which was subsequently extended by an additional year.
Throughout his tenure, Dr. Wasewar made noteworthy contributions to the advancement and modernization of the institute's library services. Under his leadership, the library experienced remarkable progress, including the subscription to renowned journal packages such as APS and SAGE, greatly enriching the institute's academic resources.
He also led a number of key infrastructure and service enhancements:
Installation of prominent name boards and illuminated display panels.
Introduction of MyLOFT remote access and the one search discovery tool, enabling seamless access to digital resources.
Renovation and aesthetic enhancement of the library meeting room.
Implementation of one-way vision films, adding a modern and welcoming ambience to the library's entrance.
These initiatives significantly improved the functionality, accessibility and overall user experience of the library, leaving a lasting impact on the academic community.
The new Associate Dean, Dr. Anupama Kumar, brings with her a wealth of experience in academia and a strong commitment to furthering the mission of our Library and Information Resource Centre. We express our heartfelt gratitude to Dr. Wasewar for his outstanding contributions and wish him continued success in his future endeavors. At the same time, we extend a warm welcome to Dr. Anupama Kumar as she takes on this crucial role in leading Library forward.

LIBRARIAN'S DAY 2 0 2 4
Dr. S.R. Ranganathan
National Librarian's Day is observed on 12 August every year on the occasion of birth anniversary of Dr. S.R. Ranganathan (1892-1972) in India. He was a Librarian and Mathematician, widely regarded as the Father of Library Science in India. He pioneered modern library practices and developed the Five Laws of Library Science, which remain fundamental to the field. His 'Colon Classification System' revolutionized book classification, making information retrieval more efficient. Ranganathan's contributions laid the foundation for library and information science in India, influencing global library management. His visionary work continues to shape modern librarianship. A visionary and educator, Dr. Ranganathan's principles continue to guide libraries worldwide. His contributions earned him global recognition and his legacy remains a cornerstone of modern librarianship. th
'Five Laws of Library Science'
First Law: Books are for Use.
Second Law: Every Reader His /Her Book.
Third Law: Every Book its Reader.
Fourth Law: Save the Time of the Reader.
Fifth Law: Library is a Growing Organism.
Dr. S.R. Ranganathan's notable works Includes:
Five Laws of Library Science (1931) - Fundamental principles that guide library operations and services.
Colon Classification (1933) - A highly systematic and faceted book classification system.
Prolegomena to Library Classification (1937 ) - A foundational work on classification theory.
Theory of Library Cataloguing (1938) - A key contribution to cataloging principles.
Library Administration (1935) - Guidelines for efficient library management.
Documentation and its Facets (1963) - Contributions to the field of information science.
Classified Catalogue Code (1934, revised in 1964) - A set of cataloging rules widely adopted in libraries.
SWACHH BHARAT CAMPAIGN
Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, launched on October 2, 2014, is a nationwide initiative aimed at promoting cleanliness, sanitation and effective waste management across India. Inspired by Mahatma Gandhi's vision of a clean and healthy nation, the campaign focuses on improving public hygiene and eliminating open defecation to ensure environmental sustainability. As an Institute of National Importance, VNIT actively supports and participates in this mission. The VNIT Library team joined hands in the effort by cleaning the library lawns and surrounding areas. Following the cleanliness drive, all participants took a Mass Swachhata Pledge, reaffirming their commitment to maintaining a clean and hygienic environment.
ONE-DAY AWARENESS PROGRAMME ON ORCID AND INFLIBNET SERVICES FOR SCHOLARLY COMMUNITIES
( S P O N S O R E D B Y O R C I D O U T R E A C H P R O G R A M M E )
On 25th October 2024, a one-day ORCID awareness program was organized by LIRC Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, in collaboration with INFLIBNET Centre, Gandhinagar Gujrat. This workshop, aimed to help research scholars understand the importance of ORCID and IRINS. The event featured two eminent speakers from INFLIBNET, Dr. Kannan P., Scientist-E(LS) and Dr. Abhishek Kumar, Scientist-E(CS), who delivered insightful sessions. ORCID, which stands for Open Researcher and Contributor ID, is a free, unique, persistent identifier for individuals involved in research, scholarship and innovation, enabling them to be uniquely identified and connected to their contributions across disciplines, borders and time.
Experts highlighted the importance of IRINS, which is a web-based Research Information Management (RIM) service developed by the Information and Library Network (INFLIBNET) Centre. The portal facilitates the academic, R&D organisations, faculty members and scientists to collect, curate and showcase the scholarly communication activities and provide an opportunity to create the scholarly network. The IRINS is available as free software-as-a-service to the academic and R&D organisations in India. Moreover to this, Dr. Abhishek Kumar gave detailed information about other INFLIBNET research activities such as Shodhganga, Shodhgangotri, e-Shodhsindhu etc. The sessions were highly interactive, which kept the audience attentive till the end.
Prof. V.R.Kalamkar, Dean Academics, graced the event as the Guest of Honour and was warmly welcomed by Dr. Anupama Kumar, Associate Dean (ET&LS).The program began with a welcome address by Dr. Jameel Ansari and witnessed the enthusiastic participation of over 100 attendees. The event was smoothly hosted by Mrs. Poonam M. Harode, Assistant Librarian. The success of the program was a result of the dedicated efforts of the entire library team, whose meticulous planning and coordination made the event truly memorable.

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was a pioneering social reformer, economist and the chief architect of the Indian Constitution. He was highly educated, earning multiple degrees, including a Ph.D. from Columbia University and a D.Sc. from the London School of Economics. His education shaped his vision for social justice, equality and empowerment.
Ambedkar's reforms focused on eradicating caste discrimination, promoting education among Dalits and ensuring fundamental rights for all. He played a key role in advocating for reservations in education and employment for marginalized communities. His efforts led to landmark reforms in Indian society, emphasizing dignity, equality and social justice.
