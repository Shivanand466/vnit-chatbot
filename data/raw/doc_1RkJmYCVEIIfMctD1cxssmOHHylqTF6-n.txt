VISVESVARAYA NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, NAGPUR
Instructions for Physical Institute Reporting - 2026
For those students who are allotted VNIT as finally allotted institute/ Freezed their seats in Central Counseling process of Admission (B Tech / B Arch / M Tech / M Sc / Ph D/ IPDF/MBA
As informed by JoSAA/CSAB/ CCMT/ CCMN/DASA, all UG/ PG students allotted Seat in VNIT for Academic Year 2026-27 are required to report at VNIT, Nagpur as per the schedule given by the respective admission authority. Reporting dates mentioned for UG/ PG are irrespective of discipline. For reporting to VNIT, all students are required to fill the details on VNIT online admission portal and complete the reporting procedure. Following are the steps to complete the reporting procedure:
(Step 1-3 to be completed by the students before reporting at the institute)
STEP 1: Download the latest / updated 'Provisional Offer and Seat Acceptance Letter' from JoSAA/CSAB/DASA/CCMT/CCMN (compulsory to complete VNIT Reporting).
STEP 2: Log in on VNIT online admission portal (link http://aimsreg.vnit.ac.in/mproaimsreg/#/)
* To avoid technical difficulties while filling the information on VNIT online portal, Candidates are advised to clear the Browser History every time before logging to VNIT online admission portal.
Fill all the details on VNIT online admission portal and upload the documents. Students are requested to go through the FAQs before logging on online portal. (FAQ Link: FAQs
Considering the duration of physical reporting students are advised/ requested to complete the formalities for VNIT online admission portal one before the last reporting date i.e., before reporting to VNIT physically. PLEASE NOTE THAT, IN ORDER TO AVOID INCONVENIENCE AND DELAY, ALL CANDIDATES ARE INSTRUCTED TO COMPLETE THE VNIT ONLINE ADMISSION PORTAL PROCEDURE AND THEN ONLY REPORT TO VNIT.
STEP 3: Pay the Balance fees (if any) by Re-login student registration portal after completing Step 1 with the Registration ID (viz. 2026*****). Instruction regarding the same is in Annexure I
( ** The payment instructions are for the students who have FREEZED the admission at VNIT Nagpur and are not going to participate for any special round onwards. It is suggested/ Insisted to make the payment by using Desktop computer/ Laptop in order to avoid any inconvenience. If the student makes the payment and dose not confirm the admission at VNIT Nagpur the refund of the amount will be at the discreet of VNIT Nagpur.)
(step 4-5 will be completed at the institute during the physical reporting)
STEP 4: After completely filling up the information on VNIT online admission portal, report physically at VNIT Auditorium along with all the documents in original and 1 set of self-attested photocopies of all documents. Copy of documents is to be arranged in the sequence mentioned in VNIT Institute Reporting Instructions Notice.
VISVESVARAYA NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, NAGPUR
STEP 5: After reporting to VNIT, Nagpur, documents and other details of the candidate will be verified by the VNIT officials and Provisional Reporting Letter would be issued to the candidate. This completes the Physical reporting of the candidate.
Time for Physical Institute Reporting: 10.00 am to 4.00 pm
Note: Actual Processing time for one candidate: Approx. 20-30 minutes (after submission of form at reporting counter). However, total time required for reporting will depend on the number of candidates reported before the candidate at the reporting counter(s).
