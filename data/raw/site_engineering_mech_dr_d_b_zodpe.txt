Dr. D. B. Zodpe
Brief Bio
Dr. D. B. Zodpe is an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. He obtained his M.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Kanpur in 1987 with First Class distinction and completed his Ph.D. from VNIT Nagpur in 2007.With more than three decades of academic, industrial, and research experience, Dr. Zodpe has contributed significantly to the fields of Thermal Engineering, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, Renewable Energy, Heat Transfer, Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), and Energy Conservation. Prior to joining academia, he worked as a Sales Engineer at Voltas Ltd. He has served VNIT in various academic positions including Lecturer, Senior Lecturer, Lecturer (Selection Grade), and Associate Professor.His current research focuses on energy-efficient refrigeration and air-conditioning systems, solar energy technologies, alternative fuels, and sustainable energy solutions. He has guided several Ph.D. scholars in the areas of biodiesel-fueled engines, solar air heaters, and biomedical flow systems, leading to publications in reputed national and international journals.Dr. Zodpe is a member of the Indian Society of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Engineers (ISHRAE), Nagpur Chapter, and has served as Institute In-charge for Air Conditioning Maintenance Contracts. He regularly teaches undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Refrigeration and Cryogenics, Air Conditioning, Advanced Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, and Renewable Energy Sources.
