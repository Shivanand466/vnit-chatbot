Dear Students,
We are excited to inform you that the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 , organized by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, is approaching. V N IT will participate in SIH 2026, with team selection carried out through an Internal Hackathon (IH) as per the guidelines received on participation in SIH2026.
Participation in the Internal Hackathon (IH) requires submission of your problem statements and solution approaches by 15th September 2026, 11:59 PM . Kindly refer to the following guidelines for team formation, submissions, and presentations.
Team Formation Guidelines:
Each team must consist of six members, with at least one female member.
Interdisciplinary teams are encouraged but not mandatory.
Teams must elect a team leader.
A team can have a mentor from amongst the faculty guiding the team. (Optional)
Every team must have a unique name ( Ensure the name does not reference the institute ).
Submit the Google form to fill in details for all team members:
https://bit.ly/4gNmXmz *
Please note: When you open the link, please wait for the automatic security check. (for all links in this document).
(Details required are StudentID, Enrolment N o., Team N ame, N ame of Student, Member Status, Gender, EmailID, Mobile N o., Degree Program, Branch/Stream, Year etc. )
Guidelines for Problem Statement and Solution Submission for IH:
Each team should select at most two problem statements* from the SIH portal through the link https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS

Offline Presentation Guidelines for IH:
Each team will have 10 minutes for their presentation:
5 to 7 minutes for presenting the problem statement and solution approach.
3 to 5 minutes for Q&A with the judges.
