INSTRUCTIONS FOR UPLOADING PH.D. THESIS ON SHODHGANGA
Ensure your thesis PDF adheres to the following specified format before visiting the VNIT library for the upload:
Instruction:
Before uploading the thesis, make sure you have created your ORCID ID . ( Link - http://orcid.org)
The Consent Form for Digital Archiving, approved by the Library Advisory Committee, must be submitted to the Library.
Ensure to split the thesis in the following sections for uploading on Shodhganga and submitting the same to Library & Information Resource Centre for archiving :
 01_Title
 02_prelim pages
 03_Content
 04_Abstract
 05_Chapter 1
 06_ Chapter 2
 07_Chapter 3
 08_Chapter 4
 09_Chapter 5 (If there are more than five chapters, continue the numbering sequentially, e.g., 1, 2, 3...)
 10_Annexures
 80_Recommendation
 90_Plagiarism_Report (In percentage format)
 Other remaining pdf if you have
A one-page plagiarism summary/ report must be uploaded to the Shodhganga portal. Additionally, a detailed plagiarism analysis report (soft copy) must be submitted to the Library for backup and archiving.
After uploading your Ph.D. thesis, collect a receipt for the uploaded Ph.D. thesis.
