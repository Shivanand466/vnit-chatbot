Dr.  N. R. Thote​
Brief Bio
Dr. Niranjan R. Thote is presently Professor, Department of Mining Engineering, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur. I have been graduated (B.E.) in Mining Engineering and subsequently M. Tech (Mine Planning) & Ph.D (Rock Blasting), from Indian Institute of Technology - BHU, Vanarasi. I have joined Lecturer, Mining Engineering in 1988 and subsquently Associate Professor (2004), Professor (2014). During my tenure in VNIT I  held various responsibility like HOD, Associate Dean (IIIA), Nomiated Director (VNITAA) etc. Consequently working as an academician and researcher since nearly four decade, published more than 155 Research Papers, 51 consultancy, many R&D Projects and also authored a book on ' Surface Mine Blast Evaluation'. Supervised 12 PhDs, 6 M.Tech by Research and many PG dissertions. Presently nominated on 'Board of Governors' (BOG) Member on National Fire Engineering Service College, Nagpur. Also Peer Review Committee Member of National Institute of Rock Mechanics, Bangalore of 2016-24 and National Institue of Minors Health under Ministry of Mines (2012-16) and Mining Experts on many BOS of various universities.Being a twice member-deligation to UNESCO (Saint Petersburgs Minng University, Russia) 2016-18 along with Hon. Director VNIT. Being a part of research interaction abroad, visited UK, Germany, Czech Republic, Singapore, Russia and Netherlands etc. I am also a Fellow of Institute of Engineer (I ) lifetime, MGMI, IME etc. and Local Management Committee IE Nagpur.                                                                      I conferred by 'Dr. Anireddy Memorial Award' and 'Best Academic and Innovative Excellence Award' and many more social awards. Worked as Consulting Editors for IMEJ and ‘VISFOTAK’ Journals and also members of many professional bodies. Record has been credited to me for supervising Doctoral work of the First Lady Mining Engineer of India (Chandrani Prasad Verma). I am a leading Academician, Consultant and Administrator in the field of Rock Mechanics, Blasting, Mine Planning and Environment Management from last 37 Yrs.
