Advertisement: M.Tech. (Research) Programme
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur invites applications for admission to the M.Tech. (Research) Programme under supernumerary seats. The Institute offers 23 M.Tech. programmes , with a total of 115 supernumerary seats (5 per programme) , over and above the regular intake. GATE qualification is not mandatory.
Eligibility Criteria
 B.E. / B.Tech. / B.Arch. or equivalent degree in a relevant discipline
 Minimum CGPA of 6.0 (or equivalent)
 Minimum 5 years of industry/teaching experience, or non-teaching staff of academic institutions with at least 5 years of experience
The programme is open to candidates from:
 Engineering disciplines
 Architecture
 Interdisciplinary domains
Key Features
 Limited coursework ( 4-5 courses + Research Methodology )
 Flexible learning through departmental courses and online platforms such as NPTEL/MOOCs
 Programme duration:
3 to 5 years
For detailed information regarding programmes, application procedure, and schedule, please visit the Institute website: www.vnit.ac.in
Important Date:
The last date for submission of the application form in hard copy (via speed post or hand delivery), as well as for uploading the Google Form, is 30 th June 2026 .
Associate Dean Academic (PG & PhD)
