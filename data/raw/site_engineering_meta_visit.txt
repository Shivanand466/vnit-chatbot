Mr. Vasant Krishna Divekar, Director of Perfect Alloy Component Pvt. Ltd, Shimoga Karnataka and a Member of Board of Governors, VNIT along with Mr. Rohit Divekar (Executive Director of Perfect Alloy Component) visited the Department on 10/09/2021. They interacted with the faculty and visited laboratories in the department. They discussed possibilities of collaborative projects with our department related to the issues commonly observed in the foundry industry.
