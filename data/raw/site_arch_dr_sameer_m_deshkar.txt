Dr. Sameer M.Deshkar
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
Sameer Deshkar is serving as Associate Professor at the Department of Architecture and Planning, VNIT, Nagpur. An Architect and Environmental Planner by qualification, he is also leading the IRDR International Center of Excellence in Resilient Communities & Settlements as Coordinator. He has a unique blend of experience interacting with communities at grass-root to global level. He has secured funding from various National and International agencies towards research projects that have been focusing on mainstreaming community resilience into development planning, Food-Energy-Water security under climate change, Regional and Circulating Ecological Sphere (RCES) studies, disaster risk resilience of urban & rural regions, smart & sustainable rural development in India. His other areas of interest include urban biodiversity conservation and Socio-Ecological System (SES) studies. Sameer is a recipient of prestigious research fellowships viz., MEXT fellowship by Govt. of Japan, Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructures (CDRI) fellowship & Integrated Research on Disaster Risk (IRDR) fellowship. He has to his credit papers published in reputed peer reviewed journals and book chapters mainly focusing on disaster risk resilience. He has travelled across parts of South-East Asia and is an avid nature lover.
