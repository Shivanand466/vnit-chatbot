Online Master of Technology (M.Tech, 2 Years) and P.G. Diploma (1 Year) in Computational Fluid-Thermal Sciences (CFTS)
Overview of Foundation Batch: Our journey began with a group of 11 dedicated individuals, each bringing with them a wealth of industrial experience and a shared passion for advancing their careers in Computational Fluid-Thermal Sciences (CFTS).
Legacy Exchange Sessions: Building Bridges: Join us as we bridge past and present with Legacy Exchange Sessions. These gatherings unite our new and returning students, honoring the invaluable insights of our foundation batch. Through these sessions, incoming students gain practical advice and guidance from seasoned professionals.
Duration and Structure:
M.Tech Program (2-4 years) (Total Credits 52) : This program is designed for candidates who aspire to deepen their understanding of computational fluid-thermal sciences by undertaking a research project in addition to completing the required coursework.
P.G. Diploma Program (1-2 years) (Total Credits 40): This program is designed for individuals who want to enhance their knowledge and expertise in fluid dynamics and heat transfer without taking on a research project.
