Procedure / Instructions for AWR Registration Winter 2026

Attention: All AWR Category 'A' and Category 'B' Students
AWR Registration Steps: (for Session W-2026)
I. AWR Registration Category A:For students who are having,
3 to 7 backlogs (including practical), or
Tech. students with4 to 6 years stay or
Arch Students with more than 5-to-7-year stay
Registration Procedure:
Foacl - ( l /l / s lgog
Pay the fees as per instructions given in a separate Notice.
Students who are not required to pay fee (under scholarship schemes) shall contact Academic Section (through email: dr_acd@vnit.ac.in).
Refer Branch wise / Admission Year Wise List of Courses to be offered on Student Notice board (under "Sem. Course Registration" tab) of VNIT website to know courses being offered by your department. (http://vnit.ac.in/ >> Student Notice Board >> Sem. Course Registration)
Consult your Faculty Advisor (FA) (on phone / email / personally) to decide the courses to be registered.
This is important for students with backlog.
All students will do online registration (Including AWR).
Students with backlog courses shall carefully discuss courses to be registered with FA.
Only after finalizing courses to be registered student shall proceed for online registration.
Allotment Process of OC / HM Courses Registration (for Winter 2026), has been already done though Google Form (on First Come First Serve Basis). Those students who have applied for OC / HM Courses, are instructed to confirm their name (if selected) in the list displayed on institute website under >> (http://vnit.ac.in/ >> Student Notice Board >> Sem. Course Registration >> OC-HM Allotment for Winter 2026). If student is allotted an OC or HM Course in Winter 2026, then he / she is instructed to select the remaining DC / DE course(s) accordingly (i.e. 7 Theory & 5 Prac., including OC or HM), and the OC or HM course will be added by Exam Section after Registration Process is completely over for Winter 2026.
Online Registration module is available on URL- https://vnitaims.vnit.ac.in/mproaims/#/
Login to AlMS using Login Id and Password (Same as used earlier)
Complete registration as per instructions & click "APPLY FOR REGISTRATION" button.
After applying for the course registration you can see the selected courses under Registration Management >> Reports >> Registration Form Print >> View Report. Here you can see the list of courses you have selected and the status (for fee / for registration approval). Status will be shown as "Watermark" and will be it will be one of the following,
"PENDING FOR FEES" "PENDING FOR ARROVAL" &
After Approval by Faculty advisor
"REGISTRATION APPROED"
All Students are required to select all backlog courses (Compulsorily) (if any), without selecting Backlog courses (if offered by the department) registration process will not move ahead. Student must keep in mind that, even after selecting backlog courses & the courses student wants to register must not exceed the 8 TH & 5 PR (including Backlog) criteria per semester.
Approach Faculty Advisor (FA) for Approval of Registration. FA will do Approval online. Student will have to abide by the suggestions of FA on changes in the registered courses.
Without Approval of registration by FA, student's registration will remain incomplete and student name will not appear in the roll list of the subjects he / she is registering for.
After approval of registration by FA, student shall login to his/her login area and ensure that registration status has become "REGISTRATION APPROVED". Else, registration will be treated as incomplete.
If the backlog courses are appearing in the same slot and students are not able to select both the course (as it will show message "You Cannot Select 2 Courses in Same Slot"), then these students are instructed to report to Exam Section by Email (Given below) or in-person through their Faculty Advisor, mentioning the backlog course to be register for W26.
W26 course registration of these students will be initiated by Exam Section and remaining courses are to be added by their respective Faculty Advisor.
Student should take a print of Registration form under, Registration Management >> Reports >> Registration Form Print >> View Report. (Make Sure Session Selected as 2026-27/W26), sign that copy of registration. Scan Copy of that Signed Registration Form is to be sent to examination section by Email (Email ID: vnitsemregister@vnit.ac.in). The scanned PDF file have to be named as?????.pdf (????? should be the ID No. of the student).

Student comes under the criteria mentioned above are instructed to directly go to the link,
