Brief Bio
Dr. Pankaj is an Architect-Planner and Assistant Professor in the Department of Architecture and Planning at the Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur.
He holds a Ph.D. (2020) and a Master of Urban and Rural Planning (MURP, 2013) from IIT Roorkee, where his doctoral research focused extensively on smart city planning. He completed his Bachelor of Architecture at Uttarakhand Technical University, Dehradun, in 2012.
With over 12 years of academic and research experience across premier Institutes of National Importance—including SPA Vijayawada and NIT Hamirpur—Dr. Pankaj has authored numerous peer-reviewed research papers in reputed international journals, conference proceedings, and edited volumes. An active mentor, he has guided several undergraduate and postgraduate dissertations and is currently supervising three doctoral scholars.
His research interests span urban and rural dynamics, strategic policy planning, and the computer modeling and simulation of cities.
