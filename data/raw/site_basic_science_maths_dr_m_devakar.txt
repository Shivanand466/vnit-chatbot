Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. M. Devakar, a Professor of Mathematics at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur, is having an experience of about twenty one years in teaching and research. He has qualified the National Eligibility Test (NET) conducted jointly by the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR) and the University Grants Commission (UGC). After completion of M.Sc. Applied Mathematics from Hyderabad Central University, Dr. M. Devakar completed his Ph.D. in Mathematics from National Institute of Technology Warangal, India, as UGC JRF and/or SRF. His broad area of research is Fluid Dynamics and Numerical Fluid Dynamics. Focus of his research includes the analytical and numerical treatment of fluid flows concerning Newtonian, non-Newtonian and polar fluids. Dr. M. Devakar has completed a research project sponsored by the National Board of Higher Mathematics (NBHM), Department of Atomic Energy (DAE), Government of India. He also completed a couple of consultancy projects. He published about 47 research articles in journals of international repute, and guided more than 24 M.Sc. Dissertations. Dr. Devakar guided five students for their Ph.D. and currently three students are pursuing their Ph.D. under his supervision.  He has been active in administrative activities at the Department as well Institute level. A few of the notable institute level administrative activites that he was associated with include Chairman of International Admissions, Chairman of M.Sc. Admissions, Professor-in-Charge of AXIS (VNIT Technical Festival of Students), Member of Internal Complaint Committee (ICC) to address the complaints of Sexual Harassment of Women at Work Places etc.
