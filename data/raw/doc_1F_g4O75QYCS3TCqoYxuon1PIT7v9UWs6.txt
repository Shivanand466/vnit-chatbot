Greetings of the day!
We are pleased to inform you about the launch of Adani Gyan Jyoti Scholarship 2024-25 , which is currently open for first-year students pursuing courses related to JEE, NEET, CLAT, CA Foundation, and Economics . This scholarship initiative is part of Adani Group's commitment to contributing to India's educational landscape and supporting the next generation of leaders.
Sharing more program details below for your reference:
Eligibility:
Only first-year students pursuing BA Economics, BSc Economics, BEc, B.E./B.Tech., Integrated 5-Year DualDegree M.Tech., MBBS, and LLB courses are eligible.
Applicants must have passed their higher secondary/pre-university/intermediate/CBSE/ISC or equivalent board exams after 2023.
Admission must be based on the merit rank from state or national-level entrance exams.
Family income must not exceed INR 4,50,000 per annum from all sources.
Children of employees of Adani Group and Buddy4Study are not eligible.
Note: Diploma students admitted to B.E., B.Tech., or B.Arch. courses on a lateral basis, as well as BDS students, are not eligible.
Award: Scholarship of up to INR 3,50,000 (Annual)
