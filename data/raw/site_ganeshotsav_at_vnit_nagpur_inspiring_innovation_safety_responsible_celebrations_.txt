Ganeshotsav at VNIT Nagpur | Inspiring Innovation, Safety & Responsible Celebrations 🙏 vnit_web
September 22, 2026
News
Ganeshotsav celebrations at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, were enriched by the presence of Hon’ble Chief Minister of Maharashtra Shri Devendra Fadnavis and Nagpur Police Commissioner Shri Vishwas Nangare Patil.
✨ Shri Devendra Fadnavis interacted with students and emphasized the importance of innovation, technology and entrepreneurship in India’s development. He encouraged students to harness their knowledge and technological capabilities to contribute meaningfully to the nation’s progress.
👮 Shri Vishwas Nangare Patil, Police Commissioner, Nagpur, addressed students on anti-ragging, drug abuse and narcotics, highlighting their serious impact on education, careers and personal lives. He urged students to stay away from harmful substances and contribute towards building a safe, disciplined and responsible campus environment.
🔊 He also highlighted the potential health concerns associated with excessive exposure to DJ sound, loud music and laser lights, encouraging students to celebrate festivals responsibly while keeping health, safety and well-being in mind.
🌟 Dr. Lohit Matani (IPS), SP, State Highway Police, also interacted with the students, adding to the meaningful engagement and reinforcing the importance of discipline, awareness, road safety and responsible citizenship among the student community.
The visit added special significance to the Ganeshotsav celebrations at VNIT Nagpur, bringing together an inspiring message of innovation, responsible citizenship, student safety and healthy celebrations. 🇮🇳✨
