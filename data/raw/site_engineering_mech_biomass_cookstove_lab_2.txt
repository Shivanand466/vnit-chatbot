It is a known fact that millions of people in India and around the world still use biomass stoves for multiple applications. The purpose of the biomass cookstove lab is to carry out research in the unexplored area to enhance the performance of the stove and develop efficient, economical, and durable stove for the benefits of society. Research conducted involves both experimental and computational analysis..
