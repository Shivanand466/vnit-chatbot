Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Sangesh P. Zodape is currently working as an Associate Professor in Department of Chemistry, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur , Maharashtra, India. Sangesh P. Zodape born and brought up in Nagpur, Maharashtra.  He has completed B.Sc. (Chemistry) from  R.T.M. Nagpur University, Nagpur  in 2004 and M.Sc. (Physical Chemistry)  from Department of Chemistry, R.T.M. Nagpur University, Nagpur in 2006. He also completed M. Phil. (Physical Chemistry) from Department of Chemistry, R.T.M. Nagpur University, Nagpur in 2008. He has been awarded the Ph.D. degree in Chemistry entitled "Thermodynamic and transport properties of some Biologically active compound in aqueous solution at different tempertaures" in 2012 from R.T.M. Nagpur University, Nagpur, Maharashtra, India under the supervision of Dr. Sudhakar S. Dhondge. He has one-year industrial experience from Asian Paint LTD. Gujrat and Lupin Pharma. Bhopal. His research interests include thermodynamic, acoustic, and transport properties of binary and ternary mixtures containing biologically active compounds and ionic liquids, as well as the synthesis and applications of nanomaterials and composites.
