National Overseas Scholarship Scheme (NOS) for ST candidates for the Selection vear 2026-27.
The Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, invites online applications for the National Overseas Scholarship Scheme (NOS) from ST candidates for the selection year 2026-27. The scholarship is awarded to students selected for pursuing higher studies abroad at the Masters level and Ph.D. programmes.
Number of slots forScholarship: 20 (Number of slots may vary)
Interested Candidates may applyonline through NOS Portal https://overseas.tribal.gov.in/ The portal will remain open from 15th May 2026 to 30th June 2026 (5:30PM) for submission ofapplications.
For more details candidates may visit Ministry's portal: https://overseas.tribal.gov.in/
