DEPARTMENT LIBRARY
The Library is present on the first floor of the department. It has a collection of over 525 books covering all major topics taught in the UG and PG curriculum such as Data structures, Computer Architecture, Operating systems, Theory of Computation, Web Programming, Distributed systems, Networking and Network Security etc. as well as a few miscellaneous books.
The library is open on all working days from 9:30am to 6pm.  There is a provision to borrow books and they can be kept for the entire duration of the semester. Almost 50% of the books in the library have been donated by students and faculty. To donate books to the department library please contact Mrs. Khanjali Mohod, Ph. +919881367487.
