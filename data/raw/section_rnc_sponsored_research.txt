Sponsored Research Projects
VNIT’s sponsored research spans smart & sustainable infrastructure, renewable energy and EVs, advanced/functional materials, AI/ML & 6G/quantum communications, water & environmental systems, biomedical engineering, mining & critical minerals, manufacturing & robotics, and fundamental sciences (physics/mathematics). Projects are supported by ANRF, DST (TIH/FIST/CEST/AMT/STARS), DRDO (ARDE/ARMREB/ER&IPR/DIPAS/CVRDE), ISRO, DoT (TTDF), CSIR, ICMR, NBHM-DAE, UGC-DAE CSR, Ministry of Mines, Ministry of Jal Shakti, MoHUA–CPHEEO, DSIR, Royal Society (UK), INSA, and industry partners.
