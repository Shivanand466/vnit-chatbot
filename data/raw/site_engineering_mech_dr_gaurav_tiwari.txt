Dr. Gaurav Tiwari
Brief Bio
Dr. Gaurav Tiwari completed  his graduation from University of Rajasthan in 2003. Thereafter he completed his M.Tech from IIT delhi from Applied Mechanics Dept (2005-07) and Ph.D from IIT Roorkee from Dept. of Civil engineering in 2011-14. He has three years industrial and 12 years teaching experience. His area of reasearch is Crashworthiness and ballistics. He is expertize in Machine design, Finite element analysis, strcuture analysis, Fracture mechanics etc.
Dr. Tiwari has more than 50 scopus indexed papers. He has granted three reaserach projects from DSTand ISRO. Moreover he has grated one patent on Doubly curvature sandwich structure. He has guided three Ph.D. student and almost 20 M.Tech students.
