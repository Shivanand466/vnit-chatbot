Document: First Strategic Meeting of International Advisory Committee on 09.06.2025 (dated 2025-06-18)
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur
Office of Dean International Affairs & Alumni
Date: 17.06.2025
VNIT Nagpur organized the first strategic meeting of its International Advisory Committee to discuss the road map for enhancing the institute's internationalization of education, activities and overall globalization. The session was chaired by Director Prof. Prem Lal Patel, Chairman of this Committee, with a warm welcome and coordination by Prof. K. M. Bhurchandi, (Dean, IAA), Member Secretary, IAC, and introductions by Dr. Makarand M. Lokhande, Associate Dean(IAR). Esteemed experts including Mr. M. S. Unnikrishnan (Member, IAC), CEO, IITB-University of Monash Australia, Prof. Sudarshan Kumar (Vice Chairman, IAC), Dean International Affairs, IIT Bombay, Prof. Nemkumar Banthia (Member, IAC), Professor of Structure, University of British Colombia, Canada, and Prof. Mohan Lal Kolhe (Member of IAC), Professor, University of Agder, Norway shared valuable insights on international collaborations, research-driven visibility, and capacity building. Discussions emphasized the importance of active MoUs, regional partnerships, student and faculty exchange, and research funding mechanisms. The minutes of the meeting will be shared with the committee members who could not attend the meeting. Their valuable inputs will also be considered and dually included as part of the minutes in shaping VNIT's internationalization strategy.
