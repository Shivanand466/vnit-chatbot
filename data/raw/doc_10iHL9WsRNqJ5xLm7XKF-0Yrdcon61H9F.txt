PRAYAAS CLUB ACTIVITIES REPORT 2024-25
One can't help everyone, but everyone can help someone.
About Prayaas:
PRAYAAS is a socially-driven club at VNIT, Nagpur that brings together a select group of thoughtful individuals with the aim of uplifting the less fortunate and promoting personal growth. Founded in September 2006 with an initial membership of 10 individuals, the club has since grown to encompass around 100 members, all of whom receive guidance and mentorship from esteemed Advisor Dr. Dilip Peshwe and Professors In-charge Dr. G.N. Nimbarte and Dr. Srinivasan.
At the core of our mission is the belief that every activity we undertake must instill a sense of responsibility within us to give back to society and make a positive impact. We continually strive to expand our outreach and extend our services to those who are in dire need of assistance.
For us, service is a thing of beauty, and we approach it with a wholehearted dedication and a free mind, finding joy in the act of giving and the knowledge that we are making a tangible difference in the world.
List of events activities organized in the academic year 2024-25 Winter Semester:
Club orientation program for first year students
On September 1st, 2024 , we conducted an orientation program for newly enrolled students, providing them with a comprehensive overview of our club's mission, vision, and core values. The session also highlighted past events, ongoing initiatives, and future plans. We emphasized the importance of self-development and the positive impact their participation can have on society. New members were encouraged to actively engage in our activities to contribute to our collective goals.

Blood Donation Camp
We hold high regard for every single drop of blood and strongly advocate for blood donation as the ultimate form of giving. Consequently, we organized two blood donation drives on August 15, 2024 , right within CRC. The event was a collaboration with four prominent blood banks in Nagpur, attracting 300+ students who voluntarily donated blood for a noble cause. Furthermore, the ceremony saw the felicitation of frequent donors who had donated blood six times or more. The event proved to be a tremendous accomplishment, and we were able to make a significant contribution towards the aid of those in need.

Raksha Bandhan
On August 20th, 2024 , the Prayaas Club organized a Raksha Bandhan event at the Upay NGO. The event was aimed at celebrating the bond between siblings and promoting the spirit of unity and love. It was a festive occasion where people came together to tie rakhi, exchange gifts and sweets, and enjoy cultural activities. The Prayaas Club worked hard to ensure that the event was a success and that everyone had a memorable time. Overall, the Raksha Bandhan celebration brought joy and happiness to all who participated, strengthening the community's bonds and spreading positivity.

Swachhata Abhiyaan
As part of the Swacch Bharat Abhiyaan initiated by the Government of India, Team Prayaas from VNIT Nagpur conducted a visit to Gram Vikas Vidyalaya, Marodi, NTPC Mauda, and Zilla Parishad School, Jirvha village on 12th September 2024 . During the visit, the Prayaas Club organized an awareness campaign on cleanliness, which included a dramatic performance to engage and educate the local community. In addition to raising awareness, our volunteers actively participated in cleaning the roads around the village and the school premises. The visit was part of the 'Swacchata Pakhwada 2024' celebrated by VNIT Nagpur, marking a successful and impactful initiative to promote cleanliness and hygiene.

Daan Utsav
From October 12th to October 20th, 2024 , Prayaas Club organized a week-long event called Daan Utsav, also known as the Joy of Giving Week. The event was aimed at promoting the spirit of giving and making a positive impact on society. This year, Prayaas conducted four significant side events combined with the main initiative, which collectively contributed to various social causes.
1. Shram Daan (20th October 2024)
The Shram Daan initiative focused on cleanliness and sustainability, with Prayaas volunteers joining hands with Nagpur Ploggers to conduct a cleanliness drive in Nagpur. This collaboration aimed to promote sustainable living and raise awareness about the importance of environmental responsibility. Volunteers worked tirelessly to clean public spaces, contributing to a greener and cleaner future for the community. The drive encapsulated the ethos of mindful living and sustainability, reinforcing the importance of collective action
2. Vidya Daan (15th October 2024)
On October 15th, Prayaas organized Vidya Daan, focusing on empowering young minds through the gift of knowledge. Volunteers dedicated their time to the children of UPAY NGO, engaging with them in educational activities and promoting learning. This initiative sought to open doors to brighter futures by nurturing the potential of underprivileged children and fostering a sense of hope and opportunity for them.
3. Shiksha Daan (17th October 2024)
The Shiksha Daan initiative focused on providing educational materials to students in need. Prayaas volunteers collected stationery donations, which were then distributed to students at a local NGO. The aim was to support their educational journey by providing essential tools for learning. This initiative helped ignite a spark of hope in students, empowering them to pursue their academic dreams. The contributions made a lasting impact on the educational aspirations of many young minds.
4. Vastra Daan (19th October 2024)
On October 19th, Prayaas organized Vastra Daan, a clothing donation drive aimed at providing warmth and support to those in need. Volunteers collected clothes and distributed them to individuals who required them the most. This initiative not only provided essential garments but also conveyed a message of care, hope, and compassion to the less fortunate. The smiles of gratitude from the recipients underscored the importance of community support and kindness.
Other Activities
We also worked as Scribe for blind people through Atmadeepam NGO. We helped them write their competitive examinations. Also, we provided books to first years through Prayaas Book Bank at the start of semesters.
