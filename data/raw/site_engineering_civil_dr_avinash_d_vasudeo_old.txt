Dr. Avinash D. Vasudeo
Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. A D Vasudeo is an Associate Professor in Civil Engineering Department at VNIT Nagpur. He has completed his Ph.D. from VNIT Nagpur and M.Tech from IIT Kanpur. His research interests include Hydrology, Hydraulic Engineering, Groundwater Monitoring, Stepped Spillways, Labyrinth Weirs.
