Report on Participation in the Pre-APAIE Indo-French Day
Date:
March 24, 2025
Venue:
Alliance Française de Delhi, New Delhi
Organizer:
French Institute in India and Campus France
Introduction
The Pre-APAIE Indo-French Day was organized by the French Institute in India and Campus France as a precursor to the 2025 Asia-Pacific Association for International Education (APAIE) Conference and Exhibition. The event aimed to provide an overview of the Indian and French higher education ecosystems, discuss challenges and opportunities in international collaborations, and facilitate networking among institutions from both countries. The event was attended by representatives of over 30 French higher education institutions, along with distinguished guests and Indian academic representatives.
Event Highlights
Presentations on Higher Education Systems
From 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM, presentations were delivered on the structure, policies, and challenges of higher education in both India and France. The speakers highlighted key areas such as research collaborations, student exchange programs, joint degree opportunities, and funding mechanisms available for academic partnerships.
Official Reception and Address
At 6:00 PM, the official reception was inaugurated by His Excellency Mr. Thierry MATHOU, Ambassador of France to India, and Ms. Donatienne HISSARD, General Director of Campus France. The address emphasized the importance of strengthening Indo-French academic ties, fostering innovation through joint research, and promoting student mobility between the two nations.
Networking and Discussions
Following the official address, a networking session was held where participants engaged in discussions with representatives from leading French institutions such as Université Paris-Saclay, Sorbonne Université, INSA Lyon, Institut Mines-Telecom, and Paris School of Business, among others. The session facilitated meaningful interactions and potential future collaborations in education and research.
Key Observations
 No Language Barrier: It was observed that learning or speaking French is not a prerequisite for studying or working in France, making it accessible to international students and professionals.
 Scholarships Available: Various scholarship opportunities are available for Indian students aspiring to study in France, providing financial support and making education more affordable.
 Job Opportunities: France offers job opportunities for international graduates, making it an attractive destination for higher education and career prospects.
Key Takeaways
 Strengthened Academic Collaborations: The event provided an opportunity to explore potential MoUs and institutional partnerships with renowned French universities.
 Exchange Program Opportunities: Discussions were held on student and faculty exchange programs, scholarships, and funding options for Indo-French academic exchanges.
 Research and Innovation: The need for collaborative research initiatives in emerging areas such as artificial intelligence, sustainability, and digital transformation was highlighted.
 Networking and Future Prospects: The event served as a platform for establishing new professional connections and discussing the roadmap for enhanced Indo-French cooperation in higher education.
Institutions were categories into Engineering, Science and Business Schools for easy interaction.
Attendees included academic administrators, researchers, faculty members, and representatives from Indian and French universities.
Alumini Outcomes Survey: Survey indicate 94% of Indian Alumni are currently working in intellectual or management jobs, and 38 % of Indian alumni use the French language on a daily basis.
Conclusion
The Pre-APAIE Indo-French Day was an insightful and productive event that provided valuable exposure to the French higher education system and opportunities for collaboration. The event successfully facilitated discussions on academic partnerships, mobility programs, and research collaborations, paving the way for stronger Indo-French educational ties. Participation in this event was highly beneficial in terms of networking, knowledge exchange, and exploring avenues for institutional cooperation.
