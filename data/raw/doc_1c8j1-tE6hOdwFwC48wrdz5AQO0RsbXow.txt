We are pleased to inform you about the Reliance Foundation Postgraduate Scholarships 2026-27 , currently open for students pursuing first-year, full-time regular postgraduate courses in select future-ready courses from Engineering, Technology, Energy and Life Sciences. Sharing the programme details below for your reference:
ELIGIBILITY
Indian students who are enrolled in the first-year, full-time regular postgraduate programme in Engineering, Technology, Energy and Life Sciences can apply. Please visit the RFS website for a Eligible course list: Click here for the full list.
Must have a CGPA of 7.5 or above in their undergraduate studies (or equivalent percentage normalised to CGPA) if the student has not attempted GATE
Award: Up to INR 6,00,000 over the duration of the degree
