Dr. Vishal R. Satpute
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
I obtained my B.E. (in Electronics Engineering) in 2001 from Nagpur University, M. Tech. (in Communication Systems) from IIT Madras in the year 2003 and Ph.D. from VNIT Nagpur in the year 2015. I am working as Associate Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at VNIT Nagpur. I am member of IEEE, ACM, IAENG, WIAA and ISTE. Working in the areas of image processing, computer vision, biometrics, cryptography, video processing, video surveillance and communications. Completed three sponsored research project funded by BRNS and MeiTY and SERB and have published more than 60 international journal and conference papers.
