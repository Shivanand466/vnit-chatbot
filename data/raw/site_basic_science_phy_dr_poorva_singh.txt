Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Poorva Singh is a theoretical and computational condensed matter physicist whose chief interest lies in the electronic structure calculations of novel bulk and low–dimensional systems, particularly, topological insulators and semimetals, van der waals materials and magnetic materials, employing density functional theory.  Currently she is Assistant Professor, Grade 1, at Department of Physics, VNIT Nagpur. She joined as an Assistant Professor at Department of Physics, VNIT Nagpur in May, 2015. Prior to joinning Department of Physics, VNIT Nagpur she worked as a post-doctoral researcher (visiting scientist) under Prof. Luc Stafford, University de Montreal, Quebec, Canada from 2014-2015. Before that she was working as a post-doctoral researcher (project scientist) under Prof. Rajendra Prasad, Department of Physics, IIT Kanpur under a DST project from 2013-2014. She has obtained her Phd degree from Department of Physics, IIT Roorkee under the guidance of Prof. Tashi Nautiyal, Dept of Physics, IIT Roorkee (2007-2012). She has completed her integrated Masters and Bachelors (Hons. School in Physics) from Department of Physics, Panjab University, Chandigarh (2002-2007). She has published several journal articles in the area of topological materials in international journals of repute (Physical Review B, Journal of Physics Condensed matter, Journal of Applied Physics etc. ). Under her guidance, two PhD students have been awarded Ph.D degree in April, 2022 and January, 2024, respectively. She has also supervised 17 M.Sc .project thesis since 2015. Currently four PhD students and two MSc students are working under her guidance in her research group. In the past, she has completed several institutional level responsibilities like warden girl's hostel, faculty mentor to btech students, member of annual convocation committes etc. At departmental level she has worked as an exam coordinator, PhD seminar coordinator, computer lab incharge, DST-FIST grant coordinator, student activities coordinator, etc. Dr. Poorva Singh is also acting as an active reviewer for some AIP and elsevier journals.
