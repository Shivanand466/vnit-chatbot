❤️ YOUR UNUSED ITEMS CAN BRING SOMEONE A SMILE! 🎁
Be a part of Daan Utsav 2026 – The Festival of Giving Happiness, organized by Prayas Club, VNIT Nagpur, in collaboration with Goonj, from 11–18 October 2026.
✨ Don’t let usable items go to waste—let them become someone’s reason to smile!
👕 Donate clean, usable clothes, books, toys, footwear, blankets, bedsheets, and household essentials. Your small contribution can make a meaningful difference in someone’s life.
📍 Drop-off Centres:
– VNIT Health Centre
– Bajaj Nagar Gate, opposite the Girls’ Hostel
🕒 Donation Timings:
– Monday–Friday: 6:00–8:00 AM & 6:00–8:00 PM
– Saturday–Sunday: 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
📞 Contact: Vivek Singh – 98355 55871 | Aditya – 80743 55583
🤝 Your surplus could be someone else’s treasure. Open your cupboards, share what you can, and help us turn Small Efforts into Big Smiles!
Donate today. Spread happiness. Make kindness count!❤️
