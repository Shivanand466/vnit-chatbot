Dr. Nachiket Bhagade
Brief Bio
I am from Nagpur and I joined VNIT on July, 2022. I take interest to work in rock excavation technology, rock excavation methods and blast optimization.  Non blasting interventions are very pertinent in todays very fast developing urban and sub-urban areas. So, popular alternatives such as rock breaker, vibro ripper, plasma blasting, abrasive water jet cutting, CARDOX and pyrotehcnics are my areas of interest. I am keen to work in underground coal mining and mass production technology for underground coal. The subjects entrusted to me to teach are Mining Economics, Mass Production for Underground Coal, Mine Surveying and Mine Legislation and Safety.
