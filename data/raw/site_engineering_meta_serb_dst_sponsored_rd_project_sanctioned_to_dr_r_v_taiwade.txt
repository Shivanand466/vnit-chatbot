Title of the project:
Tailoring the actuation characteristics of SMA/Polyimide sandwich-structured composite for its viability towards actuator development for MEMS and soft robotic systems.
About the project:
The main focus of the project is to design and develop thin film SMA-Polyimide composite and investigate its viability in soft robotic application. SMA is considered to be low-bandwidth, large strain actuator restricting its application in high frequency under water and aerial soft robotic systems. We therefore propose a novel approach to fabricate thin film SMA-Polyimide sandwich-structured composite to realize the improvement in actuation frequency along with the actuation force to boost the load carrying capacity.
