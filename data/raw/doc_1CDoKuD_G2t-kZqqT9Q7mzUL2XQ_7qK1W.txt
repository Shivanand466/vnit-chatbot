VISVESVARAYA NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, NAGPUR HOSTEL SECTION
Room Retention notice for present 3rd year students staying i in Vikram Sarabhai Hostel
Present 3rd year students who are staying in Vikram Sarabhai Hostel and wish to retain their current rooms need to follow the following procedure –
They must follow the complete procedure as given in the Notice No. HS/2025/26 dated 15.04.2025 for hostel registration for Winter 2025 on VNIT Website at Hostel Notices.
2)Only those students who have paid the hostel fees for Winter 25 session will be eligible to retain their rooms as mentioned in point no.1.
They have to report the Hostel Supervisor and submit the Hostel admission form along with the photo copy of Hostel fee payment receipt.
Students have to register for room retention in the register kept with the hostel supervisor.
Those who have followed the above procedure shall not be considered for allotment through lottery system and their rooms will be retained. However, the possession of the room will be given only after the commencement of the new session i.e. on 28th July 2025.
Those who did not pay the hostel fees within prescribed time will not be considered for room retention / lottery. Their rooms will be allotted to other students who have paid the hostel fees and wants to stay inVikramSarabhai hostel.
To,
Mr. Ankush Nikalje, to display it on the Notice Board of Vikram Sarabhai Hostel.
