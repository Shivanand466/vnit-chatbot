MISSION
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur is one of the thirty one National Institutes of Technology in the country. The Govt. of India conferred on the Institute, the Deemed to be University status (under University Grants Commission Act. 1956 (3 of 1956)) with effect from 26th June 2002. Subsequently, the Central Govt. by Act of Parliament (National Institutes of Technology Act. 2007 (29 of 2007)) declared VNIT Nagpur as an Institute of National Importance along with all other NITs. The Act was brought into force from 15th August 2007. Earlier, the Institute was known as Visvesvaraya Regional College of Engineering (VRCE). It was established in the year 1960 under the scheme sponsored by Govt. of India and Govt. of Maharashtra. The college was started in June 1960 by amalgamating the State Govt. Engineering College functioning at Nagpur since July 1956. In the meeting held in October 1962, the Governing Board of the College resolved to name it after the eminent engineer, planner, and statesman of the country, Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya.
The mission of VNIT is to achieve high standards of excellence in generating and propagating knowledge in engineering and allied disciplines. V.N.I.T. is committed to provide education that combines rigorous academics with the joy of discovery. The Institute encourages its community to engage in a dialogue with society to contribute for the betterment of humankind.
VISION
To contribute effectively to the national endeavour of producing quality human resource of world class standard by developing a sustainable technical education system to meet the changing technological needs of the country by incorporating relevant social concerns and to build an environment to create and propagate innovative technologies for the economic development of the Nation.

With a vision to bring the Industry, Academia and ISRO triad under one umbrella to contribute towards the R&D initiatives relevant to Indian Space Programme, the Capacity building program office of ISRO signed an MOU with the honourable Director of VNIT, to set up a space technology incubation center (S-TIC) at VNIT. This will serve as nodal centre for the western region of India covering states of Maharashtra, Goa, Gujrat, Rajasthan and union territories of Daman Diu and Dadra & Nagar Haveli.
The objective of the S-TIC is to create space entrepreneurs in India. This initiative is envisaged to attract and nurture the young academia with innovative ideas/research aptitude who can carry out research in the domain of Space Science and Technology and realise them towards indigenous development of the spaceworthy products. The research outcome of the students working on various projects under the banner of S-TIC, will be translated into a Proof of Concept or prototype through industries tied up with the S-TIC through ISRO. The "YUKTI-Sanchita 2021" scheme has 45 product development/ innovative project proposals from Department of Space and ISRO centers/labs, which are suitable to be taken at S-TIC as their outcomes have a good market potential and can lead to initiation of a start-up. On a whole, the establishment of S-TIC intends to transform students as future entrepreneurs and encourage them to contribute positively towards the Indian Space Programme.

TECHNICAL CLUBS
The club developed two innovative projects: an eye-tracking system that controls digital cursors using computer vision to enhance accessibility for individuals with limited mobility, and an AI-powered interview platform that conducts realistic mock sessions to provide personalized performance feedback. Together, these tools leverage advanced technology to deliver practical, hands-free solutions for both daily digital interaction and professional career development.
llabs lvLabs
Two of the most significant projects undertaken by the club are focused on the development of advanced UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) systems. One of the projects involves a self-guided UAV capable of autonomous takeoff, flight navigation, landing, and terrain analysis. The system is designed using intelligent control and sensing technologies that allow the UAV to operate with minimal human intervention while collecting and analyzing environmental data in real time. The project is being developed with the aim of contributing to research initiatives associated with IROQU and ISRO-related robotics applications, highlighting its potential relevance in surveillance, mapping, and autonomous exploration missions.
Another major project undertaken by the club is the complete design and development of a UAV built entirely from scratch. From frame design and aerodynamics to electronics integration and flight control systems, every component of the UAV was developed and assembled by the team. The project provided hands-on experience in drone engineering, system integration, and testing while emphasizing innovation and practical implementation. The UAV demonstrates the club's technical capabilities in aerospace systems, embedded electronics, and autonomous technologies.
TECHNICAL CLUBS
TESLA is a student-driven technical club dedicated to fostering innovation, collaborative teamwork, and hands-on engineering experience through multidisciplinary projects. Demonstrating exceptional technical versatility this year, the club achieved a major milestone by having four of its diverse projects selected for the CBDE Program. Alongside these achievements, the team also engineered an EV Water Tanker, reinforcing the club's commitment to delivering sustainable, eco-friendly, and practical transportation solutions for real-world challenges.
Team Velocity Club is a dynamic, student-driven initiative dedicated to designing and manufacturing competitive go-karts for national motorsport events. This year, the team built a custom kart entirely from scratch-sourcing a motorcycle engine, tailoring the chassis, and fabricating essential braking, steering, and gear systems under a strict budget. Combining hands-on assembly with academic software training in Fusion 360, the club secured a milestone sponsorship from Vega Helmets, signaling major industry recognition as they prepare for the upcoming Morphin Motorsports competition.
The Finance Club (TFC) is a student-driven club dedicated to promoting financial awareness, analytical thinking, and practical learning in the field of finance. The club brings together students who are interested in finance, economics, business, and investment-related domains, providing them with opportunities to explore these areas beyond the academic curriculum. By encouraging collaborative learning and real-world problem solving, TFC helps students build a deeper understanding of financial concepts and market dynamics while developing a strong analytical mindset.

STUDENT ACTIVITIES
VNIT Nagpur provides an environment that nurtures leaders in addition to sound engineers. Notwithstanding the tight academic schedule that students have, they manage to indulge in various extra-curricular activities. A host of activities ranging from inter-hostel competitions and national level festivals to managing facilities like hostel mess andl infrastructure are entirely managed by students. These activities provide multitude of holistic improvement platforms for students to hone their technical and managerial skills, which are imperative for one to excel in his her workplace. Here is the glimpse of activities.
AXIS, the annual technical festival of VNIT and one of Central India's largest innovation platforms, brings together students from over 170 colleges for a vibrant celebration of engineering excellence. Anchored by title sponsor Lloyds Metals and supported by brands like Zomato, SBI, and Vivo, the fest features over 35 competitions-including the high-speed "Drone at Work" racing challenge-alongside workshops, exhibitions by ISRO, and talk shows with prominent figures like Sudhanshu Trivedi and Acharya Prashant. By blending intense robotics, electronics, and architecture contests with pro shows and hands-on event management, AXIS offers participants a powerful arena to sharpen both their technical skills and leadership abilities.
CONSORTIUM
One of the major highlights organized under E-Cell is Consortium, the institute's E-Summit, which features a variety of entrepreneurship-focused events and competitions. These include Startup Expo, where emerging startups and innovative ideas are showcased; Startup Conclave, which involves discussions and interactions with entrepreneurs and industry experts; Hult Prize, a globally recognized social entrepreneurship competition; Jugaad, which promotes creative problem-solving and innovation; and Pitch in the Car, an engaging pitching event where participants present startup ideas in a dynamic and interactive format. Alongside these events, TEDx sessions provide a platform for inspiring speakers to share experiences, ideas, and stories aimed at motivating and broadening students' perspectives.
Aarohi 25 was one of the biggest and most awaited cultural festivals in Central India, bringing together students and participants from various colleges and institutions to celebrate talent, creativity, and art. The fest created a vibrant and energetic atmosphere filled with performances, competitions, and large-scale cultural events that attracted huge audiences throughout its duration. The festival featured a wide range of cultural competitions and activities, including dance competitions, singing events, band wars, and stage plays. Participants showcased their skills across different art forms, making the event a platform for creativity, teamwork, and entertainment. Along with the competitions, the fest also served as an opportunity for students to interact, collaborate, and experience large-scale event management and cultural engagement.
TEDxVNIT
Operating under an official, independent TED license, this organization focuses entirely on curating a powerful platform for "ideas worth spreading." It brings a multidisciplinary lineup of speakersfrom technical innovators to social changemakers-directly to the campus community to inspire lateral thinking and global perspective. ECELL
Operating as the primary catalyst for startup culture, E-Cell helps students transition technical ideas and engineering prototypes into viable business models. The club actively introduces the student community to essential commercial concepts like venture capital, market research, and intellectual property. Its flagship inaugural event kicks off the academic year by laying out the roadmap for upcoming hackathons and the annual E-Summit, serving as a massive networking hub for aspiring student founders and potential co-founders.
