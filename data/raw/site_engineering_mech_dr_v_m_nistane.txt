Dr. V. M. Nistane
Brief Bio
Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, with a strong commitment to excellence in teaching, research, and academic mentorship. Over the course of my career, I have developed extensive experience in delivering undergraduate and postgraduate courses, guiding students in both theoretical understanding and practical applications of mechanical engineering concepts.
I have made significant contributions to the research community, with approximately 25 publications in reputed national and international journals as well as conference proceedings. My research work spans diverse areas within mechanical engineering, emphasizing innovation, interdisciplinary approaches, and real-world applications. Beyond teaching and research, I am committed to academic development activities, including curriculum design, student mentoring, and participation in institutional responsibilities.
