A team of Two faculty members Prof Kishor Bhurchandi, Dean International affairs and Prof Rahul Ralegaonkar, Professor of Civil Engineering visited Vietnam for participating Namaste Vietnam 2024. More than 50 universities; both from India (34) and Vietnam (17) participated the activities for engaging students, researchers and teachers for admissions, collaborations, exchanges and MoUs. More than 500 students from Vietnam attended the event to understand the prospective universities for potential studies in India. More than 20 students showed keen interest in the graduation studies, a few have shown interest for higher studies at VNIT, while a few researchers and teachers are interested in joint activities. More than 25 contacts of students and teachers have been extracted for their interest in admissions to UG and PG courses at VNIT and joint activities. A few university faculty members from universities Like RMIT, Australia- Vietnam Campus and Can Tho University, Vietnam also showed the interest for potential MoU signing. Their contacts also have been collected and added to our data base. They will be contacted by email before the next admission season.
