Online Registration Instructions for Institute Reporting PhD Admission Cycle-2 (July - 2026)
Online Registration Instructions for Institute Reporting PhD Admission Cycle-2 (: Dates for Institute Reporting (Online) | From 09/07/2026 to 22/07/2026
Documents required - For online reporting Scanned Copies of following documents (One set of selfattested photocopies of all the following documents will be required at the time of Joining, along with the originals for verification )
For Online reporting Scan Copies of following documents to be uploaded in Online reporting Portal of VNIT (Online reporting link: http://aimsreg.vnit.ac.in/mproaimsreg/#/ )
GATE Score Card of the candidate (if applicable, as per Brochure) for Full Time candidates.
Photo ID Proof as per Govt. of India Norms (Copy of Aadhar card is must).
Birth Certificate or Class X (SSC) Board Certificate mentioning Date of Birth.
Qualifying Exam Degree Certificate.
Mark sheets of all semester of qualifying degree of which results are declared.
Copy of Transfer Certificate
Sponsor Certificate (If Applicable) (Compulsory for Sponsored candidates)
Copy of Fee payment receipt VNIT Portal
Caste Certificate / Caste Validity (If Applicable)
Copy of 'No objection certificate' and 'relieving letter' from current employer in the format given in the brochure (For sponsored and Part Time Candidates only)
Experience certificate (For sponsored and Part Time Candidates only)
Those scholars who are eligible for institute fellowship have to submit their bank details (Mandatory) either of STATE BANK OF INDIA or CANARA BANK
VNIT Online portal link : http://aimsreg.vnit.ac.in/mproaimsreg/#/
Fees to be paid by Candidate at the time of Institute Reporting: Please refer Annexure - I
Mode of Payment - Online through VNIT PAY; Please see separate instructions. DD will not be accepted.
Date of Joining (OFFLINE)
22/07/2026 to 28/07/2026
Students are instructed to visit VNIT website www.vnit.ac.in for regular updates regarding Institute Reporting and information regarding Commencement of Classes etc.
Students have to pay complete fees.
The fees paid during Institute Reporting does not include charges for Hostel admission/ accommodation. For queries pertaining to Hostel, please contact Hostel Section on 0712-2801233 / 2801373; hostelmanager@vnit.ac.in / ar_hostel@vnit.ac.in
