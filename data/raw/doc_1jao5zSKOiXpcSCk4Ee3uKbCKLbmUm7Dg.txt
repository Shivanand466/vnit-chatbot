Important Instructions
Students should not wait (until last date as mentioned above) for receipt of scholarship or bank loan or examination result or any other reason for the purpose of registration. Procedure for online registration for Winter-2026 (For All UG & PG Program)
Instructions:
Online Registration for students will be available during the dates given above (PrOgram Wise). NO REGISTRATION WOULD BE ALLOWED AFTER THE ABOVE GIVEN LAST DATES.
General Procedure:
Pay the fees as per instructions given in a separate Notice.
Students who are not required to pay fee (under scholarship schemes) shall contact Academic Section (through email: dr_acd@vnit.ac.in).
Refer Branch wise / AdmissionYear Wise List of Courses to be offered on StudentNotice board (under "Sem. CourseRegistration" tab) of VNIT website to know courses being offered by your department. (http://vnit.ac.in/ >> Student Notice Board >> Sem. Course Registration)
Consult your Faculty Advisor (FA) to decide the courses to be registered.
Only after finalizing courses to be registered student shall proceed for online registration.
Allotment Process ofOC / HM Courses Registration (for Winter 2026), has been already done though Google Form (on First Come First Serve Basis). Those students who have applied for OC / HM Courses, are instructed to confirm their name (if selected) in the list displayed on institute website under >> (http://vnit.ac.in/>> Student Notice Board >> Sem. Course Registration >>OC-HM Allotment for Winter 2026). If student is allotted an OC or HM Course in Winter 2026, then he / she is instructed to select the remainingDC / DE course(s) accordingly Li.e. 8 Theory & 5 Prac., including OC or HM), and the OC or HMcourse will be added by Exam Section after Registration Process is completely over for Winter2026.
Online Registration module is available on URL-
https://vnitaims.vnit.ac.in/mproaims/#/
Login to AlMS using Login Id and Password (Same as used earlier)
Complete the course registration by selecting the courses as per instructions & click "ApPLY FOR REGISTRATION" button.
After applying for the course registration you can see the selected courses under Registration Management >> Reports >> Registration Form Print >> View Report. Here you can see the list of courses you have selected and the status (for fee / for registration approval). Status will be shown as "Watermark" and it will be one of the following,
"PENDING FOR FEES" "PENDING FOR ARROVAL" &
After Approval by Faculty advisor
"REGISTRATION APPROED"
All Students are required to select all backlog courses (Compulsorily) (if any), without selecting Backlog courses (if offered by the department) registration process will not move ahead. Student must keep in mind that, even after selecting backlog courses & the courses student wants to register must not exceed the standard 8 TH & 5 PR (including Backlog) criteria per semester.
Approach Faculty Advisor (FA) for Approval of Registration. FA will do Approval online. Student will have to abide by the suggestions of FA on changes in the registered courses.
Without Approval of registration by FA, student's registration will remain incomplete and student name will not appear in the roll list of the subjects he is registering for.
After approval of registration by FA, student shall login to his/her area and ensure that registration status has become "REGISTRATION APPROVED". Else, registration will be treated as incomplete & name of student will not appear in roll list.
If the backlog courses are appearing in the same slot and students are not able to select both the course (as it will show message "You Cannot Select 2 Courses in Same Slot"), then these students are instructed to report to Exam Section by Email (Given below) or in-person through their Faculty Advisor, mentioning the backlog course to be register for W26.
W26 course registration of these students will be initiated by Exam Section and remaining courses are to be added by their respective Faculty Advisor.
Student should take a print of Registration form under, Registration Management >> Reports >> Registration Form Print >> View Report. Pay Attention: Student with 1** Year (1* Sem / 2™d Sem) Courses as BacklogAll UG Students (admitted in 2024 and before) take note that:
All students intended to register the backlog (1st Year) courses for Winter 2026, are instructed to register the course(s) in the available (given) Section showing in their AlMS login. Furthermore, as per the newly implemented NEP (National Education Policy), students admitted in 2022 and after are allow to opt for "Semester Long Industry Internship" after receiving permission from respective department. These students can opt for 8 theory and 5 Practical in winter 2026 registration (subject to permission from their respective department). These students are instructed to opt their regular 8 Theory & 5 Practical during regular W-26 course Registration and then report to Exam Section (after taking registration approval from Faculty advisor) with an application and permission to register remaining courses (Courses more than 8 Th. & 5 Pr.) in AIMS.
Similarly, student opted for Minor Degree Program, are instructed to register their regular courses, and their minor course (if any) will be added in only after successful registration and final approval from their Faculty advisor.
