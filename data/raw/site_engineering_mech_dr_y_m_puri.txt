Dr. Y. M. Puri
Brief Bio
Dr. Yogesh M Puri is presently Professor in Mechanical Engineering Department at Visvesvaraya National Institute of technology (VNIT) Nagpur India. Dr Puri joined VNIT (Then VRCE) in 1998.Dr. Puri began his career in 1993 after Bachelor of Engineering (Mechanical) from Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli, Maharashtra, by joining Raymond Ltd. Saunsar, India. Dr. Puri has served NECO Industries and KEMP & Co. (Then B P Ergo, Pramal Group Company) before joining VNIT.He obtained his M. Tech and PhD from VNIT. His core of research is optimisation of WEDM and EDM process parameters, automation, metal additive manufacturing and exploring novel areas of applications of the same for customized engineering applications.He has guided 7 PhD students till date and 2 PhD students are pursuing PhD research under his guidance. He has earned 4 patents for niche work undertaken.Dr. Puri has very good networking with industry and academia locally and globally. Dr Puri is instrumental in executing 4 MOUs with industries and academia like Dassault Aviation France, CESI school of engineering, Paris, France, CIPET, Chandrapur etc.Dr Puri has published more than 25 papers in reputed journals and has published and presented more than 30 papers in various national and international conferences. Dr Puri has visited countries like Australia, USA, Italy, Hog Kong for presenting papers and for delivering lectures.
