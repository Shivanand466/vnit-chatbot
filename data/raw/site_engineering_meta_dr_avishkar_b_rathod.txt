Dr. Avishkar B. Rathod​
Assistant Professor
Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Avishkar Rathod joined the department as Assistant Professor in 2020. His reseach area of interest is to understand the iron making and steel making processes. Control the process parameters and minimize the coke consumption per tonne of hot metal. Aim to manufacture Steel. He also studies wear behavior of hardface coatings and thermal spray coatings to develop materials for industrial applications.
