Brief Bio
Dr. Atul V. Wankhade is a Professor in the Department of Chemistry at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, India, an Institute of National Importance. He obtained his Ph.D. in Chemistry from Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University, Nagpur, in 2014. Prior to joining VNIT, he served as an Assistant Professor at SMM College of Science, Nagpur, from 2003 to 2009. He is currently also serving as the Associate Dean (Examinations) at VNIT, a responsibility he has been entrusted with since January 2026.
He has been actively involved in academic, research, and professional activities and previously served as the Joint Secretary (National Body) of the Indian Society of Analytical Scientists (ISAS). He has made significant contributions to teaching and research and has successfully guided 05 Ph.D. scholars. Currently, 07 Ph.D. scholars and 01 postdoctoral fellow are pursuing their research under his supervision. In addition, he has supervised 22 postgraduate research projects, with 02 projects currently underway.His research focuses on the development of novel nanomaterials, morphology-tunable nanomaterials, and nanocomposites for energy and environmental applications. His research group has developed novel silver zirconate (Ag₂ZrO₃)-based photocatalysts, which have demonstrated promising performance for visible-light-driven photocatalytic degradation of environmental pollutants. His research also encompasses solar-driven green hydrogen generation through photocatalytic water splitting, with an emphasis on developing efficient and sustainable materials for clean energy production.
Dr. Wankhade has also engaged in international research collaboration and academic exposure through a research visit to King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), Saudi Arabia, where he interacted with leading researchers and explored advanced research facilities in the field of nanomaterials and energy-related applications. He has authored more than 60 SCI/SCOPUS-indexed research publications and holds 02 patents (Granted) to his credit. His research contributions are reflected in an h-index of 21 and an i10-index of 34, highlighting the impact of his work in the areas of nanomaterials, photocatalysis, and sustainable energy technologies.

Ø Pioneered the development and application of silver zirconate (Ag₂ZrO₃)-based nanophotocatalysts, establishing Ag₂ZrO₃ as a versatile visible-light-active material for environmental remediation, persulfate/peroxymonosulfate activation, photocatalytic water splitting and antibacterial applications.
Research Objective: Our research group aims to develop innovative and sustainable catalytic systems for solar energy utilization and environmental remediation, particularly wastewater treatment. Our research explores nanomaterials for applications such as UV/visible-light-driven photocatalytic water splitting for green Hydrogen generation, activation of persulfate/peroxymonosulfate (PS/PMS), catalytic organic transformations, antibacterial activity, CO₂ reduction, microplastic degradation, and degradation of emerging organic pollutants from simulated wastewater containing anionic and cationic dyes. The group is also working on the development of nanoenzymes for biomimetic and catalytic applications.
