ABOUT Us
Established in 1966, the Department of Applied Mechanics offers two post-graduation programs i.e. M. Tech. in Structural Engineering (STR) and Structural Dynamics & Earthquake Engineering (SDE). Currently, in India the specialized course on Structural Dynamics is available in selected institutes like IIT Roorkee, VNIT Nagpur, and NIT Silchar. The branch of structural dynamics has been started in 2008 with the mission of achieving excellence in structural and earthquake engineering education, research and professional service.
The Department has played a key role in the formulation of Indian Standard Codes of Practice (IS 1893 and IS 13920) for earthquake resistant design of structures and other special publications like GSDMA (Gujrat State Disaster Management Authority) EQ22 and EQ08. A team from the faculty of department (Structural Dynamics and Earthquake Engineering) at the institute was asked by NGO SEEDS to visit Andaman and Nicobar Islands in 2004 when a tsunami struck thereafter a massive quake in Sumatra (Indonesia), Bhuj (Gujarat) in 2001 and Nepal in 2015, aftermath of the earthquake for providing technical support to them. The Structural Dynamics Lab is equipped with three small shake tables while the Earthquake Engineering Lab is having a servo-hydraulic shake table of 3m x 3m for seismic ground motion simulation, which provides a unique facility for research and testing in central India.
Mission
The mission of the department is to achieve excellence in structural and earthquake engineering education, research and the professional service. It is endeavored to equip students to assume the leadership positions in engineering practice, education, research and serve mankind with structures designed for safety, serviceability and economy.
Vision
The Department is committed to provide post graduate academic and research programs to produce high quality human resource with ability to meet the global challenges associated with the built environment and to emerge as centre for advanced studies in the field of structural engineering.
Applied Mechanics Department is one of the oldest departments of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. Applied Mechanics Department offers Master's Degree in Structural Engineering and Structural Dynamics & Earthquake Engineering. This department is also teaching all the subjects related to the structural engineering to the undergraduate Civil engineering and Architecture students. Further, department offers Full-Time and Part-Time Ph.D. programme leading to the award of Doctoral Degree. The department has highly qualified faculty members with specialization in various Civil, Structural Engineering and Structural Dynamics disciplines. The faculty members have very good research record and they are publishing very good numbers and quality research papers in SCI/SCOPUS journals each year. Presently, department has about 26 doctoral research scholars working on the solution of technological advanced research problems.
Faculty members are also providing consultancy services to several Government and Private organisations in all the aspects of Civil and Structural Engineering. Further, department is providing testing and consultancy services in the area of water tanks, bridges, buildings and other structures. The department is actively involved in third party quality control audit and structural stability audit of various structures including railway bridges. The department is regularly organising several short-term training programs in the field of Structural Engineering. Faculty members of the department have several patents to their credit. Department has several ongoing Research and Development (R&D) projects from ISSA (DRDO), DEBEL (DRDO), Mahindra EPC Services Pvt. Ltd., National Rural Roads Development Authority, PDPL, DST-CCP SPLICE Department of Science and Technology, SERB (DST) etc. with a total budget of more than Rs. 10 million. The 13th Structural Engineering Convention (SEC-2023) was held at the Applied Mechanics Department.
Department has 12 laboratories to conduct various experiments by the students related to their course work. These laboratories are equipped with the state of art equipment, thus providing advanced knowledge to the students in the field of Structural Engineering. The department has excellent work environment wherein focus is to aspire the students to excel and improve by learning and solving real world problems through inputs from external experts too. In order to understand our strength from expert's perspective, academic audit of the offered programs is carried out at regular interval and it involved experts from IITs and industry. The department has extensively involved student community who are ready to venture and contribute in the advancing technological world. Even in the most challenging recent situation, my colleagues have achieved all milestones in time and in most appropriate manner with full reciprocation from the students of the department.

ACTIVITIES
Department of Applied Mechanics provides an environment that nurtures leaders in addition to sound engineers. Notwithstanding the tight academic schedule that students have, they manage to indulge in various extra-curricular activities. Students from our department manage host of activities ranging from inter- department competitions and national level festivals to managing facilities like messing and hostel infrastructure. These activities provide multitude of holistic improvement platforms for students to hone their technical and managerial skills, which are imperative for one to excel in his/her workplace.
Department is actively involved in various R&D projects from various Government, Public and Private Agencies. Department is pursuing research in earthquake engineering, wind, concrete corrosion, advanced engineering materials, blast, impact and structural fire engineering related activities. Department team has visited earthquake affected areas after Bhuj earthquake (2001), Andaman earthquake (2004) and Nepal earthquake (2015).
The department is also involved in third party audit and structural stability audit of various structures. Department has facilities for material testing, Proof load testing, non-destructive testing, vibration testing of buildings and bridges.
ACTIVITIES
SITE VISIT
A field visit was conducted by the department which covered multiple sites along the NH53 bypass, and a girder casting yard. Students observed live construction techniques, design practices, and quality control processes, gaining practical insights into bridge components and seismic safety provisions. The visit effectively bridged the gap between theoretical learning and real world engineering applications.
MULTISTORIED BUILDING EXAM
This year a very effective and industry-oriented exam pattern was adopted for the course of Multistoried Buildings. There was a 2 Day Modelling and Designing examination in which every student was given a different architectural plan of a G+10 storey. Students had completed the model and developed the schedule of beams, columns and slabs for the given floor. This was to give the emerging engineers a hands-on experience of the industry.
TECHNICAL AND NON TECHNICAL SEMINARS
The department conducted a series of technical and non-technical seminars aimed at enhancing both academic knowledge and personal development. These sessions included expert talks on industryrelevant technologies as well as workshops on soft skills, leadership, and career guidance.
