Dr. Rajesh K. Khatirkar​
Professor
Brief
Bio bio
– Dr. Rajesh K. Khatirkar is Professor in Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering , Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur. His areas of interest are Microstructure and Texture development in materials, Electron Microscopy, Diffraction Analysis of Materials, Electron Backscattered Diffraction, and Additive Manufacturing.
– He has worked extensively on various types of steels, aluminium and titanium alloys. His current areas of interest are development of biomaterials for wound healing application, development of coatings for biomedical applications, alloy design by using machine learning and artificial intelligience.
– He did his Ph.D from Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB) under the guidance of Prof. Indradev Samajdar. During his Ph.D, he worked on Low Carbon Steels.
– He is working in VNIT Nagpur from 2006, developed micromechanical testing laboratory, purchased rolling mill (under FIST, DST), EBSD and other related equipments for studying the microstructures and mechanical properties of materials at various length scales.
