HOSTEL ADMISSION INSTRUCTIONS FOR 1ST YEAR M.TECH. AND 1ST YEAR M.SC. STUDENTS ADMITTED IN 2025
NOTICE:
01.08.2025
M.Tech. and M.Sc. students admitted in the admission year 2025 are hereby instructed to follow the procedure given below for Hostel Admission.
All students should note that the rooms will be allotted on first come first serve basis. Number of rooms are limited in all hostels. Students have to make their own accommodation arrangements if there is no vacancy in hostels.
