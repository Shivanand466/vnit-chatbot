Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Rajendra Patrikar is working presently as Professor at VNIT Nagpur at Center of VLSI and Nanotechnollogy. He has done his M.Tech in Electrical Engineering from IIT Bombay. He joined as Research engineer in the Microelectronics Project at in Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay after that. He also completed his Ph.D from the Department of Electrical Engineering IIT Bombay. He joined as faculty at IIT Bombay after working for an year at Computervision R&D Pune. Later he moved to Singapore to work in TECH Semiconductor (Now Micron) in Advance Device Technology Department. After working there for three years he moved to Institute of High Performance Computing , Singapore which is a supercomputing center, where he initiated work in the area of nanoelectronics and worked for three years. He was a Team Leader of Nanotechnology group at Computational Research Laboratory Pune (Super computing center by TATAs) for one Year. He has published about 45 papers in International Journals and 80 papers in international refereed conferences and 20 papers in national conferences.. He is a Fellow of IETE, Life member of ISSS, a senior member of IEEE and Member of SPIE. He has obtained project grants from various agencies and he is the coordinator of various projects, including projects sponsored by Ministry of Communication and Information Technology (MCIT) , Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA), Board of research in Nuclear Science (BRNS), Rajiv Gandhi Science and Technology Centre RGSTC). He is also part of a team responsible for starting incubation of technology companies at VNIT and heading a project on Incubation center. Recently he has edited a book MEMS Resonator Filters published by The Institute of engineering and technology (IET).
