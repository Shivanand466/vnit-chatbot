Important Instructions
All students under Minor Degree Program are instructed to opt "OC / HM" Course of Slot "A" only, as all Minor courses are going to be offered compulsorily in Slot "B".
List of a new OC course offered for Winter 2026 is enclosed with this notice.
Registration for these courses will be done on First cum First Serve Basis via Google Form. There are separate Google Forms for each course (details would be notified along with this Note).
Students have to register on Google Form using VNIT email ID only (xxxxx@students.vnit.ac.in). Request through email ID other than VNIT email ID is liable for rejection.
For every course maximum number of students to be allowed is mentioned against individual course.
Registration for the above-mentioned courses via Google form shall be final and will not be changed/modified further.
Registration of these courses is not linked with the payment of fee.
A student can register for maximum ONE OC or ONE HM course, in a semester.
Registration done for OC or HM or IE type course shall not be changed or cancelled in any circumstances.
If it is found that, a student has registered in 2 or more OC/HM courses, his / her name will be deleted from all the OC/HM courses. Requests for consideration in such cases will not be entertained.
AWR Students / Extra Year students are also instructed to follow the above procedure. Prevailing practice of allocating OC / HM courses at the time to registration to such students is discontinued. Request for allocation / allotment of OC / HM courses at the time of AWR Registration will not be entertained.
A student can earn maximum 12 credits for OC & maximum 6 credits for HM (Excluding 5 credits of 1s Year HM / HU Courses) during his/her program of study, however total credit can be earned for OC + HM Courses should not be exceeded 12 Credits (during the complete duration of course).
No offline requests for these OC or HM courses will be is taken into consideration.
