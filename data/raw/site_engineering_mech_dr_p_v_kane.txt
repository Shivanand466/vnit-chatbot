Dr. P. V. Kane
Brief Bio
Dr. Prasad V. Kane is B.E., M.Tech (Productionn Engg) from RTM Nagpur University and Ph. D. from VNIT, Nagpur, India. He is
Currently working as Assistant Professor in Department of Mechanical Engineering at
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur. He has work experience of Twenty Six years years in teaching and two years in Industry. His research interest areas are Machine Condition
Monitoring, Quality, Reliability and Maintenance Engineering. He worked as supervisor for PhD , PG and UG Projects
