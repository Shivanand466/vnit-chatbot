Document: Infosys Foundation Stem Stars Scholarship Program 2024-25 (dated 2024-10-07)
Scholarship of up to INR 1 lakh per year for female students from underprivileged families
Requesting support for circulation of this message
Dear Sir/Madam ,
Greetings of the day!
We are pleased to share that applications for the Infosys Foundation STEM Stars Scholarship Program 2024-25 are now open for female students in India who aspire to pursue undergraduate degrees in STEM courses (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics), Medical Sciences (MBBS), Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS), Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm).
Sharing more program details below for your reference:
Eligibility:
Applicants must be female students who are citizens of India.
Students must be enrolled in the
First year of a 3 year B.Sc Degree (Courses such as Bio-chemistry, Physics, Chemistry, Nursing, Geography, IT, Biology, Computer Science, Forensic Science, etc.)
First or 2nd year of B.Tech, MBBS, B.Pharm, BDS, Integrated Masters, Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech)
Students must be enrolled in the first year of their 4-year undergraduate course in engineering, medicine (MBBS), and other related STEM fields at institutes of repute listed in the top NIRF ranking for 2023 and 2024
Refer NIRF Top 100 Engineering Colleges: Refer 2024 List, Refer 2023 List
Refer NIRF Top 50 Medical Colleges 2024, Refer NIRF Top 50 Medical Colleges 2023 o
Refer NIRF List of Top 100 Pharmacy Colleges 2024 o
Refer NIRF List of Top 100 Pharmacy Colleges 2023 o
Refer NIRF List of Top 40 Dental Colleges 2024 o
Refer NIRF List of Top 40 Dental Colleges 2023 o
Refer NIRF List of Top 100 Universities 2024 o
Refer NIRF List of Top 100 Universities 2023 o
Candidates must have obtained admission into the identified colleges and completed their Class 12.
Applicants must have an annual family income of less than or equal to ₹8 lakh.
Candidates must not have availed other scholarships for the same expenses.
Renewal scholarship eligibility condition:
Awarded scholars must fulfil the below-specified academic criteria for continuation of scholarship throughout the course duration:
A minimum CGPA of 7 in engineering and related courses until course completion.
A pass qualification in all subjects for the year in MBBS for continuous enrolment until course completion.
Scholarship: The selected candidates will receive a scholarship of up to ₹1 lakh per annum, covering tuition, living expenses, and study materials for the duration of the course against paid receipts.
Application Deadline:
October 15, 2024
