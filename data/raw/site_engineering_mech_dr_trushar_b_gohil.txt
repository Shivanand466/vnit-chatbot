Dr. Trushar B. Gohil
Brief Bio
Dr. Trushar B. Gohil, a distinguished scholar with a Ph.D. from IIT Kanpur, is an expert in Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and Artificial Intelligence (AI). He is pioneering novel AI architectures for rapid CFD predictions. With 8 years of industrial experience and 12 years of teaching experience, Dr. Gohil has guided one Ph.D. student to completion and is currently supervising three others. His prolific research output includes 23 journal papers and 50 conference papers. He has successfully completed an R&D project and has recently launched a new online M.Tech program in Computational Fluid Thermal Science.
