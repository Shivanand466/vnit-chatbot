NOTICE
Subject: AWR Registration – Category B (W-26 Session)
All concerned students are hereby informed about the guidelines for AWR - Category B for the Winter 2026 (W-26) Session.
Students falling under any of the following categories will be considered in AWR Category B:
Students having 8 or more backlogs (including practical courses) up to RS-26 Exam.
B.Tech. students with more than 6 years of stay in the program
B.Arch. students with more than 7 years of stay in the program
Students who have earned less than 10 credits in the last two consecutive semesters
As per Academic Rule No. 5.3, such students are liable for termination of admission. However, they may apply for continuation of their admission by submitting a request, which will be reviewed by the Academic Performance and Progress Evaluation Committee (APPEC). Based on APPEC's recommendation, permission to register for the W-26 session may be granted.
All UG / PG students falling in any of the categories (1 to 4) must submit an application along with the AWR Form in person to the Examination Section. The submission must be made only by the student only. The form must be duly signed and commented upon by the Faculty Advisor and Head of Department (HoD).
Note: Applications submitted via email or WhatsApp or by Dak from the respective department or by Post will not be considered in any case.
IMPORTANT NOTE: Students must report to the Associate Dean (Examination) along with their parent(s) at the time of submission of AWR Form. Last date for submission of application is June 20, 2026. Presence of parents is compulsory at the time of AWR Form Submission.
Documents Required:
Formal application
Duly filled AWR Form
Grade Card (up to the RS-26 examination attended)
Undertaking (attached with the AWR Form)
Dean (Academics)
