NOTICE
Subject : Inviting Applications ( fresh and renewal ) under various scheme on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP 2.0) for the financial year 2025-26.
As per the Government of India, various Ministries are inviting application for various schemes on National Scholarship Portal i.e. NSP (www.scholarships.gov.in) run by respective ministries.
The National Scholarship Portal is now open for registration. The closing date of Application Registration by students and other details of the scholarship schemes is given on the NSP portal.
All the fresh and higher-semester students should immediately visit National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at the site www.scholarships.gov.in and submit information online for the award of financial assistance under this scheme. Students are advised to read carefully the guidelines & instructions given on the site, before submission of the application form online. (students of batches 2022-23 UG, 2023-24 , 2024-25 (UG/ PG)-Renewal batch and 2025-26 (UG/ PG)-Fresh applicants.)
Students of First-year B. Tech., M.Tech., and M. Sc. programs must register as new applicants on www.scholarships.gov.in as per the applicable scheme.
Students of Second year to Final year have to Renew Scholarship scheme with their already generated login.
After final submission students' have to submit the hard copy of the form along with the uploaded documents on NSP portal to Academic section
Apart from the uploaded document list of documents to be submitted to Academic section:
Print of the Final submitted form duly signed.
Institute Bonafide certificate, to be obtained from Academic section.
Fees receipts (Academic & Hostel fees)
For Fresh applicants - Class XII mark sheet along with JEE score card (for UG students) and Last qualified exam marksheet (for PG students) is to be uploaded on NSP portal & submitted in Academic section. Whereas for students applying for Renewalshould upload & Submit copy of Grade-card of last semester available in students' login (AIIMS LOGIN).
Institute shall further verify the documents and process the documents for online approval to the.
Online submission of documents by the student is essential for institute approval. Students who fail to submit online submission of documents shall not be considered for the scholarship scheme and in case if any student is found to have not submitted online information, fees shall be collected from the student concerned for the period of study and their further semester course registration will be withheld.
Institute will only verify the documents and process the application for online approval to the Ministries.
Students are requested to submit the online application along with required self-attested documents (uploaded during registration) to Mrs. Mugdha Lamsoge, Sr. Superintendent, Academics, positively prior 10 days before scheme closing date.
As per NSP guidelines, students must submit a hard copy of the application along with all required supporting documents. Please note that failure to do so will result in your application not being verified by the Institute .
