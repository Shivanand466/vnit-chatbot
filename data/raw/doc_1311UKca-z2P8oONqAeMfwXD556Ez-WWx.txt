Interaction with International Faculty
Dr. Swatantra Kumar Shrivastava, a distinguished expert in the field, was the primary guest faculty for this course. His insightful lectures were instrumental in providing participants with a comprehensive understanding of critical topics.
On Day 1, Dr. Shrivastava delivered a lecture introducing the course and participants to the impact of climate change, carbon dioxide management, and life cycle analysis. His ability to distill complex concepts into accessible explanations ensured an engaging start to the program.
On subsequent days, Dr. Shrivastava covered essential topics such as stoichiometric and engineering calculations, process safety, and energy technologies, with a special focus on hydrogen plant design and the hydrogen economy. His lecture on energy transition and sustainable technologies highlighted innovative solutions to modern energy challenges, emphasizing practical applications. The sessions were enriched with real-world examples, case studies, and open discussions, making the content relevant and thought-provoking.
Dr. Shrivastava also conducted a significant number of interactive tutorials, particularly on project execution, plant economics, and sustainability. On the final day, his sessions addressed project management and engineering skills, including practical insights on freelancing and start-ups. His ability to engage participants in critical thinking exercises and provide hands-on guidance on project deliverables was highly appreciated. His involvement made a lasting impression on the attendees, leaving them equipped with actionable insights and strategies for their professional growth.
Interaction of Host Faculty
The host faculty from VNIT Nagpur played a crucial role in ensuring the program's success. Lectures delivered by the host faculty, such as Dr. Shende, covered key topics in heat transfer, integration, and stoichiometric calculations. Dr. Shende's expertise in engineering calculations and process safety provided a strong foundation for participants, complementing the insights shared by Dr. Shrivastava.
Moreover, the faculty ensured seamless coordination of the program by managing logistics, organizing engaging interactive sessions, and providing technical support. Their dedication to participant learning was evident in the well-structured schedule and their active involvement in discussions, feedback sessions, and Q&A forums.
Interaction of other faculty from VNIT, Nagpur
Dr. Wasewar, Dr. Kalpana Yeole, Dr. Kanti Kumar Athankar and other faculty members conducted group activities and tutorials, focusing on sustainability and project industrialization. These sessions offered a collaborative platform for participants to apply theoretical concepts to practical scenarios, fostering teamwork and critical analysis. The host faculty also facilitated discussions on modern engineering challenges, ensuring participants could explore diverse perspectives.
