CIWG Quota - Fees for CIWG students will be at par with Resident Indians as per DASA 2016 brochure. Any Revision in Tuition Fees for Resident Indians as per Govt of India MHRD shall be applicable to CIWG students seeking admission.
In addition to above fees, Convocation fees of US$ 58 and Thesis/Dissertation evaluation fees of US$ 570 for M Tech and US$ 86 for B Arch shall be charged in final semester of program. Hostel charges are separate.
NOTE - Total fees to be paid at the time of admission reporting includes One Time Fees and Fees of One Semester. An initial fee paid at the Counseling is adjusted against the total fees to be paid during admission reporting. At the Institute reporting, fees will have to be paid in INR in equivalent of USD rate. DD of USD amount will not be accepted.
