Dr. Aditi Roy
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Currently working as an Assistant Professor at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur (VNIT) in the department of Computer Science and Engineering since September 2026. Before joining VNIT, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, working on hardware and embedded system security. My research focuses on hardware security, particularly side-channel analysis, masked cryptographic implementations, and secure RISC-V SoC design, with FPGA platforms for prototyping and evaluation. I am also interested in the efficient and secure implementation of AI/ML models on hardware, from FPGA accelerators to resource-constrained edge devices. More broadly, I work across core areas of computer science, including computer architecture, digital design, and operating systems.
