Brief Bio
Dr. Maithili Barahate-Paikane is an Assistant Professor in the Department of  Humanities and Social Sciences, Visvesvaraya National Institute & Technology, Nagpur. A Gold Medalist and Doctorate in English Literature, she acquired her PhD degree for research work in the area of Indo-British Literature.She has a rich teaching experience of 25 years in the academic field. She has an expertise in the field of Communication Skills, Language teaching, Medical Humanities, Translation and Film Studies.
