Dr. Anil Kumar​
Assistant Professor
Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Anil Kumar is an Assistant Professor in Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, VNIT Nagpur from 2022. He pursued B.Tech. (2014) in Metallurgical Engineering from NIT Srinagar, M.Tech. (2016) in Materials Science and Engineering from IIT Patna and Ph.D. (2021) in Metllurgical and Materials Engineering from IIT Kharagpur. He has authored in more than 20 research articles published in journals and conferences. He is a recepiant of ‘National Post-Doctoral fellowship (N-PDF 2021)’ by Science and Engineering Research Board (SERB) India. His research area includes- Wear & Tribology, Corrosion, Thermal Spraying Coatings, Metallic glass coating, Nanomechanical properties, Composite Materials, Biodegradable Plastic.
