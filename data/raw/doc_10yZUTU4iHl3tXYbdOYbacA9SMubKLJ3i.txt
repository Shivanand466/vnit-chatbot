All scholars who are allotted Seat in VNIT for MBA in ACM program for Academic Year 2026 are required to report at VNIT, Nagpur as per the schedule given. For reporting to VNIT, scholars are required to fill the details on VNIT online admission portal and complete the reporting procedure. Following are the steps to complete the reporting procedure:
STEP 1:
Log in on VNIT online admission portal (link https://aimsreg.vnit.ac.in/mproaimsreg/#/ )
To avoid technical difficulties while filling the information on VNIT online portal, Candidates are advised to clear the Browser History every time before logging to VNIT online admission portal.
Fill all the details on VNIT online admission portal and upload photo, signature and the documents as listed below.
PLEASE NOTE: Select Fees category as 'GENERAL(OPEN/ OBC)'
only
Scholars are requested to go through the FAQs before logging on online portal.
(FAQ Link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/13iCIsDXpKABLVUItoCV2bx7lsRsC6jyn/view
STEP 2 : Procedure for payment:
Re-login student registration portal after completing Step 1 with the Registration ID (viz. 2026*****)
Amount to be paid will appear (please select correct fee category carefully. Once selected fees category will not be modified)
Make the payment through payment gateway.
Download the receipt generated after payment for future reference.
STEP 3: After completely filling up the information on VNIT online admission portal, Scholar need to email the signed copy of the submitted form on the email acadsection@vnit.ac.in acknowledging the completion of the process.
STEP 4: Once the email is received, documents and other details of the candidate will be verified by the VNIT officials and Provisional Reporting Letter would be issued to the scholar. This completes the Physical reporting of the candidate.
