The Metallurgical and Materials Engineering Department is remarkably proud to announce the awardees of the Indian
Institute of Metals
(IIM) Students Prize. Our heartfelt congratulations to Poojitha, Vidyaadhari, and Vinayak! They will be awarded the IIM Prize on 14th November 2021. The prize is awarded every year to the top 3 rankers of the department.
Poojitha on her achievement:
I am immensely obligated for the award given by the Indian Institute of Metals. It is a great feeling for me to be chosen as a deserving person for getting this recognition. I want to express my earnest thanks to Prof. Dr. D.R.Peshwe, Prof. Dr. Jatin Bhatt, and Prof. Dr. Khatirkar. I enjoyed every minute of their lectures and I am sure their teachings will help me to achieve success in the future as well. I also humbly thank all the Professors for their priceless contribution and support.
Vidyaadhari on her achievement:
I am very much thankful for the guidance provided by my Professors. The curiosity which the professors incited in me further guided me to pursue my M.Tech in Materials Engineering. The B.Tech journey at VNIT has been great with lots of challenges. I am very grateful to my family and friends for being the constant source of motivation all this while. Last but not the least, I would also like to thank IIM for considering me for the award.
Vinayak on his achievement:
Firstly, I would like to thank IIM for considering me worthy of this prize. Talking about my 4-year journey at VNIT, I’ve learned a lot throughout this journey, and it has helped me grow a lot as a person in my life. I feel lucky that I could grab the opportunities that came my way and do some great internships and project work. I feel that my aims and goals have always motivated me to do good in my academics and which have helped me in my career till date. Lastly, I would like to thank all of my professors who have taught me various courses all these 4 years and showered their wisdom upon me. Thank you!
