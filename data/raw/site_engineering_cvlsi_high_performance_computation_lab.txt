High Performance Computation Cluster
Cluster of 1 Head node,  10 rack mount compute nodes, 5 tower model compute nodes.     Head node with 2 CPUs Intel Nehalem, 24 GB DDR3, RAIDS, 256 MB cache,  4 x 300 GB SAS HDD, each compute node with 2 CPUs Intel Nehalem, 24 GB DDR3, 1TB SATA storage.
