Report on Conduct of GIAN Course
Title of the course: Electric Power Distribution Systems: Meeting New Challenges with Sustainable Solutions
Date:
10-02-2025 to 15-02-2025
Number of Participants:
39 (outside participants: 23; VNIT participants: 16)
Total number of lectures in the course:
14 (lecture) + 05 (Tutorial)
Number of lectures by International Faculty:
09 (lecture) + 04 (Tutorial)
Number of lectures by Host Faculty:
05 (lecture) + 01 (Tutorial)
Prof. Alberto Borghetti (IEEE Fellow), Professor, Bologna University, Italy, arrived at VNIT guest house on 6 th Feb 2025. Both the host faculty interacted with him during his stay at VNIT and discussed related to the ongoing course as well as planning for future research collaboration. Joint publication through the collaborative research work was also discussed. It was altogether a very fruitful interaction. Other than technical discussion and interactions, Prof. Alberto also has a good exchange of cultural information through visits to nearby temples and Gandhi Ashram.
An official meeting was arranged separately with the department faculty members on 14 th Feb afternoon, besides one-to-one talks with different faculty members from various departments. The interactions include various technical discussions and the future scope of collaborative research. Also, a few non-technical get-togethers with different faculty members took place to make his stay comfortable.
A total of 08 faculty members attended the course. Almost all of them showed keen interest in some kind of collaboration with Prof. Alberto, either by inviting him to some webinar or as keynote speaker in various events or collaborative research activities.
Dr. Arghya Mitra & Dr. Soumyabrata Das Course Coordinators
