Dr. Arijit Das​
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Arijit Das currently holds the position of Assistant Professor at the Chemical Engineering Department in Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur. He has 8+ years of academic experience. Dr. Das has been working in the field of “Advance Separation Processes” for the past few years, primarily researching on advance oxidation processes, nanostructured photocatalysts, natural products extraction, modelling and process optimization.
