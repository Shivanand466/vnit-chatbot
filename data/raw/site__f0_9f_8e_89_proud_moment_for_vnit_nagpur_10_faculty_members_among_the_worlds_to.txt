🎉 Proud Moment for VNIT Nagpur! | 10 Faculty Members Among the World’s Top 2% Scientists – 2025 🌍🔬 vnit_web
October 15, 2026
News
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, proudly celebrates the achievement of 10 faculty members recognized among the World’s Top 2% Scientists for 2025, as listed in the prestigious ranking published by Stanford University, USA.
🏆 Recognized Faculty Members and Their Research Areas
Prof. Babasaheb Raghunath Sankapal, Deputy Director – Materials | Department of Physics
Prof. Kailas Lachchhuram Wasewar – Chemical Engineering | Department of Chemical Engineering
Prof. Makarand Sudhakar Ballal – Electrical & Electronic Engineering | Department of Electrical Engineering
Prof. Sachin A. Mandavgane – Chemical Engineering | Department of Chemical Engineering
Prof. S. S. Sonawane – Chemical Engineering | Department of Chemical Engineering
Prof. Anjan Patel – Geological & Geomatics Engineering | Department of Civil Engineering
Prof. Manmohan Dass Goel – Materials | Department of Applied Mechanics
Dr. Pradip Roul – Numerical & Computational Mathematics | Department of Mathematics
Dr. Sukanta Roga – Energy | Department of Mechanical Engineering
Dr. Arvind Kumar – Networking & Telecommunications | Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering
This distinguished recognition highlights VNIT Nagpur’s strong research culture, scientific innovation, and commitment to academic excellence across diverse disciplines. The faculty members’ contributions continue to advance knowledge and address contemporary scientific and technological challenges.
👏 VNIT Nagpur congratulates all the recognized faculty members on this remarkable achievement and wishes them continued success in their research endeavours!
