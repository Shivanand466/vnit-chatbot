How to reach VNIT
From Railway Station
It takes1 7 minutes to travel from Nagpur Railway Station to Vnit Nagpur. Approximate driving distance between Nagpur Railway Station and Vnit Nagpur is 5.9 kms or 3.7 miles or 3.2 nautical miles . Travel time refers to the time taken if the distance is covered by a car.
