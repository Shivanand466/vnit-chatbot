6. Student Functionalities
Login Procedure
Students log in using institute credentials.
Select appropriate Academic Year (2024-25) and Session (S25) .
Note: Google Chrome is the compatible browser for the student council election module. Students are advised to use the same browser only for the election process (for filing Nomination and voting).
If dark mode is enabled, change it to the light mode in setting. The setting can be found in the right top corner. Click on the photo icon in the right top corner and choose setting from dropdown.
Nomination Master
Path: Election Management → Master → Nomination Master
Functions:
o View election events and available posts
o Check own eligibility (CGPA, hostel status, program type)
o Upload required documents for nomination
o Submit nomination forms online
Admins can also update student documents submitted during nomination filter.

Voting Master
Note: The system is tested for use on the Google Chrome browser only . If the AIMS portal is in Dark Mode, please switch to Light Mode for better visibility and functionality.
Path: Election Management → Master → Voting Master
Functions:
o View available posts and list of nominees
o Cast vote per post
o Submit vote securely within the election window
o NOTA (None of the Above) option available if enabled
Nomination Document Details

Path: Election Management → Master → Nomination Document Details
· Functions:
o View nomination details for each post

7. Email Notifications
Automated email notifications are sent to students upon nomination submission and filtering.
Email content includes:
o Nomination Mail : Confirms application submission.
o Selection Mail : Confirms nomination acceptance.
o Rejection Mail : Informs of rejection with reasons, if configured.
7. Conclusion
The Election Management System is a comprehensive platform for conducting digital elections within VNIT. This user manual is intended to facilitate smooth usage of the platform by both Election Officers and Students.
For any technical assistance, please contact the system administrator or Micropro support team.
