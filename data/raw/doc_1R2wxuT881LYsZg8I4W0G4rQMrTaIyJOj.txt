CLUB REPORT
Iridescence Creative Club, VNIT Nagpur, conducted the following events in the session of 2024-25:
Art Competition on The 78th Independence Day
15/08/2024
To celebrate patriotism and creativity, the Iridescence Club organized an Art Competition on the 78th Independence Day. The event aimed to unite art enthusiasts and provide a platform for students to express their love for the nation through their creativity.
The competition's theme, 'CAN YOU PAINT FREEDOM?' , encouraged participants to depict India's rich culture, history, and progress from their unique perspectives. Students had complete artistic freedom, using various mediums, from traditional sketches and paintings to digital art, resulting in a diverse and vibrant collection of entries.
Enthusiastic students from all years participated, bringing their imagination and skills to life. Their artwork ranged from detailed portraits of freedom fighters to abstract representations of India's unity in diversity. Each piece reflected deep thought, artistic excellence, and patriotic pride.
The event was a resounding success, seamlessly blending art and patriotism. It not only showcased students' incredible talents but also served as a powerful reminder of the values and spirit that define our nation. The competition left a lasting impact, inspiring everyone to celebrate freedom through creativity.

IRIDESCENCE CREATIVE CLUB
● Ganesh Idol Making Workshop
01/09/2024
The Iridescence Club's Ganpati Modelling Workshop was a massive success, with over 100 enthusiastic students coming together to create beautiful idols of Lord Ganesha. Working in groups, they showcased incredible creativity and teamwork.
To make the event even more special, we invited Ms. Kalode , a talented local artisan specializing in hand-painted sarees and handmade idols. She guided students with her expertise and shared inspiring stories about the art's cultural significance, making the experience more meaningful.
The workshop was filled with excitement, laughter, and a strong sense of togetherness. Students experimented with designs, crafting impressive idols that reflected both skill and devotion. More than just an art session, it was a celebration of tradition and festive spirit.
Participants proudly took their idols home for Ganesh Chaturthi, carrying with them not just their creations but also a deeper appreciation for art and culture. The event truly united creativity, tradition, and community.

IRIDESCENCE CREATIVE CLUB
● First Year's Orientation 11/09/2024
The Iridescence Club's Orientation Programme was a lively and welcoming event designed to introduce new members, foster creativity, and build connections. Freshers got an insight into the club's history, goals, and achievements, helping them understand its journey and opportunities.
To create a friendly atmosphere, first-year students introduced themselves, sharing their artistic interests and aspirations. A group activity allowed them to showcase their talents in various art forms, both digital and traditional, displaying an incredible range of creativity.
Club members then outlined exciting plans for the year, including workshops, competitions, and collaborative projects. They encouraged active participation and even leadership opportunities. A Q&A session followed, addressing any doubts and strengthening the bond between new and existing members.
The event was a great success, filled with enthusiasm and inspiration. It set the stage for a vibrant year ahead, uniting a passionate community of artists ready to create, collaborate, and grow together. The Iridescence Club is all set for an amazing journey ahead!

IRIDESCENCE CREATIVE CLUB
● TARANG -Fresher's Day 19/10/2024
TARANG-The Fresher's Day celebration was a grand event filled with excitement, creativity, and talent. To showcase the artistic excellence of the Iridescence Club, First year students performed a breathtaking live sand art of Cristiano Ronaldo, mesmerizing the audience with intricate details and transitions. The performance not only highlighted the club's creativity but also introduced freshers to the artistic talent and opportunities within our community. The audience was captivated, and the appreciation we received was overwhelming. The event successfully welcomed the new batch, inspiring them to explore their own creativity and be part of the Iridescence Club's journey.
