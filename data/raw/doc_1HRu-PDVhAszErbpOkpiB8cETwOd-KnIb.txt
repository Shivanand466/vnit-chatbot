FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS FOR FEE REMISSION APPLICATIONS
1. My father is not earning member in my family then what should I submit & in father's Income Certificate* Ans -Upload &Submit original no Income affidavit on Rs. 100/- stamp paper in the prescribed
Format.
2. My mother is not earning member in my family then what should I submit & in mother's Income Certificate? Ans - Upload & Submit original no Income affidavit on 100 Rs - stamp paper in the prescribed
Format.
3. My father is no more then what should I submit & in Father's Income Certificate?
Ans -Mention NIL in the Colum of Fathers Income, Upload & Submit a copy fathers's Certificate along with the application form.
4. My mother is no more then what should I submit & inmother's Income Certificate? Ans - Mention NIL in the Colum of Mothers Income and submit the copy of mother's death Certificate along with the application form.
My Brother/Sister is an earning member in my family his/her income will be included in my
family income. Ans - Yes under the head income of other family member.
6. Do I need to submit the income certificate of Brother/ Sister?
Ans - Yes to be uploaded and submitted under other family members income
7. Is it possible to submit google form for Fee Remission after last date? Ans - No. you cannot submit it.
8. Is Fee Remission applicable belongs to OpenPwD/ OBCPwD/SC/ST students?
Ans - No. Since Open PwD/ OBC PwD/SC/ST students are already in Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme.
Is Fee Remission applicable to Open/OBC students who cancelled admission from the Institute? Ans - No. Fee Remission is not applicable to Open/OBC students who cancelled admission from the Institute.
10. Can I apply for Fee Remission as I already applied or beneficiary for Home State/Other State/Private scholarship?
Ans- No, you cannot apply for Fee Remission.
11. What should I upload and submit document if mother & father divorced? Ans-Submit & upload divorce certificate.
12. Is income certificate valid as authorized by Gram Sarpanch or Gram Panchayat Karyalaya? Govt.
Ans - No. Income Certificate should be signed and sealed by Tahsildar/State Authority/Central Govt. Authority/District Revenue Office/Office of District Collector.
13.Can I make the changes in the information after submission of Google Form?
Ans - No, changes in the information after submission are not permitted.
