About Us.
Department of Mechanical Engineering is the oldest department started from the inception of the institute (1960). Over the last Six decades, we have grown our expertise and competence in the core Mechanical Engineering curriculum and research. The Department has one UG program (B. Tech in Mechanical Engineering), four PG programs (M. Tech in Production & Industrial Engineering, CAD/CAM and Heat Power, BME). The department also offers the PhD program. The department is devoted to quality teaching, outstanding research and development activities with its highly qualified 30 faculty members. Department has shown significant contribution in research in the domains like Condition Monitoring, CFD, solar energy, advanced machining, robotics and bio-medical engineering.
The Mechanical Department at VNIT has been awarded research projects totaling INR 2.03 crore under various funding schemes.Department has 30+ patents and 1000+ publications. Three technologies have been successfully transferred to industries. The department organizes training programs/workshops at regular intervals. In the recent past, department has hosted 38th National Convention of Mechanical Engineers, one of the reputed conferences in India. An international Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is recently signed between Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, INDIA and CESI Graduate School of Engineering, Paris, FRANCE.
Mission.
To produce quality human resource of high standard in mechanical engineering who can contribute favorably to the technological and socio-economic development of the nation.
Vision.
To develop state of the art facilities related to mechanical engineering. To attract highly qualified faculty to the mechanical engineering department. To promote participation of industries in academic research and consultancy. To undertake research at regional and national level.
FROM THE HOD'S DESK:
DR. A.B. ANDHARE Head Of Mechanical Department VNIT Nagpur
The department of Mechanical Engineering is one of the prominent departments of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology known for its own way of standalone teaching. The department with its quality in provision of education nurtured many students to their desired professions. It even gives its best to produce students who wish to pursue Masters and Doctorate. Apart from the academics, the department also encourages students to excel in other extra- curricular and Co- curricular activities. Many national and international conferences have been organized by the department and got appreciated for the way they have been held and the quality of content they provided for the audience. VNIT stands at 6th position among all NITs with an overall NIRF rank of 39.
The students of the department always strive to put efforts in making it the best of all the other standing departments. Not only in academics, but also in other extra-curricular activities they try hard to achieve the top most position. They even manage and organize college related festivals, which help them in their future to handle such things relatively to that situation. They even actively participate with the same enthusiasm in the conferences and workshops that are held frequently by the faculty in various domains of the department.
The faculty of the department is quite special where everyone possess a Doctorate degree in one of the domain of Mechanical Engineering which makes it so special among all the other ones. The former director of the institute was also a Mechanical department professor. All the professors have worked for many R&D projects and often share their experience regarding them besides providing students the best quality of knowledge that they already possess. Keeping this high profile, the students also work hard and smart in making themselves and the department stand tall among the others. Discipline is also one of them where they maintain and often surprise their peers from other institutes. The students have full knowledge and thoughts on how to maintain high profile wherever they try to work. Since last 3 years, institute permits our PG students to undertake 1 full year industry oriented projects or internships.
