At the start of the semester, we continued the work on the vehicle built by our senior batch and completed it to make it fully functional for operation.

3) Solar Charger for vehicle
Using the club's existing solar panels, we aim to develop a solar charger incorporating an MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking) charge controller. This charger will be used to charge our vehicle, promoting renewable energy adoption.
As the next step, we will design and implement a converter for this charger to ensure a constant and smooth power supply.
Additionally, we plan to establish a solar charging station in front of the club's premises as an extension of this project.
-Required funds - Rs20,000
4) Battery Management system
We will design a detailed plan for a Battery Management System (BMS) and a battery pack with a configuration of 72V, 42Ah for our new Electric Vehicle (EV).
This includes studying various sensors and auxiliaries essential for an efficient BMS.
required funds - Rs 45000/-
5) Energy from Mechanical Vibrations
We will explore methods to harness energy from mechanical vibrations using the piezoelectric effect and linear generator mechanisms.
The focus will be on extracting energy from sources like railway platform vibrations and movements of trees.
-Required funds - Rs 15,000
6) Delivery drone
Drone is designed to autonomously transport small packages over short to medium distances, optimizing delivery times and reducing human labor. The team is focusing on ensuring high efficiency, safety, and precision in flight, incorporating advanced technologies such as GPS navigation, obstacle avoidance, and real-time communication Systems.
Required funds - Rs 20,000/-
· Workshops: -
1) SolidWorks workshop :
On 19th October 2024, we conducted a SolidWorks workshop for second-year students.
During this 2-hour session, participants were introduced to CAD software and its applications, with a focus on designing EV components.
2) Fusion workshop:
We have scheduled this workshop for January 11, 2025, aimed at first-year students to provide them with a basic understanding of CAD software. Through a combination of hands-on activities, insightful discussions, and guided projects, students will explore design software.
3) MATLAB workshop:
This workshop is scheduled for January 18, 2025, and is designed for first-year students to introduce them to MATLAB. Participants will have the opportunity to enhance their technical skills by working on hands-on projects, learning how to design, simulate, and analyze circuits .
4) PCB design Workshop:
Scheduled for the beginning of March, this workshop is aimed at providing participants with essential knowledge and skills in circuit design using software and transferring those designs to PCB boards. Key topics covered will include the basics of PCB design, component selection, schematic creation, and layout techniques.
5) Prayas club collaboration with other technical clubs:
The Tesla Club of Innovation, in collaboration with the Prayas Club, is organizing an exciting initiative aimed at school students. This program will provide them with a basic understanding of electrical, electronics, and mechanical components through hands-on projects and experiments. The goal is to engage students with practical knowledge and inspire them to explore the world of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics in an interactive and fun way.
