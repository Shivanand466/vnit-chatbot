Pre-Final year B.Tech Students have provision to pursue 8 week long internship during their summer vacation.
Companies offer Summer Internships to B.Tech 2 nd year, 3 rd year and M.Tech 1 st year students. Summer internships offer an opportunity to the students to work in a professional space, on the technologies they are learning on campus. This exposure helps them when they decide to join the industry as working professionals. The internships are a great way for the companies to attract the good students and monitor them during their tenure. Based on their performance of the intern, the companies extend a Pre-Placement offer (PPO) to the interns and inform the T & P department about their decision. If an intern accepts the PPO he/she are not eligible to appear for Campus Placements (depending on the category of the offer as per Placement policy).
To visit the campus for intern’s selections, please download the Internship Announcement Form, fill it up and mail it back to us at tepvnit_ngp@vnit.ac.in
Note:
B.Tech students, upon completion of their 3 rd year, can pursue summer internship for 8-10 weeks. (Tentatively 13 th
May 2024 to 19th July 2024)
M.Tech students have provision to pursue year-long internship (July 2023 to April 2024) in their final year.
