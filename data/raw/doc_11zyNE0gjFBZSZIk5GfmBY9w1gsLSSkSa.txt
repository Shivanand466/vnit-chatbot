Document: Extension Notice for NSP Scholarship (dated 2026-11-16)
NOTICE
Subject : Inviting Applications ( fresh and renewal ) under various scheme on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP 2.0) for the financial year 2026-27.
All the eligible students please take note that the last date for applying for NSP scholarship has been extended till November 30, 2026. For further update please visit NSP portal at https://scholarships.gov.in/home
However, in order to get your application verified, students are requested to submit the online application along with required self-attested documents (uploaded during registration) to Mrs. Mugdha Lamsoge, Sr. Superintendent, Academics, positively prior 10 days before scheme closing date.
As per NSP guidelines, students must submit a hard copy of the application along with all required supporting documents. Please note that failure to do so will result in your application not being verified by the Institute .
By Order
