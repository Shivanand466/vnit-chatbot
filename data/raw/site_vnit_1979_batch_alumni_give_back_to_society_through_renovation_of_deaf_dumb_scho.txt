VNIT 1979 Batch Alumni Give Back to Society Through Renovation of Deaf & Dumb School, Nagpur.
vnit_web
October 31, 2026
News
Nagpur | October 30, 2026: Demonstrating a remarkable spirit of social responsibility and inclusive education, the VNIT 1979 Batch Alumni renovated and developed four essential facilities at the Deaf & Dumb School, run by Mook Badhir Audyogik Sanstha, Shankar Nagar, Nagpur — School Library, Playroom, Administrative Office and Early Intervention Room, along with furniture, equipment and essential infrastructure.
The School Library was dedicated to the memory of Late Mr. Dilip Giradkar, while the School Playroom was dedicated to Late Mr. Rajiv Shukla, both members of the VNIT 1979 batch.
The facilities were inaugurated by Dr. Premalal Patel, Director, VNIT Nagpur, in the presence of Mr. Amir Vali, Mr. Madhusudan Dhamangaonkar and Mrs. Rita Soni. The occasion was graced by Dr. Mukund Pathankar, Mrs. Minakshi Sarvate and Mrs. Draupadi Chauhan.
Led by Mr. Chandrashekhar Deshpande, the initiative reflects the alumni’s commitment to creating a nurturing, inclusive and better-equipped learning environment for children with hearing and speech disabilities.
A heartfelt example of alumni solidarity, compassion and the enduring spirit of giving back to society!
