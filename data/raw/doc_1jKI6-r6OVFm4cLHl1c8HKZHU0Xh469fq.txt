Important instructions for Hostel Admission proccss:
Payment of hostel fee is to be made by any of the procedures given in Annexure-1.
After completion of all the academic admission formalities, Boys should report directly to A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Hostel and Girls shall report to Kalpana Chawla Hostel for Hostel Admission process.
Student should submit a copy of the self-attested Hostel Fee payment Receipt to the counter.
Hostel Room Allotment will be done on First ComeFirstServe basis.
Keeping personal furniture, appliances etc. are not allowed in the hostels.
Use of all types of motorized vehicles and electrical appliances viz. iron/hotplates/ induction cookers, refrigerators etc. is strictly prohibited (except Air Coolers in summer) for hostel inmates.
Parents, relatives, friends are not allowed to stay in the hostel with students.
Student should note that it is mandatory to open a savings bank account at Canara Bank, VNIT Branch or State Bank of India, VRCE Branch, Nagpur.
Items which are required for personal use viz. Mattress, Bed sheets, Bucket & mug etc., are to be brought in/required to be purchased by students.
VNIT campus is approx. 6 Kms from the Nagpur Railway Station.
