IDFC FIRST BANK ENGINEERING SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAMME 2026-30
Financial support for your engineering aspirations
Receive up to INR 1 lakh/year to pursue your B.E./B.Tech journey with confidence.
APPLICATIONS OPEN NOW
Dear Sir/Madam,
Greetings of the day!
We would like to inform you that the IDFC FIRST Bank Engineering Scholarship Programme 2026-30 is now open for applications. The program supports deserving first-year students pursuing B.E. or B.Tech. programs at eligible colleges, with financial assistance of up to INR 1,00,000 per year for four years.
We would appreciate your support in reaching eligible students by circulating this scholarship opportunity among relevant students within your institution and encouraging them to apply.
ELIGIBILITY
Applicants must be Indian citizens.
Open to students currently enrolled in the first year (academic year 2026-27) of their B.Tech or B.E. program.
Candidates must have secured admission to one of the FIRST IMPACT empanelled colleges (click here to refer to the list of eligible colleges).
The gross annual family income from all sources must be less than or equal to INR 6 lakh.
Applicants must have a valid mobile number linked to their Aadhaar number.
The earning member should not be an IDFC FIRST Bank employee
Note: Students pursuing a diploma course, part-time program, distance learning, dual degree course in engineering, or student exchange programs are not eligible.
Award
Application deadline INR 1,00,000 per year for up to four years.
20 September 2026
