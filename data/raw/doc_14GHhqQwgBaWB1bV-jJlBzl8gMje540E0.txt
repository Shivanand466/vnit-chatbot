REPORT ON THE VISIT TO
Seminar and Networking Event on EU-India Higher Education Cooperation held on 20 April 2026 at The LaLiT, New Delhi
VNIT, Nagpur recently participated in the European Universities conclave at Delhi. Prof. K. M. Bhurchandi (Dean, IAA) and Prof. Dhananjay A. Jolhe (Associate Professor, Mechanical Engg.) from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur attended the Seminar and Networking Event on EU-India Higher Education Cooperation held on 20 April 2026 at The LaLiT, New Delhi.
The event was organized by the European Commission with the support of the DAAD Regional Office, New Delhi, and witnessed participation from representatives of over 50 universities and institutions from around 15 European countries, along with delegates from Indian higher education institutions. The primary objective of attending this event was to explore opportunities for international academic collaboration, strengthen institutional partnerships, and identify potential avenues for student and faculty exchange, joint research, and academic cooperation between VNIT, Nagpur and European universities.
The event commenced with plenary sessions focusing on cooperation opportunities between India and Europe, including presentations on internationalisation strategies, academic mobility frameworks, and best practices adopted by universities. In the next session, one-to one matchmaking events were conducted, enabling direct interaction between representatives of Indian and European institutions.
Prof. Bhurchandi & Prof. Jolhe engaged with representatives from the interested European universities the enlisted below.
Queen Mary University of London
University of Twente, Netherlands
Dresden University of Technology, Germany
Universita Degli studi di milano, Italy
University of Bonn, Germany
University of Freiburg (faculty of engineering), Germany
Discussions were initiated regarding possible Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs), student and faculty exchange programmes, and joint academic and research initiatives.
