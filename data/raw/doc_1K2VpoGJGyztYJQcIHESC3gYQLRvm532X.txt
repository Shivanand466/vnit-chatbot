Hostel Information for UG 1st year Students:
VNIT has separate hostel buildings for Boys & Girls. Accommodation will be provided to students, on First Come First Serve basis. Hostel rooms are equipped with chair, cot, study table, cupboard, ceiling fan, lighting and Internet facilities etc.

Important instructions for Hostel Admission process:
Payment of hostel fee is to be done ONLINE after completing academic physical reporting. For Hostel Fee Details please see Annexure – I and Annexure – Il of Notice "Hostel Admission Instructions for 1st Year UG students Admitted in 2025" at NEWS AND UPDATES onVNIT Web-Site.
After completion of all the academic admission formalities students should report to Hostel Counter for Hostel Admission.
Hostel Room Allotment will be done on First Come First Serve basis.
4)Keeping personal furniture, appliances etc. are not allowed in the hostels.
Use of all types of motorized vehicles and electrical appliances viz. iron / hotplates/induction cookers, refrigerators etc. is strictly prohibited (except Air Coolers in summer) for hostel inmates.
Parents, relatives, friends are not allowed to stay in the hostel with students.
Items which are required for personal use viz. Mattress, Bed sheets; Bucket & mug etc. are to be brought in / required to be purchased by students.
