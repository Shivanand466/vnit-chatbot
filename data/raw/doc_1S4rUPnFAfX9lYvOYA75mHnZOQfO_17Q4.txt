NOTICE FOR CYCLE DONATION FOR 2021-22 (OUT GOING) BATCH
Hostel Section at VNIT Nagpur is conducting a bicycle donation drive from final year students to provide a reliable mode of transportation to needy junior students and to NGOs. Donated cycles will offer a sustainable solution for daily commute, and we encourage outgoing students to donate their bicycles before leaving. This will be great help to needy students, and we look forward to its successful implementation with the support of all the outgoing final year students.
All final year students (2021-22) batch who wish to donate their bicycle are requested to fill up the following Google form.
https://forms.gle/X5TYp34c9hepRBJr6
As a gesture of appreciation a donation certificate from the institute will be provided.
Those who are not willing to donate their bicycle are advised to take back their bicycles while vacating the hostel, else it will be retain by Hostel section in the Cycle Bank. Sogsyl
