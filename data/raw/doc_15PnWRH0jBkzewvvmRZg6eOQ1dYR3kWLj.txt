Dear Students,
Discipline and code of conduct are essential elements for maintaining a conducive and harmonious environment within the Institute and its hostel premises. It ensures that every student abides by the rules and regulations set forth by the Institute, thereby creating a safe and secure environment for everyone. You all are aware that staying in VNIT hostel is a unique experience that allows you to develop your social skills and learn to live independently. However, with freedom comes responsibility, and students must adhere to the Institute and hostel's guidelines to maintain order and decorum. Unfortunately, there have been instances where a few students have disregarded the rules, leading to disruptions, inconvenience, and sometimes even endangering the safety of thyself and others. In such cases the disciplinary actions are essential to ensure that the students who violate the rules are held accountable for their actions. It not only creates a sense of responsibility among students but also sends a clear message that such behavior will not be tolerated. Moreover, disciplinary actions acts as a deterrent for others who might think breaking the rules in the future.
In conclusion, following the discipline and code of conduct is crucial for staying in the Institute, hostel and within its premises. It not only ensures a safe and secure environment but also fosters a sense of community and responsibility among the students. While only a few students may not follow these guidelines, disciplinary actions are necessary to ensure that the rules are respected and followed by all. The general code of conduct is provided and signed by every student during admission. However, the Institute has constituted a Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC) which has set forth the act of indiscipline and corresponding disciplinary actions in the meeting held on 19-04-2023.
The list of acts of indiscipline and corresponding action is indicated in Table 1 (Category-I) and 2 (CategoryII). If a student is involved in any act of indiscipline set forth in Table 1 (Category-I), he/she will not be allowed to participate in any sports /club/council activity and his/her membership of all such committees will be treated as cancelled. Students shall also note that every act of indiscipline mentioned in both Table 1 (Category-I) and 2 (Category-II) will be informed to parents by letter / telephonically / E-mail.
VISVESVARAYA NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, NAGPUR DAC-VNIT, NAGPUR
