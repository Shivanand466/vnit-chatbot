Document: Grievance Steps for TOP CLASS Students (dated 2024-03-03)
Step -1:- Open URL of Grievance-
URL :- https://tribal.nic.in/Grievance/
Step -2:- Register Yourself with proper information [for new user only]
Click 'Register Yourself'
button to get registration. It will open Registration Form for Student.
On Registration Form, Select 'Student' and 'Top Class' option and fill all required information.
Note :- Your given email id is your USER ID for Login in Grievance.
Step -3:- Login [for registered user only]
Copyright © 2018 All rights reservod. Contont Managed by Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Last Updated on : 06/06/2019
After successful registration, you can login with your given EMAIL ID and PASSWORD.
Once you get login, you can send your Query/ Grievance and you will see the response of Query/ Grievance also after login.
