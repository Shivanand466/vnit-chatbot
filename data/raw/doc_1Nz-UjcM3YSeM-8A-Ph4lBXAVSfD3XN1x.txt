Visit of Ms. Camille Triouillois, Representative of ISEN Méditerranée, France, to VNIT Nagpur on May 20, 2026
Ms. Camille Triouillois, Indian representative of ISEN Mediterranee, France visited VNIT Nagpur on Tuesday, 20 th May 2026. Her visit is a part of the possible ongoing Academic Collaboration between the two Institutes. Ms. Camille visited and had a first-hand experience of various R&D Labs and facilities at VNIT Nagpur. The broad areas of mutual interest for ISEN Mediterranee are Centre for VLSI & Nanotechnology, Pattern Recognition, 5G Lab, Bio Lab & Center for AI, Hyundai Centre of Excellence, Embedded Systems, Cloud Computing & IoT, Quantum Technology, Center for E-mobility.
Ms. Camille was welcomed by Dr Yashwant Katpatal, Dean R&C; Dr Vilas R Kalamkar, Dean Academic; Dr T V K Gupta, Associate Dean (International Affairs) & Dr Makrand Lokhande. She interacted with Dr Prem Lal Patel, Director VNIT and briefed the purpose of Academic Collaboration with VNTI Nagpur. Deliberations were carried between the ISEN India Representative and faculty members, research scholars working in the above areas.
The visit includes discussion & interaction with Dr. Nikhil Deep Gupta, Dr Praveen Kumar, Dr Anamika Singh, Dr Deep Gupta, Dr Pradyumn Chaturvedi, Dr Ankit Bhurane, Dr Deepti Shrimankar, Dr Praveen Pawar, Dr B R Sankapal, Dr Ritesh Keshri at their respective laboratories.
Ms Camille emphasized to identify the modalities for Semester long Student Exchange Program and Collaborative R&D projects between VNIT Nagpur and ISEN Mediterranee, France. This visit was result of the VNIT international activities team to the EU universities conclave at Delhi on 20th April 2026.
