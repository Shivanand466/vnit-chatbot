Dr. Ravikumar Dumpala
Brief Bio
Dr. Ravikumar Dumpala is specialized in the area of manufacturing, mechanical testing, surface engineering, tribo-wear analysis of hard coatings and non-ferrous alloys, and machining of aerospace materials, currently working as a Professor at the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. He is a life member of Materials Research Society of India (MRSI) and Tribology Society of India (TSI). His teaching interests are Manufacturing Technology, Metrology, Design of Fixtures in Manufacturing, Machine Drawing, and Surface Engineering. Before joining VNIT Nagpur on 1st June 2015, he has worked for Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) from 2007 to 2009 and for GMRIT Rajam (Aaffiliated to JNTU Kakinada) for about a year (2014-2015).
