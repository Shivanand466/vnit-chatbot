India has rich scientific and technological knowledge that dates back to thousands of years. The ancient Indian knowledge system (IKS) has significantly contributed to mathematics, astronomy, metallurgy, civil engineering, architecture, and medicine. However, these contributions are often overlooked in mainstream academia and engineering curricula, which predominantly rely on western scientific paradigms. This conference aims to bridge this gap by integrating the IKS into contemporary engineering education, research, and practice.
The National Education Policy (NEP 2020) emphasizes integrating Indian Knowledge System (IKS) across educational levels, aiming to revitalize India's ancient wisdom within modern academic and professional settings. By promoting the inclusion of IKS in STEM education, NEP 2020 encourages a deeper understanding of India's scientific and cultural heritage and advocates for curricula that explore traditional sciences,mathematics, philosophy, and linguistics. Specifically, in engineering, NEP 2020 envisions a framework where students can draw on sustainable practices and innovative solutions inspired by IKS, bridging traditional knowledge with contemporary approaches to address global challenges. This holistic educational vision aims to cultivate wellround individuals grounded in India's intellectual traditions while fostering interdisciplinary skills and cultural appreciation.
Reviving traditional tools and techniques through modern technology
Case studies of successful integration of IKS in contemporary engineering projects
Exploration of IKS-inspired innovations in science and engineering
The conference on "Indian Knowledge System with Science and Engineering Perspective" seeks to:
Highlight India's Engineering Heritage: Present the achievements of Indian knowledge system in engineering, such as the construction of ancient temples, water management systems, metallurgy, and early scientific inventions.
Promote Sustainability in Engineering: Study the sustainable and eco-friendly practices embedded in IKS, aiming to integrate these principles into contemporary engineering solutions for climate resilience and resource management.
Bridge Traditional Knowledge with Modern Technology: Discuss ways traditional Indian engineering practices can complement and enhance modern technological advancements, fostering innovation through a fusion of ancient wisdom and contemporary science.
The concept of Pancha Mahabhuta (five elements: earth, water, fire, air, and space) emphasizes the interconnection of natural elements. In engineering, it aligns with material sciences (earth and fire in metallurgy), hydrology (water in river management), mathematics and astronomy (space and patterns of the cosmos), and environmental sustainability, advocating balance and harmony with nature, directly contributing to SDGs like clean water, sustainable cities, and climate action.
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, established in 1960 is one of the esteemed institutes among the thirty one National Institutes of Technology in India. It was named after Bharat Ratna Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya an eminent engineer, planner and statesman of the country. The Institute has highly qualified faculty in various departments. VNIT offers B.Tech degrees in eight disciplines, a B.Arch, and M.Tech degrees in twenty disciplines. The Institute also offers master's programmes in M.Sc (Mathematics, Chemistry, and Physics) and doctoral research programs in Engineering, Architecture, Sciences and Humanities disciplines. The Institute has collaborated with various reputed research /academic organizations. The distance of the campus from Nagpur Airport/Railway Station is approximately 6 kms.
Educationalists, academicians, faculty members, research scholars and students of IISc, IITs, NITs, IISER, IIITs and other Institute with a keen interest in the area of IKS.
This conference, the first of its kind, will amalgamate various engineering disciplines to revisit ancient wisdom and knowledge so that the road map for the future technological trajectory of Bharat can be further augmented based on the ancient engineering disciplines. The proceedings and research papers will create empirical documents in Pure Sciences, Engineering, and Technology.Thus providing a manual for India's science, engineering and architecture students.
