Greetings from the Indian Institute of Technology, Madras!
In reference to our letter dated 17 September 2025, we are pleased to inform your esteemed institution to nominate a deserving student as a beneficiary for the Vidyalakshmi PAN India Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for the Academic Year 2025-2026 , under the sponsorship of Power Grid Corporation of India Limited .
You may access the Vidyalakshmi PAN India Scholarship Portal at the following link: [ Portal Link ]. Kindly note that single-user access has been allocated to each institute. Kindly create an account and then sign in. ( If you require any assistance with the portal, please refer to the Sitemap section for guidance. )
We kindly request you to nominate a deserving student for the scholarship, based on the eligibility criteria detailed below.
Scholarship Selection Criteria:
(i) The student must be enrolled in a B.Tech / B.E. / B.S. (Biological Sciences) / Dual Degree program.
(ii) First-year students can be nominated based on their JEE rank or first-semester CGPA.
(iii) The annual parental income of the student should be between Rs. 1 lakh and Rs. 5 lakhs.
(iv) The student should belong to the EWS, OBC, or General (GE) category.
(v) Female students will be given 50% preference ; in the absence of female candidates, a male student may be nominated.
(vi) Each selected student will receive a scholarship amount of Rs. 41,667.
(vii) The student must not be a recipient of any other scholarship.
(viii) The institute is requested to verify all supporting documents thoroughly before submitting the application.
Kindly submit the application through the 'Beneficiaries' section of the portal, ensuring that all required details are provided and that AY 2025-2026 is selected at the time of submission.
Important: Upon submission of the applications, we kindly request you to provide the list of five nominated students on the institution's official letterhead (template attached) , duly signed by the authorized signatory , and share the same with us via email for record purposes.
The last date to complete the nomination is February 10, 2026 .
