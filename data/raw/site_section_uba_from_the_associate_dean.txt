From The Associate Dean
Dear Alumni,
Welcome to the online community of our Alma Mater, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) or, the erstwhile Visvesvaraya Regional College of Engineering (VRCE)! The name itself brings out the nostalgia. Many of you, especially those visiting the institute after a long gap, will notice a significant change in the landscape, facilities, infrastructure and may as well miss some people, whereas the fresh alumni will feel like homecoming. A common thread that binds all of us is our institute, our Alma Mater and the fragrance of the beautiful lingering memories.
As the institute continues to grow and evolve, your involvement and support to the institute remains crucial to its success. Our alumni network is a testament to the strong foundation laid at our institution. Your achievements and success stories bring immense pride to all of us, alumni as well as the institute. This platform is designed to keep you connected with the latest developments, upcoming events, and opportunities to engage with fellow alumni and current students.
We encourage you to share your experiences, insights, and ideas to inspire and guide the current students and future brand ambassadors for our Alma Mater, just as you are today, Together, we can build a stronger, more vibrant community.
Thank you for being an integral part of our family and for your continued dedication to our Alma Mater.
Warm regards,
Prof. Ajay Likhite (Alumnus 1983 batch)
Associate Dean (III& Alumni)
