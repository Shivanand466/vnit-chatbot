Dr. Ashish S. Tiwari​
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Ashish Tiwari has a PhD in Computer Science and his areas of Research have evolved from Adhoc networks to Machine Learning and now modelling of Climate Systems. He is actively involved in guiding Undergraduate, Post Graduate and Doctoral fellows. He has guided 03 doctoral students and is currently guiding over 05 doctoral students right now. He has actively participated in various administrative roles at VNIT. He is on the BoS of several institutions and also on the BoG of one institution. His teaching interests are in the area of Computer Organization, Operating Systems, Algorithms, DBMS and Mathematics for Machine Learning. He is passionate about music and playing Table Tennis.
