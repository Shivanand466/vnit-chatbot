REPORT ON ORIENTATION EVENT

REPORT ON SOLAR AMBASSADOR WORKSHOP
The Solar Ambassador Workshop, conducted on October 6, 2024, at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, Organized by Think India VNIT Chapter was a significant initiative to promote solar energy adoption and sustainability. This impactful event, organized by the Think India VNIT Chapter, brought together students, educators, and professionals to learn, innovate, and lead in renewable energy practices. Think India's dedication to fostering a ' Nation First ' attitude and inspiring young minds played a pivotal role in the success of this workshop.
Inaugural Session: A Call for Solar Leadership
The workshop began with an inspiring address by VNIT's Honourable Director, Dr. Premlal Patel. He emphasized the importance of transitioning to renewable energy and the pivotal role academic institutions like VNIT play in fostering innovation and environmental stewardship. Dr. Patel highlighted India's immense potential for solar energy and urged participants to adopt sustainable practices and become ambassadors of change.
Workshop Overview
The hands-on training aimed to equip participants with practical skills to assemble and test multipurpose solar lamps. Participants were introduced to the various components required for assembly. After a detailed explanation of each component's function, participants, under the guidance of 14 master trainers, successfully assembled their own solar lamps. This practical activity allowed participants to experience the technology firsthand, symbolizing their commitment to energy self-reliance and environmental sustainability. The seamless execution of this event stood as a testament to the dedication and efforts of Think India VNIT's organizers.
Leadership and Guidance from Prof. Chetan Singh Solanki
Prof. Solanki, founder of the Energy Swaraj Foundation, guided the workshop with his vision of achieving energy self-sufficiency through decentralized solar energy systems. His philosophy, "Avoid, Minimize, Generate (AMG)," inspired participants to rethink their energy consumption habits and embrace sustainable practices.
Diverse Participation
The workshop attracted 114 participants from various institutions, including: Impulse Coaching Classes, Nagpur, PM Shri KV Vidyalaya Ajni, Nagpur, Dharampeth Polytechnic College, Nagpur, NIT Andhra Pradesh, VNIT Nagpur. This diversity enriched the learning experience, fostering collaboration and exchange of ideas. Think India's outreach and coordination were instrumental in achieving this diverse representation.
Expert Contributions
Distinguished professors from VNIT contributed significantly by bridging theoretical knowledge and practical applications. Their insights on solar technology and renewable energy systems underscored the broader implications of sustainability for India's development. The esteemed faculty included: Dr. Rasipuram Periasamy Vijayakumar · Dr. Sushama M Giripunje · Dr. Uday T Bhosle · Dr. Makrand Lokhande · Dr. Prakash Kulkarni · Dr. Gali Hemachandra · Dr. Ratnesh Kumar · Dr. Y.B. Katpatal · Dr. Sameer Deshkar.
Key Highlights and Outcomes
Participants gained practical skills by assembling solar lamps, providing direct exposure to renewable energy solutions.
The workshop emphasized the social, technical, and environmental impact of solar energy, aligning with Think India's mission to promote sustainable, India-centric solutions.
Solar lamps were highlighted as tools to combat energy poverty and environmental degradation, reinforcing Think India's focus on rural and sustainable development.
Many participants engaged with solar technology for the first time, finding it both educational and motivating.
The event empowered future leaders with skills and a sense of responsibility to address India's energy and environmental challenges.
