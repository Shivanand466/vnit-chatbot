VNIT Pioneers Holistic Student Wellness for Campus Placements!
vnit_web
August 10, 2026
News
The Training & Placement (T&P) Section, VNIT Nagpur, has launched a first-of-its-kind Placement Fitness Initiative, ensuring graduating engineers are not only academically strong but also medically and physically fit.
🏥 Students underwent comprehensive health assessments—CBC, blood sugar, colour blindness, BMI and blood pressure—followed by a Physical Efficiency Test (PET) measuring VO₂ Max, strength and flexibility.
📊 Fitness scorecards are integrated into placement resumes, offering recruiters transparent insights into candidate wellness and resilience.
❤️ Highlighting VNIT’s inclusive spirit, a final-year ECE student with Cerebral Palsy successfully completed a modified 12-minute walk test, inspiring the campus community.
🔬 The initiative also supports interdisciplinary research linking health indicators with physical performance.
