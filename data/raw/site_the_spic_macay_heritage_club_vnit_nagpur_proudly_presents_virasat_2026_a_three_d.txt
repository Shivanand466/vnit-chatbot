VIRASAT 2026 | Celebrating India’s Living Cultural Heritage 🇮🇳
The
SPIC MACAY – Heritage Club, VNIT Nagpur proudly presents
‘Virasat 2026’
, a three-day celebration of India’s rich classical arts and cultural traditions, from 24–26 September 2026 at VNIT Nagpur.
✨
Experience. Learn. Connect with Tradition.
🎨
Daytime Workshops
🖌️

– Dr. Sneha Gangal
💃

– Vidushi Kalapini Komkali, eminent vocalist & Sangeet Natak Akademi Awardee
🎼
Evening Classical Concerts 24 Sept | Kathak
– Pandit Rajendra Gangani 25 Sept | Hindustani Vocal
– Vidushi Kalapini Komkali 26 Sept | Sitar
– Pandit Kushal Das
From the intricate artistry of miniature painting to the rhythmic elegance of Kathak, the soulful traditions of Hindustani music and the melodic richness of the sitar,
Virasat 2026 offers students an opportunity to experience India’s cultural heritage through direct engagement with accomplished maestros.
The festival will be graced by
Prof. Prem Lal Patel, Director, VNIT Nagpur
, along with the esteemed Deans of the Institute.
🤝 Coordinated by
Dr. Arghya Mitra and Dr. Sarika Bahadure
, with enthusiastic support from the student members of the
SPIC MACAY – Heritage Club, VNIT Nagpur
.
