PLACEMENT POLICY: BATCH 2027
The Training & Placement Section will provide placement assistance to all the registered and eligible UG and PG students of VNIT, for securing a job in industry, academic and research Institutions.
The Institute has implemented Career Readiness Programme comprising of a series of Skills based and Job-role based Assessments, Mock Interviews, Group Discussions, Expert Lecture Series, Physical Efficiency Test and Health Checkup, for all the Final year UG and PG students.
3. Placement Process followed by the Institute:
Invitations with the placement timelines, Institute Placement Policy, Internship policy, Job Announcement Form (JAF) / Internship Announcement Form (IAF), and other relevant information are sent out to the companies by the T&P Section.

8. Offer Upgradation Clauses:
Students placed in Category-1 can upgrade to any higher category, withran restrictions.
Students placed in Category-2 can upgrade to Category-3, only if 75% of their class is placed AND the new offer is 1.5 times higher than the existing offer.
Students placed in Category-1 and Category-2 can upgrade to Category-4. However, decision to allow placed students will be at discretion of the Category-4 Organization.
Students placed in Category-3 shall not be able to upgrade to Category-4.
Student can have only ONE offer in each category and cannot downgrade to lower category.
Upon offer upgradation, the student will automatically lose any previous offer witn n n o giy..
Offer upgradation (including PSUs) can be done only till 30th April 2027.
9. Pre-Placement Offer:
Student can accept ONE Pre-Placement Offer either after completion of Internship, or through Hackathon/ Codathon / Any competition. i.
Companies hiring the students for Summer Internship, are urged to extend the Pre-Placement Offer to the interns and send an official communication to the T&P Section ON OR BEFORE 17th July 2026, along with the salary package details. Accordingly the candidate will be locked as per the offer category (Point no.7). Failing which, the student will be allowed to appear for Campus Placements.
Once a student is placed through campus placement, he/she cannot accept the PPO, if it is in the same offer category (Ref. point 8)
The student is eligible to appear for Campus Placements till the official communication of the PPO is received by the T&P Section.
Companies hiring the PG students for year-long internship, are urged to extend the Pre-Placement Offer ON OR BEFORE 31st December 2026.
It is mandatory for the students to inform the T&P Office about any PPO (as mentioned in point i.) received through Hackathon / Codathon /Any Competition.
If two or more companies have interviews on the same day / (schedule their process in parallel), then student will have to ACCEPT the offer from the company declaring the results first. All other companies will be notified about the student's selection and the candidature will be withdrawn accordingly.
A student can accept the PPO from the Internship only if he/she is not placedthough CampusPlacements.
12.SELECTION RESULTS: The company officials are expected to announce the Final Selections on the same day of the completion of process. All the necessary approvals are expected to be taken in advance. The students will be allowed to appear for newer job opportunities, as long as the T&P Section does not receive an official communication (Email or Letter) regardingtheir selection.
TO MAINTAIN WAITLIST: There are chances of scheduling two or more companies in parallel. Also, students have opportunities to upgrade their existing job offer to higher categories (Point No. 8). Insuch cases, we recommend companies to maintain a waitlist at each stage of the selection process. The waitlisted candidates can be considered in case of dropouts, if any. Companies shall be notified about dropouts as and when the upgradation happens.
14.Once the selection process is over and the selections are announced, the hiring companies are required to furnish below information on Company Letterhead along with the Sign and Stamp of the competent authority. This is required by the Govt of India for parliamentary questions, international and national rankings and the recording keeping purposes of the Institute.
15.Issuance of Offer Letters:Companies are required to share the Offer Letters only to the T&P Section and NOT directly with the candidates. Offer Letters are required to be released within TWO months after the selection. Failing which, students will be allowed to appear for placements in other companies with immediate effect. This is to ensure that the company honours the jobs offered to the candidates.

24.Accommodation can be arranged in the Institute Guest House depending on the availability of rooms. Pickup and drop from Nagpur airport to VNIT can be arranged for the company officials, on request.
Any company that revokes the job offers, is expected to support the affected candidates in placing in other company at the same or higher salary, within 3 months.
26.Any company that delays the onboarding for more than three months from the Date of Joining specified earlier OR does not onboard candidates till 31st October 2027 is expected to compensate all the hired candidates proportionately for the loss of salary. This is to ensure that the Company honours the jobs offered to the candidates.
If any Company is implementing Employment Bond, it should provide all the related details viz. Bond duration, Bond amount, etc., before commencement of the selection process. If the company does not provide any bond information initially, it will be assumed that it does not have any bond policy. In case the bond policy is implemented or communicated to us after the selection process, it will be deemed as offer revoked, and the T&P Section shall not make it compulsory for selected students to join the company. Point no. 26 will be applicable here.
