Dr. Neeraj Rao
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Neeraj Rao, currently serving as an Assistant Professor at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, earned his Master's degree in 2011 and completed his Ph.D. in 2017, specializing in Microstrip Antenna at PDPM Indian Institute of Information Technology, Jabalpur. Prior to his doctoral studies, he obtained a B.Engg. from Government Engineering College, Jabalpur. His research focuses on Antenna and Non-Destructive Testing Evaluation using electromagnetic waves. Neeraj is keen on exploring novel applications and methodologies to understand the behavior of electromagnetic waves.
