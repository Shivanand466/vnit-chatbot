TITLE: Congratulations to Divya Pachbhai for securing first position in Thinkwonders Competitive Exam (TCE) – Department of Metallurgical & Materials Engineering
FETCHED: 2026-11-19
CRAWL: full-2026-11-19

Divya Pachbhai, a third year student from our department, has achieved first position from all institutes/colleges over India in Thinkwonders Competitive Exam.
Thinkwonders is a educational organization that focuses on the academic competition and coaching of students which helps in the development of competitive spirit and enhancing thinking ability.
They provide internship opportunities to top rankers in the TCE exam which is guided from alumni from top IIT/NIT/IIM and cash prices with certificate of merit.
