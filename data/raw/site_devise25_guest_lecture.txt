DEVISE'25 & AXIS'25, in Collaboration with IIA Nagpur, Host an Inspiring Guest Lecture by Ar. Hiren Patel
Nagpur, 22nd March 2025
– DEVISE’25, under the aegis of AXIS’25, in collaboration with the Indian Institute of Architects (IIA) Nagpur Chapter, successfully organized a guest lecture by renowned architect
Ar. Hiren Patel at the Multi-Activity Centre, VNIT Nagpur. The event saw enthusiastic participation from architecture students, faculties, and professionals eager to gain insights from the esteemed speaker.
The session commenced with a warm welcome and the ceremonial lighting of the lamp, symbolizing knowledge and enlightenment.
Dr. Sameer Deshkar
, Head of the Department of Architecture and Planning, VNIT, addressed the gathering, emphasizing the significance of industry-academia collaborations in architectural education.
Delivering an inspiring lecture,
Ar. Hiren Patel shared his distinctive design philosophies, professional journey, and thought-provoking perspectives. His dynamic presentation, enriched with compelling anecdotes, deeply resonated with the audience, making the session both insightful and engaging.
As a token of appreciation, a handcrafted memento—a delicately carved leaf—was presented to
Ar. Hiren Patel
. This unique piece, crafted by
Shashank Basude
, a 4th-year VNIT student, reflected the creativity and craftsmanship nurtured within the institution.
The event concluded with a vote of thanks, followed by a photo session capturing the memorable moments.
DEVISE’25 and AXIS’25, in collaboration with IIA Nagpur, express their gratitude to
Ar. Hiren Patel for his invaluable contribution, reinforcing the importance of architectural discourse and professional engagement in shaping future architects.
