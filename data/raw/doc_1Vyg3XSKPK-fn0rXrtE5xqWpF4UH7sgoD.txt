Sub- Regarding Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj Foreign Scholarship Scheme Ref- Advertisement dated 11.06.2024
Dear Sir/Madam,
This is to inform you that we, Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance, Government of Maharashtra State, India are providing several Scholarship and Free ship programs to sponsor the socially deprived students residing in State of Maharashtra, India. With an aim and objective that, due to weak economic and financial condition Scheduled Caste students cannot afford the higher education in prominent universities in the foreign countries. To overcome this difficulty Government of Maharashtra has introduced this scheme as per G.R. Same scheme has been Implemented by other department as well for the other caste students.
Sensing this fact, since year 2003-04, Maharashtra State Government has introduced the scholarship scheme for the Scheduled Caste Students studying in Aboard Universities for PG and Phd Courses.
The eligibility criteria for the said scheme as below
Student should be Scheduled Caste and shall be resident of State of Maharashtra.
For this scholarship family income limit is Rs. 8.00 lakhs per year. Maximum age limit for PG shall be 35 years and for PhD. shall be max. 40 years.
The foreign university must fall under first 200 ranks of QS World ranking. 2024
In this regard, we request you to kindly appeal the SC students who are residents of Maharashtra State, India and pursing their higher education in your esteemed institute to apply for the said scholarship. We also request you to kindly display this letter along with enclosed advertisement on your Notice Board. For details please log on to www.maharashtra.gov.in./whats new The applications shall be sent in Hard format prescribed by the department and should be filled by student then submitted it to the following address :
