Scheme for Award of Engineers India Limited (EIL) Scholarships For SC and ST Undergraduate Engineering Students
1. INTRODUCTION
.
Engineers India is a premier design and Engineering Consultancy Organization with capability in design and implementation of mega projects in varied areas such as ol refineries, petrochemicals, chemicais, oil and gas processing, fertilizers, power plants, non-ferrous metallurgical plants, offshore platforms, cross couintry pipelines, etc., with headquarters at New Delhi and a network of project sites and Offices in various parts of India and abroad.
Engineers India Limited is committed to operate its core business in a socially responsible way. As a socially responsible organization, ElL aims to assist socially and economically weaker segments of society to overcome hardship and impoverishment. To fulfill this objective, Engineers India Limited has devised the Scholarship Scheme whereby financial assistance is offered to meritorious and deserving SC and ST students.
2. OBJECTIVE
.
4
The objective of this scheme is to award Engineers India Limited (EIL) scholarships to meritorious and which are duly recognized by AICTE and/ or from University/Deemed University duly recognized by UGC.
3. VALUE & DURATION OF EIL SCHOLARSHIPS
Value of each ElL scholarship will be Rs.3000/- per month. The duration of EIL Scholarship shall be for the period of the course. The scholarship shall be payable for all the 12 months in a year, commencing from the 1* year of the study based on the marks scored in Class Xil/Diploma in Engineering, The scheme shall. be continued during the 2rd, 3 and 4th years of study, based on marks scored in 1, 2rd and 3rd year of study respectively.
4. ELIGIBIUTY FOR AWARD
EIL scholarships shall be awarded to meritorious and deserving SC & ST students (SC- 10 nos. & ST- 5 nos.) pursuing full-time undergraduate engineering programmes in Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science, Industrial, Chemical or Instrumentation engineering disciplines. 7 nos. of the scholarships [SC- 5 nos. & ST- 2 nos.) shall be reserved for girl students. However, in case of non availability of students in either category, students from other category may be considered.
Gross Annual income from all sources of the parents/guardian of the selected candidate shall not exceed Rs. 3,00,000/-.
A candidate in receipt of any other financial aid/ stipend from any other source whatsoever shall not be eligible for the EIL Scholarship.
Selections shall be made on the basis of performance in Class Xil/Diploma in Engineering. Candidate should have secured 55% or equivalent CGPA/CPI in aggregate in Class XIl/Diploma in Engineering, as per details mentioned below:

5. CONTINUANCEOFAWARD:
Award of scholarship for 2 year onwards shall be strictly subject to continuous satisfactory performance and good conduct.
The scholarship shall be renewed every year from 2™d year till the completion of course. The renewal/continuation of scholarship will depend on the promotion to the next semester/session/year. If the student has failed in any paper/obtained less than aggregate 55% marks or equivalent CGPA/CPI of 2.0/2.7/3.3/4.4/4.9/5.9on a scale of 4/5/6/7/8/9/10 respectively for any semester/session/year due, he/she will not be paid scholarship till he/ she clears all papers of such semester/ session with not less than aggregate 55% marks or equivalent CGPA/CPI..
For renewal of scholarship, the candidate will send a Progress Report, In the prescribed format, duly recommended by the Head of the Institution within 30 days of announcement of the result of the semester / session/year. The format shall be forwarded to the concerned shortlisted candidates.
Candidates who fail to pass the semester or annual examination in prescribed duration of course or indulge in any activity amounting to misconduct will be disqualified for further scholarship. Recommendations in this regard to ElL from the Appropriate Authority of the Institute/Coliege shall be final.
6. COMMENCEMENT OF THE SCHOLARSHIP:
This is an ongoing scheme and for each academic session, the scholarship shall be effective from start of session.
7. MODE OF PAYMENT:
All payments on account of ElL scholarship shall be made through electronic transfer to the concerned candidates. In this regard, shortlisted candidates shall be intimated to furnish requisite details.
