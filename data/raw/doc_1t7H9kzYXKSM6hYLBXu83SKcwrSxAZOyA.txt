Document: The Department of Civil Engineering, VNIT Nagpur, is organizing a two-day workshop on “Application of Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning and Opt (dated 2026-07-06)
TWO-DAY WORKSHOP ON
APPLICATION OF AI, ML AND OPTIMIZATION IN WATER MANAGEMENT
(Funded by Centre of Australia-India Relations Maitri Grant Program, Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade)
Organized by: Department of Civil Engineering, VNIT, Nagpur
In association with Victoria University Australia, IIT Roorkee and RV College of Engineering, Bengaluru
Date: December 15-16, 2026
Venue: Department of Civil Engineering, VNIT, Nagpur
Time: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
 Key Topics Covered
Water management: specific challenges and actions to address
Smart sensing and real-time monitoring of water and case studies
Predictive modelling through AI/ML for water management
Optimization practices in urban and agriculture water management
Current status of AI/ML adoption and policy needs (technical and governance)
Future of AI/ML and sensor technology in water management
 Who Can Attend?
Faculty Members from Engineering Institutions
Scientist and Researchers from Research Institute
Water Supply Policy makers
Industry Professionals, Irrigation and Water supply, CWC, State Water Corporations, Water Supply Service Providers
·
Smart Water technologies developers/ manufacturers
Research gaps/needs in mainstream adoption of new technologies
PG/PhD scholars
