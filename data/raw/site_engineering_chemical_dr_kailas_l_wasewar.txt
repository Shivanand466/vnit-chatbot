Dr. Kailas L. Wasewar​
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Professor and Head of the Department of Chemical Engineering, VNIT Nagpur
Since 2008 (more than 15 years) in Chemical Engineering Department, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur India
Visiting Professor at Kyung Hee University, South Korea, 2012,2013,2014
More than 25 years industrial, research, and teaching experience from various reputed organization such as Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee India and Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS) Pilani.
Presently, 3rd time Head of the Department (HoD) with earlier two tenures: 2008-10 & 2013-16.
Associate Dean, Education Technology & Library (2021-24) , VNIT Nagpur
Member of board of governors (BoG) of VNIT Nagpur (2021-23).
World’s top 2% scientist in Chemical Engineering (since 2020) as per survey done by Stanford University. Wworld rank is 299 (top 0.55%) and 18th rank in India
More than 300 SCI/Scopus indexed journal publications, more than 200 conference papers, 6 Indian Patents, 1 Australian Patent, 1 Edited Book, 5 Edited Proceedings, and 35 Book Chapters
Guided one PostDoc Fellow, 20 PhD, 18 M.Tech., and 102 BTech with 7 ongoing PhD students.
More than 10470 google scholar citations with h-index: 60, and i-10 index: 186.
Bachelor of Technology in Chemical Engineering (1995) from Laxminarayan Institute of Technology (LIT) Nagpur, and Masters (2000) and full time PhD (2004) from Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT), Mumbai India.
Completed / Ongoing prestigious Department of Science & Technology (DST) Young Scientist Project, Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) project, Defence Research and Development Organization (DRDO), Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Institution of Engineers India (IEI), UGC-DAE, and few Industrial projects and consultancies.
Research interest is in Biotechnology, Nanotechnology, Reaction Engineering, Process Intensification, Separation Technology, Biomedical Engineering, and Environmental & Sustainable Engineering
Delivered more than 135 invited expert lectures in various conferences / workshops / seminars
Fellow of International Congress of Chemistry and Environment (FICCE), Fellow of Indian Chemical Society (FICS)
Fellow of Institution of Engineers (India) (FIE)
Fellow of Indian Institute of Chemical Engineers (FIIChE)
Fellow of Maharashtra Academy of Science (FMASc)
Keenly occupied in social activities through Vijyana Bharati, Druva (Prayas) Club, Actively involved in academic administrative and extra-curricular activities
