Last Date of Fees payment for selected SII students in various courses of B.Tech./B.Arch. programs- 26/07/2025.

The selected SII students in various courses of B.Tech./B.Arch. programs have paid the Seat Acceptance Fee of INR 35000/- to accept the offered seat. Further, please note that the last date of Institute/Semester Fees Payment is 26/07/2025 (2400 hrs IST) through the online payment links on https://pay.vnit.ac.in/index . Please be careful while selecting your B.Tech. / B. Arch. program and your category of country i.e. SAARC and Non-SAARC in the dropdown. The amount is worked out as: Institute/Semester Fees + Tuition Fees - 30000 (adjustable amount with Seat Acceptance Fees); as applicable to B.Tech./B. Arch. Program and applicable country.
(A) The Institute/Semester fees for candidate from SAARC country:
B.Tech.: INR 15500 + INR 5225 + USD 2000 - INR 30000
B.Arch.: INR 16800 + INR 5225 + USD 2000 - INR 30000
(B) The Institute/Semester fees for candidate from Non-SAARC country:
B.Tech.: INR 15500 + INR 5225 + USD 4000 - INR 30000
B.Arch.: INR 16800 + INR 5225 + USD 4000 - INR 30000
In case of clarification of Fees Calculations, Mr. Hardik Jogi (Academic Section) may be contacted on +91-712-280-1819. You are required to send the Payment Receipt on international.admissions@vnit.ac.in on or before 26/07/2025 (2400 Hrs IST) .
It is to be noted that in the case of non-payment of Institute/Semester Fees on or before 26/07/2025 (2400 hrs IST), the offered seat/admission will be cancelled and his/her Seat Acceptance Fees (35,000/-) will be forfeited.
The admitted students who paid the Institute/Semester Fees will have the choice of selecting hostel facility in International Rooms-Extra facilities or General Rooms with the respective fees/charges to be paid at the time of Institute Reporting at VNIT Nagpur-India (August 20-23, 2025).
Also note that the discrepancy in any of documents or not producing the appropriate document during Institute Reporting and Physical Original Documents Verification process, will lead to cancellation of admission. The decision of Director, VNIT Nagpur will be final in any of the case/stage.
