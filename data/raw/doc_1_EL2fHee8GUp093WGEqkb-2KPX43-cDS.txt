Document: Criteria for Admission in ICCR (dated 2024-05-06)
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur
Criterion for Admissions in ICCR Category (2024-25)
Admissions shall be through ICCR (Indian Council of Cultural Relations), Ministry of External Affairs, Govt. of India.
The candidate should not be a citizen of India.
The candidate should have a valid passport for the entire duration of the programme admitted to.
Eligibility conditions for
B.Tech. Programme :
XII (or equivalent) pass with PCM& English Subject; overall performance should be 60% or above (or equivalent grade).
ii) TOEFEL Score ≥ 60 or IELTS Score ≥ 6.
iii) Age should be less than 20 years.
B.Arch. Programme :
XII (or equivalent) pass with PCM& English Subject; overall performance should be 60% or above (or equivalent grade).
ii) TOEFEL Score ≥ 60 or IELTS Score ≥ 6.
iii) Age should be less than 20 years.
iv) Candidate should have valid NATA / JEE (Mains) score/rank for the year of admission.
M.Tech. Programme :
4 years UG course in Engg. (For Urban Planning, 5 years UG course in Arch.); overall performance should be 60% or above (or equivalent grade).
ii) TOEFEL Score ≥ 60 or IELTS Score ≥ 6.
iii) Age should be less than 25 years.
Ph.D. Programme :
Masters Degree (PG) with 60% or above (or equivalent grade); M.Sc. 1.5 - 2 years or M.Tech. in relevant discipline;
ii) TOEFEL Score ≥ 60 or IELTS Score ≥ 6.
iii) Age should be less than 30 years.
Branch once allotted, will not be changed.
Hostel Accommodation if available may be provided (Hostel charges shall be charged separately).
The details of Tuition fee and Hostel fee are given in separate documents.
The candidate should join the Institute as per the registration schedule.
VNIT reserves the right of admission.
