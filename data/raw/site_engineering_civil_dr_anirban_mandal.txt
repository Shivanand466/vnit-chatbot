Brief Bio
Dr. Anirban Mandal, Professor.Dr. Mandal worked in EIL(PSU) and in NHPC(PSU) as Design Engineer for more than eight year before joining at VNIT-Nagpur. Dr. Mandal is involved in development of robust design techniques of underground structures.
