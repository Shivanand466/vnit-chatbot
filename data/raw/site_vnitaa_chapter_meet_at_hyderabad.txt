TITLE: VNITAA Chapter Meet at Hyderabad
FETCHED: 2026-11-19
CRAWL: full-2026-11-19
---
The second VNITAA Hyderabad Chapter Get-together was held on 9th November 2025 at the University of Hyderabad Auditorium, attended by around 125 participants, including Alumni and spouses. Dignitaries included VNIT Director-Dr. Prem Lal Patel, Dean International Alumni Affairs-Dr. Kishor Bhurchandi & Associate Dean Alumni Affairs-Dr. Ajay Likhite from VNIT. The Chapter Meet also witnessed the presence of Shri. Sharad Heda-Chairman VNIT AA, Shri. Shashikant Chaudhary-Founder Chairman-VNIT AA, Shri. Aditya Tohare-Director, & Shri. Siddharth Shah-Director from VNITAA. Director- Ghanshyam Krishna, Dean-Jai Prakash Gautam from the University of Hyderabad and Dr. Kota Bhanu Shankar from IITH also graced the occasion.
The event began with Lamp Lighting, followed by insightful discussions on Institute–Industry–Alumni collaboration and Startups, which inspired young Alumni. Bala Kishore shared thoughts on Digital Wellness and well-being. Alumni across batches relived VNIT nostalgia — from the Library and Admin Staircase pungi to Chandrama evenings.
Fun-filled moments included Shayari, birthday bumps, spouse interactions, games, and a campus tour, strengthening the bonding spirit. The meet concluded with a promise to reconnect every three months to sustain Alumni engagement.
