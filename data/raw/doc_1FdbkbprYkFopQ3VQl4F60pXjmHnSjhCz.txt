Interaction with German Universities on February 7, 2025 , at

VISIT REPORT
Submitted by: Dr. Ravikumar Dumpala, Associate Dean (Research)
Dated: 10
th February 2025
1. Objective of the Visit
The 1:1 Talk with German Universities provided an opportunity to explore potential collaborations between VNIT and leading universities from Baden-Württemberg, Germany , with a focus on:
Student and faculty exchange programs
Joint research collaborations
Industry-academia partnerships
Funding opportunities through DAAD, IGSTC, and Horizon Europe
2. Key Interactions and Universities Engaged
I engaged in discussions with representatives from eight German universities , each interested in collaborating with VNIT across different domains. The details of these universities, their key strengths, and potential collaboration areas are outlined below:
1. Karlsruhe University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr. Rose Marie Beck (President)
Website: HKA - Karlsruhe University
Core Competencies: Electrical, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering, Computer Science, Business, Media, and Mobility Studies.
Existing Engagement: VNIT faculty members are already engaged in collaborative research projects with HKA.
Potential Collaboration: Expanding research initiatives, student exchanges, and faculty mobility programs.
2. Reutlingen University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr. habil. Arjan Kozica (Vice President for Study, Teaching & Continuous Education)
Website: Reutlingen University
Core Competencies: Teaching, Education, Applied Research in Management, Informatics, Textile and Design, Mechanical Engineering, and Life Sciences.
Potential Collaboration: Joint degree programs (Bachelor/Master), student exchange, and collaborative research in applied sciences.
3. Mannheim University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr. Angelika Altmann-Dieses (President)
Website: Mannheim University
Core Competencies: Engineering, Technology, Design, and Social Work.
Potential Collaboration: STEM-focused research projects, student and faculty exchange, and industry tie-ups in biotechnology and engineering.
4. Offenburg University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr. Stephan Trahasch (Rector)
Website: Offenburg University
Core Competencies: Renewable Energy, IoT, Robotics, and AI.
Potential Collaboration: Research partnerships in AI, IoT, and Renewable Energy , student exchange programs, and collaboration with German industries.
5. Aalen University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr. Harald Riegel (Rector)
Website: Aalen University
Core Competencies: Material Sciences, Photonics, Resource Efficiency & Sustainability, AI, and Machine Learning.
Potential Collaboration: Joint research in AI, machine learning, and sustainable materials , faculty mobility, and student training programs.
6. Ulm University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr.-Ing. Christian Dettmann (Vice President for Research, Transfer, and International Affairs)
Website: Ulm University
Core Competencies: Engineering, IT, Modern Mobility, Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, and Energy Technology.
Potential Collaboration: Research in sustainable energy, mobility, and AI applications , student exchange, and double-degree programs.
7. Pforzheim University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr. Ingela Tietze (Vice President)
Website: Pforzheim University
Core Competencies: Sustainable Energy Economics, Carbon Footprinting, and Sustainability.
Potential Collaboration: Joint projects in sustainability, environmental impact assessments, and carbon management , student exchanges, and best practice sharing.
8. Stuttgart University of Applied Sciences
Representative: Prof. Dr. Volker Coors (Vice-President Research and Digitization)
Website: Stuttgart University
Core Competencies: Urban Digital Twins, Geospatial Intelligence, and GeoVisualization.
Potential Collaboration: Smart City initiatives, geo-informatics research, and collaboration with municipal bodies for urban development projects.
Delegation from the German Universities & Supporting Team from the Consulate and DAAD
3. DAAD Interaction & Support for VNIT
I also had a detailed discussion with Ms. Shikha Sinha, Senior Advisor - International Cooperation, DAAD , who assured:
Support for VNIT faculty and student mobility programs.
Guidance on DAAD funding programs for research collaborations and exchanges.
4. Key Takeaways & Future Course of Action
Immediate Next Steps:
Identify faculty and student nominees for exchange programs and joint research initiatives.
Organize a virtual follow-up meeting with interested German universities.
Engage with DAAD & IGSTC for funding applications.
Long-Term Goals:
Establish structured student/faculty exchange agreements .
Develop and submit joint research proposals with German partners.
5. Conclusion
The visit was highly productive, opening doors for VNIT to collaborate with multiple German institutions. The discussions will be followed up with formal actions to strengthen VNIT's global academic and research footprint through structured partnerships, funded projects, and faculty/student exchange programs.
Submitted by: Dr. Ravikumar Dumpala Associate Dean (Research) VNIT Nagpur
