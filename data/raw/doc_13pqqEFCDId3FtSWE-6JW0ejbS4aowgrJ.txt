Admission Offer Letters (Round-1 and Round-2) have been sent to the interviewed candidates for International Admissions in various courses of B.Tech., M. Tech., and Ph. D. programmes through Study in India (SII) - SOPs thereof.
Admission Offer Letters (Round-1 and Round-2) have been sent to the interviewed candidates for International Admissions for various courses of B. Tech., M. Tech., and Ph. D. programmes through Study in India (SII) by email messages.
The interested candidates have paid to accept the offer on or before Round-1: 15/06/2026 (2359 hrs IST) and Round-2: 13/07/2026 (2359 hrs IST) by paying the Non-refundable Seat Acceptance Fees of INR 35000/- (INR 5000/- as admission processing charges and INR 30000/- to be adjusted in semester fees, if admission is taken in the institute). If the candidate does not accept the offered seat with paying Seat Acceptance Fees by Round-1: 15/06/2026 (2359 hrs IST) and Round-2: 13/07/2026 (2359 hrs IST), he/she will loose the offered seat and the next Round/waitlist will be operated. If the admission offer is rejected by the candidate for any of the reasons at the latter stage, the Seat acceptance fees will be forfeited.
The Remaining First Semester Fees (One Time Fees, Semester Fees including Tuition Fees of USD2000 for SAARC/USD4000 for Non-SAARC) is to be paid on or before 27/07/2026 (2359 Hrs IST) . INR 30000/- (paid with SAF) is being adjusted in the semester fees. Please note that NRI SII-STUDENTS will not be considered as under SAARC country, even their passport is from INDIA.
If the candidate does not pay the Remaining First Semester Fees on or before 27/07/2026 (2359 Hrs IST), his or her admission will be cancelled and the SAF of INR 35000 paid will be forfeited.
After paying the Institute/Semester Fees if the student wants to cancel the admission before joining VNIT Nagpur, his/her SAF will be forfeited and 10% of Tuition fees will be deducted.
After paying the Institute/Semester Fees if the student wants to upgrade the accepted seat and joins in other branch of VNIT Nagpur in SII channel only, his/her SAF and Institute/Semester fees will be adjusted in full for new branch.
After paying the Institute/Semester Fees if the student wants to cancel the accepted seat and joins in other branch/same branch of VNIT Nagpur through other channel than SII, his/her SAF will be forfeited and after deduction of 10% of tuition fees, the remaining amount will be adjusted/refunded.

After paying the Institute/Semester Fees if the student wants to cancel the admission in VNIT Nagpur within one month of starting of session / commencement of classes, his/her SAF will be forfeited and after deduction of 10% of tuition fees, student will receive refund of other fees, Library & Caution Deposit paid by the student. After one month 100% of the Tuition Fees will be deducted, while other fees like Library & Caution Deposit will be refunded.
Refund of fees in case of admission cancellation at 2 nd year or higher year of studies: If a student is requesting admission cancellation within one month of starting of any session / commencement of classes, after deduction of 10% of tuition fees (paid in that current semester), student will receive refund of other fees + Library & Caution Deposit paid by the student at the time of admission. After one month 100% of the Tuition Fees will be deducted, while other fees like Library & Caution Deposit will be refunded.
Please note that the Physical Reporting at VNIT Nagpur is August 13-18, 2026. Immediately after, commencement of classes will be there.
