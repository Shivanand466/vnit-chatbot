2. Eligibility Criteria:
Educational Qualification: B.E. / B.Tech / B.Arch or equivalent degree in a relevant discipline.
Minimum Academic Requirement: Minimum CGPA of 6.0 (or equivalent).
Professional Experience: Candidates must satisfy at least one of the following:
 Minimum 5 years of relevant industry experience , or
 Minimum 5 years of teaching experience in academia , or
 Non-Teaching staff of academic institute with minimum 5 years of experience
2.4 GATE Qualification
 GATE qualification will not be mandatory for admission to this programme.
 Candidates who have qualified GATE may upload their GATE scorecard during application.
3. Application Requirements
Applicants must submit the following documents along with the required academic qualification documents:
Experience Certificate (as per prescribed format)
No Objection Certificate (NOC) from current employer
Sponsorship Certificate (if applicable)
Statement of Purpose (SOP)
SOP should consist of Research Execution Plan (maximum two pages):
o Tentative title
o Objectives
o Plan of work
o Domain of expertise
Application fee receipt
4. Selection Procedure
The selection process shall consist of:
 Presentation of the research proposal before the committee
 Personal interview
 Written entrance test (as decided by the Chairman, Board of Studies)
The evaluation will be conducted by a committee constituted by the Chairman, Board of Studies (BoS) of the respective department, comprising all BoS members.
5. Course Work
Course work will follow a structure similar to the PhD coursework mechanism , with a limited number of courses.
The typical coursework requirement may include:
 4-5 courses relevant to the research area
 Research Methodology (mandatory) - Pass/Fail
Courses may include:
 Departmental courses
 NPTEL courses
 MOOCs
 Hybrid learning modules
Course selection shall be recommended by the Research Progress Committee (RPC).
6. Programme Duration
The duration of the M. Tech. (Research) programme shall be:
 Minimum: 3 years
 Maximum: 5 years
7. Evaluation Mechanism
The evaluation process will follow a mechanism similar to the PhD programme , including:
 Research Progress Seminars (RPS)
 Final thesis submission and defense
A tentative credit structure is as follows:
The evaluation process will follow a mechanism similar to the PhD programme , including: | 7. Evaluation Mechanism: Course Work — Credits: 12-15
The evaluation process will follow a mechanism similar to the PhD programme , including: | 7. Evaluation Mechanism: Research Progress Seminars (5 Credits per RPS) — Credits: 30
The evaluation process will follow a mechanism similar to the PhD programme , including: | 7. Evaluation Mechanism: Thesis Defense — Credits: 10
The evaluation process will follow a mechanism similar to the PhD programme , including: | 7. Evaluation Mechanism: Minimum Total Credits Required for award of Degree with minimum 6 CGPA. — Credits: 52
8. Monitoring Mechanism
Academic monitoring will be carried out by:
 Supervisor
 Research Progress Committee (RPC)
 Chairman, Board of Studies
9. Thesis Submission Criteria
The candidate shall be eligible to submit the thesis after completion of a minimum of three (3) years from the date of registration, including the prescribed coursework and six research progress seminars (RPS), and subject to fulfilment of at least one of the following research publication requirements.
The candidate shall be eligible to submit the thesis after completion of a minimum of three (3) years from the date of registration, including the... | 9. Thesis Submission Criteria: Minimum ONE research publication in a Science Citation Index (SCI/SCIE/SSCI/AHCI) journal.
The candidate shall be eligible to submit the thesis after completion of a minimum of three (3) years from the date of registration, including the... | 9. Thesis Submission Criteria: Minimum TWO research publications in Scopus indexed journals, including Emerging Sources Citation Index (E-SCI) journals.
The candidate shall be eligible to submit the thesis after completion of a minimum of three (3) years from the date of registration, including the... | 9. Thesis Submission Criteria: Minimum ONE research publication in a Scopus indexed journal (including E-SCI journals) and minimum ONE granted patent based on the research topic.
10. Exit Policy
The candidate shall be eligible to submit the thesis after completion of a minimum of three (3) years from the date of registration, including the... | 10. Exit Policy: Exit Level 1 — Minimum Duration: After completion of 1 year; Academic Requirement: Completion of required coursework credits but research work not completed; Exit Award: Postgraduate Certificate in Engineering (Specialization Area)
The candidate shall be eligible to submit the thesis after completion of a minimum of three (3) years from the date of registration, including the... | 10. Exit Policy: Exit Level 2 — Minimum Duration: After completion of 2 years; Academic Requirement: Coursework completed and partial research work; Exit Award: Postgraduate Diploma in Engineering (Specialization Area)
11. Re-registration
If a candidate is unable to submit the thesis within the maximum duration of five (5) years, the candidate shall be required to apply for re-registration with the consent of the supervisor and approval of the Research Progress Committee (RPC) through the proper channel.
Candidates who do not submit the thesis after completion of the maximum duration must apply for re-registration every year until submission of the thesis, subject to the recommendation of the supervisor and approval of the RPC.
A re-registration fee of ₹25,000 per year shall be applicable for candidates seeking reregistration after completion of five (5) years from the date of initial registration.
12. Reservation Policy
The proposed seats are supernumerary in nature and hence no reservation shall apply .

Applications must be submitted exclusively through the designated Google Form.
o Candidates applying to multiple departments or programs must submit separate forms for each, with a corresponding Rs. 500/- fee per application.
o The Google Form link and related details are available on the official VNIT website: https://vnit.ac.in/section/academics/admission/ under ' M. Tech (Research) '
After submission, candidates will receive a PDF copy of their completed application via email. The printed and signed application form must be sent along with self-attested photocopies of relevant certificates and documents, and the payment receipt via speed post or hand delivery before the specified deadline to;
The Deputy Registrar (Academic),
Academic Section,
VNIT, South Ambazari Road,
Nagpur - 440010 (M.S.)
Candidates should clearly mention on the envelope: 'M.Tech (Research), Name of the Department, and the Name of the M.Tech Program for which they are applying.'
The last date for submission of the application form in hard copy (via speed post or hand delivery), as well as the deadline for uploading the Google Form, is 15.06.2026 .
Incomplete application and/or applications received after the last date are liable to be rejected.
