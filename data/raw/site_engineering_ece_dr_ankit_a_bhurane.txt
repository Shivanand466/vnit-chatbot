Dr. Ankit A. Bhurane
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Ankit A. Bhurane has received his B.E. in Electronics and Communication, M.Tech. in Electronics, and Ph.D. in 2008, 2011, and 2016 respectively from SGBAU University, SGGS Nanded, and Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB). He is following teaching as his passion and has 10+ years of experience teaching undergraduate and postgraduate courses at various institutes of national importances like Indian Institute of Information Technology, National Institute of Technology etc. He has published several papers in various International conferences and journals.  He has been active reviewer for reputed IEEE conference and Elsevier journals. He has 6 Indian Patent Granted, and 3 filed with IPR office, India. His research interested include AI for everything, wearables, biomedical signal processing, scalable video coding and green communication.
