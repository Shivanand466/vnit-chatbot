Dr. Saugata Sinha
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Saugata Sinha received his PhD degree in Imaging Science from Chester F Carlson Center for Imaging Science, Rochester Institute of Technology, USA in December 2014. He did his B. Tech in Optics & Optoelectronics from University of Calcutta in 2007. He worked as a Research Scientist in Opto electronics and Medical Electronics divisions of SAMEER [A Govt. of India Research & Development Institute], IIT Mumbai from 2007 to 2010. Following his PhD, he joined the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, India as an Assistant Professor in 2015.
