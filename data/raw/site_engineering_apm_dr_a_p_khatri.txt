Dr. A.P. Khatri
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. A. P. Khatri is Assistant Professor in the Department of Applied Mechanics at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, India. With a profound academic background, he obtained his B.E. in Civil Engineering in 2001, followed by an M. Tech. in Structural Engineering in 2003, and a Ph.D. in Structural Engineering in 2017. He has 5 years (during the year 2003-2008) of industrial experience in which he was instrumental in designing many residential buildings and office buildings for Government organizations and private industry. He has 15 years of research and teaching experience. His area of interest includes steel structures, RC structures, composite structures, buckling analysis, non-linear analysis, structural fire behaviour. He has 20 numbers of research publications in reputed Journals and Conferences. He is a life member of various institutions of engineers, civil engineers, and structural engineers. Dr. A. P. Khatri is dedicated to work as a driving influence in the fields of academics and research.
