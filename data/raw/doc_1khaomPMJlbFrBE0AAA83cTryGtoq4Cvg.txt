Applications Open!!!
We are delighted to announce that applications for the Narotam Sekhsaria Scholarships for Higher Studies 2026 are now open. In its commitment to fostering excellence, the Narotam Sekhsaria Foundation offers meritbased, interest-free repayable financial assistance to students pursuing postgraduate studies at leading institutions in India and abroad. Alongside financial support, recipients will also benefit from our mentoring programme, designed to guide them towards success and distinction in their chosen fields. Through this initiative, we aim to build a community of scholars, where each member can draw strength, inspiration, and knowledge from one another.
The scholarships are open to students throughout the country and across the fields of Pure Sciences, Applied Sciences, Social Sciences, Humanities, Engineering, Law and Management.
We encourage eligible students to review the programme details and apply within the timelines. Complete information on eligibility criteria and the application process is available on the website.
The applications are open until Wednesday, March 18, 2026.
Apply here: https://webportalapp.com/sp/narotam_application_portal26
For more information, visit us here: https://pg.nsfoundation.co.in/programme/
