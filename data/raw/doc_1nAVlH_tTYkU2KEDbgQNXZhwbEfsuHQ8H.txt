Dear Ma'am/ Sir,
We would like to bring to your attention the Shiksha Shakti Scholarship 2026-27 , an initiative of the Swami Dayanand Education Foundation (SDEF) supporting deserving young women as they begin their undergraduate education.
The scholarship provides up to ₹20,000 per academic year , renewable for up to three years, to eligible female students entering the first year of a full-time, nontechnical undergraduate programme in 2026-27.
Eligible programmes include: B.A. (all streams), B.Com., B.Sc. (non-engineering subjects), BBA, BCA, BA JMC/BJMC, BHM, BA LLB, BBA LLB, and other approved eligible programmes.
Key eligibility requirements:
Minimum 80% in Class XII or equivalent CGPA
Annual family income of ₹12 lakh or less
Admission to an eligible undergraduate programme
No gap/drop year after Class XII
Application Deadline: October 31, 2026
