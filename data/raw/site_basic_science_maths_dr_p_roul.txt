Brief Bio
Dr. Pradip Roul is an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics, VNIT, Nagpur. He has received his Ph.D degree in Mathematics from Otto von Guericke University, Germany in 2009. After finishing his doctoral studies, Dr. Roul joined the applied Numerical mathematics group at the University of Duisburg-Essen, Germany in June, 2009 as a postdoctoral researcher. Dr. Roul afterwards moved to the Institute of Applied Numerical mathematics, Potsdam University, Germany for another postdoctoral work. Prior to joining the VNIT, Nagpur, Dr. Roul was employed as a postdoctoral research fellow at the Institute of Mathematics, University of Paul Sabatier, France. He has 127 publications in international peer reviewed journals (SCI/SCIE indexed)  and contributed in book chapter. He has graduated 6 PhD and 26 M.Sc students. He is currently guding 5 more PhD students. His work has been sponsored  by the external funding agencies DST-SERB, NBHM, BRNS, CSIR and DST (MATRICS). Dr. Roul has completed sucessfully SIX R and D projects, as principal investigator. Dr. Roul is currently working as an editor for Mathematical Methods in the Applied Sciences (Wiley) and Journal of Applied Mathematics and Computing (Springer) and also serves as reviewer for many international scientific journals. His research focuses on design, analysis and implementation of robust numerical schemes for the efficient numerical solutions of boundary value problems for ordinary or partial differential equations which model various physical phenomena in applied science and engineering. Mainly, Dr. Roul studies degenerate parabolic partial differential equation of integer or fractional order for financial market (American, European and Asian options), hyperbolic and elliptic problems for elastic solid mechanics, parabolic problems for biological sciences, a system of partial differential equation of fractional order arising in nuclear reactor dynamics and a strongly nonlinear singular BVP governing electrohydrodynamic flow of a fluid in an iron drag configuration in a circular cylindrical conduit and modelling and simulation of ground water pollution problem.
