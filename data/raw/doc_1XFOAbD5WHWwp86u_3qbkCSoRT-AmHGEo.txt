General Guidelines for the Use of Air Coolers (Summer Season Only)
Air coolers are permitted for use during the summer season only in the hostels.
The cooler shall be placed only at the designated location identified for such installation and shall not obstruct movement, passages, or common areas.
Residents shall ensure that the electrical load of the cooler is within the permissible limit and does not cause overloading of the hostel electrical system.
Only reputed brand air coolers in good working condition with proper earthing, should be used.
Loose wiring, temporary connections, or substandard electrical accessories are strictly prohibited. Proper plug points and safe wiring must he used at all times
Fibre body air coolers must not be operated without water, as it may lead to overheating and potential fire hazards.
Residents must switch off the cooler when leaving the room and must not leave it running unattended.
Residents shall ensure regular cleaning and maintenance of the cooler, including timely replacement of water, to avoid unhygienic conditions and breeding of mosquitoes.
Residents shall strictly adhere to all electrical and safety compliance norms while installing and operating the cooler.
Any damage caused to Institute/Hostel property due to installation, operation, leakage, or improper use of the cooler shall be recovered from the concerned resident(s).
Residents are required to take all necessary precautions to ensure their own safety as well as the safety of fellow inmates. Any additional safety measures, even if not explicitly mentioned in these guidelines, shall be deemed applicable.
Violation of the above guidelines may lead to removal of the cooler and other disciplinary action as per hostel rules.
