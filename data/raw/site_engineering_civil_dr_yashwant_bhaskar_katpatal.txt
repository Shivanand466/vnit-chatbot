Dr. Yashwant Bhaskar Katpatal
Department Head, Professor
Brief Bio
Dr. Yashwant B. Katpatal is a Professor (HAG) at VNIT Nagpur. He joined VNIT Nagpur in 1993 after a brief stint at National Environmental Engineering Research Institute Nagpur from 1992-93. His research interest are in the area of Spatial Analysis through Remote Sensing data and Geographical Information System. Dr. Yashwant Katpatal was awarded several research projects sponsored by external agencies like MHRD, DST, CPHEEO, ISRO, BALCO, NARL etc. He has been granted 7 patents. Dr. Katpatal is passionate about ‘Earth Systems’ and human interaction, hence his area of research focusses on impact assessment using spatial analyses. Dr Katpatal has also worked as Dean (SC) during 2005-2009, Associate Dean (International Affairs) during 2017-2019, Chairman Special Cell of VNIT during 2014-2017, Head of Civil Engineering Department during 2021-2024. He is curently Dean ( Research and Consultancy) at VNIT since Jan 2024.  Dr Katpatal has Published more than 300 Research Papers in Int./National Journals/conferences/books. He has been invited to several international conferences as Speaker and member of the jury including Int. Symposium on Environment Management at Athens, Greece (1997); International Conference on Solid Waste Management at Philadelphia, USA (2008), Int. Conference on Earthquake Engineering at London, UK (2013) (Jury Member), AGU Int. conference at Washington, USA (2019) where he was also Paper presentation Judge.He believes in sharing scientific information and has delivered many Invited/ Expert lectures at various platforms like AICTE/ISTE/UGC/CPHEEO STTP, IWWA, Institution of Engineers, GSI, GTZ, WAPCOS, IPHE, MRSAC, RTMNU, RRSSC, NIT Bhopal, NIT Raipur, IIT Kanpur, GHRCOE, Amravati University, GSDA, CGWB, NBSS&LUP, Institution of town planners, Rotary & Lions club, ESRI, MOIL, NEERI, ACCE, RKNEC,PCOE,BDCOE,TIAG,IBM, DST, UGC Academic Staff College, etc. He has Guided 26 students for Ph. D. Degree, 103 students for M. Tech Thesis on Remote Sensing and GIS applications for water resources management, environment engineering, and urban planning. He is reviewer for many International Journals. Based on his research contributions, Dr Katpatal has been granted 7 patents while another 4 Patents on RS and GIS have been filed. Dr. Katpatal has completed more than 50 Consultancies in the areas of RS GIS applications to several organizations.He has been awarded several awards and best paper prizes for his contributions. He received ‘Young Scientist Award on his Doctoral Work, ‘Kamakshi Gold Medal’ by World Geospatial Forum, ‘Brijnandan Sharma Award’ from IWWA, University Gold Medal for standing First in the Country in Managment Program of IGNOU. He has Membership of various Professional Societies : Fellow/Life Member of Indian Society of Remote Sensing, Indian Water Works Association, Indian Water Resources Society, Indian Geological Congress, Indian Society for Technical Education, The Indian Society for Hydraulics, Institution of Engineers (India), Maharashtra Academy of Sciences, Mining Engineers Society, Agrometeorological Society, American Geophysical Union (AGU), European Association of Geophysicist and Engineers (2013-14) etc. He has organized several International /National Conference and Training Programs as Convener/Organizing secretary / Coordinator. Dr. Yashwant Katpatal believes that ‘Earth System and its sustainability’ is most important for mankind. He very much likes teaching and interaction with students.
