Dr. Sunil Tonpe , Retired general manager at Nuclear Fuel complex, Hyderabad an alumnus of VNIT of 1983 batch interacted with the students of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering on 2nd February 2022 giving career insights of a metallurgist and addressing students queries regarding academics, higher studies and future scope of metallurgy and materials engineering.
