Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Yogesh Y Mahajan joined the department in 2006. He has taught subjects like Composite Materials, steel Making Technology, Theory and Technology of Heat Treatment, Thermodynamics and Kinetics, and Non-Destructive Testing. His research is focused on the Synthesis of Aluminum Metal Matrix Composite and studing the Effect of Heat Treatment parameters on material properties.
