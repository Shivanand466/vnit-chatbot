Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Mohan Kumar Mallick is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics,Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur. He received his Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences from IIT Madras in 2019 and subsequently worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow at TIFR-CAM, Bangalore. Prior to joining VNIT Nagpur, he served as an Assistant Professor at SRM University-AP.
His research lies in nonlinear analysis and partial differential equations, with emphasis on fully nonlinear elliptic and parabolic PDEs, Pucci extremal operators, principal eigenvalue problems, shape optimization, singular nonlinearities, multiplicity theory, and regularity theory. He has also worked on reaction-diffusion equations, mathematical biology, ecological models, nonlinear boundary conditions, and optimal control problems. His research articles have appeared in reputed journals including Communications on Pure and Applied Analysis, Topological Methods in Nonlinear Analysis, Monatshefte für Mathematik, Evolution Equations and Control Theory, Mathematical Methods in the Applied Sciences, Journal of Evolution Equations, and Journal of Differential Equations.
Dr. Mallick is the Principal Investigator of an ARG-MATRICS research grantfunded by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation, Government of India.He is currently guiding research students in fully nonlinear PDEs and has supervised 5 M.Sc. projects. He has delivered invited talks at various national andinternational academic events and has also served as Convener of the five-dayworkshop “Partial Differential Equations: Theory and Numerics” at VNIT Nagpur in March 2026.

Research
Interest
Research Interest 1. Fully Nonlinear Elliptic and Parabolic Partial Differential Equations: Study of Pucci extremal operators, non-divergence form equations, existence, multiplicity, and regularity theory.
2. Nonlinear Analysis, Eigenvalue Problems and Shape Optimization: Investigation of principal eigenvalues, Pólya-type spectral problems, singular nonlinearities, bifurcation, and nonlinear boundary conditions.
3. Mathematical Biology, Population Dynamics and Optimal Control: Analysis of reaction-diffusion models, ecological systems, harvesting problems, and control-theoretic approaches.
