Assistant Professor
Brief
Bio bio
Dr. Amrut Agasti has joined the department as Assistant Professor in May 2020. He did his B. Tech. form VNIT, Nagpur and M. Tech.+Ph.D. from IIT Bombay. His doctoral thesis was on “Electrodepotion of CZTS thin films for application in solar cells”. His area of research is on batteries, solar cells, and electrochemistry. He has taught courses like Principles of Non-Ferrous Metal extraction, Corrosion science and Surface Engineering, Alloy Steels and High Temperature Alloys, Materials for Biomedical Applications.
