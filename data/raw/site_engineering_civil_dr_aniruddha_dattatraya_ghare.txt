Dr. Aniruddha Dattatraya Ghare
Professor
Brief Bio
Dr A D Ghare is a Professor of Civil Engineering with specialisation in Hydraulics and Water Resources Engineering. His research interests include Computational Hydraulics, Baffled Flumes, Stepped and Labyrinth Spillways, River Analysis and Flood Routing.
