All scholars who are allotted Seat in VNIT for IPDF program for Academic Year 2025-26 are required to report at VNIT, Nagpur as per the schedule given by the respective admission authority. For reporting to VNIT, scholars are required to fill the details on VNIT online admission portal and complete the reporting procedure. Following are the steps to complete the reporting procedure:
STEP 1:
Log in on VNIT online admission portal (link https://aimsreg.vnit.ac.in/mproaimsreg/#/ )
To avoid technical difficulties while filling the information on VNIT online portal, Candidates are advised to clear the Browser History every time before logging to VNIT online admission portal.
Fill all the details on VNIT online admission portal and upload photo, signature and the documents as listed below. Scholars are requested to go through the FAQs before logging on online portal.
(FAQ Link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/13iCIsDXpKABLVUItoCV2bx7lsRsC6jyn/view
STEP 2: After completely filling up the information on VNIT online admission portal, Scholar need to email the signed copy of the submitted form on the email acadsection@vnit.ac.in acknowledging the completion of the process.
STEP 3: Once the email is received, documents and other details of the candidate will be verified by the VNIT officials and Provisional Reporting Letter would be issued to the scholar. This completes the Physical reporting of the candidate.
