1 Preamble
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) (formerly VRCE), Nagpur, is one of the first six Regional Engineering Colleges established under the scheme sponsored by the Government of India and the State Governments. The College was established in June 1960 and named after the eminent engineer, planner, and statesman of the country Bharat Ratna, Sir M. Visvesvaraya. Over the years, this Institution has gained recognition as an important National Institution in the field of Technical Education & research. Apart from imparting professional education at undergraduate and postgraduate levels, the Institute has been extending available expertise to various Govt./Semi-Govt. and private organizations through testing and consultancy works. The Institute also organizes short-term courses and seminars for the benefit of those in the profession. The Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology was enacted under NIT Act 29 of 2007 by Govt. of India and declared an Institute of National importance.
VNIT campus is spread over 214 acres near Ambazari Lake. All departments and laboratories are housed on the campus, which also offers hostel accommodation for students and residential accommodation for teaching and non-teaching staff. The campus has retained and increased its green cover, which is rich in flora and fauna and attracts bird watchers.
Institutional Vision
To contribute effectively to the national and international endeavor of producing quality human resources of a world-class standard by developing a sustainable technical education system to meet the changing technological needs of the country and the world, incorporating relevant social concerns and building an environment to create and propagate innovative technologies for the economic development of the nation.
Institutional Mission
The mission of VNIT is to achieve high standards of excellence in generating and propagating knowledge in engineering and allied disciplines. VNIT is committed to providing an education that combines rigorous academics with the joy of discovery. The Institute encourages its community to engage in a dialogue with society to be able to effectively contribute for the betterment of humankind.
2 Programs Offered
The institute offers four-year undergraduate programs in Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Computer Science Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mining Engineering, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, Chemical Engineering, and a five-year course in Architecture. In addition, the institute offers fulltime (M. Tech) and Ph. D. programs. A three-year Ph.D. program under the QIP scheme is also offered by the institute in various departments. The laboratories in the departments have excellent research and testing facilities. The institute has acquired highly sophisticated equipment for various Departments, and new equipment has been added to various laboratories in recent years.

Further, note that all these candidates will also be eligible to apply for the Institutional Spot Round (ISR) against vacancies after Phase-III (National Spot Round) of the CCMT 2026 schedule. These candidates need to submit separate online application forms to VNIT for participation in ISR.

11 Conduct and Discipline

Candidates applying for admission against the seats reserved for backward classes (SC/ST/OBC/OM) should note that only those candidates who actually belong to the respective castes and communities will be eligible for admission under the reserved category.
