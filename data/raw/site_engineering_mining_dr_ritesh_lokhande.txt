Dr. Ritesh Lokhande
Brief Bio
Dr. Ritesh D. Lokhande received his Ph.D. from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad in 2013 after earning his B.E. in Mining Engineering from Nagpur University in 1999. His research areas are ground control, mine design, instrumentation and monitoring, and slope stability. Over 70 technical research papers have been published in national and international journals and conferences. He has completed six R&D projects and more than 40 consultancy projects related to various research areas with different industries. Presently, He has more than 23 years of experience in research and academic institutions. In recognition of his research efforts, Dr. Lokhande has won accolades. Two Ph.D.s were awarded under his direction, and ten M Tech students finished their project work
