Guidelines for Hassle-Free Physical Reporting at VNIT
To ensure a smooth and time-efficient physical reporting process at VNIT, please follow these steps carefully:
Pre-Reporting Checklist
Read All Instructions Thoroughly
Visit the VNIT Admission Instructions page and carefully go through the guidelines under
INSTITUTE REPORTING INSTRUCTIONS . (Link : https://vnit.ac.in/section/academics/admission/)
Complete Online Registration
Before arriving at VNIT, complete your registration via the VNIT AIMS Portal. (Link : https://aimsreg.vnit.ac.in/mproaimsreg/#/ )
Pay the Balance Fees
Make the necessary fee payment at Step No 4 in registration portal. Refer to the details of balance fee to be paid in Common Fees_2026-27 - Balance Fee Payment Details. (link : know the Balance fee : https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rJPhf1A-H_BRghbiET-dAJfHWNSDKTPv/view )
Required Documents
Bring all documents listed in the Common Documents Checklist. Ensure you have both originals and self-attested photocopies. Self-attested copies must be arranged in the same order as listed in the Common Document Checklist. (Link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1olwvg_4GXlEyT8pwEb82kYPP0HLpv2Sx/view )
No Printing Facilities Available Nearby
Please carry all required hard copies to avoid delays or inconvenience. Printing services are not available near the reporting center.
Keep Digital Copies as a backup
Carry a pen drive containing PDFs of all required documents, Passport-size photo (JPEG format), Signature file (JPEG format)
Consult FAQs Before Reaching Out
Refer to the FAQs section on the VNIT Admission Page for common queries before calling or emailing the institute (Link : https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kaGQPS1Ev3PXkKhblag9zCNvuzABFQlj/view).
ı . Important Note :
To avoid delays and complications during physical reporting, come fully prepared . Candidates who fail to complete online registration, fee payment, document uploads, or printouts in advance may face difficulties.
Due to heavy / high mobile usage at the venue (by both candidates and parents), accessing online portals or making payments on-site may be difficult.
We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to streamlined and effective Physical Institute reporting
