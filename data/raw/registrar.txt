REGISTRAR: CMA S. S. JAGDALE.
CMA Shri S.S. Jagdale joined VNIT Nagpur as Dy Registrar in Aug-2016 and worked as Section Head of Accounts Section. He was granted up-gradation as Joint Registrar w.e.f. 04th October 2021. He is B.E. (Mechanical Engg.), M.B.A. (Financial Management) and a qualified Cost and Management Accountant (CMA). He is a fellow member of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India.
He has over 19 years of experience in Accounts, Audit, Taxation, Banking and Financial Management in corporate and educational institutions. Before joining VNIT Nagpur, he has served as Assistant Registrar (Finance) in Defence Institute of Advanced Technology, Pune  (Deemed to be University under DRDO, Ministry of Defence), Manager (F&A) in Maharashtra State Power Generation Co.Ltd. (a State PSU) and Officer (Credit) in Dena Bank.
He has worked in various Institute level Committees as Finance/ Accounts Member. He was an external member in purchase committees of NIT Goa and NIT Andhra Pradesh. He was a member of the Finance Committee of IIITDM Kurnool.
He assumed the charge of the post of the Registrar on 30th  December 2024.
The Registrar is responsible to the Director for the proper discharge of his function
He is the custodian of record and common seal of the Institute and such other property of the Institute as the Board of Governors commit to his charge.
He deals with legal matters pertaining to administration.
He is the Chief Administrative Officer of the Institute.
