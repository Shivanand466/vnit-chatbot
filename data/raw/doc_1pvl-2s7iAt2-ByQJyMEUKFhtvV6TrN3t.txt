Dear Sir/Madam,
Greetings of the day!
We are pleased to share that applications for the SDEF "Smt. Shyam Lata Garg" India Scholarships 2024-25 are currently open for students pursuing professional courses, including engineering, medical, architecture, etc., and other undergraduate programs at government or private institutions in India. Sharing more programme details below for your reference:
Eligibility:
Eligible Courses: Students must be enrolled in professional courses (engineering, medical, architecture) or other undergraduate programs at recognized institutions in India.
Academic Excellence:
Class 12: Minimum 80% in CBSE or 70% in other boards.
Second Year: Minimum CGPA of 8.0.
Income Limit: Annual family income should not exceed INR 8 lakhs.
Year of Study: Open to first and second-year students; one-year drop allowed for technical courses.
Additional Requirements:
Engineering & Architecture: All India Overall Rank must be less than 90,000.
Medical: All India Overall Rank must be less than 40,000.
Age Limit:
First-year: Under 19 years.
Second-year: Under 20 years.
Award: Up to INR 50,000 per annum over the duration of their degree
Application Deadline: December 31, 2024
Application URL: http://www.b4s.in/a/em_SDEFSL1_20241001_1
Helpline: scholarships@swamidayanand.org or (+91) -120-4146823
If you know someone eligible for this scholarship, please share this information with them.
