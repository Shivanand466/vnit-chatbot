DearSir/Madam.
This is to inform you that the members of the IEI Students' Chapters (both engineering college and polytechnic) are eligible to apply for scholarships instituted by The Institution of Engineers (India).
01 October 2024
Award of Scholarship for the Financial Year 2024-25
The scholarships are awarded only to members of the IEI Students' Chapters, fulfilling the following criteria:
(i) Possesses good conduct;
throughout (that is in Class X/Class XIl/equivalent/all semester of engineering college/polytechnic) (iv) Not availing scholarship or any other financial assistance from any other sources;
(ii)Regular full-time student of an engineering college / a poiytechnic; (iii)Have passed all subjects of the qualifying examination with an aggregate of minimum 60% marks
A copy of the Blank Application Form along with the format of Income Certificate is enclosed for your perusal. The participating students can use photocopies of the Application Form. The application form alongwith the enclosures can also be downloaded from our website following the link https://www.ieindia.org/webui/IEl-Network.aspx#students-chapters
Please, arrange to circulate the above information through the Principal to all eligible student members. The Chapter must forward applications (duly filled in and signed by student/s, after endorsing the same by the Principal)latest by 15November 2024. Applications received at the Seeretariat after the last date shall not be considered.
We sincerely hope that this scheme of the Institution would be of great encouragement to your students. We seek your kind co-operation and active support in popularizing the scheme.
Thanking you.

EXTRACT OF NORMS FOR AWARD OF SCHOLARSHIPS TO MEMBERS OF THE STUDENTS' CHAPTERS AT ENGINEERING COLLEGES AND POLYTECHNICS
An applicant must fulfill the following criteria:
S/he should be a member of the Students' Chapter of the Institution of Engineers (India),
S/he possesses a good conduct,
S/he must be a regular and full-time student,
S/he should cleared all subjects in Class X / Class XII / odd and even Semester Examinations in one chance (Annual Examinations, if the annual pattern is followed) with an aggregate of minimum60% marks ; and
S/he must be a non-recipient of any other scholarship/ free stipendship.
The applicant should use the prescribed application form, complete the same in all respect, and submit along with Income Certificate in the prescribed form. Photocopy of the prescribed form can also be used.
