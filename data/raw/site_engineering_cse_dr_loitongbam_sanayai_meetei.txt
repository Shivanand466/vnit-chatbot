Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Loitongbam Sanayai Meetei is an Assistant Professor at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. Prior to joining VNIT, he worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati (IIT Guwahati) and as an Assistant Professor at Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) University, Bhubaneswar. During his PhD, he was also a Visiting Researcher at Saarland University, Germany. He has prior industry experience with multinational and IT companies. His research interests include Machine Translation, Human Language Technology, Multimodal AI/ML and Low-Resource Language Processing.
