ABOUT THE CONFERENCE
NSC51
Clich here for the conference brochure
VNIT is proud to host the 51st edition of the National Symposium on Crystallography, in association with the Indian Crystallographic Association
.
The NSC is an annual event of the ICA that provides a platform for crystallographers from across the country to meet and discuss their latest research findings. Scientists, post-docs, and students working in different areas of crystallography/structural biology are welcome to participate and present their research.
Crystallography is the experimental science of determining the arrangement of atoms in crystalline solids. It has to do with figuring out how the atoms in crystalline solids are arranged and bound, as well as the geometric layout of crystal lattices. NSC provides the perfect platform to scientists and researchers from all facets of crystallography to present their latest research work in the form of a lecture or a poster.
Conference Topics
• Macromolecular Crystallography
• Structural Bioinformatics
• Small Molecule Crystallography
• Crystallography in drug design and pharmaceuticals
• Crystal growth and characterization
• Crystals for device applications
• In situ Cryo- and high temperature-crystallography
• Synchrotron radiation, Small-angle X-ray Scattering
• Powder diffraction & Rietveld refinement
• Quantum Crystallography & Chemical Bonding
• Metal / non-Metal-organic frameworks
• Solid State Nuclear Magnetic Resonance
• Functional materials: structure and defects
• Smart and advanced Materials
• Electron and Neutron Diffraction
• Crystals for Molecular Magnetism
• Mechano-responsive Crystals

Originality and outcomes of the research
Clarity of objectives and methods
Significance and potential impact of results
Adherence to ethical standards
Language, clarity, and completeness

The Satellite Workshop on Materials Characterization Using Powder X-ray Diffraction will take place ahead of the main seminar NSC51 on crystallography. This workshop will focus on the techniques and applications of powder X-ray diffraction, which is widely used for the structural analysis and characterization of materials. Participants will learn about the principles of PXRD, data collection, and interpretation, offering valuable insights for researchers working in material science and crystallography. The SWMC-PXRD will be highly interactive, combining lectures with hands-on sessions where participants will solve problems ranging from basic to advanced, reinforcing key concepts. SWMCPXD will cover both data mining using the ICDD PDF-5+
® database and methods of analysis embodied in ICDD PDF-5+
®
Sieve+ and ICDD MDI JADE
®
Pro software. Hands-on access to PDF- 5+
® and
JADEPro
® will be available through a remote desktop server via web access. Attendees should bring their laptop computer with wireless web access (Edge, Chrome, Firefox).
Note:
Mac laptop (macOS) users should have Windows emulator installed.
The first day of the workshop will present the PDF-5+® databases, database overview, features, datamining capabilities and Sieve+ for phase identification and quantification (RIR method).The second day will be qualitative and quantitative XRD analyses (Rietveld) using MDI JADE® Pro software. In addition, materials characterization using the JADE Pro Toolkit features will be demonstrated.
The PDF-5+ database and JADE Pro software will be accessible through a remote desktop server for a period of 3 weeks after the workshop.
