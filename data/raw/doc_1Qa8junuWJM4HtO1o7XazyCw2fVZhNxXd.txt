APPLIED MECHANICS DEPARTMENT Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur (India)
Post Graduate Diploma (PGD) in Forensic Structural Engineering (Online Mode)
This one-year program is tailored for Civil and Structural Engineers seeking specialized knowledge in Forensic Structural Engineering, Structural Health Monitoring, Structural Condition Assessment, and Retrofitting of Structures.
A diverse selection of courses is offered, allowing students to choose subjects that align with their interests while completing a few mandatory courses.
To complete the program, students must submit a thesis based on a substantial case study or research project, demonstrating their acquired expertise and practical application in the field. The laboratory courses shall be held offline for two weeks per semester.

 No requirement of GATE score
