Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Associate Professor
Anil Mokhade (he/him) is a dedicated educator and mentor who fosters a supportive and integrity-driven learning environment. With a rich blend of academic and professional experience, Anil is committed to educational excellence and continuous learning, contributing significantly to the field of Computer Science & Engineering, while nurturing the growth and success of students
Professional Experience:
Former positions at Wipro InfoTech and the National Institute of Science, Technology, and Development Studies, New Delhi
Three years of administrative experience as Deputy CEO in the Rural Development Department, Government of Maharashtra
Teaching Experience:
27 years to date at VNIT, Nagpur in the Department of Computer Science &amp; Engineering
Courses taught:
Business Data Processing, Business Information Systems, Software Architecture, Computer Networks, Computer Graphics, Operating Systems, Software Engineering
Currently teaching:
Software Engineering to B. Tech. CSEs;  Introduction to Systems Engineering, Learning From Software Technical History to B.Tech, M.Tech, and Research Scholars of CSE dept.

Additional Roles and Contributions:
Undertook two research projects and developed a lab.
Conducting consultancy services
Organizing Faculty Development Programs (FDPs) and delivering guest lectures at various engineering colleges
Serving on the Board of Studies for two engineering colleges
Undertaking various responsibilities assigned by the department and the institute with utmost dedication
Organized professional training for the Student Mentor Program for six years from its inception.
