Prof. Rakesh Parmar
Assistant Professor
Brief Bio
Rakesh Parmar is a Landscape Architect and Academician, holding a Post-Graduation with Gold Medal from the School of Planning and Architecture, New Delhi, and an undergraduate degree with Gold Medal from the School of Planning and Architecture, Vijayawada. With notable corporate experience at TATA Consulting Engineers, he contributed significantly to Smart City projects, specializing in Landscape planning and design for Urban open space development, including Smart Street design and Landscape Planning for Ranchi, Agartala, Rourkela, and Bhopal Smart Cities. His career seamlessly blends practical expertise from handling Government and Private Sector projects with a passion for education, focusing on integrating real-time issues into his teaching approach and aligning architectural learning with industry transformations.
