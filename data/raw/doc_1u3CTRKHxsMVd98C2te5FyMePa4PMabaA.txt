Document: Financial Assistance by NSFDC to SC students under Education loan scheme admitted during 2024-25 (dated 2024-08-06)
Madam/Sir,
The National Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (NSFDC) a Public Sector Undertaking of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India is implementing a Key Initiatives in the first 100 days (June-September, 2024) to provide Education Loans to eligible SC Students selected for professional courses of IIMs, IITs, IIITs and other institutes of excellence for the Academic Year starting 2024. It is now been decided by the Government to extend this initiative to 165 Institutions of National Importance as per the list displayed on the Portal of Ministry of Education, Government of India.
The Education Loans are extended to Scheduled Caste Students having Annual Family Income of up to Rs.3.00 lakh, through the refinance model wherein the 10 Public Sector Bank Partners of NSFDC first provide Education Loans to the eligible SC Students and then claim refinance from NSFDC. Post refinance by NSFDC, the interest rate is reduced from the Bank rate of 8-9% to NSFDC's concessional rate of 5.5% for female students and 6% for male students applicable for full time professional /technical courses in India. The 10 Public Sector partners of NSFDC are PNB, CBI, IOB,UBI, Punjab & Sind Bank, Indian Bank, Bank of Maharashtra, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank & Bank of India.
In case your Institution being an Institution of National Importance has admitted SC Students for the current and recent past Academic Sessions and if they have been sanctioned Bank Loans, kindly furnish the list of such SC Students along with their contact details (address, mobile number, E-mail ID etc.) so that NSFDC can capture their caste and income details through a Google Form and link them to the Public Sector Banks for granting Education Loans and obtaining refinance claims from Banks to extend the benefit of concessional interest rate as per NSFDC's Education Loan Scheme. The shortlisted particulars of SC Students in case admission is finalized, may be shared to NSFDC latest by 09.08.2024 .
