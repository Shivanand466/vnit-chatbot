Exploring the Campus Birdlife!
The Campus of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur is a birding hotspot in Central India and is home to over 100 species of birds, both residents, and migrants. To cherish and share the beauty of the amazing Birdlife at our campus, a concise literary and pictorial compilation – ‘Amusing Avifauna of VNIT’ was released by the Institute. This book has been crafted by putting together character sketches of birds through prose and poetry to help readers get familiar with them, along with a detailed photo gallery of the winged species found at VNIT. The link to the book can be found below. Hoping the book is an enjoyable read and inspires more nature lovers into the Birding Fraternity of VNIT!
