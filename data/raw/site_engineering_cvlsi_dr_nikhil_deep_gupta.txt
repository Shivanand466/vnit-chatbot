Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Nikhil Deep Gupta is currently serving as an Assistant Professor with the Centre for VLSI and Nanotechnology at VNIT, Nagpur, India. Nikhil Deep Gupta is a recipient of prestigious IUSSTF, INSA Visiting Scientist and NPDF awards. He was a visiting scholar at Purdue University, West Lafayette, Indiana, USA for six months. Dr. Nikhil's research interests are in wide bandgap semiconductors based optoelectronic devices including solar cells, power electronics devices, photonic crystals and photonic crystal based devices. His research group aims to collaboratively solve societal challenges in energy, security, and water for a safe and sustainable future for all. Dr. Nikhil is currently involved in the design of various active photonic devices including both sources and detectors and the solar cells.  Nikhil and his team has recently proposed an exciting multi-pixel photodetector design to operate in UV-blue region of spectrum for low intensity light condition in collaboration with ISRO that achieved a TRL level of 6. The work which was done under a Indian Space Research Organization grant, published in IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices and already has one patent granted and another published for the work done. In another front, Nikhil’s group has been working for the design and development of perovskite-Silicon tandem solar cells design and already a PhD student has completed his PhD on the topic and an Indian patent has granted to the group. Nikhil has also other ongoing project for designing high power LASER diode for Indian defence organization (DRDO), Royal Society of UK, CSIR as well as a project under India-Taiwan cooperation for the design of LED sources among others. His group has achieved major funding milestone in the form of DST FIST (India) having value of INR 2.7 Crores.One thing which is common in all the subject fields is the use of nanophotonic structures and their study for the performance enhancement for various photonics applications. Some of the major breakthrough from the research group is the successful design of sub-nanometer designs using e-beam lithography technique and proving their role in performance improvement of solar cells and photodiodes.He has bachelor’s degree in the field of Electronics and Communication Engineering followed by masters in Energy Engineering. He did work as a project fellow for a design of Photonic Crystals based devices and later did his Ph.D. degree for the design of photonic crystal based solar cells. Following PhD, he worked towards the design of photolectrochemical cells for hydrogen production. He has authored more than 50 research papers and has one patent. He is the senior member of the Optica, IEEE and SPIE; and Life Fellow of Optical Society of India. He has served as the Chair of the Optica – Optics for Energy Technical Group from 2022-24. He has also served as the Secretary of the IEEE Nagpur sub-section and the Treasurer of the IEEE Photonics Society Bombay Section for different periods.
