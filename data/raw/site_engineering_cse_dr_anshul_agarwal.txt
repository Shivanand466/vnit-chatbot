Dr. Anshul Agarwal​
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Anshul Agarwal is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at VNIT Nagpur, a position he has held since 2020. He received his M.Tech and Ph.D. from the CSE department, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay (2014–2019). He holds two patents in the areas of sensor networks and fault detection. He is currently leading three AI-focused research and technology development projects sponsored by the Ministry of Defense. He has been recognized with several prestigious honors, including the Young Researcher Award by the STEM Research Society and the TCS Research Scholarship. He has authored more than 30 research papers in reputed international journals and conferences, including a paper on minimal sensor placement that was nominated for the Best Paper Award at ACM BuildSys, which he presented and demonstrated at Stanford University, California, USA. His paper was also conferred the Best Paper Award in the Cyber-Physical Systems theme at the International Conference ISAMET 2026. He has additionally contributed two book chapters on smart grid analytics. His research interests span Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Game Theory, Cyber-Physical Systems, Internet of Things (IoT), and Smart Grids. He actively contributes to the research community as a reviewer for leading journals and conferences. He has independently designed and developed the course CSL426: Game Theory for undergraduate and postgraduate students of the Department. The course provides a comprehensive treatment of game theory and its applications to solve real-world problems. It covers strategic games and Nash equilibrium, mixed-strategy equilibria, extensive-form games with perfect and imperfect information, repeated games, Bayesian games, and applications including duopoly markets (Cournot, Bertrand, Stackelberg), auctions, electoral competition, and the war of attrition.
