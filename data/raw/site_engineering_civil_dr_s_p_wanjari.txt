Dr. S.P. Wanjari
Associate Professor
Brief Bio
Myself Swapnil P Wanjari, working as Asst. Professor at VNIT since 2006. Prior joining to VNIT Nagpur, I had worked various project like Power Plant Construction,Refinery work construction, Tendering, Contracting etc at during my  work tenure 1999-2005 at L&T ( Construction Division). After joining VNIT since 2006, I had been founder member of starting M.Tech in Construction Technology & Management in VNIT Nagpur from  2009. There after worked with various consultancy and reserach work on Concrete design; Structural audit etc for various Govt Power Plant Projects, Building , Bridges etc.  My areas of research are Concrete Technology; Construction Materials and Bio- cementation.
