Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026
Student Advisory for Internal Hackathon Date: 21 Aug 2026
The Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 provides an excellent opportunity for students to solve real-world problems proposed by various Ministries, Government Departments, Industries, and Organizations. Students interested in participating are advised to start preparing immediately.
What Should You Do?
Visit the Official SIH 2026 Website
Carefully study the published Problem Statements .
Select problem statements that match your interests, skills, and domain knowledge.
Understand the background, objectives, expected outcomes, and constraints of the chosen problem statement(s).
2. Form Your Team
Teams must consist of six (6) students .
At least one member must be a female student.
All team members must belong to VNIT.
Students from different branches are encouraged to form multidisciplinary teams , as diverse expertise often leads to better solutions.
Teams may consist of UG, PG, and Research Scholars (pursuing PhD) across any branch, any Class/Year.
3. Begin Your Preparation
Study the domain related to your chosen problem statement.*
Review existing solutions and identify gaps.
Brainstorm innovative, practical, scalable, and implementable ideas.
Prepare an initial concept note, architecture, and implementation strategy.
Build a Proof-of-Concept (PoC), if possible, before the internal hackathon.
Focus on Innovation
Your solution should aim to be:
Innovative
Technically feasible
Scalable
Sustainable
User-centric
Capable of creating measurable social or industrial impact
The Problem Statements are displayed on the SIH2026 website:
https://sih.gov.in/

Internal Hackathon - VNIT Nagpur
The institute will conduct the SIH 2026 Internal Hackathon during September 2026 . The exact date and schedule will be announced next week.
Participation in the Internal Hackathon is mandatory for teams seeking nomination to represent the institute in Smart India Hackathon 2026 . The institute will evaluate all participating teams, and only the best-performing teams will be nominated for SIH 2026, as per the guidelines.
Important SIH 2026 Guidelines
Teams must consist of 6 members .
Every team must include at least one female member .
All team members should be from VNIT.
Multidisciplinary teams are encouraged.
Teams may choose problem statements from any theme available on the official SIH 2026 portal.
Maximum student participation is encouraged to foster healthy competition.
Start Early!
The best SIH teams usually spend several weeks refining their ideas before the internal hackathon.
Students are therefore advised to begin:
exploring problem statements,
forming teams,
studying the domain,
identifying mentors,
and developing innovative solutions well before the Internal Hackathon .
