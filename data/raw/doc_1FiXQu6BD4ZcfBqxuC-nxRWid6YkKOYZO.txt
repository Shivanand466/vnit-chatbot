VNIT Welcomes Distinguished Alumni from the 1992 CSE Batch
Strengthening Lifelong Bonds and Global Alumni Engagement
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur, greeted two distinguished alumni from the 1992 Computer Science & Engineering batch - Venkat Mukkamala, Senior Director at Google, New York and Hari Krishna Nandiyala, Senior Director at Meta, New Jersey. Their visit turned into a nostalgic journey through the VNIT campus, rekindling memories of student life and the cherished days. Accompanied by the VNIT Alumni Association (VNITAA) team, they revisited several iconic campus landmarks, including Block 9, the popular Chandrama Night Canteen, the Olympic-size Swimming Pool, the VISWA Life Skill Club (VLSC), and Gurudakshina - the flagship alumni-driven project that symbolizes gratitude and lifelong commitment to the institute. During their visit, the alumni interacted with VNIT leadership, including Director Prof. P. L. Patel, Dean (International & Alumni Affairs) Prof. K. M. Bhurchandi, and Associate Dean Prof. Sunil Bhatt. In recognition of their generous contributions and enduring support to their alma mater, Venkat Mukkamala was honored with a Gratitude Plaque as a 'Diamond Sponsor,' while Hari Krishna Nandiyala received a Gratitude Plaque as a 'Gold Sponsor.' The honors were presented by Director Prof. P. L. Patel. From VNITAA side Mr. Joginder Singh Sond and Mr. Uday Kamat contributed to the discussion on future initiative while Mr. Anand Kamat presented his ideas on Brand building. A fruitful discussion was also held on strengthening alumni engagement and enhancing VNIT's
global presence. Key areas deliberated during the meeting included:
Enhancing outreach and global visibility of VNIT through strategically positioned alumni across leading organizations worldwide.
Encouraging alumni contributions towards academic excellence, research collaborations, mentoring, and industry engagement for further improvements.
Creating a strong global network to connect VNIT alumni with current students and researchers across different countries.
Facilitating short-term (up to 2 months) and long-term (one semester) international internship opportunities for VNIT students through alumni networks in foreign countries.
The VNIT alumni have promised VNIT strong support for these activities and assured the institute, of their wholehearted commitment in advancing these initiatives and fostering stronger international opportunities for VNIT students and faculty.
The visit served as a powerful reminder that while alumni may travel across the globe and achieve remarkable success, their connection with VNIT remains strong. Such interactions continue to strengthen the institute's vibrant alumni network and inspire future generations of students.
