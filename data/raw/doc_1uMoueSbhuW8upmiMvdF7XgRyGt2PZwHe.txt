Dear Ma'am/ Sir,
We are pleased to announce that applications are now open for the SDEF India Scholarship 202627 , offered by the Swami Dayanand Education Foundation (SDEF) to support academically deserving students from financially constrained families pursuing professional undergraduate education in India.
We kindly request your support in sharing this scholarship opportunity with eligible students at your institution .
SDEF India Scholarship Program 2026-27
Award: Up to ₹50,000 per academic year , renewable for up to four years, with total support of up to ₹2,00,000 .
Eligible students must:
Be entering the first year of a full-time undergraduate programme in 2026-27
Have achieved a minimum of 80% in Class XII or equivalent CGPA
Demonstrate financial need
Meet the applicable entrance examination criteria
Have no gap/drop year after Class XII
Eligible Undergraduate Programmes
The scholarship supports students pursuing:
B.Tech / B.E.
MBBS
BDS
B.Sc Nursing / B.Nursing
B.Pharm / Pharm.D
B.Arch
BVSc & AH
Scholarship renewal is reviewed annually. Students must maintain satisfactory academic progress and have no academic backlogs to remain eligible for renewal.
Applications are currently open.
