Report on Visit of French Delegation to VNIT Nagpur
High-Level Interaction Meeting and Student Session on Opportunities in France
Date:
17 March 2026
A distinguished delegation from Campus France visited Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur on 17 March 2026 to explore avenues for academic cooperation, research collaboration, student mobility, and higher education opportunities in France.
Distinguished Members of the Delegation
Dr. Philippe Maurin - Scientific & Higher Education Attaché, Consulate General of France, Mumbai
Ms. Shalaka Joshi - Manager, Campus France (Mumbai & North Maharashtra)
Dr. Mukesh Barange - CESI France
1. High-Level Meeting with Institute Leadership
A formal meeting was held in the Board Room with the Hon'ble Director, Deans, Associate Deans, Heads of Departments, Coordinator CESI of VNIT Nagpur.
The delegation was formally welcomed by the Hon'ble Director, Prof. Kishor Bhurchandi , who delivered the opening remarks. In his address, he highlighted the institute's growing international engagement and various global initiatives undertaken by VNIT, including:
Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with foreign universities
Student and faculty exchange programmes
International internships
Joint research collaborations
Admission of international students
Participation in international education fairs and academic platforms such as RUSH
Efforts to strengthen VNIT's global academic footprint
This was followed by a round of introductions of all participants.
A video presentation showcasing VNIT's academic activities, research strengths, infrastructure, and campus life was screened for the delegation.
Subsequently, the Director delivered his remarks emphasizing VNIT's commitment to expanding international partnerships and welcoming collaboration with French institutions.
An open discussion followed, during which both sides explored concrete areas of cooperation, including academic exchange, joint research projects, mobility programmes, and funding opportunities.
The meeting concluded with a formal vote of thanks delivered by Prof. Kishor Bhurchandi.
The session was moderated and smoothly coordinated by Dr. Makarand M. Lokhande, Associate Dean (International Affairs & Ranking) .
2. Student Interaction Session - Opportunities in France
Following the leadership meeting, an interaction session was conducted for students separately for UG and PG students and research scholars.
During this session, the French delegation provided comprehensive information about pursuing higher education and research in France through Campus France. Key aspects covered included:
Study opportunities in France (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, Postdoctoral)
Application procedures and admission pathways
Apprenticeship-based education models
Scholarships, funding schemes, and financial support
Student support systems available in France
Healthcare and medical facilities
Accommodation through CROUS residences
Student life, culture, and tourism opportunities
Job prospects during and after studies
Presence of numerous French companies in India and Indian companies in France, enhancing employment opportunities
The speakers emphasized France's strong industry-academia ecosystem and global career prospects for international graduates.
Students from B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD programmes attended in large numbers and actively engaged in the session.
3. Future Collaboration and Exit Meeting
During the concluding interaction, both sides expressed interest in developing concrete collaborative initiatives. Discussions included:
Expanding institutional partnerships between VNIT and French universities
Facilitating student mobility and research exchanges
Participation in academic and industry networking platforms
Exploring joint programmes and research projects
It was also proposed to maintain continued engagement through participation in B2B academic and industry interaction events regularly held in Pune and Mumbai.
The visit was highly productive and strengthened the foundation for future Indo-French collaboration in higher education, research, and innovation.
VNIT Nagpur expressed its appreciation to the French delegation for their valuable visit and looks forward to translating the discussions into meaningful long-term partnerships.
The programme concluded with a group photograph.
