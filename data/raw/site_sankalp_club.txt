SANKALP Club at VNIT Nagpur is a student-led organization dedicated to fostering social responsibility, awareness, and community development. Through various initiatives, the club works towards health advocacy, education, and societal welfare, motivating to acquire a healthy cum positive lifestyle. With an enthusiastic and committed team, SANKALP Club strives to bring about meaningful change. It created under the initiatives of NMBA, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Govt. of India.
About NMBA – https://nmba.dosje.gov.in/content/about-us
Founded in: September, 2024
Professor incharge(s): Dr. Syed Taqi Ali & Dr. Sonal Dhanvijay
NMBA Nodal officer: Dr. Vijayakuma R.P., Associate Dean (HA)

Event 1: On 13 th
November 2024, an online Guest Lecture is conducted at LH-2 smart classroom VNIT, by Shri Rajesh Ahuja, Managing Director, Citi, on the topic “Technology Trends in IT Industry, Implications for Employment and How to Prepare for the Future”.
