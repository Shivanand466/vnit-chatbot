Dr. M. Ghosal​
Brief
Bio
Brief Bio
Architect-Urban Designer, having an experience of fifteen years in teaching, research and consultancy at different institutions. Research interest is to explore the application of Complexity Science concepts in Urban Design, with emphasis on complexity of built environment in public realm. Presently working on building agent-based-model of public realm to have better insight into public realm considered as a complex system.
