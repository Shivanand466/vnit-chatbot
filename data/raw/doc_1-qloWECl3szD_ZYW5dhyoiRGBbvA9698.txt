ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT
Established in 1960, the Department of Civil Engineering at VNIT Nagpur is one of the oldest and most prestigious departments of the institute. The department offers B-Tech in Civil Engineering, and M-Tech programs in Environmental Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, Structural Engineering, Transportation Engineering, and Water Resources Engineering, along with a Ph.D. program in multiple specializations. The department is actively engaged in cutting-edge research and consultancy projects funded by government bodies, public sector undertakings, and private industries. It has a rich history of contributing to nation-building through projects in infrastructure development, water management, environmental sustainability, and transportation systems. Faculty members and students are involved in innovative research areas such as sustainable materials, smart infrastructure, remote sensing and GIS, wastewater treatment, traffic safety, and climate-resilient structures. Consultancy works like structural design, stability audits, thirdparty inspections, and environmental assessments are routinely undertaken.
