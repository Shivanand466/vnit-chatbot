Programme Outcomes (POs)
The program enables the students with enhanced know how in Design process, Technology, and socio-cultural domains to address the emerging challenges in the field of Architecture.
The program enhances the student’s skills in handling various tools and techniques relevant to architectural design and embrace the emerging changes at global level.
The program induces the thought process adapting to the varying needs of the society due to socio-technical transformations and sensitizing students to the allied discipline of architecture.
The program provides an opportunity to the students to explore upcoming domains in built environment through the introduction of relevant electives.
Programme Outcomes (POs)
The programme endeavors to develop and promote national and international standards in urban planning education and practice by addressing emerging demands reflected through national and global policy decisions towards creating sustainable human settlements. It further promotes the ability of a student in performing technical research and development activities.
It aims to provide information of practical value to the community, local and state government authorities, developers, engineers, designers, academics and students to promote leading edge and world’s best practices in the fields of urban planning and development.
The programme offers motivated individuals with all the necessary spatial design and development planning skills to meet the challenges of a rapidly urbanizing world. It equips the future urban planning professionals towards preparing policies that help shape economically viable, socially suitable, environmentally sustainable, safe and healthy communities.
