Dr. Meera Dhabu
Brief Bio
Brief Bio
Dr. Meera Dhabu is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at VNIT, Nagpur, with over 25 years of experience. She has supervised 4 doctoral and 32 Master's students and published more than 20 papers in esteemed international journals. As the Professor In-charge of the Student Mentor Programme (SMP) at VNIT, she demonstrates her commitment to student development and mentorship.Her research interests include Machine Learning, Optimization for Cyber Physical Systems, and Data Analytics. Dr. Dhabu has made significant contributions through consultancy projects for organizations such as FDCM Gorewada Zoo Limited and Maharashtra State Electric Transmission Company Ltd. (MSETCL), and Chandrapur Forest Academy.Dr. Dhabu has been an active participant in academic and professional conferences, organizing the 7th ACM COMPUTE International Conference in 2014 at VNIT Nagpur and the 16th International Conference on Management of Data (COMAD) in 2010. She also served as Registration Co-chair for the 8th ACM COMPUTE International Conference in 2015 in Delhi and has conducted over 10 workshops on Advanced Data Analytics and Machine Learning.As a core committee member of the Rashtriya Avishkar Abhiyan (RAA), a MHRD initiative, Dr. Dhabu guides students and teachers from six NMC schools in Nagpur in Science and Mathematics. Her extensive experience and dedication make her a valuable asset to the academic and research community.
