I invite your attention to the references cited.
In the reference cited, it has been informed that the Government of Tamil Nadu has introduced a scholarship scheme, in which the BC/MBC/DNC students belongs to Tamil Nadu whose Parental Annual income is below Rs.2.50 lakh pursuing UG/PG courses in IIT, IIM, NIT, IIIT and other Central Government Universities could avail scholarship for the actual fees paid or Rs.2.00 Lakh per annum whichever is less till the completion of Course.
The eligibility and other criteria for availing scholarship is enclosed with this letter. I request you to exhibit the details of this Scholarship scheme annexed to this letter on the notice board at conspicuous places in your institution for creating awareness among the students about this scheme.
The format of Application for applying Fresh and Renewal scholarship is enclosed herewith and you are requested to get the filled Fresh and Renewal scholarship application form for the year 2025-26 from the eligible candidates along with required documents (i.e Bonafide, Fee receipt, Aadhar, Community certificate, Income certificate, Nativity certificate, 12th mark sheet, Proof of Residential Address, Bank passbook first page copy) and forward the same to this department with your recommendation in a covering letter (format enclosed) on or before 30.09.2025 for Renewal Application and 31.10.2025 for Fresh Application for further action.

Annexure II CIRCULAR
The Govt. of Tamil Nadu have issued orders to grant Scholarship to the students belonging to BC/MBC/DNC Communities who are pursuing UG/PG Courses in the Institutions of Govt. of India like IIT, IIM, IIIT, NIT and Central Universities.
Scholarship to the BC/MBC/DNC students pursuing UG/PG Courses in the Institutions of Govt. of India (viz) IIT, IIM, IIIT, NIT and Central Universities.
Conditions:
The student should belong to BC/MBC/DNC
The student should be a native of Tamil Nadu.
The student should pursue UG/PG Courses in IIT, IIM, IIIT, NIT and other Central Universities.
The family annual income should not exceed Rs.2.50 Lakh Per Annum
If any false information found during the field inspection, necessary penal action will be taken against the student.
Those students who receiving Scholarship from other schemes of Government of India and State Government of India are ineligible to receive this Scholarship.
Scholarship Amount:
The Government will reimburse to the students Scholarship amount comprising of Tuition Fee, Special Fee, Exam Fee and other compulsory Fees as paid by the student or Rs.2.00 Lakh per year whichever is lesser.
The above scholarship amount will be reimbursed to the student every year for the entire duration of their UG/PG Courses.
If more no. of applications were received, the eligible students will be shortlisted with respect to their marks secured in the +2 public exams.
