Top 3 students securing the highest marks in previous year’s examination are eligible to apply for
OPJEMS
. To determine the scholarship awardees, multiple rounds were conducted. First, the business proposal round(based on innovative and sustainable solutions to the problems faced by India) and the online test. Shortlisted candidates appeared for the interviews. Between 15th September – 22nd October the selection procedure was conducted. We are extremely proud to announce that three of our department students, Yash Singh (second year), Parth Mohril (third year) & Abhishek Mukherjee (final year) are among the top 100 awardees to secure the OPJEMS scholarship for the academic year 2021 in the engineering domain.
