ABOUT THE INSTITUTE
The Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), (formerly known as VRCE) Nagpur, is an Institute of National importance established by an Act of Parliament, National Institute of technology (Amendment) Act 2012 (No. 28 of 2012) in continuation of Principal Act (29 of 2007) and fully funded by Central Govt. of India. Earlier, the Institute was known as Visvesvaraya Regional College of Engineering (VRCE) named after the eminent Bharat Ratna awardee engineer, planner, and statesman of the country Sir M. Visvesvaraya. It was established in the year 1960 under the scheme sponsored by Govt. of India and Govt. of Maharashtra. The institute is centrally located in the state of Maharashtra and has green residential campus of 214 acres.

INSTITUTE VISION
To contribute effectively to the national and international endeavour of producing quality human resource of world class standard by developing a sustainable technical education system to meet the changing technological needs of the country and the world, incorporating relevant social concerns and to build an environment to create and propagate innovative technologies for the economic development of the nation.
INSTITUTE MISSION
The mission of VNIT is to achieve high standards of excellence in generating and propagating knowledge in engineering and allied disciplines. VNIT is committed to providing an education that combines rigorous academics with joy of discovery. The Institute encourages its community to engage in a dialogue with society to be able to effectively contribute for the betterment of humankind.

1 ORDINANCES
These Ordinances will be called and referred as B. Tech. / B. Arch. (UG) and M. Tech. / M. Sc. (PG) Rules of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur (VNIT).
This booklet provides comprehensive information on the Rules for B. Tech. / B. Arch. (UG) and M. Tech. / M. Sc. (PG) programs.
The provisions in these Rules govern the conditions for admissions, structure of program, imparting course instructions (imparting courses of instructions), conduction of examinations and evaluation of performance of students leading to the B. Tech. / B. Arch. (UG) and M. Tech. / M. Sc. (PG) degree programs at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur (VNIT).
This ordinance supersedes all the previous ordinances, rules and regulations and are effective from the academic year 2024-2025 onward.
The curriculum intends to provide broad based knowledge and simultaneously builds a temper for the lifelong process of learning and exploring. At undergraduate level, a student needs to do compulsory foundation courses in the areas of Humanities, Basic Sciences and Social Sciences apart from the departmental requirements. Departmental courses (core and electives) constitute minimum 50% of the total curriculum. Further, students are allowed open electives to broaden inter-disciplinary knowledge base and to specialize in an area outside the parent discipline.
The Institute follows the semester system. An academic year is from July to June next year. Each academic year is divided into two semesters, each lasting 20 weeks, inclusive of evaluation, grade finalization, and other academic activities. Each semester provides at least 70 teaching days, ensuring a minimum of 40 teaching contact hours per week, spread over five working days. The semester running from the third week of July to the first week of December is referred to as the ODD SEMESTER (Autumn / Winter) , while the semester from January to mid-May is termed the EVEN SEMESTER (Spring / Summer) . This is followed immediately by the Summer Term which usually ends in the second week of July. Detailed schedule is provided in the Academic Calendar , which is made available before the start of the academic year.
Keeping in view the new technical developments and to allow students some freedom to explore topics of their interest, an Undergraduate Program curriculum provides additional opportunities and flexibility for students to optimize their learning experience. This needs continuous and meticulous planning of the academic profile on the part of each student to fully utilize the opportunities. The students, and parents / guardians, are therefore, advised in their own interest to get fully familiar with the academic system of the Institute. Student's attention is brought particularly to the assessment procedures and the specific rules governing the grading system, academic performance requirements etc. Institute offers its students best opportunities that are needed to create outstanding scientists, architects and engineers. It is
hoped that students will strive hard to utilize the resources available to fully develop their potential as an engineer / scientist / designer / economist / social scientist / architect and planner.
The medium of instruction of all the UG / PG courses in the Institute is English .
The provisions of these Rules shall also be applicable to any new discipline that will be introduced from time to time and added to the list of Academic Programs.
Notwithstanding all that has been stated in the Ordinance and Rules, Senate of the Institute has the right to review, change, and modify the rules from time to time or as the case may be.
In case of any doubt or dispute arising about the interpretation of the Ordinance and Rules, the same shall be referred to the Chairperson, Senate whose decision shall be final and binding for all the concerned.
2 INTRODUCTION
The curriculum is designed to provide a comprehensive knowledge base while fostering a mindset for lifelong learning and exploration. At the undergraduate level, students are required to complete foundational courses in basic sciences, humanities, social sciences, and engineering, in addition to fulfilling departmental requirements. Additionally, open elective courses enable students to develop a diverse interdisciplinary knowledge base. At the postgraduate level, students are encouraged to specialize in a chosen domain.
To keep pace with evolving technological advancements and provide students with the flexibility to explore their areas of interest, the program curriculum incorporates additional opportunities, allowing them to tailor their learning experience for optimal academic and professional growth.
The medium of instruction for all courses is English .
2.1 Organizational Structure for Academic Administration
The academic programs of the institute are governed by Rules and Regulations approved by the Senate from time to time. The Senate is a statutory and supreme body that governs all academic matters of the institute and the rulings of the Senate Chairman (Director of the Institute) are final in regard to all the academic issues.
A definite time schedule is set by the Senate for various academic activities, through an Academic Calendar approved by Senate and issued at the beginning of each academic session / year. The Senate continuously assesses the academic programs and makes appropriate revisions / modifications / improvements as and when required. Performance of each student is monitored by a Faculty Advisor . Administrative back-up for all academic matters is provided by the Academic Section and Exam Section .
2.2 Academic Calendar
The academic activities of the Institute are regulated by Academic Calendar approved by Senate. It is mandatory for students / faculty to strictly adhere to the academic calendar for completion of academic activities. Academic Calendar can be seen at the Academic Office homepage of the institute website ( www.vnit.ac.in ).

3 COURSE STRUCTURE, CREDIT SYSTEM AND EVALUATION
3.1 Credit System
Education at the institute is structured around a semester-based credit system . Key features of this system include continuous evaluation of a student's performance / progress and flexibility to enable students to progress at an optimum pace tailored to their abilities and convenience, while ensuring they meet the minimum requirements for continuation.
A student's performance is assessed based on the number of credits earned, i.e., those completed satisfactorily. Using the course credits and the grades obtained, the Grade Point Average is calculated. Students must maintain a minimum grade point average for satisfactory progress and continuation in the program. Additionally, a minimum number of earned credits and a minimum grade point average are required to qualify for the degree. Details can be found in the Section 5 - Degree Requirements, Regulations, and Procedures.
3.2 Course Credits Assignment
Each course, except a few special courses, has certain number of credits assigned to it depending on lecture, tutorial and laboratory contact hours in a week.
Lectures and Tutorials: One lecture hour per week per semester is assigned one credit .
Practical / Laboratory / Studio: One laboratory / studio hour per week per semester is assigned half credit.
Example: Course XXXXXX: (3-0-2: 4 cr) has 3 hr. Lectures + 0 hr. Tutorial + 2 hr. Practical per week. The credits indicated for this course are computed as follows:
3 hours/week lectures = 3 credits 0 hours/week tutorial = 0 credit 2 hours/week practical = 0.5 x 2 = 1 credit. Total = 3 + 0 + 1 = 4 credits
Some courses do not carry any credits and are referred to as Non-Credit (NC) courses.

3.9.2 Description of Grades
AA grade: This grade stands for outstanding achievement.
CC grade: The CC grade stands for average performance. This average performance refers to 'average' as per instructor's expectations in a holistic sense and not only the average marks obtained by the class.
DD grade: The DD grade stands for marginal performance. It is the minimum passing grade in any course. Minimum marks for award of DD grade should not be higher than 45% for L type of courses and 50% for other types of courses. However, individual course coordinators may set a lower marks requirement. However, the lower requirement is not expected to be below 30.
FF grade: The FF grade denotes very poor performance, i.e. failing a course.
A student is required to repeat all the core courses in which he / she obtains FF grade, till he / she obtains a passing grade.
For other (Department Elective / Open) courses if FF grade is obtained, then, a student may register for the same course again or any other course from the same category.
Further, FF grades secured in any course stay permanently on the grade card.
Student securing FF grade in End Semester Examination is eligible for Re-Examination and also for the subsequent Summer Term. (See Section 5.4).
LL grade: When a student is absent in End Semester Examination or Re-Examination, LL grade is awarded to the student. (See for 3.11.1.4 for more details)
NP and NF grades: These grades are awarded in a course opted as an audit course by a student. The Audit Pass (NP) grade is awarded if the student's attendance is above 75% in the class and he / she has qualified for at least DD grade. If either of these requirements is not fulfilled, the Audit Fail (NF) grade is awarded. The grades obtained in an audit course are not considered in calculation of SGPA or CGPA. No credits are earned through Audit courses.
SS and ZZ grades: The SS grade denotes satisfactory performance and completion of a non- credit course / attendance based courses. The ZZ grade is awarded for non- completion of the course requirements. In case, if it is a core / compulsory course, the student must register for the course until he / she obtains the SS grade
GG grade: When an M. Tech. student needs to undergo additional courses, after earning all the requisite credits, for meeting the minimum 6.0 CGPA criterion, this grade is used. Details are available in Section 5.2.3.
XX grade : XX grade is awarded to indicate the semester withdrawal of the student.
3.10 Evaluation of Performance
The performance of a student will be evaluated in terms of two indices, viz, the Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) which is the Grade Point Average for a given semester and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) which is the Grade Point Average for all the completed semesters at any point in time.
CGPA will be rounded up to second decimal place.
The Earned Credits (ECR) are defined as the sum of course credits for courses in which students have been awarded grades between AA to DD. Grades obtained in the audit courses are not counted for computation of grade point average.

The evaluation process for "L Type" courses including assessment criteria and methodologies to ensure comprehensive and fair evaluation, is detailed in this section.
3.11.1.1 Course Assessment
Course evaluation of Lecture / Tutorial component shall be on the basis of:
One Mid Semester Examination,
One End Semester Examination and
Teacher Defined Assessment through tests, quizzes, assignments, seminars, group discussions etc.
Teacher Defined Assessment shall be on the basis of at least two independent assessment heads, with at least one assessment before the mid semester exam and at least one assessment after the mid semester exam. Refer Table 5 for weightages to be assigned to different evaluation components.
3.11.1.2 Written Examination
Mid Semester Examination and End Semester Examination will be conducted and result shall be declared as per schedule in Academic Calendar.
For mid semester and end semester examinations, numbered answer booklets with the department stamp are used. During the exam, students must record the serial number of their answer booklet on the exam attendance sheet while signing it.
Evaluated answer books shall be shown to the students. There is no provision of revaluation / rechecking of the answer books. Students are advised to utilize this opportunity to get their doubts / grievances, addressed properly by the course coordinator. Students are advised not to pressurize the Course Coordinator for undue increase in marks or lowering the 'pass' cut off. Any act of this or similar nature will be treated as unfair means. In spite of all these measures, if grievance exists, the student will immediately appeal to the concerned Head of the Department for redressal.
3.11.1.3 Absentee in Mid Semester Examination
A student who remains absent for mid semester examination without intimation shall be awarded zero marks in the mid semester examination. Students who remain absent for mid semester examination, can be subjected to suitable mode of evaluation by the course coordinator within 10 working days from the last date of the respective mid semester examination. This provision is made only for genuine cases accounting for illness and circumstances beyond control of student. A student should apply to the concerned course coordinator with recommendations of Faculty Advisor and with relevant documents.
Students who remain absent for mid semester examination due to medical reasons, shall approach Course coordinator for a make-up test immediately on return to the class. The request shall be supported with a medical certificate from the Institute Health Center. A certificate from a registered medical practitioner will also be accepted from students normally residing off- campus, provided registration and contact details of the medical practitioner appear explicitly on the certificate. Note that a medical certificate does not automatically entitle a student to an absentee test. It is at the discretion of the course coordinator to consider the application.
3.11.1.4 Absentee in End Semester and Re-Examination
A student who remains absent for end semester examination will be awarded LL grade. Such a student will not be eligible to appear for re-examination also. Similarly, if the student remains absent in re-examination, LL grade will be awarded and such student will not be eligible for subsequent Summer Term also.
If the student is absent in the end semester examination on medical ground or due to circumstances beyond control of the student, and if the student wishes to appear in re-examination, then student shall make a written application to Dean (Acd) to give permission to appear in the re-examination. This application shall be made through Faculty Advisor and HoD and shall reach Dean (Acd) before the completion of the entire end semester examination. 1 st year B. Tech. / B. Arch. students shall give application through Faculty Advisor / Faculty Mentor and 1 st year B. Tech. Coordinator. All the relevant documents which justify the reason for absence from the end semester examination shall be attached to this application.
A Central Committee under the chairmanship of Dean (Acd) shall examine the case. This committee shall include Medical Officer, and two HoDs. Committee's decision on the student's request will be final.
Similarly, if the student is absent in the Re- examination and if the student wishes to register for the subsequent Summer Term, the same procedure defined above is to be followed. There are additional conditions on registration for the Summer Term. (See Section 5.4)
Student remaining absent in End Semester Exam for reasons like appearing for other competitive exams on his / her own will not to be permitted for re-exam. Similarly, student remaining absent in re-examination for reasons like appearing for other competitive exams on his / her own will not to be eligible for subsequent Summer Term.
In case of death of close family members (parent / sibling / spouse) only, application for permission for Re- End Semester Examination / Summer Term could be considered.
3.11.1.5 Special Evaluation Methods for Students with Learning Disabilities
For students with learning disability, special evaluation methods, as approved by the Senate are also available.
3.11.2 Course Evaluation of Practical (P Type) & Practical Component of L Type Courses
The evaluation process for "P type" courses & Practical Component of L type courses is detailed, here including assessment criteria and methodologies to ensure comprehensive and fair evaluation.
3.11.2.1 Course Assessment
Course Evaluation of Practical Courses (P Type) and Practical Component of L type Courses shall be based on continuous evaluation.
3.11.2.2 Continuous Assessment
Assessment can be conducted either in every class or in phases; however, the planned assessment method should be communicated at the beginning of the course. The weightage is as per the guidelines provided in Table 5.
3.11.2.3 Phased Assessment and Freezing of Marks
The continuous evaluation assessment marks shall be finalized as per the guidelines issued at the beginning of each academic session.
3.11.2.4 End Session Evaluation
Session End Evaluation is conducted at the end of the session and is based on the student's complete work throughout the session.
3.11.2.5 Board of Evaluator for End Session Evaluation
For Session End Evaluation, if recommended by the Department, examiner other than teacher, either from within the Institute or from outside the Institute can be nominated. Approval for external examiner, including financial aspect shall be obtained from the Director.
3.11.2.6 Policy on Re-Examination for Practical Courses
There will not be any Re-Examination for Practical Courses under any circumstances. Students must complete all practical assessments, experiments, and evaluations within the scheduled duration of the course as per the academic calendar. Any missed practical sessions due to valid reasons may be addressed in consultation with the lab course coordinator. It is the students' responsibility to ensure their attendance and active participation in all practical components as per the prescribed schedule.
3.11.3 Course Evaluation of Project (D) Type Courses
The evaluation framework for "D type" courses is outlined here, incorporating assessment criteria and methodologies designed to ensure a thorough and equitable evaluation process.
3.11.3.1 Evaluation Schedule and Weightages
Course evaluation shall be done in phased manner. Mid-session evaluations, two in number, shall be of 2030% weightage each. Session End evaluation shall be of 40-60% weightage. All evaluations will be conducted as per the guidelines issued at the beginning of each academic session.
3.11.3.2 Examination Board for UG Project Phase - II
For B. Tech. Project Phase II, UG project examination board shall conduct the examination. If a student's performance is not satisfactory then, the project examination board may give additional time of maximum two months for improvement. Refer Annexure - I for details.
3.11.3.3 Examination Board for PG Project Phase - II
For M. Tech. Project Phase II, PG project examination board shall conduct the examination. If a student's performance is not satisfactory then, the project examination board may give additional time of maximum two months for improvement. Refer Annexure - II for details.
3.11.3.4 Collaboration for PG Project
M. Tech. project can be done jointly with National Laboratories, IITs, IISc, Public Sector companies and industries, subject to the conditions defined by the Institute. Refer Annexure - III for details.
3.11.3.5 Absentee in Project Evaluation
Absenteeism for assessment on genuine grounds beyond the control of student can be condoned by the HoD, on recommendations from Faculty Advisor and Project Supervisor. Under such circumstances an alternate assessment chance is given to a student. However, grades should be finalized before stipulated date.
4 REGISTRATION AND ATTENDANCE
4.1 Registration & Enrollment
Registration is a very important procedural part of the academic system. The registration procedure ensures that the student's name is on the roll list of each course that he / she wants to study. Registration for courses to be taken in a particular semester will be done according to the specified schedule as announced in the academic calendar. Each student is required to complete the registration formality by indicating the slot-wise choice of courses. Web based registration facility is available. The choice of courses must be approved by his / her Faculty Adviser. Failure to complete registration procedure within the stipulated date is liable for termination of admission of a student.
4.2 Advice on Courses
At the time of registration and enrollment, each student must consult his / her Faculty Advisor to finalize the academic program, keeping in view factors such as, minimum / maximum numbers of total credits, past performance, backlog of courses, SGPA / CGPA, pre-requisite, work load and student's interests, amongst others.
Special provisions exist for academically weak students. It is the responsibility of the student to confirm that credits earned under different categories (DC, DE etc) are more than or equal to the minimum required in those respective categories.
4.3 Addition, Deletion, Audit and Withdrawal from Courses
HoD will recommend the cases of add / drop of courses by students under peculiar circumstances like,
Pre-registered courses could not be run due to non-availability of sufficient number of students.
Some new courses are offered which were not available to the student at the time of registration.
Department is withdrawing the offered courses for whatever reasons.
Student has cleared the courses after completing the registration formalities (during reexamination or summer term) or
Any other unforeseen but critical reasons, etc.
Registration done for OC or HM type course shall not be changed or dropped.
4.3.1 Audit Course
Student can register a course as an audit course, during his / her entire program, provided
The course is not available as a credit course OR
ii) The course is available as a credit course but due to various restrictions he / she could not register in the past.
The necessary prerequisites must be satisfied for registering a course as an audit course.
Maximum 2 courses can be registered as Audit courses during entire program (but one at a time).
The audit courses will be mentioned on the grade card with pass (NP) or fail (NF) grade.
Grades obtained in the audit courses are not counted for computation of grade point average.
4.4 Limits on Registration
B. Tech. / B. Arch. :
For the students admitted in 2021 and before: A student cannot register for more than six theory courses (L type) and four laboratory courses (including project) in a semester.
For the students admitted in 2022 onwards, a student may be permitted to register for up to 8 'L' type courses and 5 'P' or 'D' type courses to facilitate minor course registration or enable early completion of coursework for pursuing an internship during the final year of UG programs.
For the students admitted in 2021 and before, requiring less than 4 credits to complete his / her B. Tech. / B. Arch. degree is allowed to register to 7 th L type course (if available) in his / her Final semester only i.e., the semester in which the student will be acquiring the credits required for the award of degree (170 for B. Tech. / 219 for B. Arch.).
