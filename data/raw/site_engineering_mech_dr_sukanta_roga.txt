Dr. Sukanta Roga
Brief Bio
Dr. Sukanta Roga (World’s Top 2% Scientists as per Elsevier and Stanford University, USA ranking) serves as an Associate Professor in the Mechanical Engineering Department at Visvesvaraya NIT Nagpur. He brings 2 years of industry experience and almost 11 years of academic experience. He has successfully completed 3 externally sponsored R&D projects. He holds 3 patents and has contributed extensively to research, with 35 international journal publications, 2 national journal papers, 36 international conference papers, and 4 book chapter contributions. His research interests span Renewable Energy, Scramjet Engines, Propulsion and Power, CFD, Autonomous Vehicles and Data Analysis. He is also actively involved in collaborative R&D initiatives with various industries and academic institutions (IIT Delhi & IIT Kanpur)
