Steps to apply for an Institute Email ID-
Step 1:
Student who is willing to create their Institute email ID should keep his / her HoD / Faculty Advisor in Cc with proper subject while making a request on ncteam@vnit.ac.in.
Step 2:
Send the scanned copy of Institute Photo ID Card (i.e. PDF, JPEG format.). Email IDs will be created with the permission of HoD or Faculty Advisor only by replying to the relevant email.
Step 3:
Student should mention their Existing Email ID so that network center team will reply you back.
