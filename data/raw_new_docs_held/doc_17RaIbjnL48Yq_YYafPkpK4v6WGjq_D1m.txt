Note for Students under DASA scheme:
CIWG Candidates need to pay at par with the Fees applicable to Indian Student. Non-CIWG Candidates need to pay as per the Dollar values mentioned above.
Separate notice shall be displayed regarding guidelines of fee payment and online course registration. .
Students who have raised Education Loan are advised to obtain fee demand from, VNIT Website >> Student Notice Board >> Registration Tab. All such students are advised to expedile the process so as to be ready with fees amount in advance of schedule of fee payment and course registration. o
Date: November 04, 2024
For S25 session, fee challan will be available in students log in (Aims login >> Browse >> Fee >> Transaction >> Online Payment). Student can take print of chalan or save pdf of chalan by log in to the web-link https://aims.vnit.ac.in
Dates & Mode of Fees payment-
Dates & Mode of Fees payment -04-Nov-2024 to 24-Dec-2024 (Online payment). Separate notice will be issued for mode of payment. Fee payment module will be closed on the given deadline at 5pm.
Online Course Registration Activation - Course registration will be activated only after payment validation of fees/ challan reconciliation.
Payment not reconciled in due time period will be considered as late payment and late registration. According fine will be imposed.
All the students are advised to pay fees well in advance to activate their ONLINE registration before the last date of registration.
Students are advised to make payment of fees in advance to avoid late registration.
