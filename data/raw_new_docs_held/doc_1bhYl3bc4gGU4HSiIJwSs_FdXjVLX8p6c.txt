Note for Students under DASA scheme:
CIWG Candidates need to pay at par with the Fees applicable to Indian Student. Non-CIWG Candidates need to pay as per the Dollar values mentioned above.
Separate notice shall be displayed regarding guidelines of fee payment and online course registration.
Students who have raised Education Loan are advised to obtain fee demand from, VNIT Website >> Student Notice Board >> Registration Tab. All such students are advised to expedite the process so as to be ready with fees amount in advance of schedule of fee payment and course registration.
Date: April 23, 2025
For W25 session, fee challan will be available in student's log in (Aims login >> Browse >> Fee >> Transaction >> Online Payment). Student can take print of chalan or save pdf of chalan by log in to theweb-link https://aims.vnit.ac.in
Dates & Mode of Fees payment - 1-May-2025 to 08-June-2025(NEFT payment) and 1-May-2025 to 10-June-2025(Online payment). Separate notice will be issued for mode of payment. Fee payment module will be closed on the given deadline at 5pm.
Online Course Registration Activation - Course registration will be activated only after payment of fees through Online. If payment is done by NEFT, it will have to be validated by Account Section for activation of registration.
Payment not reconciled in due time period will be considered as late payment and late registration. According fine will be imposed.
All the students are advised to pay fees well in advance to activate their ONLINE registration before the last date of registration.
Students are advised to make payment of fees in advance to avoid late registration.
The AlMS portal is not compatible with mobile devices. Students must use a laptop or desktop for fee payment and online registration. DO NOT USE MOBILE PHONES FOR THESE TASKS.
